# 🚀 Multi-Language Content Engine — 1 Title → 6 Languages

## Core Strategy

The system must support a **One-to-Many Content Architecture**.

One master YouTube topic is created in English and then automatically transformed into six localized versions:

🇺🇸 English
🇩🇪 German
🇫🇷 French
🇳🇱 Dutch
🇳🇴 Norwegian
🇸🇪 Swedish

The goal is **NOT** to simply translate the English video.

Each language version must be **localized for its specific audience**, while preserving the same underlying research, facts, narrative logic, visual assets, and production structure.

---

# Master Content → Localization Pipeline

```text
MASTER TITLE
      ↓
MASTER RESEARCH
      ↓
MASTER SCRIPT
      ↓
MASTER VISUAL PLAN
      ↓
MASTER EDITING TIMELINE
      ↓
      ├── 🇺🇸 English
      ├── 🇩🇪 German
      ├── 🇫🇷 French
      ├── 🇳🇱 Dutch
      ├── 🇳🇴 Norwegian
      └── 🇸🇪 Swedish
```

Each language becomes an independent YouTube publishing package.

# 3. Do NOT Perform Literal Translation

The localization engine must NOT translate sentence-by-sentence mechanically.

Instead:

**TRANSLATE THE IDEA → LOCALIZE THE CONTENT → OPTIMIZE FOR THE AUDIENCE**

The localized version should sound as if it was originally written by a native creator.

Preserve:

* Meaning
* Facts
* Numbers
* Claims
* Story logic
* Narrative structure
* Emotional progression

Adapt where appropriate:

* Expressions
* Idioms
* Humor
* Examples
* Cultural references
* Search terminology
* Thumbnail wording
* Hook wording
* CTA wording

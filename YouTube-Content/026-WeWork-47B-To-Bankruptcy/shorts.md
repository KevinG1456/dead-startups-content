# Shorts Ideas (10)

Note: no "fraud/scam/lied" language in any Short below — Adam Neumann has not been charged with any
crime. Every Short below uses ironclad, undisputed facts only (dates, dollar figures, public
filings, the S-1's own language, the SPAC listing, the bankruptcy filing's own numbers). Always
present the valuation arc with dates attached — never a bare "WeWork was worth $X" without saying
which year. Always describe the 2023 Chapter 11 as a reorganization the company is still operating
under today, never as a shutdown or liquidation.

**1. "$47 Billion. Then $750 Million."**
Hook: "This company was worth $47 billion in 2019. A few years later, it was worth about $750
million." Script: quick three-point valuation-arc reveal ($47B → $9B → ~$750M), each figure dated
on screen. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**2. "He Walked Away With $1.7 Billion"**
Hook: "This company's IPO collapsed. Its founder still walked away with up to $1.7 billion."
Script: 30-second exit-package breakdown (shares, consulting fee, credit facilities). CTA: "Full
breakdown linked."

**3. "The Metric That Sank an IPO"**
Hook: "This company invented its own accounting metric to make its losses look smaller. It didn't
work." Script: quick "community-adjusted EBITDA" explainer, plain language. CTA: "Full story on the
channel."

**4. "Went Public Twice. Still Filed Bankruptcy."**
Hook: "This company tried to go public in 2019. It finally did in 2021. It still ended up in
bankruptcy court in 2023." Script: rapid two-IPO-attempts timeline. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**5. "$18.5 Billion vs. $16 Billion — Not the Same Number"**
Hook: "One investor's total exposure to this company was $18.5 billion. Its reported loss was $16
billion. Most coverage gets that mixed up." Script: quick side-by-side explainer of exposure vs.
loss. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**6. "Bankrupt — But Still Open Today"**
Hook: "This company filed for Chapter 11 in 2023. It's still operating today. Here's why those
two facts aren't a contradiction." Script: quick reorganization-vs-liquidation explainer. CTA:
"Full breakdown linked."

**7. "The Highest Valuation On This Channel"**
Hook: "Out of every company covered on this channel, this one reached the highest valuation ever —
$47 billion. It still went bankrupt." Script: quick scale-of-the-fall reveal. CTA: "Full story on
the channel."

**8. "He Started a New Company. A Top VC Backed It."**
Hook: "Three years after this founder's company collapsed, one of the biggest venture capital firms
in the world backed him again." Script: quick, neutral Flow/Andreessen Horowitz reveal, no
editorializing. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**9. "517 Companies, One Filing"**
Hook: "This company's bankruptcy filing included 517 other legal entities at the same time."
Script: quick explainer on the scale of the corporate structure behind the filing. CTA: "Full story
on the channel."

**10. "No Fraud. No Conviction. Still This."**
Hook: "Nobody in this story has been charged with a crime. Here's how a company still lost 98% of
its value in a single year." Script: reveal 2023's stock decline, framed explicitly as a governance
story, not a fraud story. CTA: "New autopsy every week — subscribe."

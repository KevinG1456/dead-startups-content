# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the $47B-to-Chapter-11
  date-collision reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- $47B-to-Chapter-11 date-collision animation for the cold open ("$47 BILLION — 2019 PEAK" hard-
  cutting to "CHAPTER 11 — NOVEMBER 6, 2023")
- SoHo storefront / lease-vs-membership mismatch diagram for the founding segment
- Funding timeline graphic climbing to $47B, paired with a split-box callout showing "$18.5B total
  exposure" next to "$16B reported loss" as two visually distinct, separately labeled figures —
  never merged into one number
- S-1 document graphic; "community-adjusted EBITDA" displayed as on-screen text with a plain-language
  translation beneath it
- Exit-package breakdown graphic building line by line ($970M shares / $185M consulting fee / $500M
  credit facilities) to a $1.7B total
- SoftBank rescue-package breakdown graphic building to $9.5B
- Valuation-arc graphic that updates live across the video: "$47B (2019)" appears in Segment 2, joined
  by "$9B (Oct 2021 SPAC)" in Segment 6, joined by "~$750M (post-2024 emergence)" in Segment 8 — held
  complete on screen from that point forward
- Stock-decline chart falling through 2023 (>98% decline), paired with an assets ($15B) vs.
  liabilities ($18.6B) graphic for the Chapter 11 filing
- Ownership-breakdown pie/bar graphic for the post-emergence structure (Cupar Grimmond ~60% / SoftBank
  ~20% / other lenders ~20%)
- Flow launch graphic with a generic "top-tier VC" text treatment (no real logos) and the $2.5B figure
  as the coda's final number
- Final comparison card: WeWork alongside Stay Alfred (021) and Sonder (025) — "same structural
  shape, three different scales"

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Britannica Money, SEC
  filings, TechCrunch, CNBC, Forbes, Al Jazeera, Orrick, Davis Polk, Yahoo Finance, 2727coworking.com)
- "SoftBank's total exposure" paired immediately with "SoftBank's reported loss" as two separate
  lower thirds, every single time either figure appears — no exceptions
- "Reorganization, not liquidation — still operating today" standing lower third whenever the 2023
  Chapter 11 filing or 2024 emergence is discussed
- "No criminal charges — governance story" lower third the first time Adam Neumann's exit package or
  the IPO collapse is discussed, to set the frame early
- "$47B (2019 private peak)" / "$9B (Oct 2021 SPAC)" / "~$750M (post-bankruptcy)" — always paired
  with its date, never displayed as a bare number
- "What happened next — not vindication, not condemnation" lower third at the start of the Flow coda

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/coworking imagery (no real
  readable logos/text — WeWork, SoftBank, NYSE, BowX, Andreessen Horowitz — without rights clearance)
- Exit-package line items type on screen as they're read aloud, building to the $1.7B total
- Valuation-arc numbers animate in as a literal downward staircase, each step landing with its date
- Flow coda animates in with a deliberately neutral, flat visual tone — no triumphant or ominous
  scoring cues, matching the even-handed narration

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the valuation-arc graphic build
  and the exit-package breakdown

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founding / The Raise and the Peak: brisk, confident, building energy
- The IPO Filing and Its Collapse: bright, legitimacy-signaling tone that curdles as the backlash
  details land
- The Resignation and the Exit Package: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the breakdown graphic
  carry the weight, no triumphant or outraged scoring
- The First Rescue / Going Public: measured, businesslike
- The Second Collapse: sharp tonal drop as the stock-decline chart falls and the filing details land
- Emergence, Not Liquidation: neutral, clarifying tone — this is a "correcting the record" beat, not
  a triumphant one
- The Coda: deliberately flat, neutral texture — no scoring that implies vindication or condemnation
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal in the cold open and throughout the timeline
- Distinct "step down" SFX each time the valuation-arc graphic adds a new, lower figure
- Sharp "crash" SFX as the stock-decline chart falls during The Second Collapse
- No stinger or musical cue on the Flow coda reveal that could read as celebratory or damning —
  keep it flat and factual
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and even-handed, especially during the
  exit-package and Flow-coda segments, which must never read as outrage-baiting or as endorsement

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** confident and brisk during the founding/raise/peak segments, sharpening into
  controlled tension through the IPO collapse and resignation, settling into measured, even-handed
  authority for the rescue/SPAC/second-collapse sequence, and a distinctly neutral, non-judgmental
  register for the Flow coda
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, with deliberate slowdown on the SoftBank exposure-vs-loss
  distinction and the reorganization-not-liquidation distinction — both need room to land precisely,
  on pace with their on-screen lower thirds
- **Emotional tone:** upbeat/ambitious during the founding and raise; sharp and serious (never
  outraged) through the IPO collapse and exit package; matter-of-fact authority for the two rescues
  and the SPAC; measured gravity for the Chapter 11 filing; calm and clear for the emergence segment;
  deliberately neutral, almost clinical, for the Flow coda — no vindication or condemnation in the
  voice
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine gravity on the exit-package breakdown
  without editorializing; visibly even-handed (not smug, not outraged) during the Flow coda
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  valuation-arc graphic reveal and the exit-package breakdown
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only ($47B-to-Chapter-11 date-collision animation)
- Script: "In 2019, this company was valued at $47 billion — the highest valuation of any company
  this channel has ever covered. In 2023, it filed for Chapter 11. In between, its founder walked
  away with up to $1.7 billion."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–0:55 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: confident, direct delivery
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 0:55–1:50 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: slightly warmer, teaching tone; brief tonal lift on the direct Sonder (025)
  cross-reference line
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Founding**
- Duration: 1:50–2:50 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (SoHo storefront imagery, lease-vs-membership
  mismatch diagram), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: brisk, confident, establishing energy
- Visual direction: mismatch diagram building

**Scene 05 — The Raise and the Peak**
- Duration: 2:50–4:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Alternates avatar-on-camera with full-screen funding-timeline
  graphic
- Performance direction: brisk, numbers-forward; deliberate slowdown and care on the "$18.5B
  exposure" vs. "$16B reported loss" split callout — never let the two figures blur together
- Visual direction: funding timeline climbs to $47B, exposure/loss split-box graphic holds separately

**Scene 06 — The IPO Filing and Its Collapse**
- Duration: 4:15–6:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with S-1 document graphic, full-screen for
  the "community-adjusted EBITDA" plain-language translation
- Performance direction: sharpening tone, precise and slightly incredulous (not mocking) on the
  invented-metric explainer
- Visual direction: "community-adjusted EBITDA" displayed as on-screen text with translation beneath

**Scene 07 — The Resignation and the Exit Package**
- Duration: 6:30–8:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, exit-package breakdown graphic builds
  line by line
- Performance direction: measured, deliberately non-outraged delivery — state the numbers plainly
  and let them carry the weight; explicit verbal note that this is a governance story, not a fraud
  story
- Visual direction: three line items ($970M shares / $185M consulting fee / $500M credit facilities)
  build to $1.7B total

**Scene 08 — The First Rescue**
- Duration: 8:15–9:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (rescue-package breakdown graphic), avatar VO
  under
- Performance direction: brisk, matter-of-fact
- Visual direction: rescue-package breakdown graphic builds to $9.5B

**Scene 09 — Going Public, For Real This Time**
- Duration: 9:15–10:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with SPAC-merger graphic, full-screen for
  the valuation-arc update
- Performance direction: measured, slight tonal drop on "an 80% discount from the 2019 peak"
- Visual direction: valuation-arc graphic updates to show $47B and $9B side by side

**Scene 10 — The Second Collapse**
- Duration: 10:45–12:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, stock-decline chart and assets-vs-
  liabilities graphic full-screen for the filing details
- Performance direction: measured gravity, deliberate pause before reading Neumann's one-word
  "disappointing" quote — let it land without additional commentary
- Visual direction: stock chart falls through 2023, assets ($15B) vs. liabilities ($18.6B) graphic,
  quote displayed on screen

**Scene 11 — Emergence, Not Liquidation**
- Duration: 12:30–13:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, completed three-point valuation-arc
  graphic full-screen
- Performance direction: calm, clear, deliberately emphatic on "this was a reorganization, not a
  liquidation — WeWork is still operating today"
- Visual direction: $47B → $9B → ~$750M valuation-arc graphic completes and holds

**Scene 12 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~12:45 (inside Scene 11) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into serious tone
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly alongside a small package-025 callout

**Scene 13 — The Coda: What Adam Neumann Did Next**
- Duration: 13:30–15:45 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (Flow launch graphic), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: deliberately neutral and even, almost clinical — no smirk, no outrage; this
  is the segment most at risk of tipping into editorializing, so performance must stay flat and
  factual
- Visual direction: Flow launch graphic, "top-tier VC" text treatment (no real logos), $2.5B figure
  displayed as the coda's final number

**Scene 14 — The Actual Lesson**
- Duration: 15:45–17:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with valuation-arc graphic and Stay
  Alfred/Sonder comparison card
- Performance direction: clear, patient teaching tone; this is the video's actual thesis payoff
- Visual direction: valuation-arc graphic held one final time alongside the 021/025 comparison card

**Scene 15 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 17:30–19:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: return to controlled-authority tone, steady eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements (package 025 and 021 callouts, subscribe) animate in during
  final 5 seconds only

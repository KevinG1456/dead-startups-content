# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. WeWork carries the highest brand
recognition of any company on this channel and is an evergreen search term, so timing is less
time-sensitive than a breaking-news package (contrast package 025/Sonder) — prioritize slotting this
into a strong weekly release window over rushing it out.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) with particular relevance to anyone evaluating
governance risk, founder-control structures, or lease-heavy business models, plus a very large
secondary audience drawn to a globally recognizable brand name, a well-known founder, and general
finance-news-adjacent viewers who remember the 2019 headlines or the WeCrashed drama but not the
full mechanics.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to a "Business Model Risk" playlist alongside package
021 (Stay Alfred) and package 025 (Sonder/Zeus Living) — all three share the same fixed-obligation-
versus-flexible-revenue structural risk at different scales and in different industries.

## End Screen
Package 025 (Sonder) + package 021 (Stay Alfred) + subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the valuation-arc graphic completion (Segment: Emergence, Not Liquidation) linking directly
to package 025 (Sonder), where an analyst's "WeWork of apartments" comparison originally inspired
this video; card at the mid-video CTA linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the video that led here, watch the Sonder autopsy — package 025 — where an
analyst's comparison to WeWork is what set this whole video in motion. And if your business has any
fixed obligation that doesn't flex with demand, or any governance structure that concentrates
control in one person, that's worth stress-testing before the market tests it for you. Subscribe if
you want the next one in the library."

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule, each thumbnail carries exactly
ONE idea. Do not try to cram the IPO collapse, SoftBank's bailout, the SPAC, the bankruptcy, AND the
Flow coda into a single image — those live in the video, not the thumbnail.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a single glowing "$47B" figure at the top, connected by a thin descending line to
  a small, muted "$750M" figure at the bottom — the entire valuation collapse in one glance, no other
  numbers included
- **Main subject:** the scale of the fall, not the cause
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint architectural/office-tower silhouette texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the top figure, fading to a dim glow on the bottom one
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** large number top-center, descending line, small number bottom-center, title-safe
  space reserved on the left third
- **Props:** none — pure typographic/numeral treatment
- **Thumbnail text:** `$47 BILLION. THEN $750 MILLION.`
- **Text placement:** left third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** white/gold glow on the top figure, desaturated grey on the bottom figure, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the size and brightness differential between the two numbers communicates the entire collapse before any text is read

- **Colors:** white/gold numerals on near-black
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the number

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render WeWork's real logo, Adam Neumann's real photograph/likeness, SoftBank's logo, or
  any other real trademarked brand element in the thumbnail — attribute names verbally/on-screen in
  the video itself, not in generated thumbnail imagery
- Do not use "fraud," "scam," or "lied" language anywhere on a thumbnail — Neumann has not been
  charged with any crime; the thumbnail's entire job is to sell the scale of the valuation collapse
  and the size of the exit package, not an accusation
- Do not attempt to cram all three valuation points ($47B / $9B / $750M) plus the Flow coda into one
  thumbnail — the $9B SPAC midpoint and the Flow "what happened next" beat get their own pull-quote
  moments inside the video and in Shorts, not the thumbnail
- Do not render real document text, real stock-ticker symbols, or real court-filing excerpts — build
  the visual concept generically and add any verified real text/branding in the editor with proper
  sourcing

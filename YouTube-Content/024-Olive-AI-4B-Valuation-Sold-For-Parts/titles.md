## Recommended Title
**"This 'AI' Company Was Worth $4 Billion. It Sold Itself For Parts."**

Reasoning: leads with the two strongest, most primary-sourced facts (the $4B valuation and the
piece-by-piece liquidation), rides the live 2026 conversation about AI-hype-versus-reality without
needing to overclaim, and works fully truncated on mobile. Naming "AI" in quotation marks signals
the investigative angle (something in the AI claim didn't hold up) without stating the "mostly
rules-based automation" characterization as settled fact in the title itself — that nuance belongs
in the video, per `sources-log.txt`.

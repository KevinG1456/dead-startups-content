## LinkedIn
"A real case study in what 'AI hype meets reality' actually looks like, in order:

Olive AI — founded 2012 as CrossChx, rebranded in 2017 around automating hospital admin work with 'AI' — raised close to $900 million and hit a $4 billion valuation in July 2021 (a round led by Vista Equity Partners).

In April 2022, an investigation reported the company was overpromising its capabilities and relying on rough estimates rather than delivered results. What followed wasn't a single bad headline. Two rounds of layoffs (450 in July 2022, ~200 in February 2023). Then, instead of a bankruptcy filing, Olive sold its own business units off one at a time — to Availity, then to Waystar and Humata Health — before formally winding down what remained on October 31, 2023.

What makes this case unusual: there's no single smoking-gun document. The story is assembled from a funding press release, an investigation, two layoff rounds, and three separate acquisitions — which is itself worth sitting with if you're building or evaluating anything with 'AI' in the pitch.

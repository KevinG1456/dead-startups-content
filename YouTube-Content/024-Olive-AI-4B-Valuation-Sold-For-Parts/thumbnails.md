# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — a $4B valuation broken into pieces, sold off separately.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large glowing "$4B" numeral fracturing into three pieces, each piece flying toward a small, distinct silhouette-buyer icon
- **Main subject:** the "sold for parts" sequence
- **Emotion:** shock/inevitability
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, single glow on the fracturing numeral
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** numeral centered, fragments trailing to the right, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** none beyond the fracture graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `WORTH $4B. SOLD FOR PARTS.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white/gold numeral cracking into red-edged fragments, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the fracture itself communicates the thesis before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** the fracturing "$4B" sequence from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `WORTH $4B. SOLD FOR PARTS.`
- **Why it works:** an instantly-readable numeral-breaking-apart visual creates curiosity about who bought what and why
- **CTR prediction:** high — fracture/breakdown thumbnails create strong narrative curiosity
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** a glowing "AI" text label with a red crack running through the middle, like broken glass
- **Text:** `THIS "AI" WASN'T WHAT THEY SOLD YOU`
- **Why it works:** taps directly into the live 2026 AI-hype-skepticism conversation, promises a specific investigative payoff, not a generic "AI bad" take
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three — strongest cultural relevance
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic, especially viewers already following AI-hype discourse

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small magnifying-glass-over-document icon in the corner
- **Text:** `AN INVESTIGATION FOUND THE TRUTH`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "an investigation found" framing signals real sourcing behind the claim
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

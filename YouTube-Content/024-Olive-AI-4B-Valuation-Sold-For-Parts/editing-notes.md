# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the fracturing "$4B" reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Fracturing "$4B" numeral animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- CrossChx-to-Olive logo-morph animation for the Origin segment (generic placeholder shapes only, no real logos without rights clearance)
- Valuation graphic climbing from $1.5B to $4B in the Climb segment
- "AI" label with magnifying-glass/question-mark overlay for the Investigation segment
- Headcount bar chart dropping twice (450, then ~200) across the two layoff segments
- Three separate business-unit icons flying off to three different buyer-placeholder icons, one at a time, in the Sold For Parts segment
- The "$4B" figure reduced to an empty outline for the "What Was Left" segment
- Final three-way comparison graphic (Fast: 6 days / Munchery: 1 filing / Olive: 18 months, 3 buyers) building beat by beat

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (funding press releases, the Axios investigation, Healthcare Dive, etc.)
- "Findings drawn from multiple independent reports on the original investigation — full article not directly reviewed" lower third specifically during the Investigation segment
- "Olive's own marketing claim — not independently audited" lower third under the "$1 to Olive = $5 saved" quote
- "Exact count reported inconsistently across sources — presented as an approximation" lower third during the Second Cut segment
- "Exact sale date not confirmed" lower third during the Sold For Parts segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/hospital-admin imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- The Sean Lane "fast-paced growth and lack of focus" quote types on screen as it's read aloud

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the three-way comparison and the fracturing-numeral graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Origin: measured, scene-setting
- The Climb to $4 Billion: brisk, confident, building energy toward the peak
- The Investigation: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the "AI" graphic carry the tension
- The First/Second Layoffs: slow contraction, mirrored musically by a thinning texture
- Sold For Parts: sharp tonal drop at each buyer-transfer beat
- What Was Left: measured, matter-of-fact
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Sharp "crack" SFX as the "$4B" numeral fractures in the cold open
- Soft "drop" SFX on each headcount-bar-chart decline
- Distinct "transfer" SFX as each business unit flies off to a buyer icon
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and educational, respectful of the real employees affected by the layoffs

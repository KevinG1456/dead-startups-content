# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the funding-climb segment and a clear investigative register during the Investigation and Sold For Parts segments
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the Sean Lane quote and the final comparison section — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** confident/scene-setting for the origin and climb, skeptical-but-fair for the Investigation segment, matter-of-fact during the layoffs, measured gravity for Sold For Parts
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; slight tonal shift (more skeptical, raised-eyebrow register) at the Investigation reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the three-way comparison explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

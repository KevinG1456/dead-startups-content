# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** bright and engaged for the party-era segments (mirroring the genuine scale of
  the event), turning measured and matter-of-fact from the failure segment onward
- **Speaking pace:** brisk during the founding/raise/party build-up; noticeably more deliberate
  during the 1989-conviction segment and the "unconfirmed after this point" beat
- **Emotional tone:** wry/incredulous energy is appropriate for the party segment (it's a genuinely
  absurd real event) but must never tip into mockery during the 1989-conviction segment, which
  involves real victims — that segment stays matter-of-fact and respectful
- **Facial expression:** engaged, slightly amazed during the lineup/party reveal; neutral-serious
  during the 1989-conviction and unconfirmed-outcome beats
- **Gesture style:** natural, expressive during the party segment; minimal and controlled during the
  1989-conviction segment and the exposure/collapse segments
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout, especially during the "unconfirmed after this
  point" line — this builds credibility for an honest limitation, not a smoothed-over gap

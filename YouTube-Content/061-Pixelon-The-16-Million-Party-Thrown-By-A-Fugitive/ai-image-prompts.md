# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (KISS, The Who, MGM
Grand, Pixelon), and no real people's likenesses (David Kim Stanley/"Michael Fenne" or any real
performer) — add verified text/photos/branding in the editor with proper rights clearance, not
generated.

## Thumbnail Prompts
**Prompt A:** "YouTube thumbnail, a row of simple glowing gold icon silhouettes — a microphone, a
guitar, a spotlight beam — arranged across a dark stage-lit gradient background, clean heavy
sans-serif style, minimal, high contrast, empty space at bottom third for title text, no readable
logos or real likenesses, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt B (recommended primary):** "YouTube thumbnail, a wide silhouette of a packed concert crowd
under bright warm stage lights blown out at the edges, with a single bold red circular stamp graphic
overlapping the center at a slight angle, high contrast, empty space at bottom edge for title text,
no readable text or symbols inside the stamp itself, no real faces or logos, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt C:** "YouTube thumbnail, tight three-quarter crop portrait, dramatic single top-down light,
serious measured expression, dark background, small red 'wanted poster' style tag icon graphic in
the corner, photorealistic, cinematic, high contrast, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

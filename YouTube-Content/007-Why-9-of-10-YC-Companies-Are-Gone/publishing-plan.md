# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Given the strong YC-alumni and accelerator-community relevance, consider additional distribution via Hacker News and Indie Hackers if available.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) with particular relevance to anyone evaluating an accelerator, plus a secondary audience of YC alumni and startup-history enthusiasts curious about early batches.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to a new "Startup Data & Audits" playlist as the first entry in what could become a recurring franchise format.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the audit-table reveal linking to the full sources/audit spreadsheet; card at the mid-video CTA linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy, subscribe — I'm building a full library of these, and I might audit a more recent YC batch next. And if anyone repeats a startup statistic to you without a source, ask which definition they're actually using."

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the quick-succession "quiet shutdowns" segment and more measured pacing for the OMGPOP/Xobni/Scribd stories
- **Speaking pace:** brisk during the six-company shutdown roundup; measured and precise during the two-ratio reveal — this is the video's actual thesis and needs room to land
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority throughout, with a specific beat of dry irony at the OMGPOP buy-back-refusal detail, and genuine care taken in the Zanbazaar disclosure (not glossed over)
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; slight engagement lift during the OMGPOP/Xobni pattern reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the two-ratio comparison explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

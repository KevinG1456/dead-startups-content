# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — 11 companies in, 1 survivor out.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a grid of 11 small squares, 10 of them dimmed/greyed out with an X, 1 lit up in color
- **Main subject:** the survivor-ratio itself, visualized instantly
- **Emotion:** shock/curiosity
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the one lit square
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** grid centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** none beyond the grid
- **Thumbnail text:** `11 COMPANIES. 1 SURVIVOR.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** grey/dark for the 10 dimmed squares, bright green for the 1 lit square, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the grid does the communicating before any text is read — viewers count instinctively

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** the 11-square grid from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `11 COMPANIES. 1 SURVIVOR.`
- **Why it works:** an instantly-countable visual creates curiosity about which one made it and why
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock
- **Concept:** large "9 OUT OF 10" text with a small asterisk/footnote mark
- **Text:** `9 OUT OF 10. (BUT IT'S COMPLICATED.)`
- **Why it works:** the visible caveat signals rigor and creates curiosity about the nuance, differentiating from a flat-statistic thumbnail
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well on this channel
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/focused expression, small 11-square grid tag in the corner
- **Text:** `I CHECKED ALL 11`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "I checked all 11" signals real, personal rigor rather than a secondhand statistic
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers, strong trust signal for this audience specifically
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

## A/B Testing

| Version | Emotion | CTR Strategy | Best Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Curiosity | 11-square grid, 1 lit up | Cold/browse traffic (recommended primary) |
| B | Shock | "9 out of 10" with a visible caveat | Search + browse |
| C | Authority | Face-led, "I checked all 11" framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

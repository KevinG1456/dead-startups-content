## LinkedIn
"'9 out of 10 startups fail' gets repeated constantly. I actually checked it against a real batch — all 11 companies from Y Combinator's Summer 2006 class.

8 shut down outright. OMGPOP (the studio behind Draw Something) sold to Zynga for a reported $180-200M, then got shut down by Zynga about 14 months later — reportedly, the founders tried to buy it back and were refused. Xobni sold to Yahoo for a reported $30-60M+ and got shut down about a year later. Only Scribd, pitched in 2006 as 'Social Documents,' still operates independently today.

Counting every company that no longer exists in any form: 10 of 11, roughly 91% — 'X out of 10' genuinely holds up here. But counting the OMGPOP and Xobni acquisitions as the real, multi-million-dollar exits they were for their founders: 8 of 11, roughly 73%.

Both numbers are real. The honest answer depends entirely on which question you're asking — and I think that nuance matters more than either number alone.

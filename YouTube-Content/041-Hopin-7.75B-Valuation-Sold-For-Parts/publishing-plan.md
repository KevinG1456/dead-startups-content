# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. The self-contained, extremely shareable
8-to-1,200-to-$7.75B-to-gone hook gives this package strong standalone viral potential independent of
any news cycle — no urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Broad crossover: founders/investors studying overhiring and demand-durability risk, general audience
with direct memory of Hopin-powered virtual events during 2020-2021, and viewers drawn purely by the
extreme, self-contained numeral hook regardless of prior Hopin familiarity.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist. Also strong fit for a future "Pandemic Unicorns" playlist if this
pattern gets revisited (other candidates: Peloton, remote-work tooling, delivery/logistics unicorns).

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element. Consider cross-referencing 030 (MoviePass) as a thematically
adjacent "burn-rate collapse" pairing if end-screen data supports it.

## Cards
Card at the "UP TO $50 MILLION" title card (start of Segment 8, ~8:30) linking to the "Startup
Autopsies" playlist for viewers hooked by the correction reveal; card at the mid-video CTA (~10:45)
reinforcing the "fastest rise and fall I've covered" framing; optional third card at the start of The
Actual Lesson (~11:00) linking directly to a pinned comment or community post with the sourced full-arc
timeline graphic.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library — and if you remember using Hopin during the
pandemic, or know another 'pandemic unicorn' story I should cover, tell me in the comments. One more
thing: do you think the overhiring here was avoidable, or basically inevitable given how fast the real
demand grew in 2020 and 2021?"

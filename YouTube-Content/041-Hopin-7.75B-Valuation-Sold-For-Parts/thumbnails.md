# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Never state "sold for $15 million"
alone in any thumbnail — if a dollar figure is used, it must be the full, accurate ending or the
headcount contrast, not the incomplete popular headline.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a small numeral "8" rapidly multiplying into a dense crowd-dot cluster reading
  "1,200," which then thins back down and fades to a much smaller cluster
- **Main subject:** the scale of the headcount rocket-then-collapse
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint upward-then-downward line-graph texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight tracking the growth-then-shrink motion
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** small "8" origin point, dense "1,200" cluster at peak, thinned final cluster,
  bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic numeral/dot-cluster graphics, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `8 EMPLOYEES. 1,200 EMPLOYEES. THEN GONE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** small gold "8," dense white/blue dot cluster at peak, thinning to faded gray,
  near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the density change of the dot cluster communicates the entire headcount arc
  before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Johnny Boufarhat or Badri Rajasekar in
  any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Hopin, RingCentral, Bending Spoons, or StreamYard logos/wordmarks in generated
  thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in the editor
  with proper sourcing
- Never state "sold for $15 million" alone as if it's the full ending in any thumbnail text — use the
  headcount contrast, the corrected-ending framing, or the full up-to-$50M figure instead

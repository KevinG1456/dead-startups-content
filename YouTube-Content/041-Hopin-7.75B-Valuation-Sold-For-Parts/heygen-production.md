# HeyGen Production
Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** hopeful and scrappy through the founding; building, triumphant momentum through the
  headcount and funding rockets; confident (slightly over-confident) through the acquisitions; an
  uneasy shift at the reopening; heavy and somber through the three layoff rounds; measured and
  transactional through the RingCentral deal; quiet and restrained through what was left and the real
  ending; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the cold-open numeral sequence and the "the
  part most retrospectives stop before reaching" framing card — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** genuine warmth and respect for the founding story (Boufarhat building from a
  sickbed) — not played as absurd or mockable; heavier, more somber delivery through the 2022 layoffs;
  dry, matter-of-fact delivery on the RingCentral and Bending Spoons reveals
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine warmth during the founding segment;
  visible gravity during the layoff segments; visible care and precision when reading the "could not
  independently verify" honesty framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the numeral-
  contrast graphics and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open numeral sequence
  ("8" → "1,200" → "$7.75B" → "GONE") and on the "UP TO $50 MILLION" title card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on "This is the part most retrospectives stop before reaching"
  — the single most important honesty flag in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open rapid numeral sequence: "8" → "1,200" → "$7.75B" → "GONE," each snapping in fast
- "You already know the '$15 million' headline. That's not actually where this story ends." pattern-
  interrupt card, white text on black
- Generic virtual-conference-platform UI graphic for the founding segment
- Headcount line graph, rocketing upward, for the headcount-rocket segment
- Valuation line graph, stacked beneath the headcount graph, for the funding-rocket segment
- "$250 MILLION — STREAMYARD ACQUISITION, JAN 2021" title card
- Crossover graph: virtual-event demand declining as in-person events resume, for the reopening segment
- "3 LAYOFF ROUNDS — ONE YEAR" title card, with the headcount graph now falling
- "$1.02 BILLION RAISED" title card
- "UP TO $50 MILLION — RINGCENTRAL DEAL, AUG 2023" title card
- "WHAT REMAINED" title card listing StreamYard, Streamable, Superwave
- "ACQUIRED BY BENDING SPOONS — APR 2024, TERMS UNDISCLOSED" title card
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founding (Jun 2019) → launch (Mar 2020) → seed
  through Series D (2020-2021) → $7.75B peak valuation (Aug 2021) → five acquisitions (2021) →
  in-person events return (2022) → three layoff rounds (2022) → RingCentral deal (Aug 2023) → Bending
  Spoons acquisition (Apr 2024)
- Running headcount counter visible from Segment 2 onward, climbing to "1,200," then falling across
  Segments 5-6 to "~500"

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, Axios,
  Sifted, CNBC, BusinessWire, RingCentral SEC 8-K)
- "Real, complete figure — not a flat $15M" standing lower third on the RingCentral deal reveal
- "Could not independently verify — not used" lower third if the $500M buyback claim is ever referenced
  on screen for context (otherwise omit entirely)
- "This is the part most coverage skips" lower third on the Bending Spoons reveal

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic pandemic-era remote-office/virtual-event
  imagery — no real readable logos/trademarks (Hopin, RingCentral, Bending Spoons, StreamYard) without
  rights clearance
- The cold-open numeral sequence should animate with a hard snap on each beat, fast enough to feel like
  an escalation, landing hard on "GONE"
- The virtual-event-demand crossover graph should animate the two lines crossing in real time, landing
  on the crossover point with a visible marker

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the numeral-contrast graphics,
  the crossover graph, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "UP TO $50 MILLION" reveal and the "this is
  the part most coverage skips" framing card — no camera movement competing with either for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, escalating pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding: hopeful, scrappy-startup energy
- The Headcount Rocket / The Funding Rocket: building, triumphant momentum
- Buying Everything: confident, slightly excessive — foreshadow the overextension
- The World Reopens: uneasy shift, momentum draining
- Three Rounds of Layoffs: somber, heavy
- What $1 Billion Actually Bought: quiet, matter-of-fact
- The RingCentral Deal: measured, transactional
- What Was Left / The Real Ending: quiet, restrained, tinged with irony
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (Jun 2019 founding, Mar 2020 launch, Feb
  2020-Aug 2021 funding rounds, 2021 acquisitions, Feb/Jul/Nov 2022 layoffs, Aug 2023 RingCentral deal,
  Apr 2024 Bending Spoons acquisition)
- A sharp "growth" SFX synced to each beat of the cold-open numeral sequence
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on the "$250 MILLION," "$7.75 BILLION," and "UP TO $50 MILLION" reveals
- A duller, heavier SFX (not celebratory) on each of the three 2022 layoff title cards
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured and analytical throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

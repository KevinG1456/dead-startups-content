## LinkedIn
"A real, precisely sourced case study in scaling for a demand spike as if it were permanent — and what
happens when it isn't:

Johnny Boufarhat founded Hopin in 2019, building a virtual-events platform that launched almost the
exact week COVID lockdowns began. Headcount grew from about 8 employees to roughly 1,200 in about two
years. Funding grew from a $6.5 million seed round to a $450 million Series D at a $7.75 billion
valuation — over $1 billion raised, plus five acquisitions in 2021 including a $250 million purchase of
StreamYard.

As in-person events resumed through 2022, demand for virtual events collapsed, and Hopin cut staff in
three separate rounds within a year, roughly halving headcount. In August 2023, Hopin sold its core
Events and Session product lines to RingCentral for up to $50 million total, and founder Boufarhat
stepped down as CEO. In April 2024, Bending Spoons acquired the entire remaining company outright, on
undisclosed terms — ending Hopin's run as an independent company less than five years after founding.

# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Hopin, RingCentral, Bending
Spoons, StreamYard), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Johnny Boufarhat, Badri
Rajasekar) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor with proper
sourcing, not generated.

brand names, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

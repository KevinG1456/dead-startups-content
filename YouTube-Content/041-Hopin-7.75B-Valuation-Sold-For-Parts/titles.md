## Recommended Title
**"8 Employees. 1,200 Employees. $7.75 Billion. Then It Was Gone in Two Deals."**

Reasoning: leads with the most visceral, concrete numeral escalation in the package — headcount growth
is more immediately graspable than a valuation figure, and stacking it with the $7.75B figure before
resolving with "gone in two deals" sets up maximum curiosity about an ending most viewers don't know in
full. Corrects the popular "$15 million" shorthand by promising (and then delivering) the complete,
accurate two-deal ending. Works fully truncated on mobile at "8 Employees. 1,200 Employees." alone.

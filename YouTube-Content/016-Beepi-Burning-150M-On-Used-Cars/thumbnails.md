# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the subsidize-both-sides mechanism.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a simple car icon in the center with two arrows pointing outward toward two dollar-sign icons on either side, both dollar signs "leaking" downward
- **Main subject:** the two-sided money-leak visual metaphor
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** car centered, leaking dollar icons on either side, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** car silhouette, dollar-sign icons
- **Thumbnail text:** `$150M. LOST MONEY ON EVERY SALE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white car silhouette, red leaking dollar icons, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the symmetric two-sided leak visual communicates the mechanism instantly

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "$150M" figure large and centered, tiny car icon beside it
- **Text:** `WHERE DID IT ALL GO?`
- **Why it works:** numeral-led format with a question creates strong curiosity about the mechanism
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the car/two-sided-leak combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$150M. LOST MONEY ON EVERY SALE.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of the mechanism paired with the visual proof in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/knowing expression, small car-icon tag in the corner
- **Text:** `THE MARKETPLACE MATH THAT NEVER WORKED`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "math" framing signals an analytical, educational payoff
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

| Version | Emotion | CTR Strategy | Best Audience |

| C | Authority | Face-led, analytical framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

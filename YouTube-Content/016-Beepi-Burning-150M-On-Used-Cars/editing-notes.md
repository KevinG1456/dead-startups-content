# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the two-sided-leak reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Car/two-sided-leak animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Per-transaction subsidy-math graphic: buyer cost + seller cost + operational cost vs. revenue per sale, shown as a simple visual ledger
- Timeline graphic: founding → growth/white-glove model → layoffs/pivots → shutdown → fallen-through Fair.com merger → creditor liquidation → Fair.com picks up some tech assets

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or quote is referenced
- "Per transaction" label persists during the subsidy-math segment for clarity

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic car-marketplace interface mockups

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the subsidy-math/timeline graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Rise section: optimistic, ambitious underscore (representing the genuine appeal of the trust-building mission)
- Autopsy/subsidy-math section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual ledger carry the tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Cash-register or "leak" drip SFX under the subsidy-math reveal
- Rubber-stamp thud on the shutdown/acquisition reveal
- Soft page-turn sounds under document B-roll
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative, respectful of a genuinely ambitious (if financially unsustainable) mission

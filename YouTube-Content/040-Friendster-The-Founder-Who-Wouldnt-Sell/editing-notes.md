# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open $30M-exploding-to-$30B
  reveal, and on the "$30 BILLION" title card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on "No direct quote from Abrams explaining his own reasoning
  has been found" — the single most important honesty flag in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open small glowing "$30M" numeral exploding into a massive "$30B" numeral
- "This isn't a story about running out of money. It's a story about the money he turned down."
  pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- Generic early-2000s social-network node graphic for the founding segment
- "$30 MILLION — GOOGLE'S OFFER, 2003" title card
- Neutral "DECLINED" stamp over the $30M card
- "$13M SERIES A — $53M VALUATION" title card
- "$30 BILLION — WHAT THE DECLINED STOCK WOULD BE WORTH TODAY" title card
- "FOUNDER REMOVED — 2004" title card
- User-count crossover graph, MySpace overtaking Friendster
- "111 MILLION USERS AT PEAK" title card
- "SOLD FOR $39 MILLION — DECEMBER 2009" title card
- "$40 MILLION — PATENTS ALONE, JUNE 2010" title card
- "SHUTDOWN — 2018" title card
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founding (2002) → Google offer declined (2003) →
  Series A raised (Oct 2003) → Google IPO (Aug 2004) → founder removed (2004) → MySpace overtakes
  (2004) → peak 111M users → sold to MOL (Dec 2009) → patents sold to Facebook (Jun 2010) → shutdown
  (2018)
- Running "value of declined stock" corner counter ticking upward from Segment 5 onward, landing on
  "$30 BILLION"

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Wikipedia, TechCrunch,
  Inc. Magazine, Netimperative)
- "Real, sourced figure — not $200M or $0" standing lower third on the initial $30M/$30B reveals
- "Best-sourced explanation available — not a direct quote" lower third on the investor-advice segment
- "Real, calculable figure, not hyperbole" lower third on the $30 billion reveal

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic early-2000s office/social-network imagery —
  no real readable logos/trademarks (Friendster, Google, MySpace, MOL Global, Facebook) without rights
  clearance
- The cold-open $30M-to-$30B explosion should animate slowly enough that both numbers are legible
  before the transition completes, letting the scale genuinely register
- The MySpace-overtakes-Friendster crossover graph should animate the two lines crossing in real time,
  landing on the crossover point with a visible marker

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the numeral-contrast graphics,
  the crossover graph, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "$30 billion" reveal and the honesty-flag
  framing card — no camera movement competing with either for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding: nostalgic, early-2000s optimism
- Google Comes Calling / The Decision: measured, weighty — this is the pivotal moment, treat it with
  gravity
- Betting on Independence: a brief confident lift
- What the Stock Would Be Worth: a sharp, dramatic swell — the video's single biggest musical beat
- The Board Turns on the Founder: quiet, somber
- Overtaken: uneasy, declining
- Sold / The Patents Alone: measured, matter-of-fact, tinged with irony
- The End: quiet, restrained
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2002 founding, 2003 offer/decline, Oct
  2003 Series A, Aug 2004 Google IPO, 2004 ouster/MySpace, Dec 2009 sale, Jun 2010 patent sale, 2018
  shutdown)
- A dramatic "whoosh" or "growth" SFX synced to the $30M-to-$30B explosion animation
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on the "$39 MILLION" and "$40 MILLION" reveals
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured and analytical throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

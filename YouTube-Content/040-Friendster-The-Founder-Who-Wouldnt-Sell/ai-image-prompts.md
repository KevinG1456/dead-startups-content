# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Friendster, Google,
MySpace, MOL Global, Facebook), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Jonathan
Abrams, Kent Lindstrom, Tim Koogle) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in
the editor with proper sourcing, not generated.

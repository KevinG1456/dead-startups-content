# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Never use "$200M" or "$0" in any
thumbnail — use the real, sourced $30M/$30B figures.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a small glowing "$30M" numeral rapidly multiplying/exploding outward into a massive
  "$30B" numeral filling the frame
- **Main subject:** the scale of the counterfactual value lost
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint network-connection-line texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight tracking the explosion from small to large
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** small "$30M" origin point, "$30B" numeral filling most of the frame, bottom-third
  title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic numeral graphics, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `HE SAID NO. IT WOULD BE WORTH $30 BILLION.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** small gold "$30M," massive red-gold "$30B," near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the sheer scale mismatch between the small origin numeral and the massive
  final numeral communicates the entire thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Jonathan Abrams, Kent Lindstrom, or
  Tim Koogle in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Friendster, Google, MySpace, MOL Global, or Facebook logos/wordmarks in generated
  thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in the
  editor with proper sourcing
- Never state "$200M" or "$0" in any thumbnail text — use the real, sourced $30M/$30B figures only

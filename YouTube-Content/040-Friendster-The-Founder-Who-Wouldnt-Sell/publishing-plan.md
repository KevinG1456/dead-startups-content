# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. The self-contained, extremely shareable
$30M-to-$30B hook gives this package strong standalone viral potential independent of any news cycle —
no urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Broad crossover: founders/investors studying valuation-anchoring and exit-timing risk, general
audience with nostalgic memory of early social networking, and viewers drawn purely by the extreme,
self-contained numeral hook regardless of prior Friendster familiarity.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; consider a new "Money Mechanics" or "Startup Regret" playlist if
this pattern gets revisited, since this is the channel's first entry built around valuation anchoring
specifically.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element. No single existing package is a close enough thematic match
to warrant a dedicated cross-reference pairing.

## Cards
Card at the "$30 BILLION" title card (end of Segment 5, ~6:00) linking to the "Startup Autopsies"
playlist for viewers hooked by the reveal; card at the mid-video CTA (~10:15) reinforcing the "declined
money" framing as distinct from the channel's usual stories; optional third card at the start of The
Actual Lesson (~10:30) linking directly to a pinned comment or community post with the sourced full-arc
timeline graphic.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library — and if you know another 'declined the big
offer' startup story, tell me in the comments, I want to build more of these. One more thing: do you
think the decision to say no to Google was defensible given what was known in 2003, or was it always a
mistake?"

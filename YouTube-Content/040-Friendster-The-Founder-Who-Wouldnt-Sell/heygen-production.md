# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** nostalgic and measured through the founding; weighty and deliberate through the
  Google offer and decision; a brief confident lift through the Series A; a sharp dramatic swell at the
  $30 billion reveal; somber through the ouster; declining through the MySpace overtake; matter-of-fact
  through the sale and patent sale; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "$30 billion" reveal and the honesty-
  flag framing card — both need room to land clearly and precisely
- **Emotional tone:** genuine gravity at the Google-offer decision — not played for easy hindsight
  mockery; dramatic but controlled delivery on the $30 billion figure; quiet respect through the
  ouster and shutdown segments
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine disbelief at the $30 billion
  reveal; visible care and precision when reading the "no direct quote found" honesty framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  numeral-contrast graphics and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

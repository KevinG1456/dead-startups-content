# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during each company's breakout segment and a clear educational register during the mechanism explanations
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the deliberate-vs-incidental comparison — dense, distinction-heavy content that needs room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** confident/upbeat during both breakout segments (SXSW buzz, Viddy's rocket-ship growth), matter-of-fact authority for the mechanism explainers, quieter gravity for the founder-quote and fire-sale beats
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine gravity when reading Ben Rubin's quote
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative hand movement during the "Deliberate / Incidental" comparison explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the "same day" date-collision reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Split-screen date-collision animation for the cold open (Meerkat cutoff icon breaking / Periscope acquisition headline forming), both stamped "MARCH 13, 2015"
- Twitter follower-count/growth graphic climbing during Meerkat's SXSW breakout segment
- Funding-timeline graphic clearly dated AFTER the cutoff for Meerkat's $14M raise
- News Feed visibility graphic dropping during Viddy's "10-second rule" segment
- Viddy DAU graph cratering (5M → 1M) during The Collapse
- Valuation-to-sale-price contrast graphic ($370M → $20M) for Viddy's fire sale
- Two-column "Deliberate / Incidental" comparison graphic, building beat by beat through the final lesson section

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, VentureBeat, BuzzFeed News, Engadget, CNN Money, Los Angeles Business Journal, Recode, Variety)
- "Rubin's own account — not independently confirmed by a second source" lower third during the Meerkat founder-quote segment, to keep the honesty distinction visually clear
- "Algorithm change — not an API shutdown. Facebook did not target Viddy specifically." lower third during the Viddy mechanism segment, for the same reason

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and terms
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic app-interface/office imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- Rubin's quote types on screen as it's read aloud in the Meerkat founder segment

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the comparison-graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Meerkat: The Breakout: brisk, confident, building energy (SXSW buzz)
- The Cutoff / The Raise That Happened Anyway: sparse, clinical — let the date-collision graphic carry the tension
- What Meerkat's Founder Actually Says: slower, more contemplative, slight tonal shift to signal a complication
- Viddy: The Rocket Ship: brisk, confident, mirroring Meerkat's opening energy
- The Rule Nobody Aimed At Viddy / The Collapse: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the DAU graph carry the tension
- The Fire Sale: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Deliberate vs. Incidental — The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "disconnect" SFX (not comedic) on the API-access-breaking icon in the cold open and Meerkat's cutoff segment
- Soft "click" SFX as each step in the timeline or comparison-column graphic populates
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and educational, respectful of the real teams behind both companies

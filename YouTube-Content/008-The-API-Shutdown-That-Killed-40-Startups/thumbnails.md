# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per the channel's own rule, don't try to illustrate both
companies' full stories in one frame. Pick the single sharpest visual contrast and let the title
text carry the rest.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a single word split down the middle — "ON PURPOSE" on one half, "BY ACCIDENT" on the other — with a simple broken-connection icon (a plug pulled from a socket) between them
- **Main subject:** the on-purpose-vs-by-accident contrast itself, not the companies' logos or founders
- **Emotion:** shock/injustice
- **Background:** dark, minimal, split down the center into two barely-different shades
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** centered vertical split, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** unplugged-cable/broken-connection icon
- **Thumbnail text:** `ON PURPOSE. BY ACCIDENT. BOTH DIED.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, split coloring to match the two halves
- **Color strategy:** one half cool blue (deliberate/calculated), one half warm orange (accidental/chaotic), near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the color-and-word split does the communicating before any text is fully read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** the on-purpose/by-accident split from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `ON PURPOSE. BY ACCIDENT. BOTH DIED.`
- **Why it works:** a binary contrast with no explanation creates an immediate "wait, what happened" reaction
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** a single big number "$370M" with a crack/shatter effect through it, small unplug icon in the corner
- **Text:** `$370M. THEN ONE RULE CHANGED.`
- **Why it works:** leads with a real, sourced, dramatic number — the Viddy valuation collapse — and pairs it with a visual that doesn't over-explain
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails consistently perform well on this channel
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small unplug-icon tag in the corner
- **Text:** `WHO ACTUALLY KILLED THESE STARTUPS`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; the question framing signals a real investigative explainer, not a nostalgia clip
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

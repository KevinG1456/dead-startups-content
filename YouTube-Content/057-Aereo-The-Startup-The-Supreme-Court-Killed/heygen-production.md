# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** curious and measured through the founding/product segments; dry and precise
  through the legal-precedent explainer; building confidence through the win-streak segment; tense
  anticipation approaching the ruling; sharp tonal drop at the 6-3 reveal; quiet and restrained through
  the bankruptcy/fire-sale segment; a distinct, slightly different energy (measured concern, not
  despair) for the Starry coda
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "three justices agreed, six didn't"
  framing line and the vote-count reveal — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** dry, document-forward through the legal-precedent explainer — never
  sensationalized; genuine building confidence through the win streak; restrained disbelief at the
  ruling; quiet respect, no melodrama, during the bankruptcy segment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; visible curiosity during the antenna/precedent
  explainer; a beat of genuine dry disbelief when stating the 6-3 vote; measured concern (not alarm)
  during the Starry coda
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  scoreboard graphic and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (checkmarks-then-X graphic reveal)
- Script: "This company won at the district court. Won again at the Court of Appeals. Then lost at the
  Supreme Court — and that was the only loss that mattered. Here's exactly how a single 6-3 vote ended
  a $97 million company."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–1:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: clear, measured delivery establishing the company and the two headline
  numbers ($97M raised, 6-3 vote)
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 1:00–1:40 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: deliberately clear on the "no one in this story did anything wrong" framing
  line
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Founding**
- Duration: 1:40–2:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (generic startup-office graphic), avatar VO under

- Visual direction: generic office establishing shot, no real branding

**Scene 05 — The Antenna Trick**
- Duration: 2:15–3:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with antenna-to-screen connector graphic

**Scene 06 — Borrowing a Precedent**
- Duration: 3:30–4:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with highlighted-document graphic

**Scene 07 — Sued Anyway**

**Scene 15 — The Actual Lesson**

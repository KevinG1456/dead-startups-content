# Shorts Ideas (10)

Note: state the mechanism precisely in every Short below — this company was killed by a 6-3 Supreme
Court vote after winning twice in lower courts, never compress it into a vague "legal trouble" claim.
Never imply fraud or wrongdoing by any party — this is a legal/regulatory-risk story, not a scandal.

**1. "It Won Twice. Then It Lost Once."**
Hook: "This company won at two courts in a row. Then the Supreme Court disagreed — and that was the
only vote that mattered." Script: quick two-checkmarks-then-X reveal. CTA: "Full story on the
channel."

**2. "A 6-3 Vote Ended a $97 Million Company"**
Hook: "Nine justices decided whether this company could keep existing. Six said no." Script: quick
vote-count reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**3. "The Legal Trick That Actually Worked — Twice"**

Here's what happened when it reached the Supreme Court." Script: quick precedent-to-reversal reveal.
CTA: "Full story on the channel."

in one word." Script: quick document-rejected reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**5. "$97 Million In. Under $2 Million Out."**

**7. "Three Days From Ruling to Shutdown"**

**8. "This Founder's Second Company Almost Died Too"**

**9. "Three Justices Agreed With Them. Six Didn't."**

**10. "What Actually Ends a Startup"**

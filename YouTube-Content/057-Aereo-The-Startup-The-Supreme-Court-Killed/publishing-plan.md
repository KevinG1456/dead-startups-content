# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Aereo carries real, sustained
legal/copyright-education search volume alongside the channel's core startup-failure audience — no
urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders/operators, VCs, startup employees) plus a genuine crossover audience:
viewers interested in legal-education, copyright/media law, and Supreme Court case studies. Broader
general-audience crossover potential than this channel's narrower cap-table-mechanics packages (e.g.,
033/Quixey) — pair distribution accordingly (legal/media-law communities, not just startup/VC ones).

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also start (or seed) a new "Unusual Causes of Death" playlist —
Aereo is the first entry in a category distinct from money/fraud/deal-structure failures, and a strong
anchor if more externally-caused-failure stories (e.g., platform decisions, regulatory rulings) are
added later.

## End Screen

external-force-failure story, though a different specific mechanism (platform decision vs. court
ruling).

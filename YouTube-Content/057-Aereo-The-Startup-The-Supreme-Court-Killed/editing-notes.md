# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open checkmarks-then-X
  reveal, and again on the "PRIVATE PERFORMANCE — NOT PUBLIC" highlighted-document card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the "Three justices agreed with Aereo. Six didn't. That's
  all it took." framing card — the single most important line in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open two green checkmarks stacking, hard cut to a large red X stamped beneath them
- "This isn't a story about running out of money. This company was killed by a courtroom." pattern-
  interrupt card, white text on black
- Antenna-to-screen connector graphic for the product explainer
- Document graphic with one line highlighted: "PRIVATE PERFORMANCE — NOT PUBLIC"
- Network-icon silhouettes converging on a single antenna icon for the lawsuit-filed beat
- Scoreboard graphic building: "DISTRICT COURT: AEREO WINS" → "COURT OF APPEALS: AEREO WINS"
- Generic marble-column government-building silhouette for the Supreme Court segment (no real
  landmark)
- Large red X stamping down over the two-checkmark scoreboard at the ruling reveal
- "REJECTED" red stamp overlay on a document graphic for the failed cable-license argument
- Dramatic bar-chart drop graphic for the $97M-to-under-$2M fire sale
- Second, smaller dip-and-recovery line graphic for the Starry coda, visually distinct from Aereo's
  flat line-to-zero
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founding (2012) → funding ($97M) → lawsuit filed →
  wins at district court and Second Circuit → Supreme Court ruling, 6-3 (June 2014) → suspended service
  (3 days later) → failed cable-license argument (Oct 2014) → Chapter 11 (Nov 2014) → asset fire sale
  (Mar 2015)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or ruling detail is referenced (SCOTUSblog,
  Justia, CNBC, TechCrunch, Variety, GlobeNewswire, Crain's New York Business)
- "6-3 vote, not unanimous" standing lower third any time the ruling is discussed — no exceptions
- "No wrongdoing alleged by any party in this story" note available for the intro if needed to set
  tone
- "Starry survived Chapter 11 — did not shut down" lower third during the coda segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office imagery — no real readable logos/
  trademarks (Aereo, ABC, CBS, NBC, Fox, TiVo) without rights clearance
- The scoreboard-to-X animation should build with each checkmark appearing individually before the
  single X slams down over both — the timing itself should reinforce "won, won, then lost"
- The bar-chart drop graphic should animate as a sharp, near-instant collapse from the $97M bar to the
  under-$2M bar — no gradual decline, since the real collapse was fast

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the scoreboard graphic, the
  ruling-reveal X-stamp, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "three justices agreed, six didn't" framing
  card — no camera movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding / The Antenna Trick: measured, optimistic, curious
- Borrowing a Precedent / Sued Anyway: dry, clinical, document-forward — a subtle tension undercurrent
- Winning Twice: building confidence, hopeful lift with each win
- The Case Reaches the Supreme Court: tense anticipation, slowing tempo
- The Ruling — 6-3: sharp tonal drop, the hopeful cue cutting off abruptly
- One Last Try / Bankruptcy and the Fire Sale: quiet, restrained, respectful — no melodrama
- The Coda — Starry: a distinct, separate tension-then-partial-relief cue, different from Aereo's
  ending cue
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2012 founding, 2014 ruling, 2014
  bankruptcy, 2015 asset sale, 2023 Starry bankruptcy)
- Soft "check" SFX on each of the two courtroom wins
- Sharp, decisive "gavel" SFX on the 6-3 ruling reveal — the single most important sound cue in the
  video
- Ticking-clock SFX under the "three days later" service-suspension beat
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays analytical and a little awestruck throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

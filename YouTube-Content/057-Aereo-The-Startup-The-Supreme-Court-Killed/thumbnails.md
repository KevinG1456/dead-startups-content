# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram the antenna
technology, the legal precedent, the courtroom scoreboard, and the Starry coda into a single image.
Each of those gets its own version below, or lives inside the video/Shorts instead of the thumbnail.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a small antenna icon centered directly beneath a judge's gavel mid-strike, the
  gavel's impact line intersecting the antenna
- **Main subject:** "a courtroom decision ended a piece of technology" — the single dominant idea
- **Emotion:** disbelief / finality
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint courthouse-column texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the gavel-antenna intersection point
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** antenna small and centered low, gavel large and dominant above it mid-strike,
  bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic antenna icon, generic gavel icon, no real logos or courtroom photography
- **Thumbnail text:** `THE SUPREME COURT ENDED THIS COMPANY.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** cool blue-gray gavel/courthouse tones, warm single spotlight on the impact point
- **Contrast strategy:** the scale mismatch (tiny antenna, massive gavel) communicates the entire
  thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Chet Kanojia, Barry Diller, or any
  Supreme Court Justice in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts
  only; attribute real names verbally/on-screen in the video itself
- Do not put the real Aereo logo/wordmark, real network logos (ABC/CBS/NBC/Fox), or the real Supreme
  Court building/seal in generated thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any
  verified real branding/archival imagery in the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not imply fraud or wrongdoing by any party in any thumbnail — this is a legal/regulatory-risk
  story with no misconduct on any side, keep the framing dramatic but factual, never accusatory

"A real case study in regulatory risk — and the only startup I've covered on this channel that was
killed by a courtroom, not a market:

Aereo, founded 2012 by Chet Kanojia and backed by IAC chairman Barry Diller, let subscribers stream

directly off a real, controlling 2008 Second Circuit precedent. Aereo raised roughly $97 million and
won at both the district court and the Second Circuit.

On June 25, 2014, the Supreme Court reversed, 6-3, ruling Aereo functioned like a cable system.

Chapter 11 in November 2014. Its assets sold for under $2 million in 2015.

## Recommended Title
**"This Startup Won Every Court Case. Then It Lost the One That Mattered."**

Reasoning: sets up the entire video's structure — a real win streak followed by a single, decisive
loss — without requiring the viewer to already know or care about Aereo by name. Works fully
truncated on mobile at the first sentence alone, and immediately differentiates this package from
every other failure-cause covered on the channel (money, fraud, deal terms) by implying the cause is
something else entirely, which pulls viewers in to find out what.

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with sharper energy during the Garmin/TomTom crash reveal and a quieter, more measured register during Picturelife's slower decline
- **Speaking pace:** brisk and numbers-forward during the stock-crash segment; more measured during the multi-platform explainer for Picturelife, which needs room to land its nuance
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority throughout, with genuine care taken during the "honest complication" beat for Picturelife — this isn't a villain story, and the performance shouldn't oversell it as one
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; slight gravity shift during Picturelife's shutdown segment
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the Fast vs. Slow comparison explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per the channel's own rule, don't try to illustrate both
segments' full stories in one frame.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "$1.2B" figure with a sharp downward-crashing line graph through it
- **Main subject:** the single-afternoon market-cap collapse
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the central figure
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** big number centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** downward-crashing line graph
- **Thumbnail text:** `$1.2B. GONE BY LUNCH.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** red crash line, near-black background, white number
- **Contrast strategy:** the huge-number-with-a-crash-through-it does the communicating before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "$1.2B" centered large, small downward-arrow icon overlapping the corner
- **Text:** `ONE ANNOUNCEMENT DID THIS`
- **Why it works:** numeral-led format with a small consequence icon creates immediate curiosity about the mechanism
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well on this channel
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the $1.2B-with-crash-line combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$1.2B. GONE BY LUNCH.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of a real, sourced number paired with the visual proof in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small crash-line tag in the corner
- **Text:** `WHAT PLATFORM RISK ACTUALLY COSTS`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "what it actually costs" framing signals a practical, quantified explainer
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

## A/B Testing

| Version | Emotion | CTR Strategy | Best Audience |
|---|---|---|---|

| B | Shock | Number-with-crash-line combo | Search + browse (recommended primary) |
| C | Authority | Face-led, "what it actually costs" framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

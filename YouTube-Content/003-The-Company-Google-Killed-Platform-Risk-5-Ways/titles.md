## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **Google Erased $1.2 Billion In One Afternoon. A Startup Died Slower — To Google and Two Others.** — leads with the ironclad, dramatic, fully-sourced number, sets up the video's actual two-speed thesis in one line, works fully truncated on mobile
2. This Startup Did Nothing Wrong. Google, Apple, and Amazon Killed It Anyway. — strong empathy-driven hook for the Picturelife half, honestly multi-platform
3. $1.2 Billion, Gone By Lunch — The Day Google Made GPS Devices Obsolete — vivid, specific, fully sourced
4. Platform Risk, Told Two Ways: A Single Afternoon and a Slow Squeeze — clean SEO/curiosity hybrid, honestly signals the 2-segment structure
5. Three Giants Went Free At the Same Time. This Startup Couldn't Survive All Three. — strong for the Picturelife-led alt cut
6. Google Made Navigation Free. An Entire Industry Never Recovered. — clean, credible, strong search performance
7. How One Google Announcement Ended an Entire Product Category Overnight — good pure-SEO option, lower CTR
8. What Actually Happened to the GPS Device Industry — best for the nostalgia-driven alt cut
9. Garmin TomTom Stock Crash 2009 Explained — dry, best for search-only sub-cut
10. What Happened to Picturelife? The Full Story — narrowest audience, lowest CTR, best as a companion Short/community post hook

## Recommended Title
**"Google Erased $1.2 Billion In One Afternoon. A Startup Died Slower — To Google and Two Others."**

Reasoning: leads with the single most dramatic, ironclad, fully-sourced number in the entire
package (Garmin's ~$1.2B single-day market-cap loss), sets up the video's real thesis — platform
risk has a fast version and a slow version — without overclaiming Google's sole responsibility for
either outcome, and works fully truncated on mobile. Disclose the Garmin/TomTom public-company
exception and Picturelife's multi-platform causation explicitly once the video opens, per the
package's own honesty requirement.

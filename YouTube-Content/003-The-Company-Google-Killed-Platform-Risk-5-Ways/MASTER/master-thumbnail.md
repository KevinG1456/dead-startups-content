# Master Thumbnail — Garmin/TomTom + Picturelife

Shared visual concept across all six channels. Only thumbnail TEXT is localized.

## Shared Visual Concept (do not vary by language)
- **Core visual:** a large dollar figure with a sharp downward-crashing line graph through it.
- **Background:** dark, minimal.
- **Color strategy:** red crash line, near-black background, white number.

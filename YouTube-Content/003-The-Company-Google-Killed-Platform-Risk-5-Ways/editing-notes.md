# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the $1.2B market-cap crash reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Stock-ticker-style crash graphic for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Industry-revenue graphic climbing during Garmin/TomTom's "The Industry" segment
- Side-by-side TomTom/Garmin stock charts crashing during "The Same Day"
- $1.2B counter ticking down during the same segment
- Funding graphic for Picturelife's "The Startup" segment
- "Three giants converging" graphic (Google/Apple/Amazon FREE stamps closing in) during "Free, Everywhere, At Once"
- Fast/Slow two-speed comparison graphic, building beat by beat through the final lesson section

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Fast Company, Bloomberg, TechCrunch, Gizmodo, Imaging Resource, Techmeme)
- "Garmin and TomTom are public hardware companies — a disclosed exception to this channel's usual funded-startup format" lower third during Case One's Segment 2
- "This is a multi-platform story — Google, Apple, and Amazon all mattered here, not Google alone" lower third during Case Two's Segment 2

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and terms
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic GPS-device/app-interface imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- Stock-chart lines animate downward in real time as VO reads the percentage drops

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the comparison-graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Garmin/TomTom: The Industry: confident, steady, pre-disruption calm
- The Announcement / The Same Day: sparse, clinical — let the crashing stock charts carry the tension
- What It Meant: brief, somber resolution beat
- Picturelife: The Startup: brisk, hopeful, building
- Free, Everywhere, At Once / The Shutdown: slow, minor-key, gradual decline (mirrors the slower mechanism)
- The Honest Complication: neutral, contemplative
- Fast vs. Slow — The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Sharp "crash" SFX on the stock-chart drop reveals in the cold open and Case One
- Soft, gradual "fade" SFX (not sharp) during Case Two's slower decline beats, to sonically distinguish the two speeds
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and educational, respectful of the real people and teams behind both companies

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled, analytical, brisk during the 5-case segment to maintain multi-segment rhythm; noticeably more constructive/instructional during the framework section
- **Speaking pace:** measured for the opening definition and framework sections, slightly quicker within each case
- **Emotional tone:** even-keeled authority across all 5 cases; genuinely constructive, non-mocking tone throughout, since these are real founders' honest efforts
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; empathetic warmth during the framework section's practical advice
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled; a small consistent gesture (e.g., a circling hand motion) each time a new case's pivot sequence is introduced, reinforcing the "loop" visual motif
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:20 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (looping path/tombstone animation)
- Script: "When does pivoting stop being adaptive and start being avoidance?"
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — What Makes a Pivot Good vs. Bad**
- Duration: 0:20–1:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, counterexample montage (Slack/YouTube/Twitter/Instagram references via generic/abstracted graphics, not trademarked logos) inset
- Performance direction: clear, fair-minded teaching tone — establishing credibility by addressing the counterpoint directly and early
- Visual direction: medium shot, definition graphic inset

**Scenes 03–07 — Cases 1–5**
- Duration: 1:30–9:00 (five ~90s segments per script.md timestamps)
- Avatar/B-roll: Each case opens with avatar on camera delivering the numbered case-card intro, then shifts to B-roll/document evidence with avatar VO, returning to avatar on camera for the case's takeaway line
- Performance direction: identical opening beat/gesture for each case; slight pacing increase on cases 2–4, deliberate slow-down on case 5 as the final and most memorable example
- Visual direction: case-number lower third at start of each; consistent case-card template with each company's pivot-sequence loop animation

**Scene 08 — Mid-Video CTA (inside Case 4, ~6:00)**
- Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into case rhythm
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly

**Scene 09 — The Framework**
- Duration: 9:00–10:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera alternating with full-screen framework graphic
- Performance direction: authoritative, constructive, synthesizing tone — the payoff moment; genuinely helpful delivery aimed at viewers currently facing this decision
- Visual direction: framework items populate one at a time in sync with VO, contrasted against the opening counterexamples

**Scene 10 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 10:30–11:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: steady, direct eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements animate in during final 5 seconds only

## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **Everyone Says "Pivot or Die." These 5 Companies Did Both.** — plain-language, contrarian, strong wordplay hook
2. The Pivot Graveyard: 5 Companies That Pivoted Themselves to Death — original; vivid, memorable branded phrase
3. 5 Companies That Pivoted Themselves Out of Existence — punchy, direct, clear
4. The Startup Advice That Killed 5 Companies — strong contrarian framing, verify defensibility before using
5. When "Just Pivot" Is Actually Terrible Advice — strong myth-busting framing
6. Pivot or Die? These 5 Companies Pivoted AND Died. — punchy, works well as a spoken cold-open line
7. Good Pivot vs Bad Pivot: A Complete Guide — strong practical framing, less viral
8. When to Pivot a Startup (And When Not To) — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
9. The Founders Who Kept Trying and Still Ran Out of Time — best for the emotional/human-angle alt cut
10. Startup Pivot Failure: 5 Real Case Studies — dry, best for search-only sub-cut

## Recommended Title
**"Everyone Says 'Pivot or Die.' These 5 Companies Did Both."**

Reasoning: directly engages and complicates a widely-known piece of startup wisdom, creates immediate curiosity about the contradiction, and works fully truncated on mobile. Use "the pivot graveyard" as the on-screen/thumbnail-adjacent branding for the recurring visual motif across the 5 segments.

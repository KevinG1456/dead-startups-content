# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the repeated-pivot-leads-nowhere concept, not all 5 companies at once.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a winding, looping arrow path that circles back on itself repeatedly, ending at a simple gravestone/tombstone icon
- **Main subject:** the looping-path-to-tombstone metaphor
- **Emotion:** irony/inevitability
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** looping path fills most of the frame, tombstone icon at the end, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** simple tombstone/grave icon
- **Thumbnail text:** `THE PIVOT GRAVEYARD`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white/gray path, single red accent on the tombstone, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the looping path visually communicates "wandering without direction" before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "5" numeral large and centered, small looping-arrow icon beside it
- **Text:** `5 COMPANIES. TOO MANY PIVOTS.`
- **Why it works:** number-led format promises the multi-case structure clearly
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the looping-path-to-tombstone combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `THE PIVOT GRAVEYARD`
- **Why it works:** the visual metaphor communicates "wandering, then dying" instantly, and the branded phrase is memorable and shareable on its own
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), knowing/skeptical expression, small looping-arrow tag in the corner
- **Text:** `"PIVOT OR DIE" — THEY DID BOTH`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; phrase directly engages the well-known startup mantra
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

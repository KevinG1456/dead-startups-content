## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct startup failures using primary sources. This week: not one company, but five — all sharing a pattern that complicates one of the most repeated pieces of startup advice out there: "pivot or die."

We walk through five real, documented cases of companies that pivoted — sometimes repeatedly — without ever finding lasting product-market fit. We also directly address the obvious counterpoint: plenty of companies (Slack, YouTube, Twitter, Instagram, among others) pivoted successfully into massive businesses. The goal of this video isn't to argue pivoting is bad — it's to draw a clear, practical line between a good, thesis-driven pivot and a reactive one that just burns time, cash, and team focus.

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Consistent per-segment intro zoom on the looping-path graphic each time a new case begins, reinforcing the repeatable-pattern structure

## Motion Graphics
- Numbered case-card template (1 of 5, 2 of 5, etc.) appearing identically at the start of each segment
- Looping-arrow-path animation reused from the thumbnail concept as a recurring transition between cases, each company's specific pivot sequence drawn out as its own loop
- Good-pivot-vs-bad-pivot framework graphic building progressively across the final segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third at the start of each case segment
- Case counter lower third (e.g., "Case 2 of 5") persists briefly at the start of each segment
- "Pivot count" indicator per company (e.g., "3 pivots") displayed alongside each case

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for case names/numbers and pivot sequence steps
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press/document B-roll per case

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats within each case; reserve dynamic movement for the case-card and looping-path transitions

## Background Music
- Consistent low-key bed underneath all 5 cases for structural cohesion, with a brief stinger at each case transition
- Slightly different emotional micro-tone per case is fine, but avoid full score changes that would make the 5 segments feel disconnected
- Framework section: clear, slightly upbeat, instructional tone

## Sound Effects
- Consistent transition whoosh/stinger between each numbered case
- Soft "loop" or "circling" SFX under each company's pivot-sequence animation
- Distinct tonal shift into the framework section to signal "now here's the takeaway"
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is analytical and constructive, not mocking of any specific founder's decisions

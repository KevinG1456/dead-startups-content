# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Strong potential for founder-community discussion (Indie Hackers, startup Twitter/X, relevant subreddits) given the contrarian-wisdom angle — consider direct engagement in those communities post-publish.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs), with particular relevance to founders currently navigating or considering a pivot decision.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; consider a dedicated "Founder Decision Frameworks" playlist alongside Title 010's acquisition-spectrum explainer and Title 011's concentration-risk framework.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + highest-performing prior autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the framework reveal (9:00) linking to a related decision-framework video; card at Case 1 linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy, subscribe — I'm building a full library of these. And if you're mid-pivot right now, tell me where you're at in the comments — might be useful to hear from people actually living this decision."

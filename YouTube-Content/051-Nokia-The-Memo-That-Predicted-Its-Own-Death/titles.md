## Recommended Title
**"He Warned Everyone the Platform Was Burning. They Listened. It Still Burned Down."**

Reasoning: leads with this package's real differentiator — that the warning was heeded, not
ignored, which is a sharper and less expected story than the batch title's original "ignored
warning" premise. Self-contained even for viewers with zero prior knowledge of the memo or
Nokia's history, while still rewarding viewers who DO know "the burning platform memo" by
reputation with a twist they likely haven't heard: doing everything right still wasn't enough.
Sets up the full downstream timeline (pivot, acquisition, write-off, discontinuation) as the
proof. Matches the title already used in this package's README.

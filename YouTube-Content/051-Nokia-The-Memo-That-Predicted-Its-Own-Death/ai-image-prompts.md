# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Nokia, Microsoft,
Lumia), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Stephen Elop, Steve Ballmer,
Satya Nadella) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor with
proper sourcing, not generated.

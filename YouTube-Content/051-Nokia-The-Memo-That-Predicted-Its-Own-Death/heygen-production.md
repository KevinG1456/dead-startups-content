# HeyGen Production
Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and grave through the memo build-up; a brief lift of decisive energy
  through the pivot; careful, neutral precision through the Trojan Horse segment; declining
  through the failed bet; matter-of-fact through the acquisition and write-off; reflective through
  the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially the memo quote cards and the write-off
  reveal — both need room to land clearly and precisely
- **Emotional tone:** genuine gravity reading the memo's own words; careful neutrality on the
  Trojan Horse theory — never played for gossip; quiet respect through the write-off and
  discontinuation
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine weight when reading the
  burning-platform quote; visible precision when reading the "disputed theory" framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  numeral-contrast graphics and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

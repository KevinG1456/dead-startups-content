# Editing Notes

## Pacing
- Cold open must be fast and punchy — the burning-platform image and the "they listened, it still
  burned down" line need to land in under 15 seconds
- Segments 2-4 (the memo itself) are the emotional core of the first half — do not rush the
  verified quote cards; let them sit on screen long enough to actually read
- Segment 9 (the write-off) is the video's single biggest reveal — treat it with the same weight
  the Friendster package (040) gives its "$30 billion" reveal

## Required On-Screen Disclosures
- The public-company exception (Nokia is not a funded startup) must appear on screen within the
  first 90 seconds — do not bury it or imply Nokia is VC-funded
- The "heeded, not ignored" correction must appear on screen explicitly, not just implied by the
  timeline
- The real ~4.5-5 year gap must appear on screen explicitly — never let "2 years" appear anywhere,
  including lower-thirds or chapter titles
- The Trojan Horse theory segment must include Elop's on-record denial in the SAME scene, not a
  later, disconnected segment — never let the theory stand alone on screen

## Graphics Priorities
1. The burning-platform metaphor visual (Segment 2/5) — this is the single most important visual
   asset in the package, worth the most production time
2. The $7.2B → $7.6B numeral contrast (Segment 9) — second priority, needs to be immediately
   legible even paused on a single frame
3. Full-arc timeline (closing lesson) — should build beat by beat, not appear all at once

## Sourcing/Legal Caution
- Every direct quote from the memo must match `sources-log.txt` verbatim — no paraphrased text
  presented as a direct quote
- The Trojan Horse segment requires the disputed-claim disclosure every time it's referenced,
  including in Shorts if that segment is ever extracted — check `sources-log.txt` before cutting
  any standalone clip from this segment

## Music/Tone
- Somber, tension-building underscore through the memo build-up (Segments 2-4)
- A brief, subtle lift in energy through the pivot announcement (Segment 5) — this is the moment
  of decisive action, not despair
- Return to somber, measured tone from the "bet doesn't work" segment onward through the write-off
- Warm, reflective tone for the closing lesson — this is not a mockery of Nokia or Elop

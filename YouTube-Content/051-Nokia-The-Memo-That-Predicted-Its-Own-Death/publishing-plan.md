# Publishing Plan

## Pre-Launch (T-7 to T-1 days)
- Community post teaser (see `community-posts.md` #2, #6) 5-7 days before publish
- Pin a comment on the most recent prior video hinting at "a rare exception to our usual format"
- Prepare and schedule social posts (`social-posts.md`) for the day of and day after launch

## Launch Day
- Publish main video with full SEO description (`description.md`)
- Post announcement email (`email-campaign.md` Email 1) within 2 hours of publish
- Post to X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram/Facebook same day (`social-posts.md`)
- Pin the sourcing-transparency comment (link to `sources-log.txt` summary) immediately

## Days 2-4
- Publish 2-3 Shorts from `shorts.md` (#1, #3, #8 recommended as the strongest standalone hooks)
- Post community engagement poll (`community-posts.md` #3 or #5)
- Send follow-up email (`email-campaign.md` Email 2)

## Week 2
- Post remaining Shorts if performance supports it (#4 requires the disputed-claim disclosure —
  never cut this one without Elop's denial in the same clip)
- Send value/lesson-focused email (`email-campaign.md` Email 3)
- Cross-reference in the next new video's community post if a thematic link exists (e.g. another
  "warning" or "pivot" story)

## Cross-Promotion Notes
- This package pairs naturally with package 003 (Garmin/TomTom) as the channel's only other
  deliberate public-company exception — consider a community post or end-screen link explicitly
  connecting the two once both are live
- Consider a future "warnings that were heeded vs. ignored" comparator video pairing this package
  with any future ignored-warning case, if one is built later

## Performance Checkpoints
- 48-hour CTR/retention check against Version A thumbnail; swap to Version B (numeral contrast) if
  CTR underperforms the channel's recent average
- 1-week check on comment sentiment regarding the Trojan Horse segment — if it's generating
  significant "you're spreading a conspiracy theory" pushback, review the segment's framing for
  clarity, not to remove the disclosure but to make the attribution even more explicit

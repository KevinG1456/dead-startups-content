# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Never render a real,
identifiable likeness of Stephen Elop. Never state "2 years" anywhere in thumbnail text.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a dark, minimal silhouette of an oil-rig platform engulfed in flame, a single
  small figure standing at its edge
- **Main subject:** the memo's own burning-platform metaphor, rendered literally
- **Emotion:** dread / gravity
- **Background:** near-black night sky, faint orange glow reflected on dark water below
- **Lighting:** the flame itself is the only light source
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** platform and flame fill the left two-thirds, bottom-third title-safe space
  reserved
- **Props:** generic oil-rig silhouette, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `"OUR PLATFORM IS BURNING." THEY LISTENED.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** deep black-blue background, saturated orange-red flame, white text
- **Contrast strategy:** the literalized metaphor communicates the entire premise before any text
  is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Stephen Elop, Steve Ballmer, or
  Satya Nadella in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Nokia, Microsoft, or Lumia logos/wordmarks in generated thumbnail art — build
  the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in the editor with proper
  sourcing
- Never state "2 years" in any thumbnail text — the real, sourced gap is roughly 4.5-5 years
- Never frame the Trojan Horse theory as fact in any thumbnail text

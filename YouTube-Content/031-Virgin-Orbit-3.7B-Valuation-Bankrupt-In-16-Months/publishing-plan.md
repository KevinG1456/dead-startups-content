# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Virgin Orbit has moderate-to-strong
name recognition via the Branson connection, and the story's evergreen search terms ("what happened
to Virgin Orbit," "Virgin Orbit bankruptcy") are durable rather than sharply news-cycle-driven at this
point (over a year removed from the collapse itself) — no urgency premium, so hold for the best
day/time slot rather than rushing publication.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, investors) studying SPAC-era financial exposure and
disclosure mechanics, plus a secondary audience of space-industry/aerospace enthusiasts drawn by the
Branson name and the technical launch-failure detail. Pair distribution accordingly — include at
least one space/aerospace-adjacent hashtag and community cross-post alongside the standard startup-
finance audience targeting.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to a "SPAC Casualties" playlist alongside package 028
(Bird), package 027 (23andMe), and package 026 (WeWork) as the channel's SPAC-era company cluster —
explicitly label Virgin Orbit as the fastest IPO-to-bankruptcy timeline in that playlist. Consider a
secondary placement in a "Fraud Charges" or "Sourced From SEC Filings" playlist alongside package 022
(Fast) and package 029 (Ozy Media) — but caption any such pairing explicitly as "the fraud here
belongs to an outside party, not the company," to avoid implying Virgin Orbit itself is in the same
category as those packages' central fraud cases.

## End Screen
Package 028 (Bird) + most recent autopsy; subscribe element. Bird is the strongest pairing since it's
the channel's other SPAC-to-bankruptcy story and sets up a direct, quantified comparison (25 months
vs. 16 months).

## Cards
Card at the "$228M RAISED — LESS THAN HALF OF WHAT WAS EXPECTED" title card (end of Segment 3, ~4:15)
linking to package 028 (Bird) as the companion SPAC-collapse story; card at the mid-video CTA
(~10:45) linking to the "SPAC Casualties" playlist; optional third card at the start of the fake-
bailout segment (~8:15) linking directly to a pinned comment or community post with the sourced
"$182M claimed / $0.87 real" graphic, for viewers who want the detail reinforced in text form while
watching.

## End CTA
"If you want to see this channel's other SPAC-to-bankruptcy story, watch the Bird video, package 028
— that one took about 25 months from IPO to Chapter 11. This one took 16, the fastest on this channel
so far. And subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library. One more thing before you go: did
you know about the fake $200 million bailout offer before this video, or was that new to you? Tell me
in the comments — and tell me whether you think Virgin Orbit could have survived if that one launch

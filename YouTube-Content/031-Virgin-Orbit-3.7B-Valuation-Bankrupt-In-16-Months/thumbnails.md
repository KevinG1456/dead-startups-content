# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram the SPAC IPO,
the fuel-filter failure, the fake bailout, AND the asset auction into a single image. Each of those
gets its own version below, or lives inside the video/Shorts instead of the thumbnail.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a tiny generic mechanical filter/part icon, glowing red, positioned dead-center in
  frame against a large faded rocket silhouette cracking apart behind it — the scale mismatch (a small
  part vs. a huge machine) is the entire thesis
- **Main subject:** the $100-part-vs-$3.7-billion-company scale contradiction
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint rocket-exhaust/star-field texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the small glowing part, rocket silhouette dimmer behind it
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** small part centered, cracked rocket silhouette filling the frame behind it,
  bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic mechanical filter/valve icon, generic rocket silhouette, crack/fracture lines
- **Thumbnail text:** `A $100 PART. A $3.7 BILLION COMPANY.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** red glow on the small part, white numerals, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the visible size mismatch between the tiny glowing part and the huge cracked
  rocket silhouette communicates the entire thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Richard Branson, Dan Hart, or Matthew
  Brown in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only; attribute
  real names verbally/on-screen in the video itself
- Do not put the real Virgin Orbit logo, wordmark, Cosmic Girl livery, or Virgin Group branding in
  generated thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real
  branding/footage in the editor with proper sourcing
- Never use Virgin Galactic branding, imagery, or references anywhere in this package's thumbnails —
  it is a separate company and mixing the two is this package's single biggest accuracy risk
- Never imply in a thumbnail that Virgin Orbit, Branson, or Hart committed fraud — the fake-bailout
  thumbnail (Version B) must read as being about Matthew Brown's scheme, not about Virgin Orbit's own
  conduct

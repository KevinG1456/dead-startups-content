Group, Cosmic Girl livery, Rocket Lab, Stratolaunch, Vast/Launcher), and no photorealistic likeness
of any real named person (Richard Branson, Dan Hart, Matthew Brown) — add verified text/branding and
any real archival photography in the editor with proper sourcing, not generated. Never include Virgin
Galactic branding, wordmarks, or spacecraft imagery anywhere in this package — it is a separate
company.

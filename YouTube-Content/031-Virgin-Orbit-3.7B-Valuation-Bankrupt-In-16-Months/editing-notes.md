# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open "$3.7 BILLION" vs.
  tiny-glowing-part reveal, and again on the "$228M RAISED — LESS THAN HALF OF WHAT WAS EXPECTED" card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the "$12M revenue / $42.9M loss, same quarter" reveal —
  this is the one visual beat in the video that should never feel rushed, since it's the core thesis

  rocket silhouette

  card — white text on black, held for a full beat
- "PRIVATE — NOT DISCLOSED" stamp graphic over a dollar-figure card during the pre-IPO segment
- "$3.7 BILLION" valuation card transitioning to "$228M RAISED — LESS THAN HALF OF WHAT WAS EXPECTED"

  enough to land as a genuine reveal, not a quick flash
- Launch-record scoreboard: 4 green checkmarks, 2 red marks, building up chronologically

  overheating → premature shutdown), title card "ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED — FEB 15, 2023"

- Standing "THIS FRAUD BELONGS TO MATTHEW BROWN — NOT VIRGIN ORBIT" framing card, held for a full

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Wikipedia, CNBC,
  Fortune, CNN Business, Space.com, SpaceNews, FlightGlobal, TechCrunch, The Register,
  BusinessWire/SEC EDGAR, SEC.gov Litigation Release No. 26031)
- "Not Virgin Galactic — a separate company" standing lower third the first time Virgin Orbit is
  named on screen, and again at the first mention of Richard Branson — no exceptions, this is the
  single most important lower-third rule in the package
- "Matthew Brown's fraud — not Virgin Orbit's" standing tag any time the fake-bailout material is on
  screen
- "Reported aggregate figure, not company-confirmed" lower third on the "about $1 billion" pre-IPO
  funding figure
- "Dan Hart's own words" lower third whenever his attributed quote is on screen

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic aerospace/rocket imagery — no real
  readable logos/trademarks (Virgin Orbit, Virgin Galactic, Rocket Lab, Stratolaunch) without rights
  clearance
- The "$3.7B → $36M" valuation-to-liquidation graphic should animate as a literal shrinking bar or
  numeral, so the scale collapse reads physically, not just numerically
- The private-funding-to-public-filings transition (Segment 2 to Segment 4) should animate as a
  curtain or veil lifting off the dollar-figure card, visually reinforcing "this was hidden, now it's
  not"
- The fake bank-statement screenshot should animate a visible "crack" or "tear" effect when the real
  balance is revealed beneath it

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the financial-exposure
  graphic, the failure-chain diagram, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "this fraud belongs to Matthew Brown" card
  in Segment 8 — no camera movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding / Nearly Five Years Private: measured, slightly optimistic, establishing energy
- The SPAC Deal: brief bright/triumphant cue on the opening-bell moment, curdling quickly into
  something more clinical as the "less than half of what was expected" reveal lands
- What the Filings Showed: dry, analytical, slightly ominous under the loss-figure reveal
- The Track Record / Start Me Up: measured through the scoreboard, shifting to tense/technical during
  the failure-chain diagram
- The Pause / The Fake Bailout: uneasy, slightly absurdist under the fake-offer reveal — this is the
  video's one moment of dark comedy, but keep it restrained, not played for outright laughs
- The Layoffs and Bankruptcy: quiet, funereal, minimal
- Sold for Parts: somber but not melodramatic, letting the numbers speak
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (Mar 2017 founding, Dec 2021 SPAC close,
  Jan 2023 launch failure, Mar 2023 pause/layoffs, Apr 2023 bankruptcy, May 2023 auction)
- Sharp "cash register / loss" SFX as the $42.9M loss figure lands in the financial-exposure segment
- Distinct mechanical "click/fail" SFX on the failure-chain diagram as each step (filter → pump →
  overheat → shutdown) animates in
- Soft "record scratch" or comedic-but-restrained sting on the fabricated bank-statement reveal —
  the one moment in the video allowed a touch of dark humor
- No comedic stingers anywhere in the layoffs/bankruptcy segment (10:00–11:30) — tone stays
  respectful and precise
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

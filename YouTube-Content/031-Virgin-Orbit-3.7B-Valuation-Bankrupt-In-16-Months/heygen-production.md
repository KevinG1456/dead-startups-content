# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and establishing during the founding/private-funding segments; a brief
  bright lift at the SPAC opening-bell beat, curdling into dry, analytical delivery through the
  financial-exposure segment; tense and technical through the launch-failure segment; restrained dark
  amusement through the fake-bailout segment; quiet and respectful through the layoffs/bankruptcy
  segment
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "$12M revenue / $42.9M loss" reveal
  and the "this fraud belongs to Matthew Brown, not Virgin Orbit" framing card — both need room to
  land clearly and be read precisely, on pace with the on-screen lower thirds
- **Emotional tone:** dry, analytical during the financial-exposure explainer; matter-of-fact
  authority for the launch-failure technical breakdown; restrained, slightly incredulous delivery
  during the fake-bailout segment — never played for outright comedy; measured gravity, no
  triumphalism, during the layoffs and bankruptcy segments
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine incredulity when stating the
  fabricated-bank-statement detail; visible care and neutrality when reading the "not Virgin Galactic"
  and "not Virgin Orbit's fraud" framing cards
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  full-arc timeline graphic and the failure-chain diagram
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only ($3.7B-vs-tiny-part graphic reveal)
- Script: "This company went public at a $3.7 billion valuation. Sixteen months later, it filed for
  bankruptcy. The part that helped cause it — one specific mechanical failure — cost about $100."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–1:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: clear, deliberate delivery on the "not Virgin Galactic" distinction — this
  must never feel rushed or like a footnote
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 1:00–1:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: deliberately clear on the "the fraud belongs to one man, and it isn't anyone
  who worked at this company" framing line
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Founding**
- Duration: 1:45–2:30 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (generic aerospace-hangar graphic), avatar VO
  under
- Performance direction: brisk, neutral, establishing energy
- Visual direction: generic hangar/aerospace establishing shot, no real branding

**Scene 05 — Nearly Five Years Private**
- Duration: 2:30–3:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, dollar-figure graphic with "PRIVATE — NOT

**Scene 06 — The SPAC Deal**

**Scene 16 — Final CTA + Outro**

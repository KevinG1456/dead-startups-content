# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Virgin Orbit: founded 2017 as a Virgin Galactic spin-off under Richard Branson's Virgin
Group; went public via SPAC merger Dec 30, 2021 at a $3.7 billion valuation; public SEC filings then
showed $7.4M revenue for 2021 against a single $42.9M quarterly loss in Q3 2022; a Jan 9, 2023 UK
launch failure (root cause: a dislodged fuel filter) destroyed 9 customer satellites; the company was
then targeted by a fabricated $200 million rescue offer from an unrelated Texas man, Matthew Brown
(later sued by the SEC, June 2024); laid off 675 employees (85% of staff) March 30, 2023; filed
Chapter 11 April 4, 2023 — 16 months after going public; assets sold at auction for ~$36 million to
Rocket Lab, Stratolaunch, and Vast/Launcher. Every title below keeps Virgin Orbit clearly distinct
from Virgin Galactic (a separate company) and never implies Branson, Dan Hart, or Virgin Orbit itself
committed fraud — that charge belongs only to Matthew Brown, an unrelated outside party.

## Recommended Title
**"Virgin Orbit Went Public at a $3.7 Billion Valuation. It Filed for Bankruptcy 16 Months Later —
After One Rocket Failure and a Fake $200 Million Bailout Offer."**

Reasoning: opens with the single most verifiable, most structurally important fact in this package —
the SPAC-to-bankruptcy timeline that anchors the "IPO'd and died within 24 months" pattern this title
was chosen to fill — then adds both major twists (the specific rocket failure and the fake bailout)
directly in the title itself, differentiating it from existing coverage that typically treats these
as two separate stories. Names the company directly (strong Branson-adjacent recognition), works
fully truncated on mobile at the first sentence alone, and sets up the financial-exposure mechanism
(what public filings revealed) without needing to front-load SEC-filing detail in the title. Matches
the title already used in this package's README.

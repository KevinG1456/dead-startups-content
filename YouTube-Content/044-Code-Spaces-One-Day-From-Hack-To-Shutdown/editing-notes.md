# Editing Notes

## Pacing
- Cold open through Segment 5 (the trap) should move briskly — this is the fastest-paced package on
  the channel by design, mirroring the actual speed of the real event.
- Segments 6-8 (the deletion, the backup reveal, the missing 2FA setting) are the technical/emotional
  core — slow down here specifically, use on-screen graphics to make the AWS mechanics land for a
  non-technical viewer.
- Segment 13 (the statement) should breathe — let the company's own words sit on screen without
  interruption for a beat before the avatar continues.

## Graphics Priorities (build these first)
1. Calendar flip (cold open + thumbnail concept)
2. Two identical folders (PRODUCTION / BACKUP) dissolving simultaneously — this is the single most
   important visual in the video, it needs to land clearly
3. Full-arc timeline (closing lesson)
4. Unchecked 2FA checkbox
5. Locked door / hidden second door

## Text-Card Discipline
- Never render "12 HOURS" or any other precise hour count anywhere in the video — use "THE NEXT DAY"
  or the JUNE 17 → JUNE 18 date pairing instead.
- Never render a founder name, funding figure, or customer count — none are confirmed.
- Always keep the "NO PERPETRATOR EVER IDENTIFIED" card sober, not sensationalized — no speculative
  visual (hooded figure, etc.) implying a specific attacker profile.

## Audio/Music Direction
- Tension should build steadily from Segment 5 (the trap) through Segment 9 (the missing setting) —
  this is the video's suspense arc.
- Drop to a quieter, more somber bed under Segment 13 (the statement) — let the company's own words
  carry the emotional weight without competing music.
- Return to a measured, slightly warmer bed for the closing lesson (Segment 16) — this is the
  takeaway, not more tension.

## Caption/Subtitle Note
- Spell out "two-factor authentication" in full the first time it's used in captions, then "2FA" is
  fine afterward — assume a portion of the audience is non-technical.

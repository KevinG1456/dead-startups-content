# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and matter-of-fact through the setup and the honesty note; rising
  tension through the ransom/password-reset/re-entry sequence; a hard dramatic beat at the "backups
  were in the same account" reveal; somber through the statement reading; reflective through the
  closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, deliberately slower and clearer during the AWS-terminology
  segments (5:00–8:00) so a non-technical viewer can follow, and during the direct quote of Code
  Spaces' own shutdown statement
- **Emotional tone:** sympathetic to Code Spaces throughout — never mocking; genuine gravity at the
  "backups were in the same account" reveal; quiet respect when reading the company's own words
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine concern/surprise at the Segment
  7 reveal; visible care when reading the honesty-flag framing card and the company's final statement
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  file-tree/deletion graphics and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

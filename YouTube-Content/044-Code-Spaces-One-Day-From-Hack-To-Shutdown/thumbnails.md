# Thumbnail Concepts

Rule for this package (channel standard): one dominant idea per thumbnail, not the whole story
crammed in. No invented founder photo — no confirmed founder exists to depict. No specific hour
count ("12 HOURS") — use the dated "TUESDAY → WEDNESDAY" framing instead, which is fully sourced.

## Concept 1 (Recommended) — The Calendar Flip
Dominant idea: a calendar page showing "TUESDAY," half-torn to reveal "WEDNESDAY" underneath, with a
red "GONE" stamp over a simple cloud-storage icon. No text needed beyond the two day names — the
juxtaposition alone communicates the speed.

## Concept 2 — The Two Identical Folders
Dominant idea: two identical folder icons side by side, labeled "PRODUCTION" and "BACKUP" — both
shown mid-dissolve/deletion at the same time. Single line of text: "SAME ACCOUNT."

## Concept 3 — The Unchecked Box
Dominant idea: one large, simple checkbox, unchecked, labeled "2FA." Nothing else on screen except a
small cracked-lock icon beside it.

## Concept 4 — The Ransom Note
Dominant idea: a stylized note/terminal-window graphic with a redacted ransom demand and a visible
"@hotmail.com" fragment — communicates "real, slightly absurd extortion attempt" instantly.

## Concept 5 — The Locked Door, Twice
Dominant idea: a single door with a padlock being closed, and a second, smaller hidden door swinging
open right behind it — communicates "they thought they locked it" without needing text.

**Recommended:** Concept 1 (Calendar Flip) — clearest, fastest-reading, no jargon required, and
directly reinforces the recommended title's hook.

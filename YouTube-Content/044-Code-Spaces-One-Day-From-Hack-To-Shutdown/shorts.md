# Shorts Scripts

Rule for this package: Shorts stay strictly to ironclad, dated facts — no precise "12 hours" claim,
no invented founder/funding detail. Each script targets 30-45 seconds.

## Short 1 — "The Backup Was in the Same Room as the Fire"
- Hook (0-3s): "This company had backups. The hacker deleted those too."
- Body: June 2014 — a hacker got into Code Spaces' cloud account and demanded a ransom. The company
  said no and tried to change its password. The hacker was already back in. Then they deleted
  everything — production data AND backups, because both lived in the same account.
- Payoff: "A backup that lives next to the original isn't a backup. It's just a second target."
- CTA: "Full story on the channel."

## Short 2 — "Hacked Tuesday. Gone Wednesday."
- Hook (0-3s): "This is the fastest company death on this whole channel."
- Body: One DDoS attack used as cover for a real console breach. One ransom demand, refused. One
  failed password reset. One missing setting: two-factor authentication. By the next day, the
  company was done.
- Payoff: "One missing checkbox. One dead company."
- CTA: "Watch the full breakdown — link in bio."

## Short 3 — "The Ransom Note Had a Hotmail Address"
- Hook (0-3s): "This company got a ransom demand from a Hotmail address. They said no anyway."
- Body: The attacker asked for money to stop a DDoS attack. Code Spaces refused and tried to lock
  them out. The attacker had already planted a backup way in — and used it to delete everything.
- Payoff: "Nobody ever found out who did it."
- CTA: "Full story on the channel."

## Short 4 — "Why Your Backup Might Not Actually Be a Backup"
- Hook (0-3s): "If your backup lives in the same account as your original data, you don't have a
  backup."
- Body: This is exactly what killed Code Spaces in 2014 — one hacker, one AWS account, and no
  separation between production and backup. When they got in, they deleted both in the same sweep.
- Payoff: "Check where your backups actually live."
- CTA: "Full story — link in bio."

## Short 5 — "They Did the Right Thing. It Wasn't Enough."
- Hook (0-3s): "This company refused to pay a hacker's ransom. It cost them the whole company
  anyway."
- Body: Refusing to pay extortion is the right call. Code Spaces did it. But the deeper problem —
  no two-factor authentication, backups stored with production — had already been built in long
  before the attack.
- Payoff: "The right decision in the moment couldn't fix a mistake made months earlier."
- CTA: "Full story on the channel."

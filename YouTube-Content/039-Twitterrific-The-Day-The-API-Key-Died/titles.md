## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **This App Invented the Word "Tweet." Twitter Killed It Anyway.** — the single sharpest, most
   surprising, most ironic hook in the sources-log, states the whole thesis in one sentence
2. The Company That Made Twitter's Bird Logo Got Killed by Twitter, in Hours. — equally strong irony
   hook, leads with the visual/brand angle
3. They Built the First Twitter App Ever. Twitter Erased It Without a Phone Call. — strong "first
   ever" framing plus the specific, human "no phone call" detail
4. Fifteen Years. Zero Warning. One Policy Change. — clean, punchy, precise summary of the whole arc
5. The Word "Tweet" Exists Because of This App. It's Gone Now. — sharp, quotable, sets up the origin
   story as the hook
6. This App Won an Apple Design Award. It Still Died in an Afternoon. — strong contrast between
   institutional recognition and the abruptness of the ending
7. The Bluebird Was Their Idea First. — short, punchy, curiosity-driven
8. One API Change. Two Apps. Fifteen Combined Years, Gone the Same Day. — sets up the Tweetbot
   companion beat as part of the hook itself
9. What Happened to Twitterrific? The Full Story — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
10. Twitter API Third Party Apps Shutdown Explained — strong secondary search title for viewers
    specifically researching the API cutoff event itself

## Recommended Title
**"This App Invented the Word 'Tweet' and the Bluebird Logo. Twitter Killed It With Zero Warning, in
Hours."**

Reasoning: leads with the single most surprising, most quotable historical fact in this package —
Twitterrific's role in naming "tweet" and creating the bluebird icon — before revealing the ironic,
abrupt ending, maximizing the payoff structure the title doc's pattern calls for ("one company, hour by
hour"). Works fully truncated on mobile at the first sentence alone, and differentiates this package
immediately from generic "Twitter API shutdown" coverage. Matches the title already used in this
package's README.

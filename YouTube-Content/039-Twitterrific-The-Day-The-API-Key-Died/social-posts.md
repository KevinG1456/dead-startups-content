## LinkedIn
"A real, poignant case study in platform-dependency risk — told through the company that helped
create the platform's own identity:

Twitterrific, built by independent software studio The Iconfactory, launched January 15, 2007, as the
first-ever Twitter desktop client. Twitter's own developer suggested the word 'tweet' instead of
'twit' for posts inside the app — Twitterrific adopted it, and the term spread from there. That same
month, Iconfactory designer David Lanham created the app's cheerful bluebird mascot — which Twitter
itself later adopted as its own official logo.

Twitterrific ran for fifteen years. On January 12-13, 2023, without warning, Twitter revoked API
access for third-party apps. Twitterrific broke instantly. Twitter stayed silent for five days.
Iconfactory's farewell post, January 19, 2023: 'the app's sudden and undignified demise is due to an
unannounced and undocumented policy change by an increasingly capricious Twitter.'

A separate company, Tapbots, lost its own app, Tweetbot, to the exact same cutoff the same day.

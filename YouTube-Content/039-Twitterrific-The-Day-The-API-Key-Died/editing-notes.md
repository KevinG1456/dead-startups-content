# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open cracked-bluebird
  reveal, and on the "5 DAYS OF SILENCE" card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the Iconfactory farewell-post quote cards — these are the
  emotional core of the video and should never feel rushed

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open cheerful bluebird silhouette icon, crack forming across it, red "ACCESS REVOKED" stamp
- "There was no call. No email. Just a broken app." pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- "FIRST TWITTER CLIENT EVER — JANUARY 2007" title card
- "TWEET" attributed origin card
- Generic bluebird silhouette icon reveal for the Ollie/bluebird origin segment
- "15 YEARS" title card
- "API ACCESS REVOKED — JANUARY 12-13, 2023" title card
- "5 DAYS OF SILENCE" countdown-style card
- Attributed Iconfactory farewell-post quote cards, held on screen for a full beat each
- "TWEETBOT — SAME DAY, DIFFERENT COMPANY" title card
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: idea (Dec 2006) → launch (Jan 2007) → "tweet"
  adopted (2007) → bluebird created (Jan 2007) → 15 years running → API cutoff (Jan 12-13, 2023) →
  silence (5 days) → farewell post (Jan 19, 2023)
- Corner bluebird icon that cracks progressively at each escalating beat, reaching full fracture
  exactly at the API-cutoff reveal

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (The Iconfactory's own
  blog, Wikipedia, TechCrunch, 9to5Mac)
- "Bootstrapped, independent studio — not venture-funded" standing lower third the first time
  Iconfactory is introduced
- "Real, documented cultural history — not a trademark claim" lower third on the tweet/bluebird origin
  segments
- "The Iconfactory's own words" lower third on all farewell-post quotes

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/coding imagery — no real readable
  logos/trademarks (Twitterrific, Twitter/X, Tweetbot, Iconfactory, Tapbots) without rights clearance
- The corner bluebird icon's progressive cracking should be visible but subtle throughout, not
  distracting, reaching full fracture only at the exact API-cutoff moment
- The "5 DAYS OF SILENCE" countdown should animate day by day, landing with a hard stop on day five
  before the vague-explanation reveal

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the origin-story reveals and
  the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the farewell-post quote reads — no camera
  movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Idea / The Launch: warm, nostalgic, early-2000s-tech optimism
- The Word "Tweet" / The Bluebird: bright, discovery-driven, a genuine sense of "aha"
- Fifteen Years: steady, warm, montage-like
- The Cutoff: sharp, sudden tonal drop
- The Silence: uneasy, minimal, waiting
- The Farewell: quiet, sincere, genuinely moving — not melodramatic
- Tweetbot, Same Day: measured, a brief echo of the same sharp tonal drop
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (Dec 2006, Jan 2007, Jan 12-13 2023, Jan 17
  2023, Jan 19 2023)
- A soft "chime" SFX on the "tweet" word-origin reveal and the bluebird-creation reveal
- A sharp "power-down" or "disconnect" SFX at the exact moment of the API cutoff reveal
- Silence (no music, no SFX) held briefly right after the cutoff reveal, before the "5 DAYS OF
  SILENCE" card animates in — reinforcing the actual silence described
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays warm-but-serious throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

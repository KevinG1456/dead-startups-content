# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** warm and nostalgic through the origin-story segments; bright discovery energy on
  the "tweet"/bluebird reveals; steady through the "fifteen years" beat; sharp tonal shift at the
  cutoff; uneasy through the silence; quiet and sincere through the farewell; measured through the
  Tweetbot companion beat; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the farewell-post quotes and the "no call,
  no email, just a broken app" framing line — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** genuine warmth and discovery during the origin story; sharp, serious gravity at
  the cutoff — never played for shock value alone; sincere, quiet respect during the farewell-quote
  reads; measured, even-handed tone toward Twitter/X and Elon Musk throughout, no accusatory framing
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine delight at the "tweet"/bluebird
  reveals; visible sincerity when reading the farewell-post quotes
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the origin-
  story reveals and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# Editing Notes

## Pacing
- Segments 6-9 (the five failed channels, 5:30-7:45) are the video's spine — keep cuts tight and
  rhythmic here, roughly 20-30 seconds per channel, with a consistent visual "stamp" (red X / FAILED
  card) marking each one to build a repeating pattern the viewer can anticipate
- Do not let the founding/product setup (1:40-4:00) run long — it's context, not the payoff; keep total
  under 2.5 minutes combined

## Graphics Priorities
- Five-channel scorecard (recurring element, first appears full at ~5:30, updates through 7:45) is the
  single most important recurring graphic in this video — build it once, reuse/update it across all
  four channel segments
- 101→22 user collapse graphic (Segment 8) and flat-revenue-vs-rising-users chart (Segment 13) are the
  two next-highest priority builds — both are core numeric-shock beats
- Full-arc timeline (Segment 17) should visually echo the five-channel scorecard's iconography where
  possible for visual continuity

## Audio / Music
- Keep music understated and measured through the founder-quote beats (Segments 9-12) — these need to
  read as respectful and factual, not dramatic or mocking
- Slight tension build through Segments 13-15 (price cut → runway → shutdown decision)
- Warmer, resolving tone from Segment 16 (the postmortem) through the outro

## On-Screen Text
- Every founder quote (Segments 9-12) must appear as an on-screen text card with clear attribution
  ("— Matthew Volm, Medium") — do not present these as paraphrase
- [VERIFY] framing card (Segment 15, shutdown date) must remain in the final cut per `sources-log.txt`
  — do not state a specific shutdown month

## Tone Check (before final export)
- Re-watch full cut once specifically checking for any moment that could read as mocking the three
  founders, the missing feature, or the naming irony — adjust music/pacing if any beat feels unkind
  rather than respectful

## Shorts Strategy Notes
- Post 2-3 days before the main video to build curiosity, remainder in the week following to sustain
  traffic back to the long-form video
- Each Short should work as a fully standalone piece of information (per channel convention) while
  still creating an open loop toward the full breakdown
- Prioritize Short 1 and Short 2 as the strongest performers — most self-contained hooks

# HeyGen Production
Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and respectful throughout; a brief lift of curiosity at the naming-irony
  detail; steady, slightly heavier tone through the churn and runway segments; warm and clear through
  the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, with a deliberate slowdown when reading each of the founder's
  five direct quotes — these need room to land as his actual words, not paraphrase
- **Emotional tone:** genuine respect for the founder's candor throughout — never mocking, even during
  the missing-auto-renewal and naming-irony beats; quiet gravity during the runway/shutdown segments
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a small, warm nod of respect when introducing the
  postmortem itself; brief wry (not mocking) expression on the pronunciation-irony beat
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  five-channel scorecard and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

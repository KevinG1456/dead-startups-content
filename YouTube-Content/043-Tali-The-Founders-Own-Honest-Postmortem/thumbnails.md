# Thumbnail Concepts

Channel rule: one dominant idea per thumbnail, not an attempt to illustrate the whole video. For Tali,
the dominant idea is the five-channel scorecard — it reads instantly at small size and needs no brand
recognition to land.

## Concept 1 (Recommended) — The Five-Channel Scorecard
- Five simple icons in a row: a phone (sales), a megaphone (digital marketing), a search bar (SEM), a
  thumbs-up/social icon (paid social), a handshake (resellers)
- A bold red X stamped over all five
- Small bottom text: "ALL 5 FAILED"
- Founder's face (HeyGen avatar frame) small in one corner, neutral/serious expression
- Why it works: single dominant idea (the scorecard), reads at thumbnail size, promises the exact
  structure of the video

## Concept 2 — The Churn Number
- Giant "78%" in bold red numerals, dominant across the frame
- Small subtext beneath: "CHURN — ONE MISSING FEATURE"
- Minimal background, single dominant idea
- Why it works: a single shocking number is the whole thumbnail; no clutter

## Concept 3 — The Pronunciation Irony
- Large stylized word "TALI" with a red strikethrough pronunciation "TALLY" beneath it and the correct
  "TAIL-eye" beside it
- Small microphone icon
- Why it works: self-contained visual joke/irony, doesn't need any other context to generate curiosity

## Concept 4 — The Runway Countdown
- Large "2.5 MONTHS" numerals with a simple depleting fuel-gauge or hourglass graphic
- Why it works: single dominant tension image, works even for viewers unfamiliar with the company

## A/B Test Pairing
Primary: Concept 1 (Five-Channel Scorecard) vs. Concept 2 (78% Churn Number) — both lead with a single
dominant idea; test structural-promise framing against shock-number framing.

## Text Overlay Rules
- Max 4-5 words on any thumbnail
- High contrast: white/red text on dark background
- No attempt to summarize the full video in the thumbnail — one idea only, per channel convention

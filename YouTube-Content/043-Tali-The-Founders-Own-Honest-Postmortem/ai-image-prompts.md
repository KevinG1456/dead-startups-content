# AI Image Prompts (for ChatGPT / Thumbnail & B-Roll Generation)

Follows the channel's thumbnail-simplicity rule — each prompt targets one dominant visual idea.

## Thumbnail Prompts

**Prompt 1 (Recommended — Five-Channel Scorecard):**
"Bold, high-contrast YouTube thumbnail. Five simple flat-style icons in a horizontal row on a dark
navy background: a phone handset, a megaphone, a magnifying-glass-over-search-bar, a thumbs-up bubble,
and a handshake. A thick red X stamped diagonally across each icon. Small bold white text at the bottom:
'ALL 5 FAILED'. Clean, minimal, no clutter, high mobile-thumbnail legibility."

**Prompt 2 (78% Churn Number):**
"Bold YouTube thumbnail, dark background, giant red numerals '78%' filling most of the frame, small
white subtext beneath reading 'CHURN'. Minimal, high contrast, no other visual elements."

**Prompt 3 (Pronunciation Irony):**
"Bold YouTube thumbnail, dark background. Large white stylized text 'TALI'. Beneath it, smaller text
'TAIL-eye' in green with a checkmark, and 'TALLY' in red with a strikethrough. Small microphone icon in
one corner. Clean, minimal, legible at small size."

## B-Roll / Section Image Prompts

**Three Matts founding graphic:** "Flat vector illustration, three simple silhouette figures standing
together, each with a small nametag reading 'Matt', muted professional color palette, clean minimal
style, dark background."

**Voice assistant / product graphic:** "Flat vector illustration of a smart speaker device with sound
waves, next to a simple document/invoice icon, connected by a dotted line, minimal professional style."

**Churn collapse graphic:** "Simple flat-style bar or icon-grid graphic showing 101 small user icons,
79 of them fading to gray/transparent, 22 remaining in full color, dark background, minimal style."

**Runway depletion graphic:** "Simple flat-style fuel gauge or hourglass icon nearly empty, dark red
warning color, minimal background, clean professional style."

**Flat revenue despite rising users graphic:** "Simple line-and-bar combo chart mockup, a rising bar
series (users) next to a flat line (revenue), minimal flat design, dark background, no real data
labels, illustrative only."

## Style Consistency Notes
- Keep palette consistent with channel's established dark-navy/red-accent scheme
- No literal renderings of a specific real person's face in generated art — use silhouette/icon style
  for founders; the HeyGen avatar handles any on-camera human presence
- Every image: one dominant idea, minimal supporting elements, legible at small size

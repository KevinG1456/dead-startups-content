# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Color Labs, Apple, Sequoia
Capital, Bain Capital), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Bill Nguyen, Peter
Pham) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor with proper
sourcing, not generated.

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** bright and optimistic through the pre-launch funding/product segments; energetic
  then uncertain through the launch/confusion segment; declining through the collapse; a small ironic
  lift on the "thrilled" quote; a brief hopeful rebuild through the pivot, fading again; measured and
  matter-of-fact through the acqui-hire; quiet and restrained at the shutdown
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "who is this for?" framing cards and
  the Doug Leone quote — both need room to land clearly and precisely
- **Emotional tone:** genuine curiosity/excitement during the funding and product explainer; dry
  disbelief during the confusion/collapse segment; careful, even delivery on the "thrilled" quote,
  letting the irony come from context rather than vocal sarcasm; matter-of-fact authority through the
  acqui-hire and shutdown
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine disbelief at the $41M-to-$7M
  reveal; visible care and precision when reading the "who is this for" framing cards
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  download-spike graph and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only ($41M-to-$7M numeral-shrink graphic reveal)
- Script: "This company raised $41 million before a single user ever opened the app. It sold, eighteen
  months later, for $7 million — and not even for the product itself."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–1:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: clear, measured delivery establishing the company and the core "everyone
  nearby" positioning problem
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 1:00–1:40 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: deliberately clear on "this is a story about product positioning, not about
  money or hype" — the video's core framing line
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Pre-Launch Funding**
- Duration: 1:40–2:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, "$41 MILLION — BEFORE LAUNCH" title card
- Performance direction: bright, genuinely curious energy
- Visual direction: funding title card

**Scene 05 — The Product**
- Duration: 2:30–3:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (proximity-radar graphic), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: measured, careful precision on the "who is this for?" framing card
- Visual direction: generic proximity-radar graphic, no real app UI

**Scene 06 — The Launch and the Confusion**
- Duration: 3:15–4:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with download-spike graph
- Performance direction: energetic building into dry disbelief
- Visual direction: download-spike graph

**Scene 07 — The Collapse**
- Duration: 4:15–5:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, title card
- Performance direction: declining energy, matter-of-fact on the departure and MAU figures
- Visual direction: "UNDER 100,000 MAU — SEPTEMBER 2011" title card

**Scene 08 — "Thrilled"**
- Duration: 5:00–6:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, attributed quote card
- Performance direction: even, careful delivery — let the irony come from the visual context, not
  vocal sarcasm
- Visual direction: quote card with attribution

**Scene 09 — Color for Facebook**
- Duration: 6:00–7:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, title card
- Performance direction: brief hopeful lift, fading as the repeat-mistake framing lands
- Visual direction: "THE SAME QUESTION, UNANSWERED AGAIN" title card

**Scene 10 — The Acqui-Hire**
- Duration: 7:30–8:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, title card
- Performance direction: measured, matter-of-fact, not triumphant or mocking
- Visual direction: "$7 MILLION — ENGINEERS AND PATENTS" title card

**Scene 11 — The Shutdown**
- Duration: 8:45–9:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, title card
- Performance direction: quiet, restrained
- Visual direction: "SHUT DOWN — DECEMBER 31, 2012" title card

**Scene 12 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~9:15 (inside Scene 11) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address

**Scene 13 — The Actual Lesson**

**Scene 14 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 11:00–12:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: return to warm, controlled-authority tone, steady eye contact through
  sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements (package 012 callout, subscribe) animate in during final 5
  seconds only

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Never reference the excluded
lawsuit content in any thumbnail concept or text.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large glowing "$41M" numeral shrinking down into a small dim "$7M" numeral
- **Main subject:** the pre-launch-funding-to-fire-sale contrast
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint app-grid/photo-tile texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the "$41M" numeral, dimming toward "$7M"
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** "$41M" large and dominant, shrinking arrow toward smaller "$7M," bottom-third
  title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic numeral graphics, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `$41 MILLION BEFORE LAUNCH. $7 MILLION AT THE END.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** gold glow on "$41M," dim gray on "$7M," near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the visible size mismatch between the two numerals communicates the entire
  thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Bill Nguyen or Peter Pham in any
  thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Color Labs, Apple, Sequoia Capital, or Bain Capital logos/wordmarks in generated
  thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in the
  editor with proper sourcing
- Never reference or allude to the excluded lawsuit content in any thumbnail text or visual concept

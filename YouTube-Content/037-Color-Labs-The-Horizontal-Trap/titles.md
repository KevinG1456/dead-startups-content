## Recommended Title
**"Color Labs Raised $41 Million Before Launch. It Sold for $7 Million, for Its Patents, Not Its
Product."**

Reasoning: leads with the two most precisely sourced, most dramatic dollar figures in this package,
while the second clause ("for its patents, not its product") sets up the video's actual differentiator
— that Apple's acquisition wasn't a validation of Color's product, it was a fire sale for engineering
talent and IP. Works fully truncated on mobile at the first sentence alone, and avoids any implication
tied to the excluded lawsuit content. Matches the title already used in this package's README.

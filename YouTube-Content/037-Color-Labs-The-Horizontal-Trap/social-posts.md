## LinkedIn
"A real, dollar-precise case study in what happens when a startup never defines its actual customer:

Color Labs, founded by Bill Nguyen (previously sold Lala to Apple) and Peter Pham, raised $41 million
entirely before its product, Color, ever launched — from Sequoia Capital, Bain Capital, and Silicon
Valley Bank. Color offered proximity-based photo sharing: see photos from anyone nearby, automatically.
Built for everyone in physical range, not for any specific customer.

The March 2011 launch drove over a million downloads and immediate confusion about the app's purpose.
By September, monthly active users had fallen below 100,000. A pivot to live video broadcasting on
Facebook repeated the same underlying mistake in a new format.

In November 2012, Apple acqui-hired Color Labs for approximately $7 million — about 17% of what it had
raised — for its engineering team and patents, not its product. The app shut down that December.

Full breakdown: [video link]

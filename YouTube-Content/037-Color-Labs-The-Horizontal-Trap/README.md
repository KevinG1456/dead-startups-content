# 037 — Color Labs Raised $41 Million Before Launch. It Sold for $7 Million, for Its Patents, Not Its Product.

**Package status:** Filled in and sourced — built around Color Labs' fully documented public history
(TechCrunch, Forbes, Wikipedia, Medium/FAIL Company — full citations in `sources-log.txt`). Seventh
title on the channel sourced from the "dead-saas-30-Content Titles new-video-ideas.md" batch (Cluster

from the same batch were skipped for lack of a real anchor (see `project_channel_overview.md`
memory). Also resolves an open `[VERIFY]` flag from package 012 (The Pivot Graveyard), which mentioned
Color Labs only briefly and unsourced.

**The real case:** Color Labs, founded by serial entrepreneur Bill Nguyen (previously sold Lala to
Apple) and Peter Pham, raised $41 million — entirely before its product ever launched — from Sequoia
Capital, Bain Capital, and Silicon Valley Bank. Its product, Color, offered proximity-based photo
sharing: automatically seeing photos from any other Color user physically nearby, no friend requests
or follows required. It was built for "everyone nearby" — not any defined customer or use case. The
March 2011 launch drove over a million downloads and immediate, widespread confusion about what the
app was actually for. Monthly active users fell below 100,000 within six months; a co-founder left
within three months of launch. The team pivoted to live video broadcasting to Facebook — also with no
defined audience — which also failed to gain traction. In November 2012, Apple acqui-hired Color Labs
for approximately $7 million, about 17% of what it had raised, explicitly for its engineering team and
patents rather than its product. The Color app shut down December 31, 2012.

**Framing requirement — read before touching any file:** a November 2012 lawsuit by a former employee
made severe personal-misconduct allegations against Bill Nguyen. This package deliberately excludes
those allegations from every file — they are unadjudicated civil claims, tangential to this title's
actual subject (product positioning strategy), and disproportionately severe relative to the story
this package tells. See `sources-log.txt` for the full reasoning. Present Nguyen neutrally throughout,
as Color's co-founder, with no reference to the lawsuit's personal claims.

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the dollar-figure/tiny-icon contrast reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Scale-mismatch animation: dollar figure growing large while a phone/usage icon stays tiny, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Delay timeline graphic: showing announced launch dates vs. actual dates, stacking up visually over roughly two years
- Red-flags checklist graphic building progressively through the diligence-focused segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or quote is referenced
- Name/title lower-third for founder/investors if photos or archival clips are used

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or archival stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the timeline/scale-mismatch graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Rise section: slightly ironic/too-good-to-be-true undertone rather than pure triumph
- Autopsy/diligence section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual timeline carry the tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Subtle "record scratch" or comedic-but-restrained stinger at the initial scale-mismatch reveal (this is one of the few titles in the batch where a light touch of irony in the sound design fits the well-known, semi-legendary tone of the story)
- Rubber-stamp thud on major milestone/delay markers
- Soft page-turn sounds under document/press-clipping B-roll
- Keep overall tone informative and diligence-focused rather than purely mocking, even where the SFX has a light touch

## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct a funded startup's failure using primary sources — no speculation, just documents and decisions.

This week: Clinkle, the mobile payments startup founded by then-Stanford student Lucas Duplan, which raised a reported $25 million seed round in 2013 — before shipping a real product. What followed was roughly two years of repeated launch delays, intense press scrutiny, and reported internal culture problems, ending in major layoffs and a product that never achieved meaningful public adoption.

This video goes beyond the well-known headline to focus on the diligence-failure angle: what specific red flags existed at the time of the round, and what founders and investors can actually learn from how this happened.

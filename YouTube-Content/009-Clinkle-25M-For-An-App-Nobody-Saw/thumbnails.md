# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the funding-vs-usage contradiction.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "$25M" figure in green, next to a phone/app icon silhouette with a tiny, almost invisible "0 users" style counter beneath it
- **Main subject:** the scale mismatch between the number and the (near-empty) usage counter
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** dollar figure top, phone/app icon bottom with tiny counter, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** simple phone/app icon silhouette
- **Thumbnail text:** `$25M. ALMOST NOBODY USED IT.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green dollar figure, gray/faded phone icon, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the scale mismatch (huge number, tiny icon) does the communicating

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "$25,000,000" spelled out in full with all zeros, tiny phone icon beside it
- **Text:** `FOR AN APP NOBODY USED`
- **Why it works:** spelling out the full number emphasizes scale; the phone icon micro-scale creates an instant visual joke/contradiction
- **CTR prediction:** high — novelty of the full-zero number stands out
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the $25M/phone-icon/tiny-counter combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$25M. ALMOST NOBODY USED IT.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of the contradiction paired with the visual proof (scale mismatch) in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), incredulous/knowing expression, small "$25M" tag in the corner
- **Text:** `HOW DID THIS GET FUNDED?`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; question format promises a satisfying investigative answer
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

## A/B Testing

| Version | Emotion | CTR Strategy | Best Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Curiosity | Full zero-spelled number + micro-scale icon | Cold/browse traffic |
| B | Shock | Direct scale-mismatch statement | Search + browse (recommended primary) |
| C | Authority | Face-led, investigative question framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

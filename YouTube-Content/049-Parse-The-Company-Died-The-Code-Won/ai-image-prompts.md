# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Parse, Facebook,
GitHub), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Ilya Sukhar, James Yu, Tikhon
Bernstam, Kevin Lacker) — add verified text/branding and any real archival material in the editor
with proper sourcing, not generated.

## Thumbnail Prompts
**Prompt A (recommended primary):** "YouTube thumbnail, a bright glowing stats-panel graphic in the
upper two-thirds of frame, dust/particle dissolve effect on a grayed-out abstract logo shape below
it, dark background with faint code-line texture, single spotlight on the stats panel, high
contrast, empty space at bottom third for title text, no readable numbers or brand names, 16:9 --ar
16:9"

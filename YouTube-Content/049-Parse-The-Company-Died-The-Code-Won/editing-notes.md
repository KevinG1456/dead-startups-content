# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open GitHub-stats-to-
  dissolving-logo reveal, and on the "STILL ACTIVELY MAINTAINED — 2026" title card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the Tikhon Bernstam/Scribd cross-reference callback —
  give returning viewers a beat to register the connection

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open GitHub-style stats panel ("21,407 stars," recent commit timestamp) zooming out to
  reveal a "SHUT DOWN 2017" stamp over a company logo
- "This isn't a story about a company that failed. It's a story about what happened after."
  pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- "Before Parse / After Parse" mobile-dev-stack graphic for the founding segment
- Split-screen Scribd logo (still operating) vs. Parse logo (company dead, code alive) for the
  Bernstam cross-reference beat
- "$85 MILLION — FACEBOOK'S ACQUISITION, APRIL 2013" title card
- "ONE YEAR'S NOTICE" title card with a calendar graphic spanning Jan 2016 to Jan 2017
- "OPEN-SOURCED — SAME DAY AS THE SHUTDOWN ANNOUNCEMENT" title card
- "JANUARY 28, 2017 — SHUT DOWN ON SCHEDULE" title card
- Live-looking GitHub repo stats screenshot, "STILL ACTIVELY MAINTAINED — 2026" title card
- Split card, "COMPANY: DEAD" / "CODE: ALIVE" side by side
- Side-by-side card, Mailbox (gone entirely) vs. Parse Server (thriving today) for the package-046
  cross-reference
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founded 2011 → YC S11 → Facebook acquisition
  Apr 2013 ($85M) → shutdown announced Jan 2016 (1 year notice) → Parse Server open-sourced same
  day → Parse.com shuts down on schedule Jan 28 2017 → still actively maintained, 21,000+ stars,
  2026
- Running "years since shutdown" counter visible from Segment 5 onward, ticking upward to "9
  YEARS" by the timeline's end

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Wikipedia,
  TechCrunch, VentureBeat, GitHub repo stats)
- "Reported figure — Facebook never officially disclosed terms" standing lower third on the $85M
  acquisition reveal
- "Aggregate GitHub activity only — no specific companies confirmed" lower third whenever present-
  day Parse Server usage is discussed
- "Verified: same founder, package 007" lower third on the Tikhon Bernstam/Scribd callback

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic mobile-dev/startup-office imagery — no
  real readable logos/trademarks (Parse, Facebook, GitHub) without rights clearance
- The cold-open stats-panel-to-dissolving-logo transition should animate slowly enough that both
  elements are legible before the transition completes
- The "died vs. won" split card should animate as a clean, simultaneous side-by-side reveal, not
  sequential

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the numeral-contrast
  graphics, the timeline, and the split cards
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "21,407 stars, active in 2026" reveal
  and the Bernstam/Scribd callback — no camera movement competing with either for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: curious, slightly mysterious pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt
  card
- The Founding / A Founder You've Seen Before: light, optimistic
- The Acquisition / Three Good Years: measured, upbeat
- The Announcement: neutral, slightly tense (uncertainty before the reveal of HOW it was handled)
- The Part That Matters: warming, hopeful lift — this is where the tone starts turning positive
- Exactly on Schedule: steady, matter-of-fact
- Nine Years Later / Died vs. Won: bright, warm, quietly triumphant — the video's emotional peak,
  distinct from this channel's usual somber peaks
- A Different Kind of Acqui-Hire: brief, wry contrast beat
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a warmer, resolved version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2011 founding, Apr 2013 acquisition,
  Jan 2016 announcement, Jan 28 2017 shutdown, 2026 present-day)
- A bright "notification/activity" SFX synced to the GitHub-stats reveal in Segment 8
- No comedic stingers, but this video can carry a slightly warmer, more optimistic sound palette
  than the channel's usual catalog — the tone genuinely earns it
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

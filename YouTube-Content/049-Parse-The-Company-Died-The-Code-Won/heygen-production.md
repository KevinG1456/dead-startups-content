# HeyGen Production
Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** curious and measured through the founding and cross-reference beat; upbeat
  through the acquisition and three good years; neutral-to-slightly-tense through the announcement;
  warming through the open-source release; steady through "exactly on schedule"; genuinely bright
  and quietly triumphant through the present-day reveal and the "died vs. won" beat; reflective
  through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, with a deliberate slowdown on the "21,407 stars, active in
  2026" reveal and the "the company is dead, the code isn't" framing — both need room to land
- **Emotional tone:** this is a tonal departure from most of this channel's scripts — genuine,
  warm optimism should come through in the second half, not the usual somber gravity; the honesty-
  flag moments (funding discrepancy, unnamed current users) still get careful, precise delivery
- **Facial expression:** neutral-curious baseline; a small, genuine smile beat at the "still
  actively maintained — 2026" reveal; visible warmth during the "died vs. won" split-card segment
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  numeral graphics, the timeline, and the split cards
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

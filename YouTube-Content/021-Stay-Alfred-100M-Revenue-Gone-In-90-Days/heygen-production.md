# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the founding/raise segments and a clear educational register during the model explainer
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the master-lease mechanics and the 13-day reversal — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** confident/upbeat during the founding and growth segments, matter-of-fact authority for the model explainer, sharper tension during The Shock, quiet gravity during The Reversal and The Founder's Own Words
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine gravity when reading Allen's "wiped out by a virus" and "foundational crack" quotes
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative hand movement during the "who bears the risk" diagram explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (date-collision animation)
- Script: "On May 7th, 2020, this company announced it was reopening. Thirteen days later, it was gone for good."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–0:55 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: flat, serious delivery
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 0:55–1:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: slightly warmer, teaching tone
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Founder and the Build**
- Duration: 1:45–3:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (founder-era imagery, growth graphic), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: brisk, confident, establishing energy
- Visual direction: growth timeline graphic climbing

**Scene 05 — The Raise**
- Duration: 3:15–4:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Alternates avatar-on-camera with full-screen funding-timeline graphic
- Performance direction: brisk, numbers-forward
- Visual direction: funding-to-revenue timeline builds

**Scene 06 — The Model**
- Duration: 4:15–6:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with the "who bears the risk" diagram, full-screen for the diagram reveal
- Performance direction: patient, clear, teaching tone
- Visual direction: side-by-side split during the diagram segment; full avatar frame during explanatory beats

**Scene 07 — The Shock**
- Duration: 6:30–8:00 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (demand graph cratering, failed funding-round graphic), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: measured, sharper tonal drop
- Visual direction: demand graph crashing, funding round graphic failing

**Scene 08 — The Reversal**
- Duration: 8:00–9:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, 13-day countdown graphic building
- Performance direction: held tension through the countdown, releasing into somber gravity at the reveal
- Visual direction: two key dates side by side, countdown ticking between them

**Scene 09 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~8:45 (inside Scene 08) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into serious tone
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly

**Scene 10 — The Founder's Own Words**
- Duration: 9:30–11:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, quote cards full-screen for key Allen lines
- Performance direction: slow down noticeably, quieter volume, genuine gravity
- Visual direction: darker grade, slower cuts, quote text appears on screen as it's read

**Scene 11 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 11:00–13:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: return to controlled-authority tone, steady eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements animate in during final 5 seconds only

## Recommended Title
**"Stay Alfred Hit $100M a Year. It Announced Reopening. 13 Days Later, It Was Gone."**

Reasoning: names the company directly (real recognition value in the short-term-rental/PropTech
space), leads with the real, sourced revenue figure, and sets up the video's most dramatic,
underused, fully-verified beat — the 13-day reversal between the public reopening announcement
and the permanent shutdown — without needing to invent or exaggerate anything. Works fully
truncated on mobile, and the specific number ("13 days") signals a real, checkable timeline
rather than a vague "COVID killed another startup" framing.

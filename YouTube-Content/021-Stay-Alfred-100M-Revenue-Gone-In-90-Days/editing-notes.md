# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the May 7 → May 20 date-collision reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Date-collision animation for the cold open ("MAY 7 — REOPENING ANNOUNCED" hard-cutting to "MAY 20 — PERMANENTLY CLOSED")
- Growth timeline graphic building through the founding-to-2018 scale-up
- Funding-to-revenue timeline graphic ($15M → $47M → ~$100M revenue)
- "Who bears the risk" diagram: landlord gets guaranteed rent at the top, Stay Alfred absorbs all occupancy risk underneath
- Demand graph cratering during The Shock, paired with a funding-round graphic failing to close
- 13-day countdown graphic between the two key dates during The Reversal
- "Autopsy Report Card" end-graphic (revenue, funding, cause of death, verdict)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Spokesman-Review, Spokane Journal of Business, GeekWire, Bisnow, HousingWire)
- "Allen's own words" lower third whenever a direct quote appears on screen
- "Range — sources vary by snapshot date" lower third when peak unit/city figures appear, to avoid implying false precision

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic short-term-rental-apartment imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- Allen's quotes type on screen as they're read aloud

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the "who bears the risk" diagram layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founder and the Build / The Raise: brisk, confident, building energy
- The Model: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the diagram carry the explanation
- The Shock: sharp tonal drop as the demand graph craters
- The Reversal: held tension through the 13-day countdown, releasing into somber tone at the final reveal
- The Founder's Own Words: quiet, reflective, understated
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal in the cold open and The Reversal
- Sharp "crash" SFX as the demand graph craters during The Shock
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and respectful, especially during the founder-reflection and tenant-dispute beats

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — a $100M company disappearing in weeks.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "$100M" figure with a calendar graphic counting down rapidly beside it, landing on a red "GONE" stamp
- **Main subject:** the scale-vs-speed contradiction
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the central figure
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** big number centered, calendar/countdown element beside it, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** calendar/countdown graphic, red "GONE" stamp
- **Thumbnail text:** `$100M A YEAR. GONE IN WEEKS.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green dollar figure, red countdown/stamp accent, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the huge-number-with-a-rapid-countdown does the communicating before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "13 DAYS" centered large, small "REOPENING" text with a strikethrough beneath it
- **Text:** `THEY ANNOUNCED A COMEBACK. THEN THIS.`
- **Why it works:** the specific "13 days" number plus a visible reversal creates immediate curiosity about what happened in between
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well on this channel
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the $100M-with-countdown combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$100M A YEAR. GONE IN WEEKS.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of a real, sourced contradiction (real scale vs. real speed) paired with the visual proof in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small "$100M" tag in the corner
- **Text:** `THE FOUNDER CALLED IT A "FOUNDATIONAL CRACK"`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; direct-quote framing signals real founder candor, not just a recap
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

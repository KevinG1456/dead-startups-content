# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large glowing "$800M" numeral with a red "LOST" stamp, connected by a dotted
  timeline arrow to a smaller glowing "$9.9B" numeral with a green "IPO" stamp
- **Main subject:** the failure-to-vindication contrast across time
- **Emotion:** disbelief/irony
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint warehouse-grid texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight split between both numerals
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** "$800M LOST" left, dotted arrow, "$9.9B IPO" right, bottom-third title-safe space
  reserved
- **Props:** generic numeral graphics, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `THEY LOST $800M. SOMEONE ELSE WON $9.9B.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** red glow on the loss, green glow on the IPO, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the visible red-to-green transition across the timeline arrow communicates
  the entire thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Louis Borders, George Shaheen, or
  Apoorva Mehta in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Webvan, Instacart, HomeGrocer, or Bechtel logos/wordmarks in generated thumbnail art
  — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in the editor with proper
  sourcing
- Do not imply any wrongdoing in the CEO-contract detail — it's a real, sourced business fact, not a
  scandal

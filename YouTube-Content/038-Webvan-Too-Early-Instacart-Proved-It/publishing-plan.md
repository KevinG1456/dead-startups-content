# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Strong dual-era relevance (dot-com bust
nostalgia plus Instacart's still-recent 2023 IPO) gives this package above-average general-audience
potential — no urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Broad crossover: founders/investors studying market-timing risk, general dot-com-era nostalgia
audience, and viewers with awareness of Instacart's 2023 IPO. This is one of this channel's strongest
general-audience packages given both company names carry high, durable search recognition.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; consider a new "Too Early" or "Timing Risk" playlist if this
pattern gets revisited in future packages, since this is the channel's first entry in that specific
category.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element. No single existing package is a close enough thematic match
to warrant a dedicated cross-reference pairing — this is the channel's first timing-risk package.

## Cards
Card at the Segment 6 "CHAPTER 11" title card (~6:45) linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist for
viewers who might drop off assuming the story is over; card at the mid-video CTA (~6:45) reinforcing
that the story continues; optional third card at the start of The Actual Lesson (~11:00) linking
directly to a pinned comment or community post with the sourced dual-timeline graphic.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library — and if you know another 'too early' startup
story like this one, with a real, later successor that proved the idea right, tell me in the
comments. I want to build more of these. One more thing: do you think Instacart really proved Webvan's
original idea, or is that too generous a read?"

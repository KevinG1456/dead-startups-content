# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Webvan, Instacart,
HomeGrocer, Bechtel), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Louis Borders, George
Shaheen, Apoorva Mehta) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor
with proper sourcing, not generated.

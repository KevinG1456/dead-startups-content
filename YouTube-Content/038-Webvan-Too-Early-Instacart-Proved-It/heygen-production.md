# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** ambitious and confident through the founding/warehouse-deal segments; bright and
  triumphant through the IPO; a subtle unease through the overextension; declining through the
  collapse; measured and hopeful through Instacart's introduction; warm and satisfied through the S-1
  reveal; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "$4.8 billion valuation, $395,000
  revenue" contradiction and the "actual, audited, publicly-filed proof" framing line — both need room
  to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** genuine dot-com-era enthusiasm during the rise; dry disbelief at the revenue-vs-
  valuation contrast; measured gravity through the collapse — never mocking; warm sincerity through the
  Instacart vindication segment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine disbelief at the $395,000-revenue
  reveal; visible warmth when reading the S-1/IPO reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  numeral-contrast graphics and the dual-timeline in The Actual Lesson
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

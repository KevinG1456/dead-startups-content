# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open $800M-to-$9.9B numeral
  reveal, and on the "$4.8 BILLION VALUATION / $395,000 REVENUE" card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on "This is the actual, audited, publicly-filed proof that
  Webvan's idea wasn't wrong" — the single most important line in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open large glowing "$800M LOST" numeral, dotted timeline arrow crossing to "$9.9B IPO" numeral
- "The idea was never the problem. The calendar was." pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- "$1 BILLION — BECHTEL WAREHOUSE DEAL" title card
- "$4.8 BILLION VALUATION / $395,000 REVENUE" side-by-side card
- "$178.5M SALES / $525.4M EXPENSES" side-by-side card
- 10-market expansion map graphic
- "CHAPTER 11 — JULY 9, 2001" title card
- "OWN THE WAREHOUSE" vs. "USE EXISTING STORES" side-by-side comparison card
- "S-1 FILED — SEPTEMBER 2023" title card
- "11 YEARS, FOUNDER TO IPO" title card
- Dual-timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: Webvan's arc (1996-2001) and Instacart's arc
  (2012-2023) shown stacked, landing on Instacart's real S-1/IPO as the visual payoff
- Running "years since Webvan" corner counter from Segment 7 onward, landing on "11 YEARS"

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Wikipedia, CNN Money,
  Seattle Times, Forbes, Bloomberg Law)
- "Business press coverage of Instacart's IPO" lower third on the "Webvan clone" quote — attributed,
  not this channel's own characterization
- "Reported business detail, not an allegation" lower third on the George Shaheen contract mention

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic warehouse/delivery imagery — no real
  readable logos/trademarks (Webvan, Instacart, HomeGrocer, Bechtel) without rights clearance
- The $800M-to-$9.9B cold-open graphic should animate the red numeral first, hold briefly, then the
  dotted arrow draws across to the green numeral — pacing the reveal so both halves land distinctly
- The dual-timeline graphic should animate Webvan's arc first (rising then crashing), pause, then
  Instacart's arc builds on top of it, ending with both visible together at the S-1 moment

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the numeral-contrast graphics
  and the dual-timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during "this is the actual, audited, publicly-filed
  proof" framing card — no camera movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding / Building It Themselves: ambitious, confident, dot-com-era optimism
- The IPO: bright, triumphant, capturing 1999-era euphoria
- The Overextension: a subtle unease creeping into the confident tone
- The Numbers Catch Up / Bankruptcy: declining, then quiet and restrained
- A Different Company, a Different Model: a measured, hopeful rebuild — distinctly different in tone
  from Webvan's brash 1999 confidence
- The Real Proof: warm, genuine satisfaction — the video's clearest positive beat
- What Changed: reflective, settling
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (1996 founding, 1999 IPO, 2000 HomeGrocer
  deal, 2001 bankruptcy, 2012 Instacart founding, 2021 valuation peak, 2023 IPO)
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on the "$4.8 BILLION" reveal, a distinctly duller "thud" on the "$395,000"
  reveal directly after
- A soft "unlock" or "chime" SFX at the "S-1 FILED" reveal, distinctly warmer than any earlier cue
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured and analytical throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the dual-timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

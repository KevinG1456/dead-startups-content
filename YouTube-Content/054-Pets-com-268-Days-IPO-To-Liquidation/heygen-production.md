# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** upbeat and slightly nostalgic through the setup, marketing, and Super Bowl
  segments; wry/amused through the SNL lawsuit segment; serious and measured through the shipping-math
  and collapse segments; warm through the Wainwright redemption close
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, slightly faster during the nostalgia beats (founding,
  marketing, Super Bowl), deliberately slower and clearer during the shipping-math segment so the
  numbers land
- **Emotional tone:** genuine affection for the puppet's cultural moment, no mockery of the company or
  its people; real gravity at the "$1 in, $2 out" reveal; unmistakable warmth at the closing
  Wainwright beat
- **Facial expression:** neutral-pleasant baseline; a genuine amused smile during the SNL lawsuit
  segment; focused seriousness during the shipping-math reveal; a warm smile returning for the closing
  redemption beat
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more animated/light during the
  nostalgia and lawsuit segments
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# AI Image Prompts (for ChatGPT / Thumbnail-Creative-Director stage)

Reminder per channel convention: keep every prompt to ONE dominant visual idea. Do NOT generate a
literal recreation of the real, trademarked Pets.com sock puppet character (IP risk) — use a generic,
unbranded sock-puppet silhouette or shape instead, never matching the real puppet's specific face/
markings. No real photos of identifiable people. No text overlay baked into the generated image beyond
what's specified — title cards are added in editing.

## Thumbnail — Concept 1 (Recommended): The Puppet, Fading to Gray
"A generic sock-puppet silhouette (plain sock shape, no specific branded face or markings) shown
half in warm, saturated color and half dissolving into gray ash-like particles, dark moody
background, dramatic side lighting. Cinematic, high contrast, minimal color palette (warm gold on one
side, cold gray on the other). No text, no people, no logos, no recognizable trademarked character
design."

## Thumbnail — Concept 2: The Ledger
"An oversized paper receipt or ledger line floating against a dark background, showing a bold red
'-$4.00' in large clean type, with a small simple flat-icon dog-food bag beside it. Minimal, high
contrast, cinematic lighting. No people, no logos, no text beyond the specified numeral."

## Thumbnail — Concept 3: The Countdown
"A large bold numeral '268' centered against a dark background, with a small simple bell icon
(stock-market opening bell) on the left side glowing gold, and a plain 'CLOSED' stamp graphic on the
right side in red. Minimal, high contrast, clean sans-serif numerals. No people, no logos."

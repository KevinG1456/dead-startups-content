## Email 1 — Launch Announcement
**Subject:** They spent $2 to make $1. For two years straight.
**Preview text:** The Super Bowl ad worked perfectly. The business underneath it never did.

Body:
This week's autopsy is the most iconic one on the channel: Pets.com.

You know the sock puppet. Maybe you remember the Super Bowl ad, or the 36-foot balloon at the Macy's
Thanksgiving Day Parade. What almost nobody remembers is the actual math.

Shipping a 50-pound bag of dog food for free cost the company $3 to $4 every single order. Merchandise
sold for roughly 27% below what it cost to buy. For every dollar in sales, the company spent about
two dollars on marketing and fulfillment.

February 2000: IPO at $11 a share. November 2000: complete liquidation. Commonly reported as 268
days, having burned an estimated $300 million.

There's also a genuinely funny lawsuit against Saturday Night Live in here that almost nobody talks
about.

# Thumbnail Concepts

Rule for this package (channel standard): one dominant idea per thumbnail, not the whole story
crammed in. The sock puppet is the obvious, universally recognizable dominant visual — resist the
urge to also cram in the Super Bowl, the parade balloon, and the shutdown date all in one frame.

## Concept 1 (Recommended) — The Puppet, Fading to Gray
Dominant idea: a sock puppet silhouette (generic, non-trademark-infringing — a simple sock-on-a-hand
shape, not a literal recreation of the real puppet's design) in full color on one side, dissolving
into gray/ash particles on the other. One line of text: "$300M."

## Concept 2 — The Ledger
Dominant idea: a single oversized receipt/ledger line reading "-$4.00" in bold red, nothing else on
screen except a small dog-food-bag icon beside it.

## Concept 3 — The Countdown
Dominant idea: a large "268" in bold numerals against a dark background, with a small IPO bell icon
on one side and a "CLOSED" stamp on the other.

## Concept 4 — The Empty Stage
Dominant idea: a spotlight on an empty stage/podium (evoking the Super Bowl ad) with a single sock
puppet silhouette lying flat/deflated at its base. No text needed.

## Concept 5 — The Balloon, Deflating
Dominant idea: a parade balloon (generic dog/puppet shape, non-trademarked) shown mid-deflate,
sinking below a skyline silhouette. One line of text: "9 MONTHS LATER."

**Recommended:** Concept 1 (The Puppet, Fading to Gray) — instantly recognizable premise, one clean
number, no jargon, and doesn't require depicting the real trademarked puppet design (avoids any IP
risk in the generated thumbnail image itself).

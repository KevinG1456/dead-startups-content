# Shorts Ideas (10)

Note: keep the SEC/DOJ fraud-allegation material precise and attributed in every Short below — never
name Stacy Spikes anywhere near the fraud/charges material, and never drop the "faces a maximum of
five years, sentencing not confirmed" hedge if a Short touches the guilty plea at all. Shorts that
don't need the criminal-case material should simply skip it rather than compress it unsafely.

**1. "$9.95 A Month. They Lost Money On Every Ticket."**
Hook: "MoviePass charged $9.95 a month for unlimited movies. It was paying theaters close to full
price for every single one." Script: quick side-by-side math reveal. CTA: "Full story on the
channel."

**2. "The Founder Was Fired Before Any Of This Happened"**
Hook: "MoviePass's founder was fired in January 2018. The $9.95 plan that destroyed the company
launched seven months earlier — under new owners." Script: quick ouster-then-collapse timeline
reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**3. "3 Million Subscribers To 225,000"**
Hook: "MoviePass grew to almost 3 million subscribers. About a year later, it had roughly 225,000."
Script: subscriber-count graph collapsing. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**4. "They Changed Subscribers' Own Passwords"**
Hook: "This company reportedly changed its own subscribers' passwords to stop them from using the
product they paid for." Script: quick reveal of the well-corroborated password-changing pattern.
CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**5. "Two Executives. One Guilty Plea."**
Hook: "Two MoviePass executives were criminally charged with fraud. One of them has already pleaded
guilty." Script: quick charges-to-plea reveal, careful sentencing hedge included. CTA: "Full story
on the channel."

**6. "The Founder Wasn't Charged With Anything"**
Hook: "The people who ran MoviePass into the ground were criminally charged with fraud. The person
who started the company was not one of them." Script: quick who's-who scoreboard reveal (Spikes on
one side, Farnsworth/Lowe/Itum on the other). CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**7. "He Got Fired For Being Right"**
Hook: "MoviePass's founder says he knew the $9.95 price would never work. He got fired for
disagreeing with it anyway." Script: quick ouster-then-vindication reveal. CTA: "Full story on the
channel."

**8. "He Bought Back The Company That Fired Him"**
Hook: "This founder got fired from his own company in 2018. In 2021, he bought it back out of
bankruptcy." Script: quick fired-to-buyback reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**9. "MoviePass Is Still Around. Here's The Twist."**
Hook: "MoviePass shut down in 2019. It's still running today — and the person running it is the
same person who got fired from it." Script: quick relaunch-and-fix reveal (tiered pricing, no
unlimited tier). CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**10. "The Redemption Arc This Channel Doesn't Usually Get"**
Hook: "Most startup collapse stories end with the founder gone for good. This one's different."
Script: rapid-fire full-arc recap (founded → fired → collapsed → charges → comeback → profitable
per the founder's own account). CTA: "New autopsy every week — subscribe."

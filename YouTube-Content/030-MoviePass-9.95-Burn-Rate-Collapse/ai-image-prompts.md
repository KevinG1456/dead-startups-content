# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (MoviePass, Helios and
Matheson, specific theater chains), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Stacy
Spikes, Ted Farnsworth, Mitch Lowe, Khalid Itum) — add verified text/branding and any real archival
photography in the editor with proper sourcing, not generated.

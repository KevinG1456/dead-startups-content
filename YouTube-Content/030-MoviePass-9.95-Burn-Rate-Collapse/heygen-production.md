# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** brisk and slightly incredulous during the founding/$9.95-math segments; measured
  and even-handed through the criminal-case segment; warmer and more energized through the comeback
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the who's-who framing beats and the
  [VERIFY] sentencing disclosure — both need room to land clearly and be read precisely, on pace with
  the on-screen lower thirds
- **Emotional tone:** dry disbelief during the $9.95 unit-economics explainer; matter-of-fact
  authority for the collapse and shutdown segments; deliberate, careful gravity during the criminal
  case segment — visibly neutral, never gleeful about the charges; genuine warmth during the comeback
  segment, without ever tipping into promotional enthusiasm for the unaudited 2023-2024 figures
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine surprise/incredulity when
  stating the $9.95-vs-ticket-cost math; visible care and neutrality when reading the who's-who
  framing card and the sentencing disclosure card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  full-arc timeline graphic and the who's-who comparison graphic
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

  the strategy."

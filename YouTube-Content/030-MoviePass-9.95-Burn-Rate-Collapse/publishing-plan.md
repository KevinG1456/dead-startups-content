# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. MoviePass has the highest general
name-recognition and nostalgia factor of any package on this channel, so evergreen search volume
("what happened to MoviePass," "is MoviePass still around") is durable rather than news-cycle-driven
— no urgency premium the way a recent bankruptcy filing would carry, so hold for the best day/time
slot rather than rushing publication.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, investors) studying the most extreme deliberate
unit-economics failure in this genre, plus a much larger secondary audience than usual: general
viewers with direct nostalgic/personal memories of being MoviePass subscribers themselves. This
package should outperform the channel's typical CTR and comment-engagement baseline because of that
crossover appeal — pair distribution accordingly (wider thumbnail/title testing, more general-
audience-facing social cuts than this channel typically runs).

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to a "Founder Comeback Stories" playlist alongside
package 025 (Sonder, with Lyric's Wheelhouse/Black Swan Holdings brand-revival as the companion
precedent) as the channel's other "brand survives the company" entry. Consider a third, standalone
placement in a "Fraud Charges" or "Executives Held Accountable" playlist alongside package 029 (Ozy
Media/Carlos Watson) — MoviePass is the second package with a real criminal fraud conviction
connected to it, but the first where the fraud is explicitly NOT the founder.

## End Screen
Package 025 (Sonder/Lyric) + most recent autopsy; subscribe element. Do not use package 029 (Ozy
Media) as the end-screen pairing despite the shared "fraud charges" theme — pairing MoviePass with
Ozy risks visually implying Spikes belongs in the same category as Carlos Watson, which is the exact
conflation this package is built to avoid. If a fraud-themed pairing is wanted, caption it explicitly
as "the executives, not the founder" before linking.

## Cards
Card at the "FOUNDER OUT — JANUARY 2018" title card (end of Segment 2, ~4:00) linking to package 025
(Sonder/Lyric) as the companion "brand survives the company" story; card at the mid-video CTA (~8:45)
linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist; optional third card at the start of the Criminal Case
segment (~9:00) linking directly to a pinned comment or community post with the sourced who's-who
graphic, for viewers who want the distinction reinforced in text form while watching.

## End CTA
"If you want to see this channel's other 'the brand outlived the company' story, watch the Sonder
video, package 025 — it includes Lyric's ending, where the brand was bought back by an outside firm
instead of the original founder. MoviePass's version is the more complete one. And subscribe if you
want the next autopsy in the library. One more thing before you go: if you ever had a MoviePass card,
I want to know what year you canceled — before the password-changing started, or after. Tell me in
the comments, and tell me whether you think Farnsworth and Lowe are getting what they deserve."

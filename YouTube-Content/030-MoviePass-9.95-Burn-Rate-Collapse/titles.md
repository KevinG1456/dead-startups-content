# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on MoviePass: co-founded 2011 by Stacy Spikes, who was fired in January 2018 — BEFORE
Helios and Matheson (which bought a 51% stake in 2017) launched the reckless $9.95/month unlimited
plan in August 2017 under CEO Ted Farnsworth and installed MoviePass CEO Mitch Lowe. Subscribers
grew to almost 3 million while the company paid theaters close to full price per ticket for a flat
$9.95 fee; Helios and Matheson reported an estimated $329.2 million net loss in the worst year;
subscribers collapsed to ~225,000 by April 2019; the service shut down September 14, 2019; Helios
and Matheson filed Chapter 7 January 28, 2020. Farnsworth and Lowe were criminally charged with wire
fraud and securities fraud in November 2022; Lowe pleaded guilty to a securities fraud conspiracy
charge in 2024 (max 5-year exposure under the plea — sentencing for either man not confirmed as of
this research pass). Spikes — long gone from the company by then, never charged — bought MoviePass's
brand and assets out of bankruptcy in 2021 and relaunched it in September 2022 with a non-unlimited,
tiered pricing model. Per Spikes' own public statements (early 2024, most recent found), the
relaunch hit $14M ARR, its first profitable year in 2023, and 2.8 million movie trips. Every title
below keeps Spikes distinct from Farnsworth/Lowe/Itum — never named together as if part of one group.

## Recommended Title
**"MoviePass Charged $9.95 a Month for Unlimited Movies. It Lost Money on Almost Every Ticket. Then
Its Fired Founder Bought It Back and Fixed It."**

Reasoning: opens with the single most viral, best-sourced fact in this package — the deliberate,
sustained unit-economics absurdity at the core of the $9.95 plan — then adds the twist ending
(the founder-comeback) directly in the title itself, which is the differentiator vs. the many
existing MoviePass videos that cover only the collapse OR only the relaunch. Names the company
directly (near-universal recognition), works fully truncated on mobile at either the first or
second sentence, and sets up the founder-distinction spine (Spikes vs. Farnsworth/Lowe/Itum)
without needing to name the fraud-charged executives in the title at all — that reveal is held for
the video itself. Matches the title already used in this package's README.

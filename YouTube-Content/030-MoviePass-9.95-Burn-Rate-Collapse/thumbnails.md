# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram Spikes, the
$9.95 math, the criminal charges, AND the comeback into a single image. Each of those gets its own
version below, or lives inside the video/Shorts instead of the thumbnail.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a MoviePass-style card sliding into frame with a large "$9.95" price tag, a red
  minus sign stamped over a stack of ticket-price icons behind it
- **Main subject:** the core unit-economics absurdity — flat $9.95 fee vs. real per-ticket cost
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint film-reel/ticket-stub texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the card and price tag
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** card and price tag centered-left, loss icon stack right, bottom-third title-safe
  space reserved
- **Props:** generic subscription-card graphic, generic ticket-stub icons, red minus/loss mark
- **Thumbnail text:** `$9.95 A MONTH. THEY LOST MONEY ON EVERY TICKET.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** red card glow, white numerals, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the price tag next to a visibly bigger "cost" stack communicates the entire
  thesis before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Image concept:** the $9.95-card-vs-ticket-cost-stack graphic from Winning Concept above
- **Subject:** the price-vs-cost contradiction, graphic-led
- **Expression:** N/A (no face in this version)
- **Objects:** generic subscription card with "$9.95" numerals, small stack of ticket-price icons
  behind it, red loss arrow
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint film-reel texture
- **Colors:** red card glow, white numerals, near-black background
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the card
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** card dominant and centered, cost stack smaller and behind it, bottom-third text
- **Thumbnail text:** `$9.95 A MONTH. THEY LOST MONEY ON EVERY TICKET.`
- **Why it works:** the specific, verified price point paired with the "lost money on purpose"
  framing is the single most quotable, most shareable fact in the entire sources-log — it needs no
  further explanation to generate curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three — numeral-led, absurdity-led thumbnails consistently
  outperform on this channel
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic, general nostalgia audience

- **Subject:** the founder's ouster-then-comeback arc, pure graphic/typographic thumbnail

- **Background:** dark, minimal, subtle grain
- **Colors:** muted red on the "exit" side, warm gold/green on the "return" side, near-black base
- **Lighting:** single spotlight split across the two icon halves
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** exit icon left, return icon right, connecting arrow between them, bottom-third

- **Thumbnail text:** `FIRED. THEN HE BOUGHT THE COMPANY BACK.`
- **Why it works:** the least-covered angle of the MoviePass story anywhere else — most existing
  coverage treats the collapse and the relaunch as two separate stories; this thumbnail promises the
  connective twist in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** high — redemption-arc curiosity performs strongly with viewers who already know
  the collapse story and assume it has no happy ending
- **Best audience:** search + suggested traffic, viewers with prior MoviePass familiarity

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Stacy Spikes, Ted Farnsworth, Mitch
  Lowe, or Khalid Itum in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts
  only; attribute real names verbally/on-screen in the video itself
- Do not put the real MoviePass card design, logo, or wordmark, or the Helios and Matheson name, in
  generated thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real
  branding/text in the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not attempt a single thumbnail that shows both the collapse AND the comeback — that split is
  the video's content and Version B's specific angle, not something to double up elsewhere
- Never pair the word "fraud" with Stacy Spikes' name or image in any thumbnail — that language is
  reserved specifically for Farnsworth/Lowe/Itum per this package's sourcing standard

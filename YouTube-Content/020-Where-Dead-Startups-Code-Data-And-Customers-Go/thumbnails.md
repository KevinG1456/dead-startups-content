# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — assets flowing out of a dead startup into unknown hands.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a simple three-branch flow diagram — a single dark "dead company" node splitting into three arrows labeled CODE, DATA, and CUSTOMERS, each arrow pointing off-frame into a question mark
- **Main subject:** the branching flow-diagram metaphor
- **Emotion:** unease/curiosity
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** flow diagram centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** simple node-and-arrow diagram
- **Thumbnail text:** `WHERE DOES IT ALL GO?`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** near-black background, one accent color (red) on the arrows, white/gray nodes
- **Contrast strategy:** three diverging arrows against a single collapsing node communicates "one thing becomes three separate unknowns" instantly

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** a large question mark made of faint binary/code texture, small closed-sign icon in the corner
- **Text:** `YOUR DATA DIDN'T DISAPPEAR`
- **Why it works:** direct second-person framing plus a strong visual question mark drives immediate personal-relevance curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** high — direct-address text performs well for broad-relevance topics
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic, general tech audience beyond the core niche

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the three-branch flow-diagram from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `THE LIQUIDATION SUPPLY CHAIN`
- **Why it works:** the branching visual communicates a hidden, systematic process instantly, and the branded phrase signals a rigorous explainer rather than a vague warning
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic, core niche audience

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/analytical expression, small three-icon tag (code bracket, database, person) in the corner
- **Text:** `CODE. DATA. CUSTOMERS.`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; the three-word breakdown signals a complete, structured explainer
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

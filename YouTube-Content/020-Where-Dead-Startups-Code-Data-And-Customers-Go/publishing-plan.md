# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Strong evergreen search-driven potential, with likely spikes around any high-profile startup shutdown or data-privacy news cycle.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, employees, VCs), plus a broader general-tech/privacy-conscious audience given the video's applicability to anyone who has used a since-shutdown app.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; strong candidate for the "Startup Finance & Legal Explainers" playlist alongside Title 010 (Acquired for $1) and Title 018 (Down Rounds).

## End Screen
"Acquired for $1" video (Title 010) + most recent autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at each track transition (Code/Data/Customers) linking to a related legal/finance-explainer video; card at the "what to check" segment linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy, subscribe — I'm building a full library of these. And next time an app you use announces it's shutting down, hopefully you'll know exactly what to ask."

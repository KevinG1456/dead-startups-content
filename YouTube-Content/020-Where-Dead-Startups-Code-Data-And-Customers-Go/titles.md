3. I Traced Where a Dead Startup's Data Actually Goes. It's Not What You Think. — personal-investigation framing, strong CTR

6. What "Shutting Down" Actually Means for Your Data — clean, curiosity-driven, practical framing
7. They Promised Never to Sell Your Data. Then the Company Died. — strongest emotional/human-angle alt cut, RadioShack-adjacent framing

9. The Question Nobody Asks When a Startup Dies: Where Does Everything Go? — strong curiosity, slightly long
10. Acqui-Hire vs Asset Sale Explained — dry, best for search-only sub-cut or Shorts

## Recommended Title
**"Where Dead Startups' Code, Data, and Customers Actually Go."**

Reasoning: plain-language, promises a complete answer across all three asset tracks rather than picking just one, and has the broadest relatability of any option — nearly every viewer has used at least one product from a company that later shut down. Keep "The Liquidation Supply Chain" as the on-screen title-card subtitle and recurring branded phrase throughout the video and description to reinforce the structural, systematic framing.

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled, analytical, clear educational register throughout — a legal-mechanism explainer that needs consistent clarity over energy, similar register to Title 018
- **Speaking pace:** measured and patient throughout, especially during the Section 363/ombudsman explanation — resist the urge to rush the legal mechanics
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the mechanics segments, slightly more pointed/concerned tone during the RadioShack case (the privacy-policy-broken-in-bankruptcy moment), warmer during the practical "what to check" segment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine concern briefly during the RadioShack segment; slight relief/approval tone shift when covering Parse as the "graceful" contrast example
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled; more illustrative hand movement when explaining the three-branch flow diagram, to reinforce the spatial "splitting into three paths" metaphor
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:20 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (three-branch flow diagram animation)

**Scene 04 — Track 2: Where the Data Goes**

**Scene 05 — Track 3: Where the Customers Go**

**Scene 06 — What to Check Before You Trust a Startup With Your Data**

**Scene 07 — Final CTA + Outro**

- Performance direction: steady, direct eye contact through sign-off

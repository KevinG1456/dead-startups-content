## LinkedIn
"A real, precisely sourced case study in acquisition-integration risk — and how fast enthusiasm at
signing can outpace what actually happens after:

Gentry Underwood and Scott Cannon built Mailbox, an iOS email app with swipe and 'snooze' gestures
nobody had combined before. A viral demo video and an 800,000-person virtual waitlist built real hype
before its February 2013 launch. Reportedly just 37 days later, Dropbox acquired Mailbox for a reported
~$100 million.

Here's what actually happened next: an Android app and a Mac app were promised in 2014 but never really
shipped. Development on Mailbox itself stalled, while Yahoo Mail, Microsoft Outlook, and Google's own
Inbox by Gmail adopted similar gestures. Dropbox announced shutting down Mailbox and its Carousel app in
December 2015; Mailbox closed for good in February 2016 — just under three years after the acquisition.

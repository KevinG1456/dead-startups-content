# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open $100M/37-days reveal,
  and on the "$100 MILLION — REPORTED PRICE, 37 DAYS AFTER LAUNCH" title card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on "The exact wording of Dropbox's announcement varies across
  retrospectives" — the single most important honesty flag in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open "37 DAYS" tag next to a large "$100 MILLION" numeral, both fading to gray
- "This isn't a story about running out of money. It's a story about what happens after the check
  clears." pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- Generic early-2010s startup-office graphic for the founding segment
- "800,000+ ON THE WAITLIST" title card with mock live-queue-counter graphic
- "FEBRUARY 2013 — PUBLIC LAUNCH" title card
- "$100 MILLION — REPORTED PRICE, 37 DAYS AFTER LAUNCH" title card
- Simple org-chart graphic showing founders moving into new Dropbox roles
- "ANDROID + MAC — PROMISED, 2014" title card
- Mailbox gesture icon "spreading" into three competitor icons for the copy segment
- "LAST MAJOR UPDATE" vs. "TODAY" date-gap graphic
- "SHUTDOWN ANNOUNCED — DECEMBER 7, 2015" title card
- "MAILBOX CLOSED — FEBRUARY 26, 2016" title card
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: Orchestra founded (2011) → pivot to Mailbox (2012) →
  viral demo video (Dec 2012) → public launch (Feb 2013) → Dropbox acquisition (Mar 2013) → Android/Mac
  promised (2014) → competitors adopt the gestures (2014-15) → shutdown announced (Dec 2015) → Mailbox
  closes (Feb 2016)
- Running "time since acquisition" corner counter ticking forward from Segment 5 onward, landing on
  "UNDER 3 YEARS"

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, Forbes,
  Engadget, Fortune, CNN Business, YourStory)
- "Reported figure — not Dropbox-confirmed exactly" standing lower third on the $100M and 37-days reveals
- "Retrospective analysis, not a company admission" lower third on the competitors-copying-the-gestures
  segment
- "This is Dropbox's own stated reasoning" lower third on the shutdown-announcement segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic early-2010s office/app imagery — no real
  readable logos/trademarks (Mailbox, Dropbox, Orchestra, Carousel, competitor apps) without rights
  clearance
- The cold-open $100M/37-days reveal should animate slowly enough that both numbers are legible before
  the transition completes
- The gesture-spreading-into-competitors animation should visibly show one icon becoming three, landing
  with a clear beat on each competitor icon

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the numeral-contrast graphics,
  the org-chart graphic, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "$100 million, 37 days" reveal and the
  honesty-flag framing card — no camera movement competing with either for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: light, quick-building pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding: light, optimistic, early-2010s startup energy
- The Video and the Waitlist / The Launch: upbeat, building excitement — this should feel like genuine
  2013 hype, not irony yet
- The Acquisition: a sharp, bright swell — the video's biggest musical beat on the reveal itself
- What $100 Million Actually Bought: measured, slightly wry
- The Promises / The Copy: flat, neutral, slightly uneasy
- The Quiet Years: quiet, drifting
- The Shutdown Announcement / The Actual Closure: quiet, matter-of-fact, no melodrama
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (Nov 2011 funding, 2012 pivot, Dec 2012
  video, Feb 2013 launch, Mar 2013 acquisition, 2014 promises, Dec 2015 shutdown announced, Feb 2016
  closed)
- A bright "cash register" SFX synced to the "$100 MILLION" reveal
- A soft "tick" SFX on the live-queue-counter graphic in the waitlist segment
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured and analytical throughout, even
  during the ironic beats
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

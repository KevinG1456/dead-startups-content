Carousel, Yahoo Mail, Outlook, Google Inbox), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person
(Gentry Underwood, Scott Cannon, Drew Houston) — add verified text/branding and any real archival
screenshots in the editor with proper sourcing, not generated.

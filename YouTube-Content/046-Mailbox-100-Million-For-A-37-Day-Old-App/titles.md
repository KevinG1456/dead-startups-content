2011); pivoted to Mailbox (email-triage app, swipe/snooze gestures), viral Dec 2012 demo video, virtual
waitlist (800K+ signups); opened to the public App Store Feb 2013; acquired by Dropbox Mar 15 2013,
reported ~$100M, reported 37 days after launch, all 13 employees joined; Android/Mac apps promised 2014
but development stalled while competitors (Yahoo Mail, Outlook, Google Inbox) copied its gestures;
Dropbox announced shutdown Dec 7 2015 (Mailbox + Carousel); Mailbox actually closed Feb 26 2016 — just
under 3 years after acquisition. Every title uses reported/attributed figures for the $100M price and
the 37-day gap, never presents either as an unqualified exact fact.

    specific deal

## Recommended Title
**"Dropbox Paid $100 Million for an App That Was 37 Days Old. Under Three Years Later, It Was Gone."**

Reasoning: leads with the single most precisely sourced, most self-contained numeral contrast in this
package — no prior familiarity with Mailbox required to feel the hook's full weight. Sets up the video's
actual thesis (a fast, enthusiastic acquisition followed by years of neglect) in one sentence, and
matches the title already used in this package's README. Avoids overclaiming the exact $100M figure or
the exact 37-day count as unqualified fact — both are presented in the video itself as reported,
attributed figures.

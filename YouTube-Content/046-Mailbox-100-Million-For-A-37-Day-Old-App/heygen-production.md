# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** upbeat and nostalgic through the founding and launch hype; a brief sharp lift at the
  acquisition reveal; measured and slightly wry through the "what $100 million actually bought" segment;
  flat, matter-of-fact through the stall and the competitors-copying-the-gestures segment; quiet and
  even through the shutdown; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** slightly quicker and more energetic through the waitlist/launch segments (matching
  the genuine 2013 hype); measured and deliberate on the "37 days" and "$100 million" reveals — both
  need room to land as real, sourced numbers, not just a punchline
- **Emotional tone:** genuine warmth and nostalgia for the product itself, never mocking; dry irony
  (not bitterness) through the stall and shutdown segments; no villain framing of Dropbox or its
  founders anywhere
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a genuine flicker of enthusiasm during the waitlist/
  launch segments; visible care and precision when reading the "reported, not confirmed" honesty framing
  cards
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more animated during the waitlist-hype
  segment and the numeral-contrast graphics
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

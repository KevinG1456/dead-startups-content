# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. The self-contained, sharply shareable
$100M/37-days hook gives this package strong standalone potential independent of any news cycle — no
urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Broad crossover: founders/PMs studying acquisition-integration risk, general audience with nostalgic
memory of Mailbox's 2013 launch hype, and viewers drawn purely by the sharp, self-contained numeral hook
regardless of prior Mailbox familiarity.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist. Cross-reference package 003 ("The Company Google Killed, Platform
Risk 5 Ways") in the description/end screen as a related-but-distinct Mailbox mention — that package
asked whether a platform killed Mailbox (no); this one asks what the acquirer did with it (neglect).

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element, plus a card to package 003 for viewers who want the earlier,
different Mailbox research angle.

## Cards
Card at the "$100 MILLION" title card (end of Segment 4, ~6:00) linking to the "Startup Autopsies"
playlist for viewers hooked by the reveal; card at the mid-video CTA (~10:45) reinforcing the "getting
acquired is what killed it" framing as distinct from the channel's usual stories; optional third card at
the start of The Actual Lesson (~11:00) linking directly to a pinned comment or community post with the
sourced full-arc timeline graphic.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library — and if you know another 'bought fast, then
neglected' startup story, tell me in the comments, I want to build more of these. One more thing: do you
think Dropbox ever really had a plan for Mailbox, or was this always going to end this way?"

# HeyGen Production
Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** curious, slightly wry through the founding and early-growth beats; building,
  escalating energy through the funding/pace-acceleration segments; credible and measured through
  the GE/Wink segment; deflating/wry through the collapse; ambivalent (not triumphant) through the
  Wink-afterlife closing beats; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, with a deliberate speed-up during the "1 to 3 products a
  week" reveal to mirror the acceleration, and a deliberate slowdown on "$30 million. Still not
  enough." and the Wink-vs-Parse contrast — both need room to land
- **Emotional tone:** curious and a little bemused, never accusatory — this is a strategy-failure
  story, not a villain story; the closing Wink-afterlife beat should read as genuinely ambivalent,
  not warmly resolved like package 049's equivalent moment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-curious baseline; a small wry half-smile at "can you name one?";
  visibly more measured/serious during the bankruptcy segment; deliberately NOT a triumphant smile
  during the Wink-still-alive reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  numeral graphics, the timeline, and the split cards
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

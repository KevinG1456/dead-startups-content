# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open gadget-montage
  reveal and on the "$30 MILLION. STILL NOT ENOUGH." title card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the Wink-vs-Parse contrast card — give returning viewers
  a beat to register the callback to package 049

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open rapid-fire montage of dozens of generic gadget silhouettes flashing past, hard-cutting
  to black
- "Most people can't name a single one. That's the whole story." pattern-interrupt card, white
  text on black
- "IDEA → VOTE → BUILD → SELL → SHARE" flow graphic for the founding segment
- Funding-rounds ladder animating upward as each round is named, "$185M TOTAL RAISED" title card
- "1 PRODUCT/WEEK → 3 PRODUCTS/WEEK" title card
- "$30 MILLION — GE'S INVESTMENT, NOVEMBER 2013" title card
- Sprawling grid of generic product-silhouette icons filling the screen for the 400-products beat
- "JULY 31, 2015 — KAUFMAN STEPS DOWN, 111 LAID OFF" title card
- "SEPTEMBER 22, 2015 — CHAPTER 11" title card
- "$30 MILLION. STILL NOT ENOUGH." title card
- "$15M → $59M" title card for the Wink resale beat
- Split card, "PARSE SERVER: THRIVING" vs. "WINK: STILL ALIVE, STILL SHAKY" for the package-049
  cross-reference
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founded 2009 → 2012 Series C → 3 products/week
  → 2013 GE $30M → 2014 Wink spinoff → 2015 bankruptcy → 2017 Wink resold → 2020 backlash → still
  shakily alive today
- Running product-count ticker visible from Segment 4 onward, climbing toward "~400"

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, Forbes,
  GE.com press releases, Fortune)
- "Reported/best-corroborated figure" standing lower third on the $185M total-funding reveal
- "Exact date not confirmed — mid-2014" lower third on the Wink spinoff beat
- "Not a clean happy ending" lower third whenever Wink's present-day status is discussed

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic startup-office/product-studio imagery —
  no real readable logos/trademarks (Quirky, GE, Wink, Home Depot, i.am+) without rights clearance
- The cold-open gadget-montage should move fast enough to feel overwhelming, then cut hard to
  silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The "Parse vs. Wink" split card should animate as a clean, simultaneous side-by-side reveal, not
  sequential

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the numeral-contrast
  graphics, the timeline, and the split cards
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "$185M / 400 products" cold-open recap
  and the Wink-vs-Parse contrast beat

## Background Music
- Cold open: fast, slightly chaotic pulse (matching the gadget montage), cutting to near-silence
  on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding / It Actually Worked: light, optimistic
- The Funding / Then They Sped Up: building, escalating energy
- General Electric Gets Involved / Wink Is Born: measured, credible, slightly triumphant
- 400 Products, No Focus: neutral shifting to slightly uneasy — the tone starts turning
- The Slide / Bankruptcy: somber, matter-of-fact
- What GE's $30 Million Actually Bought: wry, deflating
- Wink Didn't Die, Exactly: cautiously hopeful
- But It's Not a Happy Ending: ambivalent, not triumphant — deliberately different from package
  049's warm, resolved present-day beat
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: measured, unresolved-but-thoughtful — resist a fully upbeat close

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2009 founding, 2012 Series C, 2013 GE
  investment, 2014 Wink spinoff, 2015 bankruptcy, 2017 resale, 2020 backlash)
- A rapid "shutter/click" SFX synced to the cold-open gadget montage
- A neutral "notification" SFX (not celebratory) on the Wink-still-alive reveal — keep it distinct
  from the warmer chime used in package 049's equivalent beat
- No comedic stingers on the bankruptcy/collapse segments

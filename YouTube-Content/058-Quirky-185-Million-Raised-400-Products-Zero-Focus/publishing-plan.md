# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. The "name one" challenge hook is
self-contained and evergreen — no urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Founders/PMs studying product-portfolio focus and execution discipline; smart-home enthusiasts
curious about Wink's ownership history; general audience of this channel drawn to a strange,
countable "3 products a week" hook.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist. Strong paired recommendation with package 049 (Parse) under a
potential future "Did the Product Outlive the Company?" theme — note the two packages give
opposite-toned answers to the same question (Parse Server: clean thriving afterlife; Wink: real
but messy, troubled afterlife), which makes them a strong contrast pairing, not a duplicate.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element. Consider a direct card to package 049 (Parse) as an
explicit "same question, different answer" cross-reference.

## Cards
Card at the "$30 MILLION. STILL NOT ENOUGH." title card (~8:30) linking to the "Startup
Autopsies" playlist for viewers hooked by the GE angle; card at the mid-video CTA (~10:15)
reinforcing the "messy, not triumphant" ending framing; optional third card at the Wink-vs-Parse
contrast beat (~9:45) linking directly to package 049's video for viewers who want the
contrasting story immediately.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library — some end in a clean death, some in a
clean survival, and some just end messy like this one. If you ever owned a Quirky product or a
Wink hub, tell me what that was actually like in the comments."

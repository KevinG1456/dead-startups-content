# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Quirky, GE, Wink, Home
Depot, i.am+), no recognizable real product designs, and no photorealistic likeness of Ben Kaufman
— add verified text/branding and any real archival material in the editor with proper sourcing, not
generated.

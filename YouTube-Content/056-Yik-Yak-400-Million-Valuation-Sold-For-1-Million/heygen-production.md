# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** bright and upbeat through the rise (idea/money segments); neutral and restrained
  through the founding-dispute and threats segments; uneasy through the fix/backlash; quiet and
  sincere through the apology; flat and deflating through "too late"; sharp tonal drop at the sale;
  clarifying and even through "not the same company"; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the apology-video quote and the threats
  segment — both need room to land clearly and respectfully
- **Emotional tone:** genuine energy and momentum during the rise; serious, restrained gravity during
  the threats segment — never played for shock value; sincere, quiet respect during the apology-quote
  read; measured, even-handed tone toward Droll and Buffington throughout, no accusatory framing
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine energy at the funding/valuation
  reveals; visible sincerity when reading the apology-video quote
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the rise
  segments and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text beyond simple numerals, no real logos/branding
(Yik Yak, Square/Block, Sidechat), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Tyler
Droll, Brooks Buffington, Douglas Warstler, or any threat-incident individual) — add verified text/
branding and any real archival photography in the editor with proper sourcing, not generated. No
weapons, threat, or campus-crisis imagery anywhere in this package's visuals.

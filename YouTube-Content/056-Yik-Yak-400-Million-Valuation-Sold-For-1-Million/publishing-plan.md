# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Strong nostalgia pull for a specific,
large millennial/Gen Z college cohort gives this package above-average general-audience potential — no
urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Broad crossover: founders/PMs interested in feature-dependency and content-moderation trade-offs (core
channel audience), general nostalgia audience who used the app in college around 2014-2015, and
viewers drawn in by the clean $400.5M-to-$1M number collapse.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; note in the video description (not the playlist grouping) that
packages 008, 036, and 039 cover related but mechanically distinct platform/feature-dependency
stories, for viewers who want the comparison without conflating the mechanisms.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the "A DIFFERENT COMPANY — 2021" title card (Segment 10, ~9:45) linking to a pinned comment
clarifying the original-vs-revival distinction; card at the mid-video CTA (~8:45) linking to the
"Startup Autopsies" playlist; optional third card at the start of The Actual Lesson (~10:15) linking
to the full-arc timeline graphic in a community post.

## End CTA

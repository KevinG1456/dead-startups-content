# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open "$400.5M → ~$1M"
  reveal, and on the "-76% DOWNLOADS" card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the "we messed this one up" apology-quote card — this is
  the emotional core of the video and should never feel rushed

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open "$400.5M — NOV 2014" title card, hard cut to "~$1M — APR 2017"
- "The thing that made it a phenomenon is the exact thing that killed it." pattern-interrupt card,
  white text on black
- "LAUNCHED NOVEMBER 2013" title card
- "$400.5 MILLION — NOVEMBER 2014" title card
- Neutral "founding dispute" title card for the Warstler segment — no dramatization
- Neutral "REAL ARRESTS FOLLOWED" title card for the threats segment — no dramatized reenactment, text
  only
- "REQUIRED: NAME + PHOTO — AUGUST 2016" title card
- "5 POSTS A DAY" stat card
- Attributed "we messed this one up" quote card, held on screen for a full beat
- "-76% DOWNLOADS" title card
- "$400.5M → ~$1M" split-screen card at the sale reveal
- "A DIFFERENT COMPANY — 2021" title card
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: launch (Nov 2013) → $400.5M valuation (Nov 2014) →
  threats/arrests (2015) → handles required (Aug 2016) → apology, reversal (Nov 2016) → -76% downloads
  (end 2016) → sold for ~$1M (Apr 2017) → unrelated 2021 revival → 2023 Sidechat acquisition
- Corner "valuation counter" graphic ticking down from $400.5M toward $1M across the whole video,
  landing exactly at the sale reveal

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, Forbes, The
  Washington Post, CBS News, Fortune)
- "Not fraud — a content-moderation and product-pivot story" standing lower third the first time the
  framing is introduced
- "Named, independently verified incident" lower third on each of the three cited threat cases
- "The founders' own words" lower third on the apology-video quote

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic campus/office imagery — no real readable
  logos/trademarks (Yik Yak, Square, Block, Sidechat) without rights clearance
- The corner valuation-counter graphic should tick down visibly but subtly throughout, not
  distracting, reaching $1M only at the exact sale-reveal moment
- The "-76% DOWNLOADS" card should animate as a falling bar/line, landing with a hard stop on the
  final number

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the valuation-collapse
  reveals and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the apology-quote read and the threats segment
  — no camera movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Idea / The Money: bright, upbeat, dorm-room-to-unicorn energy
- A Founding Dispute: neutral, understated — not dramatized
- What Anonymity Enabled: serious, restrained, no tension-building score over the threat material
- The Fix / The Backlash: uneasy, descending
- The Apology: quiet, sincere, genuinely human — not melodramatic
- Too Late: flat, deflating
- The Sale: sharp, sudden tonal drop
- Not the Same Company: neutral, clarifying
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (Nov 2013, Nov 2014, Aug 2016, Nov 2016,
  Apr 2017)
- A sharp "value drop" SFX at the exact moment of the $400.5M → ~$1M reveal
- No SFX or score over the threats/arrests segment — keep that beat entirely quiet and text-card-based
- A soft "rewind" or "correction" SFX on the apology-video quote card
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured-but-warm throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

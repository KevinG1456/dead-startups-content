## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **Yik Yak Hit a $400 Million Valuation. It Sold for About $1 Million.** — cleanest, most quotable
   number collapse, states the whole thesis in one sentence, matches the README title
2. The Feature That Made This App a Phenomenon Is the Feature That Killed It. — the strongest irony
   hook, works even for viewers who never used the app
3. They Removed the One Thing Users Loved. It Killed the Company Faster. — sharp, specific, sets up
   the 2016 pivot as the hinge
4. Downloads Fell 76% in One Year. Here's the Product Decision That Caused It. — strong statistics
   hook with a clear causal promise
5. "We Messed This One Up" — Their Own Words, Right Before the Collapse. — human, quotable, sets up
   the apology video as a payoff
6. This Isn't the Same App You Remember. Here's What Actually Happened to the Original. — strong
   curiosity hook that also pre-empts confusion with the 2021 revival
7. $400.5 Million to $1 Million in Twenty-Nine Months. — clean numeric framing, slightly less punchy
   than #1
8. One Product Change in August 2016 Ended a $400 Million Company. — precise, strong for
   product/founder audience
9. What Happened to Yik Yak? The Full Story — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
10. Yik Yak Shutdown Explained — strong secondary search title for direct-search traffic

## Recommended Title
**"Yik Yak Hit a $400 Million Valuation. The Feature That Made It Popular Also Killed It."**

Reasoning: leads with the sharpest, most verifiable number in the package (the $400.5M valuation),
then immediately sets up the irony mechanism (the anonymity feature) that drives the entire video,
without requiring any prior familiarity with the app. Works fully truncated on mobile at the first
sentence alone. Matches the title already used in this package's README.

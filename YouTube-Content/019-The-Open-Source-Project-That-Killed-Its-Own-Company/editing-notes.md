# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the free-vs-company reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Free-vs-company two-box animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Case 1 timeline graphic (Cyanogen) building year by year, ending on the "STILL ACTIVE TODAY" LineageOS label
- Case 2 valuation-vs-time line graph (Docker) with a sharp drop at the Mirantis sale point, then a smaller stable continuation line
- Open-core risk spectrum graphic (Died / Distressed Sale / Survived) building incrementally across all three cases and staying visible in a corner throughout

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a reported figure (Cyanogen funding, Docker valuation/sale terms) is referenced
- "Reported figure — verify" label used consistently on any dollar amount flagged `[VERIFY]`

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and timeline milestones
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any real press photos/screenshots related to the case studies

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the timeline/graph graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Setup section: neutral, clear, educational tone
- Case walkthroughs: measured, slightly moody undertone that lifts briefly at each case's "twist" beat
- Mechanism-named synthesis section: warmer, more resolving tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "click" SFX as each row/milestone populates in the timeline and risk-spectrum graphics
- Distinct tonal shift/stinger at each case's twist moment (LineageOS fork reveal, Mirantis sale reveal, OpenSearch fork reveal)
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is knowing/ironic but respectful of real layoffs and financial losses involved

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** knowing, slightly wry throughout — this is an irony-driven story, and the presenter's tone should signal "watch what happens" without becoming glib about real layoffs and financial losses
- **Speaking pace:** brisk during the setup and case walkthroughs, deliberately slowing for the LineageOS-still-exists twist and the Mechanism-Named synthesis section, so both land as the video's key insight moments
- **Emotional tone:** measured authority for the case walkthroughs, genuine irony/emphasis on the LineageOS twist, more analytical and synthesizing for the "why this happens" section
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline with a slight knowing arch at each case's "twist" beat (the LineageOS fork, the Mirantis sale, the OpenSearch fork)
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled; more illustrative hand movement when explaining the risk-spectrum positioning of each case
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:20 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (free-vs-company two-box animation)
- Script: "This company raised over $100 million. Then its own free software killed it."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — The Setup**
- Duration: 0:20–1:40 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, open-core dilemma definition graphic animates in
- Performance direction: clear, teaching tone, previews the 3-case structure and the risk-spectrum framework
- Visual direction: medium shot, graphic inset

**Scene 03 — Case 1: Cyanogen Inc.**
- Duration: 1:40–5:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera for narration, cutting to timeline graphic building year by year, split-screen for the LineageOS fork reveal
- Performance direction: measured through the raise/collapse narration, clear tonal lift (knowing, slightly ironic) on the LineageOS "still active today" reveal
- Visual direction: timeline graphic is the segment's visual centerpiece; hold on the "STILL ACTIVE TODAY" label for at least 2 seconds

**Scene 04 — Case 2: Docker Inc.**
- Duration: 5:00–8:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, cutting to valuation-vs-time line graph split-screen for the numbers
- Performance direction: matter-of-fact through the valuation/sale numbers; brief clarifying beat on "distressed sale, not a shutdown" to keep the distinction from Case 1 explicit
- Visual direction: line graph with a sharp drop at the Mirantis sale point, then a smaller stable line continuing — avatar returns to full frame for the distinction from Case 1

**Scene 05 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~8:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift for the CTA
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly

**Scene 06 — Case 3: MongoDB and Elastic vs. AWS**
- Duration: 8:35–11:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, split-screen with the completing risk-spectrum graphic
- Performance direction: slightly more upbeat register — this is the "fought back and survived" case, a tonal counterweight to Cases 1 and 2
- Visual direction: risk-spectrum graphic (Died / Distressed Sale / Survived) completes its third stop here

**Scene 07 — Why This Happens (Mechanism, Named)**
- Duration: 11:00–12:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct-to-camera synthesis
- Performance direction: most analytical, slightly slower-paced segment — this is the takeaway viewers should walk away able to repeat
- Visual direction: minimal graphics, Red Hat contrast point appears as a brief text callout beside avatar

**Scene 08 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 12:15–13:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: steady, direct eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements animate in during final 5 seconds only

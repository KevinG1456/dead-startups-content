## LinkedIn
"'Open source' gets treated as pure goodwill in most founder conversations. For at least one company, it was closer to a business-model mistake.

Cyanogen Inc. raised over $100M to commercialize a mobile OS built on top of its own free, open-source project. The free version kept existing — and by the end of 2016, the company was gone. The community forked the project into LineageOS, which is still maintained today.

Docker's story is a milder version of the same risk: the free engine became the industry standard, and Docker Inc. sold its enterprise business to Mirantis in 2019 for undisclosed terms — widely seen as a distressed sale given the gap from its earlier valuation.

I also cover MongoDB and Elastic — two companies that saw the same risk coming and changed their license before it became fatal.

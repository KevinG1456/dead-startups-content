# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the free version standing over/replacing the paid company.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** two simple boxes/logos side by side — one labeled "FREE" glowing/large and thriving, one labeled "THE COMPANY" small, cracked, or fading out — with a single downward arrow over the company box
- **Main subject:** the free-vs-paid asymmetry visual metaphor
- **Emotion:** irony/shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal, slight terminal/code-grid texture
- **Lighting:** flat, even, high contrast
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** two-box comparison centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** simple box/tile graphics, no real logos or trademarked marks
- **Thumbnail text:** `THEY KILLED THEMSELVES`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green/bright for the "free" side, red/dim for the "company" side, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the size and brightness asymmetry communicates "the free thing won" instantly, before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** a single glowing open-source-style branch/fork icon casting a shadow shaped like a collapsing building/company icon
- **Text:** `THIS KILLED A $100M COMPANY`
- **Why it works:** a striking dollar figure plus a visual metaphor for self-destruction drives strong curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails with a clear irony hook perform well
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the free-vs-company two-box comparison from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `THEY KILLED THEMSELVES`
- **Why it works:** the visual asymmetry instantly communicates "something backfired," and the phrase is punchy and memorable without needing prior context
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/knowing expression, small open-source branch/fork icon tag in the corner
- **Text:** `THE OPEN-SOURCE TRAP`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "trap" signals a rigorous, named business-model concept rather than a one-off story
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

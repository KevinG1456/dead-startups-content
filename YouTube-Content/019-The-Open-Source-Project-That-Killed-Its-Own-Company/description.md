## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct startup outcomes using primary sources. This week: what happens when the free, open-source thing a company builds ends up competing directly with — and beating — the paid product it depends on.

Cyanogen Inc. raised a reported $100M+ to commercialize Cyanogen OS, built on top of the already-popular free, open-source CyanogenMod project. The free version kept existing. By the end of 2016, Cyanogen OS was discontinued — and the community immediately forked CyanogenMod into LineageOS, which is still actively maintained today. The free project outlived the company.

Docker's open-source container engine became the industry standard, adopted for free almost everywhere. Docker Inc., the company, struggled for years to convert that ubiquity into revenue, and sold its entire enterprise business to Mirantis in 2019 for undisclosed terms — widely understood as a distressed sale given the gap from its earlier peak valuation, not a shutdown.

We also cover MongoDB and Elastic's fight with AWS over hosted open-source databases — both companies saw the same risk coming and changed their license (SSPL) before it became fatal, a useful contrast to Cases 1 and 2.

# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Bird: founded September 2017 in Santa Monica by Travis VanderZanden (former Lyft COO,
former Uber executive), a dockless electric-scooter rental company that raised roughly $700
million-plus in venture funding before going public via SPAC merger (with Switchback II Corp) in
November 2021, reaching a reported market value as high as $2.3-2.5 billion. In November 2022,
Bird's own audit committee disclosed it had overstated revenue for roughly 2.5 years (Q1 2020
through Q2 2022) by recognizing revenue from unpaid rides — a self-reported accounting failure, not
a fraud charge; no individual was criminally charged for it in this package's sourcing pass. NYSE
delisted Bird effective September 25, 2023. Bird filed Chapter 11 on December 20, 2023. On April 5,
2024, its assets were sold for approximately $145 million to Third Lane Mobility — a new holding
company formed by Bird's OWN first- and second-lien lenders (led by an Apollo Global Management
affiliate and Obelysk), a debt-for-ownership conversion, not an outside acquisition. This is this
channel's first hardware-fleet unit-economics story (every scooter is a physical, damage-prone
asset) and its fourth SPAC-to-bankruptcy story after 025 (Sonder), 026 (WeWork), and 027 (23andMe).

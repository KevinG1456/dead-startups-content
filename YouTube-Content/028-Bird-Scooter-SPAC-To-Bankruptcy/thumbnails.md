# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule, each thumbnail carries exactly
ONE idea, not the whole video. Do not try to cram the SPAC deal, the restatement, the delisting, AND
the Third Lane Mobility sale into a single image — those live in the video, not the thumbnail. Do
not render Bird's real logo, the NYSE name/mark, or any other real trademarked brand element in
generated thumbnail art — attribute those verbally and on-screen inside the video itself, with
proper sourcing, not in AI-generated thumbnail imagery.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a single generic electric-scooter silhouette on a dark background with a bold red
  "RESTATED" stamp-style mark landing across it
- **Main subject:** the accounting-restatement twist — the least-covered angle of this story anywhere
  else, and the sharpest "wait, what?" hook for a near-universally recognized product
- **Emotion:** shock / vindication
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint sidewalk/pavement texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the scooter silhouette
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** scooter silhouette centered, red stamp mark landing diagonally across the upper
  body of the scooter, title-safe space reserved at the bottom third
- **Props:** generic scooter silhouette (no real Bird branding/wordmark), red stamp mark
- **Thumbnail text:** `THE NUMBERS WERE WRONG.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** white/gray scooter silhouette, red stamp accent, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the stamp lands directly on the most-recognized object in the whole package,
  communicating "this specific, familiar thing had a real accounting problem" before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render Bird's real logo, wordmark, app icon, or the real NYSE name/mark in generated
  thumbnail art — attribute those verbally and on-screen inside the video with proper sourcing, not
  in AI-generated imagery
- Do not attempt a single thumbnail that covers the SPAC deal, the restatement, AND the lender sale —
  that's the video's content; the thumbnail sells one specific irony at a time
- Do not put a specific "cost of one scooter" dollar figure in any thumbnail — that breakdown is an
  illustrative, on-screen educational device inside the video (see script.md), not a headline claim
- Do not use "FRAUD" or any fraud-implying language on any thumbnail text — the restatement was a
  disclosed, self-reported SEC filing, not a fraud charge against any individual

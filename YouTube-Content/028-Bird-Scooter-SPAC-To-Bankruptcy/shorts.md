# Shorts Ideas (10)

Note: keep every Short below to ironclad, undisputed facts — dates, dollar figures, the SPAC
listing, the delisting notice, the bankruptcy filing, the Third Lane Mobility sale structure. Never
use "fraud" language for the accounting restatement in any Short; if a Short mentions the
restatement, it must also state "no individual was criminally charged" in the same breath or caption.
Never fill in invented specific dollar figures for the "cost of one scooter" categories — present
them as categories only.

**1. "Went Public At $2.3 Billion. Then Its Own Numbers Were Wrong."**
Hook: "This company went public at a valuation as high as $2.3 billion. A year later, its own audit
committee said its financial reports 'should no longer be relied upon.'" Script: quick valuation-to-
restatement date-collision reveal. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**2. "$2.3 Billion. Then About $70 Million. In About A Year."**
Hook: "This company's valuation went from as high as $2.3 billion to about $70 million in roughly
twelve months." Script: 30-second valuation-collapse numeral reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**3. "Not Fraud. Still A Real Problem."**
Hook: "This company told the SEC its own revenue numbers had been wrong for two and a half years. No
one was criminally charged. Here's what actually happened." Script: quick, careful restatement
explainer, stated plainly. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**4. "What A Scooter Actually Costs A Company"**
Hook: "Every scooter this company owned had to be bought, charged every night, and replaced when it
got stolen or wrecked. That's a different business than software." Script: rapid-fire "cost of one
scooter" category explainer (acquisition / nightly charging / damage-theft-vandalism), no invented
figures. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**5. "Their Own Lenders Bought The Company Back"**
Hook: "When this company went bankrupt, it wasn't bought by a rival. It was bought by its own
lenders." Script: quick debt-for-ownership conversion reveal. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**6. "Two Different $700 Million Numbers"**
Hook: "This company had two different $700 million figures in its history — and they're not the same
$700 million." Script: quick Oct 2019 cumulative vs. Apr 2021 Series C distinction. CTA: "Full
breakdown linked."

**7. "The Delisting Notice"**
Hook: "This company got a formal notice that it would be delisted from the NYSE. Here's exactly why."
Script: 30-second minimum-market-cap explainer. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**8. "Four SPAC Companies. Four Bankruptcies."**
Hook: "Four companies on this channel went public via SPAC around 2021. All four later went
bankrupt. This one's own accounting turned out to be wrong." Script: quick Sonder/WeWork/23andMe/
Bird scoreboard. CTA: "New autopsy every week — subscribe."

**9. "$145 Million, Sold To Itself (Basically)"**
Hook: "This company's assets sold for about $145 million — to a company formed by its own lenders."
Script: quick lender-ownership-structure reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**10. "You've Ridden One Of These"**
Hook: "You've almost certainly seen or ridden one of these. Here's what happened to the company that
owned it." Script: product-recognition open into a fast recap of the full timeline. CTA: "Full story
on the channel."

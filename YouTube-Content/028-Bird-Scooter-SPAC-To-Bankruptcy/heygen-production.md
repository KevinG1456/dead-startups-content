# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the founding/raise/going-
  public segments and a clear educational register during the "cost of one scooter" hardware-
  economics explainer
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the accounting-restatement segment and
  the lender-sale segment — both need room to land clearly and to be attributed precisely, on pace
  with the on-screen lower thirds
- **Emotional tone:** confident/upbeat during the founding, raise, and going-public segments; patient,
  matter-of-fact teaching tone for the hardware-economics explainer; measured and precise gravity
  during the accounting-restatement segment — never sensational, never implying fraud; sharper focus
  through the collapse and lender-sale timeline
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; deliberate care and slight slowing when stating "no
  individual was criminally charged" and "not a fraud allegation" — these lines need to read as
  careful precision, not as a hedge being rushed past
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the "cost
  of one scooter" three-box graphic and the lender-ownership-conversion diagram
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (three-date timeline animation: public-at-$2.3B →
  restatement-disclosed → sold-to-own-lenders)
- Script: "In November 2021, this company went public at a valuation as high as $2.3 billion. In
  November 2022, its own audit committee said its financial reports 'should no longer be relied
  upon.' In April 2024, its own lenders bought what was left of it."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–0:55 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: flat, serious delivery, warm slightly on "you've almost certainly seen one
  of these"
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 0:55–1:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: slightly warmer, teaching tone; deliberate, careful pace on "not a fraud
  story — no individual has been criminally charged"; brief tonal lift on the direct Convoy (017)
  cross-reference line
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Founder and the Build**
- Duration: 1:45–3:00 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (founder-era imagery, generic scooter-fleet
  imagery), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: brisk, confident, establishing energy
- Visual direction: "software company vs. hardware fleet company" split-screen graphic

**Scene 05 — Why Hardware Is a Different Business**
- Duration: 3:00–5:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with the "cost of one scooter" three-box
  template graphic full-screen
- Performance direction: patient, clear, teaching tone; deliberate, unhurried delivery on the
  on-screen disclosure that these are illustrative categories, not Bird's own confirmed numbers
- Visual direction: three labeled boxes (Acquisition / Nightly Charging & Collection / Damage,
  Theft & Vandalism Loss), no dollar figures filled in

**Scene 06 — The Raise**
- Duration: 5:00–6:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Alternates avatar-on-camera with full-screen funding-round
  timeline graphic
- Performance direction: brisk, numbers-forward; deliberate care separating the "Oct 2019 ~$700M
  cumulative" figure from the "Apr 2021 $700M Series C round" — slow down specifically on this
  distinction so it doesn't blur together
- Visual direction: funding-round timeline builds, explicit on-screen callout separating the two
  similar-sounding figures

**Scene 07 — Going Public**
- Duration: 6:30–8:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with SPAC-merger graphic, full-screen for the
  valuation and combined-funding figures
- Performance direction: measured, precise on "approximately $414 million in cash proceeds" and the
  "north of $1 billion" combined figure as an approximate, not a single precise total
- Visual direction: SPAC-merger graphic, valuation climbing to $2.3-2.5B, combined total building
  toward "north of $1 billion" with an explicit "approximate" label on screen

**Scene 08 — The Accounting Restatement**
- Duration: 8:00–10:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, 8-K filing graphic full-screen for the
  restatement-period timeline
- Performance direction: slow down noticeably, quieter and more deliberate delivery; read "should no
  longer be relied upon" as Bird's own quoted language; state "no individual was criminally charged"
  and "not a fraud allegation" clearly and without rushing
- Visual direction: 8-K filing graphic, Q1 2020–Q2 2022 restatement-period timeline, $12.5M and
  $31.6M figures displayed side by side with clear labeling

**Scene 09 — The Collapse**
- Duration: 10:00–11:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (valuation-collapse graph, delisting-notice
  document graphic), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: measured, sharper tonal drop as the valuation graph falls
- Visual direction: valuation graph collapsing from $2.3-2.5B toward ~$70M, NYSE delisting-notice
  document graphic (generic, no real NYSE mark rendered)

**Scene 10 — Bankruptcy and the Lender Sale**
- Duration: 11:15–12:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, ownership-conversion diagram full-screen
- Performance direction: deliberate, careful pace stating the $145M sale price is higher than the
  prior year's ~$70M market value without implying the bankruptcy was unnecessary — this is a nuance
  that needs unhurried delivery
- Visual direction: bankruptcy-filing graphic, then "your own lenders" ownership-conversion diagram —
  equity holders at the bottom, secured lenders converting debt into ownership at the top

**Scene 11 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~11:45 (inside Scene 10) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into serious tone
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly alongside a small package-017 callout

**Scene 12 — The Actual Lesson**
- Duration: 12:30–15:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with the "cost of one scooter" graphic and
  the four-company SPAC-wave comparison graphic building beat by beat
- Performance direction: clear, patient teaching tone; this is the video's actual thesis payoff —
  hold steady, even-handed delivery through the "own lenders, not an outside buyer" restatement
- Visual direction: "cost of one scooter" graphic reappears, then four-company comparison (Sonder /
  WeWork / 23andMe / Bird) — how each went public, how each ended

**Scene 13 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 15:00–17:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: return to controlled-authority tone, steady eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements (package 017 callout, subscribe) animate in during final 5
  seconds only

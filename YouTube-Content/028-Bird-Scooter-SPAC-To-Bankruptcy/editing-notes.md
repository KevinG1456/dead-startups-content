# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the three-date cold-open
  reveal (public-at-$2.3B → restatement-disclosed → sold-to-own-lenders)

## Motion Graphics
- Three-date timeline animation for the cold open ("NOVEMBER 2021 — PUBLIC AT $2.3B" → "NOVEMBER
  2022 — RESTATEMENT DISCLOSED" → "APRIL 2024 — SOLD TO ITS OWN LENDERS")
- "Software company vs. hardware fleet company" split-screen graphic for the founding segment
- "Cost of one scooter" three-box template graphic (Acquisition Cost / Nightly Charging & Collection
  Labor / Damage, Theft & Vandalism Loss) — labeled categories only, NO invented dollar figures
  filled in; this graphic recurs at least three times across the video (Segment 2, the collapse
  segment, and the final-lesson segment) to keep the hardware-economics thesis visually anchored
  throughout
- Funding-round timeline graphic building toward "roughly $700M+ before going public," with an
  explicit on-screen callout separating the October 2019 cumulative figure from the April 2021
  Series C round (never merge these into one number)
- SPAC-merger graphic with valuation climbing to $2.3-2.5B, plus a separate "$414M cash proceeds"
  callout, and a "north of $1B combined — approximate" total graphic
- 8-K filing graphic with the Q1 2020–Q2 2022 restatement-period timeline, $12.5M and $31.6M figures
  displayed side by side with clear labeling
- Valuation-collapse graph falling from $2.3-2.5B toward ~$70M
- Generic NYSE delisting-notice document graphic (no real NYSE mark/logo rendered)
- Bankruptcy-filing graphic
- Lender-ownership-conversion diagram — equity holders at the bottom, secured lenders converting debt
  into ownership at the top (mirrors the Convoy/package-017 diagram style for visual consistency
  across the two lender-takeover videos)
- Four-company SPAC-wave comparison graphic (Sonder / WeWork / 23andMe / Bird) — how each went
  public, how each ended, landing on Bird's unique "own accounting was wrong" distinction

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Bird's own 8-K,
  TechCrunch, Forbes, Fortune, CNBC, Bloomberg, Morningstar/BusinessWire/Nasdaq, Cassel Salpeter &
  Co.)
- "Roughly $700 million-plus before going public" as a standing lower-third phrasing whenever total
  pre-SPAC funding is referenced — never a single lifetime-total figure
- "Illustrative categories — not Bird's confirmed per-scooter figures" lower third every time the
  "cost of one scooter" graphic appears — no exceptions
- "Self-reported SEC filing — not a fraud charge" lower third every single time the accounting
  restatement is discussed — no exceptions
- "No individual criminally charged" lower third paired with the restatement segment
- "Debt-for-ownership conversion — Bird's own lenders" lower third during the Third Lane Mobility
  sale segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static generic scooter/street imagery (no real readable Bird logos or
  branding without rights clearance)
- The three-box "cost of one scooter" graphic animates in one box at a time, staying on screen long
  enough to read clearly each time it recurs
- The valuation-collapse graph animates as a continuous falling line, not a hard cut, to emphasize
  the roughly 12-month timeframe

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the "cost of one scooter"
  graphic and the four-company comparison graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founder and the Build: brisk, confident, building energy
- Why Hardware Is a Different Business: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the three-box graphic
  carry the teaching weight without competing music
- The Raise / Going Public: bright, legitimacy-signaling tone
- The Accounting Restatement: quiet, even, deliberately unsensational — no ominous "scandal" stinger,
  this must not sound like a true-crime cue
- The Collapse: sharp tonal drop as the valuation graph falls
- Bankruptcy and the Lender Sale: measured, slightly somber, resolving into a neutral "structural
  explainer" tone during the ownership-conversion diagram
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each of the three cold-open date reveals
- Sharp "crash" SFX as the valuation-collapse graph falls
- Soft "click" SFX as each box in the "cost of one scooter" graphic locks into place
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and precise, especially during the
  accounting-restatement segment, which must never read as implying fraud or wrongdoing

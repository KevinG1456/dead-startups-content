# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the contradiction between real revenue and real failure.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "$22M" figure in green, with a red "STILL DIED" stamp overlapping it at an angle
- **Main subject:** the revenue-vs-failure contradiction
- **Emotion:** shock/curiosity
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the central figure
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** big number centered, stamp overlapping at an angle, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** red "STILL DIED" stamp graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `$22M A YEAR. STILL DIED.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green dollar figure, red stamp accent, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the big-number-with-a-stamp-through-it does the communicating before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "$22M" centered large, small red "DIED" stamp in the corner
- **Text:** `HOW DOES THIS EVEN HAPPEN`
- **Why it works:** numeral-led format with a small contradicting stamp creates immediate curiosity about the mechanism
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well on this channel
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the $22M-with-stamp combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$22M A YEAR. STILL DIED.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of a real, sourced contradiction paired with the visual proof in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small "$22M" tag in the corner
- **Text:** `THE FOUNDER EXPLAINS WHAT WENT WRONG`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "the founder explains" framing signals real primary-source candor, not just a recap
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

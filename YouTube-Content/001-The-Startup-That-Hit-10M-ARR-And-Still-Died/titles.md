## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **Sprig Hit a $22M Run Rate. It Still Lost $850K a Month.** — leads with real, founder-confirmed numbers, the contrast does the work without needing an invented figure, works fully truncated on mobile
2. This Founder Says He Should Have Killed His Own Company Sooner — strong, quotable, promises real founder candor
3. $22 Million a Year In Revenue. Still Not Enough. — clean, punchy, sets up the contradiction immediately
4. This Company Owned Its Own Kitchens. That's What Killed It. — strong mechanism-forward hook, signals a real explainer not just a recap
5. Uber Eats Launched One Feature. This $22M Company Never Recovered. — specific, dated, strong curiosity
6. What Actually Killed Sprig (In the Founder's Own Words) — best pure-SEO with a candor hook
7. What Happened to Sprig? The Full Story — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
8. They Raised $56 Million and Still Couldn't Make It Work — best for the emotional/human-angle alt cut
9. Sprig Unit Economics Case Study — dry, best for search-only sub-cut
10. What $850,000 a Month In Losses Actually Looks Like — strong secondary Shorts hook

## Recommended Title
**"Sprig Hit a $22M Run Rate. It Still Lost $850K a Month."**

Reasoning: uses Sprig's real, founder-confirmed numbers (the ~$21-22M run rate Biyani himself
cited, the ~$850K/month loss reported by Bloomberg) rather than the original placeholder "$10M"
figure, works fully truncated on mobile, and sets up the video's actual mechanism (owned-kitchen
cost structure, not just "ran out of money") without overclaiming. Name Sprig directly — it
carries real recognition value among the channel's founder-heavy audience, and Biyani's own later
candor is a strong retention promise once the video opens.

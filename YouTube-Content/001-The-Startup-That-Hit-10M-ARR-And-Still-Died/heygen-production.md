# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with a clear educational/explainer register during the cost-structure section
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the cost-structure mechanics and the founder-quote segments — dense content that needs room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the financial/explainer segments, genuine reflective quiet for the Founder's Own Hindsight section
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine gravity when reading Biyani's "I would have shut it down sooner" quote
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative hand movement during the "who bears the cost" diagram explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# Master Editing Timeline — Sprig

Shared structural timeline across all six languages. Localized scripts may run a beat faster or
slower naturally (German and Dutch compound phrasing tends to run slightly longer per idea than
English; Norwegian/Swedish tend to run close to English) — do NOT force identical timestamps
across languages. This is the shared blueprint, not a frame-locked cut.

```text
00:00 Cold Open — revenue-vs-loss contradiction
00:15 Hook — introduce Sprig, the "founder did the forensics himself" angle
00:50 Introduction — method/sources statement
01:40 Segment: The Raise — founding, funding rounds
03:00 Segment: The Run Rate — Biyani's own revenue quote
04:00 Segment: The Cost Structure — owned-kitchen mechanism, "who bears the cost" diagram
06:30 Segment: The Trigger — Uber Eats, growth-rate flip
08:00 Segment: The Shutdown — May 26 2017, headcount distinction, investor return
08:30 Mid-Video CTA
09:30 Segment: The Founder's Own Hindsight — slow down, reflective beat
11:30 Final CTA + Outro — one-sentence lesson, engagement prompt, end screen
13:00 [target end, ±2 min naturally per language]
```

## Per-Language Pacing Notes
- Keep the Hindsight segment (09:30 onward) noticeably slower in EVERY language — this is a
  deliberate tonal shift in the master script, not just an English pacing choice.
- The Cost Structure segment (04:00–06:30) is the highest drop-off risk in every language —
  localized scripts should not pad this section with extra explanation beyond the master beats.
- Chapter/timestamp labels in each language's `subtitles.md` and YouTube chapter markers should
  be translated, not left in English, even though the underlying timeline structure is shared.

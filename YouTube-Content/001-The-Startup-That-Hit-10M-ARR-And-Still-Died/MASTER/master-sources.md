## What Every Localized Version Must Get Right, In Every Language
1. Revenue stated as "roughly $21-22M," never a single false-precision number.
2. Total funding stated as "roughly $56-58M," never a single false-precision number.
3. Peak headcount (1,300+) and shutdown-time headcount (~200) presented as two distinct
   measures — translators must not merge or "simplify" these into one number.
4. The "I would have shut it down within three months" quote always carries its context
   (three months after the negative trend was clear, not after the 2013 launch).
5. Currency: figures stay in US dollars ($) in every language — see `master-thumbnail.md` and each
   language's `thumbnail.md` for the localized numeral-formatting convention (e.g., German "22
   Mio. $", French "22 M$"). Do NOT convert to EUR/NOK/SEK — Sprig is a US company and all
   original reporting is in USD; currency conversion would introduce inaccuracy (exchange-rate
   drift) the source material doesn't have.
6. Names stay unlocalized: "Gagan Biyani," "Sprig," "Uber Eats," "Greylock Partners," "Social
   Capital," "Battery Ventures," "Accel" — do not transliterate or translate proper nouns.

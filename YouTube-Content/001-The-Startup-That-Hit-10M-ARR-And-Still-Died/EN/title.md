## 🏆 Recommended Title
**"Sprig Hit a $22M Run Rate. It Still Lost $850K a Month."**

**Why this is strongest for the English channel:** it uses Sprig's real, founder-confirmed
numbers rather than an invented figure, works fully truncated on mobile, and sets up the video's
actual mechanism (owned-kitchen cost structure) without overclaiming. Naming Sprig directly
carries real recognition value for this channel's founder-heavy English-speaking audience, and
"still lost $850K a month" as a payoff to "$22M run rate" is a native-feeling English
contradiction structure — short clause, hard stop, short clause. (See each other language's own
`title.md` for why the recommended title differs by market — this exact clause rhythm doesn't
always localize as the strongest option.)

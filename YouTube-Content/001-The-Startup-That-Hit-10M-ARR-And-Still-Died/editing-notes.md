# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the "$22M run rate / $850K monthly loss" reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- "$22M/YEAR" vs "-$850K/MONTH" contrast animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Funding-round timeline graphic building through Series A and the 2015 round
- "Who bears the cost" side-by-side diagram: Sprig's owned-kitchen model vs. a typical restaurant-marketplace model
- Growth-rate graph flipping from +2% to -2% at the exact dated Uber Eats moment
- Shutdown/headcount closing graphic, clearly distinguishing peak operational headcount (1,300+) from final shutdown headcount (~200)
- "Autopsy Report Card" end-graphic (run rate, burn, cause of death, verdict)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Mixergy, The Full Ratchet, TechCrunch, Fortune, Bloomberg)
- "Biyani's own words" lower third whenever a direct quote appears on screen, distinguishing his primary-source account from reconstructed narrative
- "Peak operational scale, not final headcount" lower third when the 1,300+ figure appears, to prevent it reading as a contradiction with the ~200 figure

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic kitchen/delivery imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- Biyani's quotes type on screen as they're read aloud

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the cost-structure diagram layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Raise: brisk, confident, slightly triumphant undertone (building to the $22M run rate)
- The Cost Structure: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the diagram carry the explanation
- The Trigger: sharp tonal shift at the growth-rate flip moment
- The Shutdown: slow, minor-key, sparse
- The Founder's Own Hindsight: quiet, reflective, understated — let Biyani's own words carry the weight
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "flip" SFX as the growth-rate graph crosses from positive to negative
- Soft "click" SFX as each step in the funding-timeline or cost-structure diagram populates
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and respectful, especially during the founder-hindsight section

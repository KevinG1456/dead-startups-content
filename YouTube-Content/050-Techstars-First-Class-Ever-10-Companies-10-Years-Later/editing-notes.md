# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the 10-square grid resolving from
  blank to confirmed/complicated/unconfirmed in the cold open
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on "I'm not going to guess a death date for a company just
  because I can't find one" — the single most important honesty flag in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open 10-square grid animating in, several squares flickering with a "?" mark
- "This isn't the video where I give you one clean number. Here's why." pattern-interrupt card, white
  text on black
- "SHUT DOWN — 2010" title card with EventVue founder's quote overlaid
- "ACQUIRED 2008 — STILL MAINTAINED TODAY" title card for Intense Debate
- Acquisition-to-shutdown timeline stretched across nearly a decade for Socialthing!
- "MERGED 2009 → WOUND DOWN 2011-2012" title card for Brightkite
- Name/company split-screen graphic for Villij ("the name survived, the company didn't")
- Both acquisition headlines fading to a blank "?" card for Filtrbox/madKast
- Full 10-company table, color-coded confirmed/complicated/unconfirmed, revealed in full for the
  finding reveal

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, Brad
  Feld's blog, CBInsights, Crunchbase)
- "Confirmed" / "Complicated" / "Unconfirmed — no evidence found" standing lower-third tags on each
  company segment, color-coded consistently throughout
- "Absorbed into the acquirer — standalone fate not confirmed" lower third on Filtrbox and madKast
  specifically, to avoid implying a definitive kill

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic 2007-era startup-office imagery — no real
  readable logos/trademarks (Techstars, Automattic, AOL, Jive Software, ShareThis) without rights
  clearance
- The 10-square grid should resolve gradually across the whole video, not all at once, so viewers see
  it building segment by segment
- The Socialthing! acquisition-to-shutdown timeline should visibly stretch out (not compress) to
  communicate the unusually long 8.5-year gap

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the grid graphic, the timelines,
  and the full audit-table reveal
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "I'm not going to guess a death date"
  framing card — no camera movement competing with it for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: curious, slightly uneasy pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- EventVue: measured, a little somber, respectful of the founder's own candor
- Intense Debate: a genuine, warm lift — this is the video's one unambiguously positive beat
- Socialthing!: neutral, matter-of-fact, stretching subtly to underscore the long timeline
- Brightkite: quiet, declining
- Villij: contemplative, slightly melancholy
- Filtrbox/madKast/J-Squared Media/search-to-phone/KBLabs: sparse, minimal — let the silence do the
  work on the "unconfirmed" beats rather than over-scoring them
- The Actual Finding: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout
- A distinct, slightly hollow "static/glitch" SFX on each "unconfirmed" reveal, differentiating it
  clearly from the confident "chime" used on confirmed reveals
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured and analytical throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full audit-table reveal's final beat

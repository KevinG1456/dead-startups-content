# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** curious and slightly uneasy through the cold open; measured and building through
  the method statement; steady and warm through the 4 confirmed segments (a genuine lift on Intense
  Debate specifically); reflective through Villij; deliberately calm and unhurried through the 5
  unconfirmed segments — no forced drama over an absence of evidence; clear and reflective through the
  finding
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, slowing deliberately on "I'm not going to guess a death date
  for a company just because I can't find one" — this needs room to land as a genuine principle, not a
  throwaway line
- **Emotional tone:** warm and specific on Intense Debate's 18-year survival; respectful, not comedic,
  on EventVue's founder candor; calm and matter-of-fact (never frustrated or apologetic) through the
  unconfirmed segments — the uncertainty is presented as an honest finding, not a failure
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a genuine flicker of delight on the Intense Debate
  reveal; visible care and precision when reading the "unable to fully corroborate" framing cards
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the grid
  graphic and full audit-table reveal
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

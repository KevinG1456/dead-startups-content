# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Techstars, Automattic,
AOL, Jive Software, ShareThis), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (David Cohen,
Rob Johnson, etc.) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor with
proper sourcing, not generated.

## Thumbnail Prompts
**Prompt A (recommended primary):** "YouTube thumbnail, a 10-square grid, some squares glowing solid
gold, others flickering with a faint question-mark shape, dark background, even lighting with subtle
flicker on the unresolved squares, high contrast, empty space at bottom third for title text, no
readable numbers or brand names, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt B:** "YouTube thumbnail, a single glowing speech-bubble/comment icon on a dark background,
faint '2008' text fading into a bright glow, single spotlight, high contrast, empty space at bottom
third for title text, no readable text, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt C:** "YouTube thumbnail, tight three-quarter crop portrait of a generic host avatar,
dramatic single top-down light, serious analytical expression, dark background, small date-tag
graphic in the corner, photorealistic, cinematic, high contrast, not a real identifiable person, 16:9
--ar 16:9"

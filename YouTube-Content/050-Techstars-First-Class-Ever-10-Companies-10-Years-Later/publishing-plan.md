# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. No news-cycle urgency — hold for the
best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Founders/investors evaluating accelerators; startup-history and Silicon-Valley-history enthusiasts;
viewers of package 007 (YC Summer 2006 audit) looking for the companion piece.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist. Explicitly pair with package 007 as the second entry in a
recurring "real accelerator batch audit" format — cross-reference both in end screens/cards.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element, PLUS a direct card/link to package 007 (YC Summer 2006
audit) as the natural companion piece — same method, genuinely different finding.

## Cards
Card at the Intense Debate reveal (~4:00) linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist for viewers
hooked by the "still alive today" detail; card at the mid-video CTA (~7:15) reinforcing the
"more interesting half" framing; card near the end (~12:15) linking directly to package 007 for
viewers who want the other full-batch audit.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next audit in this library — and if you know what actually happened to
Filtrbox, madKast, J-Squared Media, search-to-phone, or KBLabs, tell me in the comments. I'll
actually update this page if something checks out. Also: go watch the Y Combinator batch audit next
if you haven't — same method, a completely different result."

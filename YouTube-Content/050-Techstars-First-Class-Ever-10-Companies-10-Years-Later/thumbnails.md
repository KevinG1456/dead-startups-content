# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Never claim a flat "X of 10 gone"
number in any thumbnail — the real finding is the confirmed/complicated/unconfirmed split.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a 10-square grid; 4 squares solid/lit, 1 half-lit, 5 flickering with a "?" mark
- **Main subject:** the honest confirmed-vs-unconfirmed split itself
- **Emotion:** curiosity/unease
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint grid-line texture
- **Lighting:** even, with the "?" squares subtly flickering/dimmer
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** 10-square grid fills most of the frame, bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic numeral/grid graphics, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `10 COMPANIES. HALF, NOBODY CAN CONFIRM.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** confirmed squares in solid gold, unconfirmed squares in flickering grey/red
- **Contrast strategy:** the visual split between solid and flickering squares communicates the whole thesis before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Image concept:** the 10-square confirmed/unconfirmed grid from Winning Concept above
- **Subject:** the honesty-of-the-audit thesis, graphic-led
- **Expression:** N/A (no face)
- **Objects:** generic grid/numeral graphics
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Colors:** gold (confirmed), flickering grey/red (unconfirmed), near-black background
- **Lighting:** even, flicker effect on unconfirmed squares
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** grid centered, bottom-third text
- **Thumbnail text:** `10 COMPANIES. HALF, NOBODY CAN CONFIRM.`
- **Why it works:** the video's actual differentiator (honest untraceability) is the single most quotable finding
- **CTR prediction:** high — an unresolved-mystery numeral grid performs strongly on this channel
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic, startup-history audience

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render real, identifiable photorealistic likenesses of any founder (David Cohen, Rob Johnson, etc.) — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Techstars, Automattic, AOL, Jive Software, or ShareThis logos/wordmarks in generated thumbnail art
- Never state a flat "X of 10 gone" number in any thumbnail text — the honest finding is the three-way split

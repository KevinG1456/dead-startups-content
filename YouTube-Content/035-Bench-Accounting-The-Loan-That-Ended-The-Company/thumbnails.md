# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram the founder
ouster, the loan mechanics, and the acquisition into a single image.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large generic "OFFLINE" / blank-screen icon on a laptop silhouette, dozens of tiny
  generic building/storefront icons around it going dark one by one
- **Main subject:** the overnight shutdown's scale of human impact
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint grid/dashboard texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the blank laptop screen
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** laptop/blank-screen centered, small storefront icons scattered and dimming around
  it, bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic laptop icon, generic storefront/building icons
- **Thumbnail text:** `35,000 BUSINESSES. LOCKED OUT OVERNIGHT.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** cold blue-white screen glow, red accent on the "offline" state, near-black
  background
- **Contrast strategy:** the scale of many small icons going dark around one blank screen communicates
  the entire thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Ian Crosby or Adam Saint in any
  thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put the real Bench Accounting, Employer.com, or any named lender's logo/wordmark in generated
  thumbnail art — no lender name is even confirmed for this story
- Do not name or imply a specific lender in any thumbnail text — that detail is genuinely unconfirmed
  in public reporting

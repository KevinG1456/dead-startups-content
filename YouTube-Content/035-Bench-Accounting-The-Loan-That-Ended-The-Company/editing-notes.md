# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open blank-screen reveal,
  and on the "$2.8M CASH / $65.4M LIABILITIES" card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the "the specific lender's name... have never been publicly
  confirmed" framing card — the single most important line in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open blank laptop-screen graphic, small storefront icons around it dimming one by one
- "There was no warning. Not to customers. Not to most employees." pattern-interrupt card, white text
  on black
- "$232M VALUATION — 2021" title card
- "FOUNDER REMOVED — NOVEMBER 2021" title card
- Fading timeline graphic, 2021 → 2024, representing the "slow struggle" segment
- Blank-screen graphic with storefront icons dimming, "450 LAID OFF — SAME DAY" title card
- Generic "LOAN CALLED" document icon — no lender name or logo anywhere on screen, ever
- "$2.8M CASH / $65.4M LIABILITIES" side-by-side card
- "ACQUIRED BY EMPLOYER.COM — JANUARY 1, 2025" title card
- Compact four-point date-marker timeline graphic (Dec 27 → Dec 30 → Jan 1 → Jan 7)
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founding (2012) → funding climb (2013-2021) →
  founder ouster (Nov 2021) → bridge financings (2022-2024) → shutdown (Dec 27, 2024) → acquisition
  announced (Dec 30) → deal closes (Jan 1, 2025) → bankruptcy filing (Jan 7, 2025)
- Package 017 (Convoy) cross-reference graphic: small side-by-side card, Convoy (lender took control
  over months) vs. Bench (lender called a loan, shutdown in a day)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (BetaKit, Wikipedia,
  GeekWire, CBC News, Banking Dive)
- "Lender's identity never publicly confirmed" standing lower third any time the shutdown mechanism is
  discussed — no exceptions, this is the single most important lower-third rule in the package
- "Per Crosby's own account, not independently confirmed" lower third whenever his ouster claims are
  on screen
- "Employer.com's own statement" lower third on the ARR/profitability claim if used

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/small-business imagery — no real
  readable logos/trademarks (Bench Accounting, Employer.com) without rights clearance
- The blank-screen-vs-storefronts graphic should animate with storefront icons dimming one at a time
  in rapid succession, reinforcing the scale (35,000+) without needing to render each one individually
- The compact four-point timeline should animate each date marker landing in quick succession,
  reinforcing how fast the real sequence actually was
- The two-color Convoy/Bench comparison graphic should animate with Convoy's timeline stretching slowly
  across the screen and Bench's snapping into place almost instantly, visually reinforcing the speed
  contrast

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the shutdown graphic, the
  compact timeline, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "lender's identity never publicly
  confirmed" framing card — no camera movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding / The Climb: measured, optimistic, building energy
- The Ouster: measured, slightly somber, no melodrama
- The Slow Struggle: uneasy, declining energy, fading toward silence
- December 27, 2024 / The Mechanism: sharp, sudden tonal drop — the single biggest musical cue shift
  in the video, mirroring the abruptness of the real event
- The Bankruptcy Filing: quiet, restrained
- The Rescue: a measured, cautious lift — not triumphant, since real people were still affected
- The Full Timeline: brisk, ticking, reinforcing the speed
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2012 founding, 2021 raise/ouster, Dec 27
  shutdown, Dec 30 acquisition announced, Jan 1 deal closes, Jan 7 bankruptcy filing)
- A sharp, sudden "power-down" or "screen-off" SFX at the exact moment of the December 27 shutdown
  reveal — the single most important sound cue in the video
- Soft, rapid "dimming" SFX as each storefront icon goes dark in the shutdown graphic
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays respectful and precise throughout, given
  real small businesses and employees were affected
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

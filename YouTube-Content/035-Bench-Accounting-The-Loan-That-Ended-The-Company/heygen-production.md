# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and building through the founding/climb segments; a somber dip at the
  ouster; fading, uneasy through the slow-struggle segment; sharp, sudden shift at the shutdown reveal;
  restrained through the mechanism explanation; measured-but-cautious through the rescue segment
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during "the specific lender's name... have never
  been publicly confirmed" and the "no warning to customers" framing lines — both need room to land
  clearly
- **Emotional tone:** dry, document-forward through the funding/mechanism explanation — never
  sensationalized; genuine gravity, not melodrama, at the shutdown reveal; measured caution (not
  triumphalism) through the rescue segment, since real people were still affected
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine gravity at the "35,000 businesses"
  and "450 employees" reveals; visible care and precision when reading the "lender never confirmed"
  framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  shutdown graphic, the compact timeline, and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (blank-screen-vs-storefronts graphic reveal)
- Script: "A bank called in one loan. The next morning, 35,000 small businesses couldn't access their
  own financial records. This is exactly what happened, and exactly why."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–1:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: clear, measured delivery establishing the company and the two headline facts
  ($100M+ raised, 35,000+ locked out)
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 1:00–1:40 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: deliberately clear and unhurried on "the specific lender... have never been
  publicly confirmed" — this is the single most important line in the whole video
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Founding**
- Duration: 1:40–2:20 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (generic startup-office graphic), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: brisk, neutral, establishing energy
- Visual direction: generic office establishing shot, no real branding

**Scene 05 — The Climb**
- Duration: 2:20–3:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with funding-ladder/valuation graphic
- Performance direction: measured, building toward the $232M valuation reveal
- Visual direction: "$232M VALUATION — 2021" title card

**Scene 06 — The Ouster**
- Duration: 3:15–4:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, title card

**Scene 07 — The Slow Struggle**

# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Quibi, Roku, Bird, Virgin
Orbit, Friendster, Facebook, MOL Global, Katerra, SoftBank), and no photorealistic likeness of any real
named person — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor with proper
sourcing, not generated.

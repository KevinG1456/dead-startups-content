# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on each ratio bar graphic reveal,
  especially Virgin Orbit's ~1% (the video's single biggest numeral moment)
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the Friendster "not a bankruptcy" framing card — the most
  important honesty flag in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open five peak-value bars collapsing into tiny sold-for bars
- "This isn't about why these companies died. It's about what was actually left when they did."
  pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- "$1.75B RAISED" / "$1.75B → UNDER $100M" ratio bar graphic (Quibi)
- "$2.3–2.5B PEAK VALUATION" / "$2.3–2.5B → $145M" ratio bar graphic (Bird)
- "$3.7 BILLION VALUATION" / "$3.7B → $36M" ratio bar graphic (Virgin Orbit) — the sharpest visual
  contrast in the video, hold slightly longer than the others
- "NOT A BANKRUPTCY — A VOLUNTARY SALE" framing card (Friendster)
- "$39M COMPANY → $40M PATENTS ALONE" comparison graphic (Friendster)
- "~$2B → ~$99M" ratio bar graphic (Katerra)
- Five-row side-by-side ratio ledger graphic for The Actual Lesson, building beat by beat

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced, naming which package
  (002, 028, 031, 040, or freshly-sourced for 034/Katerra) the figure is verified in
- "Verified elsewhere on this channel" standing lower third on Quibi, Bird, Virgin Orbit, and
  Friendster segments
- "Newly sourced this session" standing lower third on the Katerra segment
- "Not a bankruptcy — a voluntary sale" lower third throughout the Friendster segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and ratios
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic industrial/office/auction imagery — no real
  readable logos/trademarks without rights clearance
- Each ratio bar graphic should animate the tall bar shrinking down to the small bar in real time,
  landing with a visible percentage callout
- The five-row ratio ledger should build one row at a time, each with a brief pause before the next

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the ratio bar graphics and
  the closing five-row ledger
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the Virgin Orbit ratio reveal and the
  Friendster framing card — no camera movement competing with either for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- Quibi/Bird/Virgin Orbit/Katerra "peak" segments: a brief confident lift
- Each "what sold" ratio reveal: a sharp, dramatic downward swell — the inverse of a triumphant sting
- Friendster "not a bankruptcy" framing card: measured, distinct from the other four segments' tone
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout
- A sharp "deflating" or descending SFX synced to each ratio bar graphic's collapse animation
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on each "SOLD FOR" dollar-figure reveal
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured and analytical throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the five-row ratio ledger's final beat in The Actual Lesson

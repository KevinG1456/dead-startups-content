# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. The comparator format and concrete
dollar-figure hooks give this package strong standalone potential; no urgency premium, hold for the
best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Broad crossover: founders/investors studying distressed-asset and liquidation valuation, general
audience drawn to concrete numbers behind "the company went bankrupt" headlines, and existing viewers
of this channel's Quibi (002), Bird (028), Virgin Orbit (031), Bridge Round (034), and Friendster (040)
packages looking for the "what happened after" follow-up.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist and to "Money Mechanics" alongside packages 007, 015, 025, and 034
— this channel's established multi-company comparator format.

## End Screen
Package 034 (Bridge Round Death Spiral) + package 031 (Virgin Orbit) + subscribe — both are the
closest thematic matches (financing distress and one shared company, respectively).

## Cards
Card at the Virgin Orbit ratio reveal (~9:00) linking to package 031 for viewers who want the full
individual story; card at the mid-video CTA (~9:00) reinforcing the comparator framing; card at the
Friendster framing note (~9:15) linking to package 040 for the full declined-Google-offer story.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next comparator in the library — and if you know another startup whose
asset sale belongs in this lineup, tell me in the comments, I want to build a follow-up. One more
thing: which of these five ratios surprised you the most?"

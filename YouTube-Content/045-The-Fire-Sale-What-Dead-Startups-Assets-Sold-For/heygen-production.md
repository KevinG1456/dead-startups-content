# HeyGen Production
Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and analytical throughout; a sharp lift at each "what sold" ratio reveal
  (especially Virgin Orbit's ~1%); a distinct tonal shift into careful precision at the Friendster
  "not a bankruptcy" framing card; steady through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, deliberately slower on every ratio reveal so the percentage
  has room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** dry, analytical irony on the ratio reveals — not mocking any company or founder;
  genuine care and precision on the Friendster framing distinction
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine surprise at each ratio-bar reveal;
  visible precision when reading the Friendster "not a bankruptcy" framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the bar-
  graphic reveals and the closing ratio ledger
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

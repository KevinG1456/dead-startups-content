# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Never cram all five companies into
one thumbnail; pick the single sharpest ratio (Virgin Orbit's ~1%) or the single sharpest irony
(Friendster's patents outselling the company) as the dominant visual idea.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a tall "PEAK VALUE" bar collapsing into a tiny "SOLD FOR" bar beside it, drawn to
  scale so the disproportion is immediately visible without reading any text
- **Main subject:** the scale of the gap between peak valuation and actual sale price
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint auction-gavel/ledger-line texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the tiny "SOLD FOR" bar
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** tall bar on the left, tiny bar on the right, bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic bar-chart graphics, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `$3.7 BILLION. SOLD FOR $36 MILLION.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** tall bar in muted gold, tiny bar in sharp red, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the sheer height mismatch between the two bars communicates the entire thesis
  before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render real, identifiable photorealistic likenesses of any named individual (Katzenberg,
  Whitman, VanderZanden, Branson, Dan Hart, Jonathan Abrams, Michael Marks) in any thumbnail — use
  generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real company logos/wordmarks (Quibi, Roku, Bird, Virgin Orbit, Friendster, Facebook, MOL
  Global, Katerra, SoftBank) in generated thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add
  any verified real branding in the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not cram all five companies' names or figures into one thumbnail — pick the single sharpest ratio
  or irony as the dominant idea, per this channel's thumbnail-simplicity rule

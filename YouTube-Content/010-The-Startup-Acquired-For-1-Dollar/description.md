## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct startup outcomes using primary sources. This week: not a failure story exactly, but an explainer on one of the most misused words in the startup world — "acquired."

Acquisitions span a real spectrum: from strong, life-changing exits for founders and employees, to acqui-hires (where the acquiring company mainly wants the team, not the product), to asset sales that are functionally a shutdown dressed up in a press release. We walk through this spectrum using real, documented examples — including a company that went from a reported $850 million valuation to selling for a small fraction of that years later — and give you a practical framework for telling the difference the next time you see an "acquired" headline.

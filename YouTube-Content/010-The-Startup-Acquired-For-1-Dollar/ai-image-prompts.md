# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, real company logos, or real people's likenesses — add verified text/imagery in the editor.

## Thumbnail Prompts
**Prompt A:** "YouTube thumbnail, the word 'ACQUIRED' in large bold quotation marks with a subtle glitch/distortion effect, centered on a dark background, clean heavy sans-serif style, minimal, flat lighting, high contrast, empty space at bottom for title text, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt B (recommended primary):** "YouTube thumbnail, a large bold dollar-figure text at the top with a red strikethrough line through it, a much smaller dollar-figure text below it, dark background, flat lighting, high contrast, empty space at bottom third for title text, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt C:** "YouTube thumbnail, tight three-quarter crop portrait, dramatic single top-down light, knowing explaining expression, dark background, small tag graphic with a question mark in the corner, photorealistic, cinematic, high contrast, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

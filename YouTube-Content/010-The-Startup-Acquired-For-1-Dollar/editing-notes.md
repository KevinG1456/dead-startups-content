# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the valuation-collapse reveal moment

## Motion Graphics
- Struck-through large valuation figure collapsing into a small figure, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Acquisition-spectrum graphic (Strong Exit → Soft Landing → Acqui-Hire → Asset Sale → Shutdown) building progressively as each category is explained
- "Questions to ask" checklist graphic for the practical employee-focused segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a valuation figure, date, or quote is referenced
- Category label lower third (e.g., "Acqui-Hire") whenever a new spectrum category is introduced

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or archival stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the spectrum-graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Explainer/spectrum section: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat to keep an educational (not doom-laden) tone
- Case study section: matches the specific valuation-collapse story's emotional arc — builds then drops
- Practical/employee-advice section: clear, helpful, slightly warmer tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Sharp "record scratch" or deflate SFX on the valuation-collapse reveal
- Soft "click" SFX as each spectrum category populates on the graphic
- Avoid comedic stingers in the case-study segment specifically — respectful tone toward the real company/founder involved

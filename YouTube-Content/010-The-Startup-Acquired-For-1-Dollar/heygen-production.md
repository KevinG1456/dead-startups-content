# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled, educational, slightly more upbeat than the pure-tragedy autopsy titles since this is framed as a useful explainer, not solely a cautionary tale
- **Speaking pace:** measured for the definitions/spectrum sections, slightly quicker and more energetic during the practical checklist section
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority throughout; genuine helpfulness during the practical advice segments
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; warmer, more direct during the employee/founder advice segment
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled; a small consistent gesture pointing along the spectrum graphic when referencing specific categories
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (struck-through valuation animation)
- Script: "What does 'acquired' actually mean, and why doesn't it mean the same thing every time?"
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — The Acquisition Spectrum**
- Duration: 0:15–1:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, spectrum graphic animates in beside them
- Performance direction: clear, teaching tone
- Visual direction: medium shot, graphic inset

**Scene 03 — Case Study**
- Duration: 1:30–4:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera transitioning to B-roll (press headlines, valuation timeline)
- Performance direction: narrative, building curiosity
- Visual direction: full-screen B-roll with avatar PiP during longer VO stretches

**Scene 04 — What Acqui-Hires Actually Are**
- Duration: 4:00–6:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with definition graphics
- Performance direction: precise, clear, deliberate on definitions
- Visual direction: medium shot, definition graphics inset

**Scene 05 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~6:00 (inside Scene 04) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into teaching tone
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly

**Scene 06 — Why This Matters**
- Duration: 6:30–9:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct-to-camera practical advice
- Performance direction: warmer, more direct and helpful tone — this is the most actionable segment of the video
- Visual direction: minimal graphics, checklist bullets appear beside avatar as each point is made

**Scene 07 — How to Tell the Difference**
- Duration: 9:00–10:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with the finalized spectrum/checklist graphic
- Performance direction: confident, synthesizing tone — the payoff moment
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic reveal with avatar PiP

**Scene 08 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 10:30–11:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: steady, direct eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements animate in during final 5 seconds only

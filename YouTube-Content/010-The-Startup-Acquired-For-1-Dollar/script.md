## Complete Viral Script (Main Cut)

**Target length:** 10–13 minutes

### Cold Open (0:00–0:15)
- VO: "What does 'acquired' actually mean, and why doesn't it mean the same thing every time?"
- Visual: large struck-through dollar figure, small figure appears beneath it with a heavy SFX hit
- Pattern Interrupt: hard cut to silence, white text on black: "This happened to the same company. Twice."

### The Acquisition Spectrum (0:15–1:30)
- VO introduces the spectrum: Strong Exit → Soft Landing → Acqui-Hire → Asset Sale → Shutdown
- Visual: spectrum graphic builds as each category is briefly defined
- Open loop: "By the end of this video, you'll be able to spot exactly where any acquisition headline actually falls."

### Case Study: The Valuation Collapse (1:30–4:00)
- Introduce the anchor example: the reported $850M acquisition, and its dramatically smaller resale years later
- Visual: valuation timeline graphic, press headlines from both transactions side by side
- Curiosity gap: tease why the value collapsed so dramatically without fully explaining it yet

### Main Content — What Acqui-Hires Actually Are (4:00–6:30)
- Segment 1: define acqui-hire precisely — the acquiring company primarily wants the team, product often discontinued shortly after
- Segment 2: why companies structure deals this way, and why "undisclosed terms" often signals a below-market outcome
- Mid-video CTA (~6:00): "If you're evaluating a job offer or your own company just got acquired, the next part is exactly what you need. Subscribe for a new autopsy every week."

### Why This Matters (6:30–9:00)
- For employees: what to actually ask/check when your company is acquired (does the product continue, what happens to equity, is there a retention package or just an offer letter)
- For founders: how to negotiate and present acquisition terms honestly

### How to Tell the Difference (9:00–10:30)
- Practical checklist: product continuation, team retention terms, disclosed vs. undisclosed terms, investor return language
- Visual: checklist graphic finalizes, spectrum graphic reappears with the case study now placed precisely on it

### Final CTA + Outro (10:30–11:30)
- VO: recap the one-sentence lesson — "acquired" is a headline, not an outcome; always ask what specifically happened
- "Subscribe — I'm building a full library of these"
- Engagement prompt: "Have you been through an acqui-hire? Tell me how it actually went in the comments."
- End screen: two related autopsies + subscribe

## Alternative Scripts

### Script Version A — Data-First / Analyst Style
Opens directly on the finished spectrum graphic with the case study already placed on it, then rewinds to explain the valuation history and the definitions. Measured, graphics-forward.

### Script Version B — Narrative / Documentary Style
Opens on the human moment — an employee's account of finding out what their company's "acquisition" actually meant for them — before any numbers appear. Slower pacing, cinematic.

### Script Version C — Fast-Cut / Listicle Style
Structured as "5 acquisition headlines, ranked from best to worst outcome," using the spectrum as a countdown format. Strong Shorts-extraction potential.

---

## Audience Retention Optimization
- **Drop-off risk:** Main Content section (4:00–6:30) if the acqui-hire definition becomes overly abstract
- **Fix:** ground the definition in the case study's specifics rather than staying purely conceptual
- **Faster pacing opportunity:** trim the spectrum-intro section if the five categories can be conveyed more quickly
- **Curiosity increase opportunity:** move the "why the value collapsed" tease earlier if early-retention data shows drop-off before 2:00
- **Recommended visual change:** show the spectrum graphic with an unmarked "?" placeholder for the case study in the cold open, revealing its exact position only at the end

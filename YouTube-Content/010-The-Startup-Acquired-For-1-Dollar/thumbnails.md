# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the valuation-collapse contradiction, not the full spectrum explainer.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large dollar figure crossed out/struck through, with a much smaller dollar figure beneath it
- **Main subject:** the before/after valuation contrast
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** large struck-through figure top, small figure bottom, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** simple strikethrough line graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `"ACQUIRED" ISN'T ALWAYS A WIN`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green large figure (struck through in red), small red figure below, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the scale collapse between the two numbers does the communicating

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** the word "ACQUIRED" in large bold quotation marks, subtly distorted/glitched
- **Text:** `WHAT THIS WORD IS HIDING`
- **Why it works:** treating the word itself as the visual subject signals "this common term isn't what you think," driving curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** high — novel typographic treatment stands out
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the struck-through-vs-small-figure combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$850M → $1M`
- **Why it works:** the arrow-and-numbers format communicates collapse instantly, no text explanation needed
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), knowing/explaining expression, small "ACQUIRED" tag with a question mark in the corner
- **Text:** `WHAT "ACQUIRED" REALLY MEANS`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; phrase signals an educational reveal
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

| Version | Emotion | CTR Strategy | Best Audience |

| C | Authority | Face-led, educational-reveal framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. The practical/explainer nature of this title gives it strong evergreen search potential, so exact publish timing matters less than for news-driven content.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) plus a broader audience of startup employees currently evaluating equity/offers or acquisition news.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; consider a dedicated "Startup Explainers" or "Founder Toolkit" playlist alongside other mechanism/explainer-style videos (pairs well with Title 003's platform-risk explainer and Title 007's YC audit).

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + highest-performing prior autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the acquisition-spectrum graphic reveal linking to a related explainer video; card at the practical employee-advice segment linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy — or the next explainer like this one — subscribe. And if your company's been through this, tell me about it in the comments."

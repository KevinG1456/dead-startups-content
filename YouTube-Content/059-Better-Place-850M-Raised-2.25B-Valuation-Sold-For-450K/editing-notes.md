# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the three-date cold-open
  reveal ($2.25B valuation → liquidation filed → $450,000 sale)

## Motion Graphics
- Three-date timeline animation for the cold open ("NOV 2011 — $2.25B VALUATION" → "MAY 2013 —
  LIQUIDATION FILED" → "NOV 2013 — SOLD FOR $450,000")
- Robotic battery-swap-station animation for the founding/idea segment (generic, no real branding)
- Funding-round timeline graphic building toward "approximately $850M," with an explicit "not fully
  reconciled" callout distinguishing individually disclosed rounds (~$686M) from the commonly cited
  headline total
- Hub-and-spoke single-partner-dependency diagram — one car, one manufacturer, several other
  manufacturer icons greyed out and disconnected around it — this recurs at least twice (Segment 3
  and the final-lesson segment) to keep the dependency thesis visually anchored
- Israel/Denmark map graphic for the launch-markets segment
- Swap-station cost-overrun bar comparison ($500K budgeted vs. $2M actual)
- "$6.9M in sales / $459M lost" side-by-side graphic, clearly labeled "per PrivCo reporting" — no
  exceptions, this label must appear every time this figure is shown
- Leadership-change graphic with an explicit "$490M lost since founding — as of Oct 2012" callout,
  time-stamped clearly to avoid confusion with the later, larger bankruptcy-filing figure
- Israeli court filing graphic, "$850M raised → more than $800M lost" callout
- "DEAL 1 — $12M — CANCELED" graphic crossed out, followed by "DEAL 2 — $450,000 — CLOSED" as the
  actual ending — this two-stage reveal is the single most important sequencing beat in the video
- Three-number arc graphic ($850M → $2.25B → $450K) recurring throughout Segments 4 through 8 and
  the final-lesson segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, Fast
  Company, Quartz, MIT Technology Review, GreenCarReports, Times of Israel, Global Venturing, Gas 2,
  Green Prophet)
- "Approximately $850 million — not a single reconciled total" as a standing lower-third phrasing
  whenever total lifetime funding is referenced
- "Per PrivCo reporting — not independently verified against audited filings" lower third every
  single time the 2012 revenue/loss figures are shown — no exceptions
- "This deal was later canceled — it never closed" lower third during the Sunrise Group segment,
  timed to land clearly before the real Gnrgy sale is revealed
- "No fraud or criminal allegation in this story" lower third paired with the cold-open pattern
  interrupt

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static generic EV/battery-station imagery (no real readable Better Place
  or Renault logos/branding without rights clearance)
- The hub-and-spoke dependency graphic animates the single connected spoke in first, then the greyed-
  out disconnected spokes fade in around it
- The three-number arc animates as a continuous falling sequence ($850M → $2.25B → $450K), not a hard
  cut, to emphasize the scale of the collapse

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the dependency diagram and the
  two-stage deal-reveal graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founder and the Idea: bright, hopeful, building energy (this idea genuinely could have worked —
  the music should reflect real optimism before the collapse)
- The Raise and the Backers / The Single-Partner Dependency: brisk, confident tone shifting to
  slightly uneasy under the dependency reveal
- Building the Network: legitimacy-signaling tone
- The Real Numbers: quiet, even, deliberately unsensational — no ominous "scandal" stinger, this must
  not sound like a true-crime cue given the PrivCo attribution requirement
- The Ouster / The Liquidation: measured, somber
- Two Sales, Only One Real: sharp tonal shift at the "canceled" reveal, resolving into a quieter,
  more final tone for the real $450,000 sale
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly reflective/hopeful educational tone — acknowledge the
  idea was genuinely real without softening the outcome
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each of the three cold-open date reveals
- Sharp "crash" SFX as the valuation-collapse numeral falls from $2.25B to $450K
- Soft "click" SFX as each spoke locks into place in the dependency diagram
- A distinct "canceled" stamp SFX on the Sunrise Group deal reveal, followed by a quieter, more final
  tone on the real Gnrgy sale reveal
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and precise, especially during the
  Real Numbers segment, which must never read as implying fraud or wrongdoing

# Shorts Ideas (10)

Note: keep every Short below to ironclad, well-corroborated facts — the approximate $850M raise, the
$2.25B peak valuation, the $450,000 final sale price, and the canceled $12M deal (clearly labeled as
canceled). Never use "fraud" language anywhere. Never state the PrivCo-sourced 2012 revenue/loss
figures without attributing them to PrivCo specifically. Never present the deployed-vehicle count as
one precise number — use the hedged range.

**1. "Raised $850 Million. Sold For $450,000."**
Hook: "This company raised approximately $850 million and reached a $2.25 billion valuation. It sold
for $450,000." Script: quick three-number arc reveal. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**2. "$2.25 Billion. Then $450,000."**
Hook: "This company's valuation went from $2.25 billion to a $450,000 final sale price." Script:
30-second valuation-collapse numeral reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**3. "One Car. One Automaker. That Was the Whole Business."**
Hook: "This company's entire network depended on exactly one car, from exactly one automaker. No
second manufacturer ever signed on." Script: quick single-partner-dependency explainer. CTA: "Full
story on the channel."

**4. "The $12 Million Deal That Never Happened"**
Hook: "This company had a deal to sell its remains for $12 million. The buyer never made the first
payment. Here's what it actually sold for instead." Script: canceled-deal-then-real-sale reveal. CTA:
"Full breakdown linked."

**5. "Budgeted $500,000. Cost $2 Million."**
Hook: "This company's robotic stations were supposed to cost $500,000 each. They cost about $2
million each." Script: quick infrastructure-cost-overrun explainer. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**6. "He Could Have Run SAP. He Built This Instead."**
Hook: "This founder was reportedly in line for a future CEO role at SAP. He left to build a battery-
swap network for electric cars instead." Script: quick founder-background reveal. CTA: "Full
breakdown linked."

**7. "Not Fraud. Still One of the Biggest Failures in Israeli Tech History."**
Hook: "No one was ever criminally charged in this story. It's still one of the biggest startup
failures in Israeli history." Script: careful, plainly-stated framing explainer. CTA: "Full story on
the channel."

**8. "Fewer Than 1,500 Cars, Nearly a Billion Dollars Raised"**
Hook: "This company raised close to a billion dollars. It deployed fewer than 1,500 cars." Script:
quick raise-vs-deployment contrast. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**9. "The Company That Tried Battery Swapping First"**
Hook: "Before battery swapping came back in a different form years later, this company tried it
first — and lost almost a billion dollars." Script: quick "ahead of its time" framing, careful not to
overclaim any specific successor company. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**10. "Two Countries Launched It. It Still Wasn't Enough."**
Hook: "This company launched in two countries, built real infrastructure, and still ran out of road."
Script: quick Israel/Denmark launch recap into collapse. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

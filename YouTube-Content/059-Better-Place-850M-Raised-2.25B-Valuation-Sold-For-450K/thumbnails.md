# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule, each thumbnail carries exactly
ONE idea, not the whole video. Do not try to cram the funding history, the Renault partnership, the
ouster, AND the final sale into a single image — those live in the video, not the thumbnail. Do not
render Better Place's real logo, the Renault name/logo, or any other real trademarked brand element
in generated thumbnail art — attribute those verbally and on-screen inside the video itself, with
proper sourcing, not in AI-generated thumbnail imagery.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a single generic EV battery pack mid-swap at a robotic station, rendered as a
  clean icon/silhouette, with a bold numeral treatment showing the fall from a large "$2.25B" to a
  small "$450K"
- **Main subject:** the raise-to-sale-price collapse — the sharpest, most surprising verified number
  contrast in the whole package
- **Emotion:** shock / disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint circuit/battery-cell texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the battery-swap icon
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** battery/swap icon centered, numeral treatment above it, title-safe space reserved
  at the bottom third
- **Props:** generic EV battery pack icon, robotic swap-arm silhouette (no real branding)
- **Thumbnail text:** `$2.25B → $450,000.`
- **Text placement:** top third above the icon, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** white/gray icon, single red accent on the falling numeral, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the numeral collapse alone communicates "this went catastrophically wrong"
  before any other detail is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render Better Place's real logo, wordmark, the Renault name/logo, or any other real
  trademarked brand element in generated thumbnail art — attribute those verbally and on-screen
  inside the video with proper sourcing, not in AI-generated imagery
- Do not attempt a single thumbnail that covers the funding history, the Renault partnership, the
  ouster, AND the final sale — that's the video's content; the thumbnail sells one specific irony
  at a time
- Do not state the canceled $12 million Sunrise Group deal figure on any thumbnail — that deal never
  closed; the real, final sale price is $450,000, and using the wrong figure would misrepresent the
  ending
- Do not use "FRAUD" or any fraud-implying language on any thumbnail text — no fraud or criminal
  allegation exists anywhere in this story

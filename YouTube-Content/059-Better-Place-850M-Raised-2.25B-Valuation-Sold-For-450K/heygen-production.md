# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the founding/raise segments
  and a clear educational register during the single-partner-dependency and infrastructure-cost
  explainers
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the Real Numbers segment (PrivCo
  attribution) and the two-stage deal-reveal segment — both need room to land clearly and to be
  attributed precisely, on pace with the on-screen lower thirds
- **Emotional tone:** genuinely hopeful/confident during the founding and idea segments (the concept
  was real and the technology worked — this should read honestly, not cynically); patient,
  matter-of-fact teaching tone for the dependency and cost-overrun explainers; measured and precise
  during the Real Numbers segment — never sensational; sharper focus through the ouster, liquidation,
  and two-stage sale sequence
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; deliberate care and slight slowing when stating
  "this deal was later canceled — it never closed" and "per PrivCo reporting, not independently
  verified" — these lines need to read as careful precision, not as a hedge being rushed past
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  hub-and-spoke dependency diagram and the two-stage deal-reveal graphic
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (three-date timeline animation: $2.25B valuation →

  $450,000. In between, it raised approximately $850 million, and it still wasn't enough."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–0:55 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: warm, genuine on "tried to solve one of the real, structural problems with
  electric cars"; flat and serious on the dependency line
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 0:55–1:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: deliberate, careful pace on "not a fraud story — no individual has been
  criminally charged"; brief tonal lift on the Bird/Virgin Orbit cross-reference line
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The Founder and the Idea**
- Duration: 1:45–3:00 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (founder-era imagery, robotic swap-station

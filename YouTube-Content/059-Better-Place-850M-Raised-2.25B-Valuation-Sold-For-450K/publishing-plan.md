# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. No urgent news-cycle pressure, so no
need to rush publication ahead of the normal weekly cadence — good candidate to slot near this
channel's other hardware/infrastructure moonshot packages (028/Bird, 031/Virgin Orbit) for
cross-referencing and playlist cohesion, even though it isn't part of the same batch.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) with particular relevance to anyone evaluating
capital-intensive infrastructure or single-partner-dependency business models, plus a cleantech/EV-
history-adjacent audience interested in an early, well-funded attempt at battery-swap technology.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to a "Hardware & Infrastructure Moonshots" playlist
alongside 028 (Bird) and 031 (Virgin Orbit); also consider a "Cleantech / EV" playlist if the channel
builds one out further.

## End Screen
Most recent hardware/infrastructure autopsy (028/Bird or 031/Virgin Orbit); subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the single-partner-dependency diagram (Segment 3) linking to the "Hardware & Infrastructure
Moonshots" playlist; card at the mid-video CTA linking directly to 028 (Bird) or 031 (Virgin Orbit).

## End CTA
"If you want to see this channel's other hardware and infrastructure bets, watch the Bird or Virgin
Orbit autopsies — different mechanisms, same underlying lesson about capital-intensive physical
businesses. And subscribe if you want the next one in the library. If your business depends on one
exclusive partner saying yes, forever, that's worth stress-testing before it's tested for you."

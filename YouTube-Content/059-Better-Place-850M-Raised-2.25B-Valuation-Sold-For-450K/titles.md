# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Better Place: founded 2007 by Shai Agassi (former SAP executive), a battery-swap network
for electric cars that raised approximately $850 million (Israel Corp, HSBC, General Electric,
Morgan Stanley), reached a peak $2.25 billion valuation in Nov 2011, depended entirely on one
exclusive automaker partner (Renault's purpose-built Fluence Z.E.), deployed fewer than 1,500
vehicles across Israel and Denmark, filed for liquidation in Israel May 26, 2013 after losing more
than $800 million, and — after a $12 million sale deal fell through — was finally sold for just
$450,000 in November 2013. No fraud or criminal allegations anywhere in this story.

## Recommended Title
**"This Company Raised $850 Million. It Sold For $450,000."**

Reasoning: leads with the two best-corroborated, most citable figures in the entire sources-log —
the approximate lifetime funding total and the final, real sale price — without needing to invent
or exaggerate anything, and without naming the company (which has lower baseline recognition than
this channel's famous-brand packages, so a pure numbers hook outperforms a name-led one here). Works
fully truncated on mobile, sets up both the peak-valuation beat and the single-automaker-dependency
mechanism without overpromising.

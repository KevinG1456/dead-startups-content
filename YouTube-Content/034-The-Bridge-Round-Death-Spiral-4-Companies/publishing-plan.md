# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. This package's comparator format (four
companies) has established precedent on this channel (007, 015, 025) and should perform on pattern-
recognition appeal even though individual company name recognition is modest — no urgency premium,
hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience: founders/operators currently in or approaching distress financing decisions, and
investors studying cross-industry failure patterns. Broader appeal than a single-company niche package
due to the comparator format itself creating curiosity ("will the pattern really repeat four times?").

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to the cap-table/financing-mechanics playlist alongside
package 018 (Down Rounds), package 032 (Practice Fusion), and package 033 (Quixey) — this is the
fourth entry in that mini-series and the first multi-company one.

## End Screen
Package 033 (Quixey) + package 032 (Practice Fusion) + subscribe element — both are the channel's
other cap-table/financing-mechanics stories, making a natural continuation for viewers who finish this
one.

## Cards

companion financing-mechanics story; card at the mid-video CTA (~11:00) linking to the cap-table
mechanics playlist; optional third card at the start of The Actual Lesson (~14:00) linking directly to
a pinned comment or community post with the sourced four-column timeline graphic.

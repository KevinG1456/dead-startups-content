# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open cracked-bridge reveal,
  and on each company's single most dramatic title card ($200M×2, the failed bridge loan, the SPAC
  collapse, $523,000)
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on The Actual Lesson's four-column side-by-side timeline reveal

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open cracked bridge icon with four small building/company icons falling into darkness beneath
  it
- "This isn't a coincidence. It's a pattern. Watch it repeat four times." pattern-interrupt card,
  white text on black
- Running "bridge #1 / #2 / #3 / #4" corner counter, advancing at the start of each company segment
- Katerra: "$2 BILLION RAISED" → "RESCUE #1 — $200M — MAY 2020" → "RESCUE #2 — $200M — DECEMBER 2020"
  → "CHAPTER 11 — JUNE 7, 2021" title card sequence
- Veev: "UNICORN STATUS — MARCH 2022" → "BRIDGE LOAN ATTEMPTED — LATE 2023" with a red X stamp →
  "SHUTDOWN — NOVEMBER 27, 2023" title card sequence
- Plastiq: "$940M VALUATION — JANUARY 2022" → "SPAC DEAL COLLAPSES" → "SOLD FOR $27.5M" title card
  sequence
- Enjoy Technology: "SPAC COMPLETE — OCTOBER 2021" → "$523,000 IN THE BANK" → "SOLD TO ASURION — $110M"
  title card sequence
- Four-column side-by-side timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson, each company's distress-to-bridge-
  to-outcome sequence shown in parallel, built up one column at a time

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Bloomberg, CNBC,
  TechCrunch, Construction Dive, Forbes, Bloomberg Law, The Information, Retail Dive)
- A distinct standing tag per company's bridge type, shown once per segment: "REPEAT EQUITY RESCUE"
  (Katerra), "FAILED BRIDGE LOAN" (Veev), "SPAC AS BRIDGE" (Plastiq), "BUYER-SUPPLIED BRIDGE" (Enjoy)
- "No fraud or criminal allegations in this story" note available for the intro if needed to set tone

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/construction/retail imagery — no
  real readable logos/trademarks (Katerra, Veev, Plastiq, Enjoy Technology, SoftBank, Asurion) without
  rights clearance
- Each company segment should end with its title card literally "falling" off screen into the cracked-
  bridge graphic from the cold open, reinforcing the shared visual motif before cutting to the next
  company
- The four-column timeline in The Actual Lesson should build one column at a time, each column landing
  with the same falling-off-the-bridge motion, so the pattern reads as visually identical across all
  four

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for each company's title-card
  sequence and the four-column timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during each company's single most dramatic reveal line

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- Each company's "Rise" beat: measured, building energy, distinct instrumentation per company to help
  differentiate segments (e.g., a slightly different tonal palette for construction/proptech/fintech/
  retail) while keeping the same overall tension arc
- Each company's "bridge attempt" beat: uneasy, building tension
- Each company's "collapse/shutdown" beat: quiet, restrained — no melodrama, consistent across all four
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout, across all four companies
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on Katerra's two $200M reveals specifically, to reinforce the repeat-
  rescue pattern
- Sharp, decisive "stamp" or "crack" SFX on each company's bridge-failure moment (the red X on Veev's
  bridge loan, the SPAC-collapse card, etc.)
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays analytical and precise throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the four-column timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

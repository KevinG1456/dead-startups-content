## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **Four Companies. Four "One Last Raise" Stories. All Four Ended the Same Way.** — states the
   comparator format plainly, matches the batch title's own framing, sets clear viewer expectations
2. This Company Got Rescued Twice by the Same Investor. It Still Died. — the single most dramatic
   individual fact (Katerra), strong as a cold-open hook before revealing the comparator format
3. This Company Filed for Bankruptcy With $523,000 in the Bank. — the most viscerally specific number
   in the whole sources-log (Enjoy), strong shock value
4. The Company That Bought the Wreckage Was the Same Company That "Rescued" It. — the sharpest irony
   across two of the four companies (Enjoy/Asurion, Plastiq/Priority), strong curiosity hook
5. $2 Billion Raised. Two Emergency Rescues. Still Bankrupt. — pure number-shock framing (Katerra)
6. A SPAC Deal Was Supposed to Be This Company's Last Raise. It Fell Apart Instead. — strong for the
   Plastiq segment specifically, sets up the SPAC-as-bridge angle
7. This Startup Tried to Raise a Bridge Loan. Nobody Would Give It One. — clean, direct (Veev)
8. Four Industries. Four Companies. The Exact Same Financing Mistake. — strong pattern-recognition
   framing, good mid-funnel search title
9. Bridge Round Explained With 4 Real Companies — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
10. What Happened to Katerra? The Full Story — strong secondary search title for the single most
    dramatic individual company

## Recommended Title
**"The Bridge Round Death Spiral: 4 Companies, 4 'One Last Raise' Stories."**

Reasoning: keeps the batch's own title language, which is already clear and accurately describes the
comparator format viewers will get — four distinct, real, cross-industry proof points of the same
financing pattern. Sets expectations correctly (a multi-company comparator, not a single-company deep
dive), consistent with how this channel's other comparator packages (007, 015, 025) are titled. Works
fully truncated on mobile at the first clause alone. Matches the title already used in this package's
README.

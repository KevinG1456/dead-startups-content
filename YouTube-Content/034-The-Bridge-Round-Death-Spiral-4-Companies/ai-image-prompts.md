# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Katerra, Veev, Plastiq,
Enjoy Technology, SoftBank, Asurion, Priority Technology, Colonnade), and no photorealistic likeness of
any real named person (Michael Marks, Amit Haller, Eliot Buchanan, Dan Choi, Ron Johnson) — add
verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor with proper sourcing, not
generated.

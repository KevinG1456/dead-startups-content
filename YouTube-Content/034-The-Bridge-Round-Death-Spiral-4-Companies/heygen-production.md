# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and building through each company's "Rise" beat; tense through each
  "bridge attempt" beat; quiet and restrained through each "collapse/shutdown" beat; reflective through
  The Actual Lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during each company's single most important
  framing card (Katerra's "two rescues, a third bankruptcy-only loan, still gone," Veev's "a
  specifically named bridge loan," Plastiq's "a public-market bridge," Enjoy's "the buyer as the
  bridge")
- **Emotional tone:** dry, document-forward through each financing-terms explanation — never
  sensationalized; matter-of-fact through each rise/climb sequence; restrained dread through each
  bridge-failure reveal; quiet respect, no melodrama, through each shutdown/sale segment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine dry disbelief at Katerra's second
  rescue and at Enjoy's $523,000 balance; visible care and precision when reading each company's
  bridge-mechanism framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during each
  company's title-card sequence and the four-column timeline in The Actual Lesson
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (cracked-bridge graphic reveal)
- Script: "Four companies. Four different industries. Every single one tried the same move when
  things got bad — one more round, framed as a bridge to survival. None of them made it across."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–1:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: clear, measured delivery establishing all four companies and industries in
  sequence
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 1:00–1:40 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: deliberately clear on the "watch how differently each bridge is structured,
  and how identically each one ends" framing line
- Visual direction: medium shot

  quiet gravity at the Chapter 11 reveal
- Visual direction: sequential title cards as described in editing-notes.md

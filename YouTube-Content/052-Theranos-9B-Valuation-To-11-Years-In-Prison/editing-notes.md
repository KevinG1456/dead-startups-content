# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the split-verdict
  "CONVICTED / ACQUITTED" reveal and the "NOT A BANKRUPTCY FILING" correction card
- No snap-zoom, no punch-in, no emphasis camera move of any kind during the pending-petition
  text-card sequence — keep that segment visually flat and static, consistent with the neutral tone
  required

## Motion Graphics
- Verdict-sheet graphic animation for the cold open, checkmarks and X's animating in one at a time
- Founder graphic and age-19 callout for the founding segment
- Investor/board name graphic and "$9,000,000,000" large-numeral build for the money/board segment
- Split-icon graphic (claimed technology vs. actual process) for the technology segment, held on
  screen for the full "MOST TESTS: NOT RUN ON THE MACHINE YOU WERE TOLD" beat
- Store-rollout map graphic and "IMMEDIATE JEOPARDY TO PATIENT HEALTH AND SAFETY" direct-quote card
  for the partnerships segment
- WSJ headline treatment for the October 2015 exposé beat
- "CIVIL CASE" large-label graphic with settlement-terms checklist for the SEC segment — visually
  distinct treatment from the later "CRIMINAL CASE" material so the two never blur together
- "NOT A BANKRUPTCY FILING" correction card and $0 shareholder-distribution graphic for the
  dissolution segment
- Two-column "CONVICTED / ACQUITTED" graphic for Holmes; side-by-side "12 OF 12" stat card for
  Balwani — always shown distinctly, never implying the two verdicts were identical in scope
- Sentence-length bar comparison (Holmes 135 months vs. Balwani 155 months) and restitution
  breakdown graphic ($452M total, named recipients)
- Pending-petition text-card sequence: plain typography only — "2025: A CLEMENCY PETITION WAS
  FILED" / "AS OF JANUARY 2026: STILL PENDING" / "NO DECISION HAS BEEN MADE" — no political imagery,
  no stylistic flourish that could read as hopeful or dismissive
- Three-case comparison graphic (HeadSpin / Ozy Media / Theranos) — conviction, sentence, current
  status
- "Autopsy Report Card" end-graphic (full timeline: Founding → Peak Valuation → Exposé → SEC Case →
  Dissolution → Conviction → Sentence → Pending Petition)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or claim is referenced (SEC press release/
  litigation release, DOJ press release, Wall Street Journal, CBS News, NBC News, CNN, Fortune)
- "PER HOLMES'S OWN TRIAL TESTIMONY — DENIED BY BALWANI" lower third every time the abuse-allegation
  detail appears — no exceptions, must appear for the full duration of that beat
- "CIVIL SETTLEMENT — NOT A CRIMINAL CONVICTION" standing lower third during the SEC segment; "ACTUAL
  JURY CONVICTION" standing lower third during the criminal-case segment — never let either segment
  play without its distinguishing label
- Neutral "2025 Petition Filed — Status: Pending (as of Jan 2026)" lower third during the
  pending-petition segment — no additional editorial text appended

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic clinical/courthouse imagery (no real
  readable logos/text — Theranos, Walgreens, Safeway — without rights clearance, and no real photos
  of Elizabeth Holmes, Sunny Balwani, or any political figure without proper rights clearance)
- The "NOT A BANKRUPTCY FILING" correction beat gets a distinct, informative visual treatment —
  different pacing than the rest of the video, since it's a genuine "wait, that's not what happened"
  moment
- The pending-petition text cards animate in and hold — no bounce, no dramatic sting, no reveal
  flourish; treat it visually the same as any other neutral date-stamp moment in the video

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the timeline graphic and
  the three-case comparison graphic layers; avatar frame is fully static during the pending-petition
  segment specifically

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founding / The Money and the Board: neutral, brisk, building energy — no ominous foreshadowing
  yet
- What the Technology Actually Did: subtly tense under the reveal, no comedic stinger
- The Partnerships: quiet, somber
- The Exposé: a brief tonal lift on "this is where it starts to unravel," then serious
- The SEC's Civil Case / The Company Ends: sparse, clinical, matter-of-fact — let the graphics carry
  the information, not the score
- The Criminal Case and the Split Verdict / Sentencing: quiet, no triumphant swell
- Prison, and What's Happening Right Now: silence or a single sustained low tone — no music that
  could read as either hopeful or dismissive; this is the one segment in the video where the safest
  choice is the most neutral one
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly reflective educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout the timeline
- Distinct "correction" SFX (a soft record-scratch or reverse-whoosh, non-comedic) on the "NOT A
  BANKRUPTCY FILING" beat only
- Gavel-strike SFX at the split-verdict reveal
- No SFX at all during the pending-petition text-card sequence — let it land in silence
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and even-handed, especially during the
  abuse-allegation attribution beat and the pending-petition segment, neither of which should ever
  read as mocking, gleeful, or one-sided

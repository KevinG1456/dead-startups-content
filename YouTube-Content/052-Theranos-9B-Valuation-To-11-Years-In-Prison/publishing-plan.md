## Moderation Notes (specific to this package)
- Expect comments about the pending clemency petition to skew politically charged regardless of how
  neutrally the video presents it — this is anticipated, not a sign the framing failed
- Do not respond to comments, in the creator's own voice or in pinned replies, with any political
  commentary or speculation about whether the petition will be granted, in either direction; if
  replying at all, restate only the sourced facts (a petition was filed in 2025, it's pending as of
  the video's build date, no decision has been made)
- Do not pin or highlight comments that speculate about or editorialize on the petition's likely
  outcome or political dimension, even ones the creator personally agrees with — keep the
  pinned-comment section as factual as the video itself
- If the petition is decided after publication (granted, denied, or otherwise resolved), this video's
  pinned comment and description should be updated with a dated factual correction, sourced to
  contemporaneous reporting, the same standard applied to any other correction on this channel
- Expect some comments to reference Holmes's own trial-testimony allegations against Balwani — do not
  amplify or restate these beyond what the video already attributes explicitly as her testimony, not
  settled fact

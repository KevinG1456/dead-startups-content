# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Theranos: founded 2003 by 19-year-old Elizabeth Holmes, raised $700 million+ (SEC's own
figure), peaked at a $9 billion valuation (2013-2014) on the claim its "Edison" devices could run
200+ blood tests from a finger-prick. WSJ reporting (Oct 15, 2015) revealed most patient tests were
actually run on modified samples through conventional third-party lab machines. Safeway's $350M
in-store clinic buildout never launched (ended Nov 2015); Walgreens froze then ended its partnership
(2016) after a federal regulator found "immediate jeopardy to patient health and safety." Company
dissolved Sept 2018 via an out-of-court wind-down (not bankruptcy) — $0 to shareholders. SEC's 2018
CIVIL case ended in a settlement (fine + 10-year officer/director bar, no admission of wrongdoing) —
keep this explicitly distinct from the CRIMINAL case. Jan 3, 2022: jury convicted Holmes on
conspiracy to commit wire fraud + 3 investor wire-fraud counts; ACQUITTED on every patient-related
count. Nov 2022: sentenced 11 years 3 months. Co-defendant Ramesh "Sunny" Balwani convicted on all 12
counts he faced (broader than Holmes) — nearly 13 years. May 2023: $452M restitution ordered, jointly
liable. Holmes reported to prison May 30, 2023. As of Jan 2026 reporting, a clemency/commutation
petition Holmes filed in 2025 is PENDING with the Trump administration — NOT decided. Every title
below treats this as an open petition, never as an outcome.

## Recommended Title
**"A Federal Jury Convicted Her of Fraud. It Also Acquitted Her of Something Most People Assume She
Was Guilty Of."**

Reasoning: nearly every viewer already knows the broad-strokes Theranos story from a book, a
documentary, or general cultural osmosis — the risk for this package isn't unfamiliarity, it's
"I already know this." This title leads with the single most reliably surprising, fully accurate
fact this research pass surfaced (Holmes was acquitted on every patient-related count, convicted
only on investor-related counts), which most existing coverage doesn't foreground. It states the
conviction as settled fact, consistent with this channel's real-conviction standard (005, 029), and
creates a genuine information gap even for viewers who think they know how this story ends.

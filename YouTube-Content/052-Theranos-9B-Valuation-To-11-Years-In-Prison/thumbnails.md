# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram the $9B
valuation, the Edison device, the acquittal split, the restitution figure, AND the pending clemency
petition into a single image — those live in the video, not the thumbnail. Each version below picks
exactly one.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a single blood-drop icon, half rendered as a glowing "revolutionary technology"
  icon and half rendered as a plain, ordinary lab-vial icon — the visual is the gap between the claim
  and the reality
- **Main subject:** the technology claim vs. the technology reality
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint clinical/lab texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the split icon
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** icon centered and dominant, title-safe space reserved at the bottom third
- **Props:** one icon graphic only
- **Thumbnail text:** `IT WASN'T DOING WHAT THEY SAID`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** deep red accent on the "claim" half, cold blue/gray on the "reality" half,
  near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the split-icon communicates the entire thesis before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Image concept:** a bold "$9,000,000,000" figure rendered large, with a single crack running
  through the middle of the number
- **Subject:** the valuation number itself
- **Expression:** N/A (no face)
- **Objects:** large numeral graphic, single crack/fracture line
- **Background:** dark, minimal, subtle grain
- **Colors:** white numeral, single red crack, near-black background
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the numeral
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** numeral fills most of the frame, crack diagonal, bottom-third text space
- **Thumbnail text:** `THE NUMBER WAS REAL. THE TECHNOLOGY WASN'T.`
- **Why it works:** the $9B figure is this channel's largest-ever valuation attached to a real-
  conviction fraud case; the crack does the emotional work without needing a face or logo
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Curiosity (the acquittal twist)
- **Image concept:** two stamp graphics side by side, one reading (via a generic checkmark icon)
  "guilty," one reading (via a generic X icon) "not guilty" — no names, no case details, just the
  split verdict as an object
- **Subject:** the mixed verdict itself
- **Expression:** N/A (no face)
- **Objects:** two simple icon stamps
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint courthouse-column texture at low opacity
- **Colors:** red icon on one side, white/neutral icon on the other, near-black background
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** two stamps side by side, even weight, bottom-third text space
- **Thumbnail text:** `CONVICTED. AND ACQUITTED. SAME TRIAL.`
- **Why it works:** most viewers assume a single clean "guilty" outcome; the split-verdict visual
  previews the video's real differentiator without needing to explain the legal detail up front
- **CTR prediction:** high
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic, viewers who think they already know this story

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority (face-led)
- **Image concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/measured expression, small red "11 YEARS"
  tag in the corner — no reference to the pending clemency petition in this version, keeping it to
  one idea
- **Subject:** host/avatar, three-quarter crop
- **Expression:** serious, measured, direct eye contact
- **Objects:** small calendar/prison-bar icon tag in the corner
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Colors:** desaturated, single warm key light on the face, red accent only on the corner tag
- **Lighting:** dramatic single top-down light
- **Angle:** slight low angle, three-quarter crop
- **Composition:** face fills most of frame, small tag in the corner, bottom-third text space
- **Thumbnail text:** `A JURY CONVICTED HER OF FRAUD`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; states the conviction plainly,
  consistent with this channel's real-conviction framing standard
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render any real photo of Elizabeth Holmes, Sunny Balwani, or any political figure without
  proper rights clearance — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real imagery in
  the editor
- Do not reference the pending clemency petition in the thumbnail at all — it is an unresolved,
  live, politically sensitive request, not a settled fact, and doesn't belong in a still image; handle
  it only inside the video with the required neutral framing
- Do not put any real logo (Theranos, Walgreens, Safeway) without rights clearance
- Do not combine the $9B valuation figure and the $700M+ funding figure into one thumbnail number —
  they describe different things; if either appears, label it clearly and use only one

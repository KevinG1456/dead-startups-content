# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Theranos, Walgreens,
Safeway), and no real people's likenesses (Elizabeth Holmes, Sunny Balwani, or any political figure)
— add verified text/photos/branding in the editor with proper rights clearance, not generated.

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious throughout — this is a highly recognizable, legally
  sensitive title naming a real, currently-incarcerated convicted individual with an active,
  undecided clemency petition, and the delivery should read as measured journalism at every point,
  never sensationalism, never editorializing
- **Speaking pace:** measured; deliberate pauses before dates, dollar figures, and legal terms;
  noticeably slower and flatter during the SEC-vs-criminal distinction and the pending-petition
  segment specifically
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the founding/funding segments; sharper, more
  skeptical focus during the technology-gap segment without becoming mocking; steady, plain delivery
  for the conviction and sentence (state the facts, no triumph, no outrage); the pending-petition
  segment is the single most important tonal moment in the whole video — flat, neutral, almost
  clinical, zero inflection that could read as approval or disapproval of a decision that hasn't even
  been made yet
- **Facial expression:** neutral-serious baseline throughout; no smirking, no visible reaction
  (positive or negative) during the pending-petition segment specifically
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; stays still and minimal during the
  pending-petition text-card sequence rather than gesturing at all
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout — this builds the credibility this topic
  requires, especially during the two most sensitive segments (the abuse-allegation attribution, and
  the pending petition)

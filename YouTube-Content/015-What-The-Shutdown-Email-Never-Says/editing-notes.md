# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the "SUNSET" strikethrough reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- "SUNSET" struck-through / "WIND DOWN" reveal animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Quote-card stacking animation for the "Heavy Heart" cluster (4 cards)
- "Ending" text morphing into "the next chapter" text for the Reframe Cluster
- Tone-spectrum graphic with Yik Yak and Karhoo at opposite ends for The Outliers
- Split-screen public-letter-vs-private-quote graphic for The Homejoy Gap
- Full bar-chart graphic for The Real Numbers, building bar by bar, "sunset" visibly at zero
- Visually distinct treatment (different color grade/border) for the Tier B bankruptcy-statement segment, to keep it separate from Tier A

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a letter is quoted (company/founder name, date, source)
- "This detail was NOT in the public letter" lower third specifically during the Homejoy Gap segment
- "Different genre — not counted in the Tier A percentages" lower third during the bankruptcy-statement segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key quotes and numbers
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/laptop imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- Each bar in the final data reveal animates up from zero as its number is spoken

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the quote-card and bar-chart graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The "Heavy Heart" Cluster / The Reframe Cluster: reflective, understated
- The Outliers: a brief tonal shift between segments (light for Yik Yak, flat/clinical for Karhoo)
- The Ones That Actually Said Something: slightly warmer, more respectful
- The Homejoy Gap: quiet, contemplative
- The Real Numbers: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- A Different Genre — Bankruptcy Statements: a deliberately different, more clinical/corporate musical texture
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Sharp "strikethrough" SFX on the "SUNSET" crossed-out reveal in the cold open
- Soft "click" SFX as each bar populates in the final data reveal
- Avoid comedic stingers during quote reads — tone is investigative and empathetic, respectful of the real founders and teams behind every letter in the corpus

## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct startup outcomes using primary sources. This week is different — instead of one company, it's a real, compiled corpus of 23 independently verified startup shutdown letters, spanning 2015 to 2023: Secret, Quibi, Yik Yak, Sidecar, Juicero, Munchery, Take Eat Easy, Doppler Labs, Brandless, Vine, Katerra, SpoonRocket, Homejoy, Zirtual, Beepi, Fast, Ozy Media, Josephine, Maple, Airware, MoviePass, Karhoo, and Convoy.

This started as a plan for 50 letters. 23 real, independently verifiable ones is the honest number — and it's enough to find a genuine, surprising pattern: "sunset," the euphemism everyone assumes and jokes about, appears zero times across the real corpus. "Wind down" is the actual dominant term (3 of 23). "Heavy heart" shows up in 4 of 23. Only 7 of 23 name a specific, real cause; 9 of 23 stay vague. Only 2 of 23 address investors, employees, and customers as genuinely distinct audiences.

We also cover a specific, documented gap: Homejoy's public 2015 letter said "difficult decision" and vague "unresolved challenges" — but CEO Adora Cheung privately told journalists that pending worker-misclassification lawsuits were the real deciding factor. That gap between the public letter and the private explanation is the clearest single example of this whole video's thesis.

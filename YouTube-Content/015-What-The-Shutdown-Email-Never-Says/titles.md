1. **I Read 23 Real Startup Shutdown Letters. Nobody Actually Said "Sunset."** — leads with the real, checkable corpus size and the genuine correction, works fully truncated on mobile, promises the audience will learn something they had wrong
2. "Sunset" Is a Myth. I Checked 23 Real Startup Shutdown Letters to Prove It. — punchy, strong myth-busting framing
3. What Startup Shutdown Letters Actually Say vs. What Everyone Assumes — clean, sets up the entire video's structure in one line
4. This Founder's Public Letter and Private Explanation Didn't Match. Here's the Gap. — strong for the Homejoy-led alt cut, human-interest angle
5. I Actually Counted the Corporate Euphemisms In Startup Shutdown Letters — signals rigor directly, strong trust builder for this audience
6. Only 2 of 23 Shutdown Letters Actually Told Investors and Employees Different Things — sharp, specific, strong secondary hook
7. Startups Never Say "Sunset." Here's What They Actually Say. — clean, punchy, good Shorts-title candidate
8. Startup Shutdown Letters Decoded: A Real Analysis — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
9. What It's Actually Like to Write the Letter That Ends Your Company — best for the emotional/human-angle alt cut
10. Startup Shutdown Letter Euphemisms Explained — dry, best for search-only sub-cut

## Recommended Title
**"I Read 23 Real Startup Shutdown Letters. Nobody Actually Said 'Sunset.'"**

Reasoning: states the real, honestly-achieved corpus size (not the originally-planned but
unachievable "50") and leads with the video's actual news — a genuine, counted correction to a
widely-assumed pattern. Works fully truncated on mobile, and the specific number ("23") signals
rigor to this channel's fact-check-heavy audience before they even click, rather than inviting
the "how did you actually verify 50 of these" skepticism a rounder, less honest number would.

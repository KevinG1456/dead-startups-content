# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the word everyone assumes, crossed out, replaced by the real word.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** the word "SUNSET" in large text with a hard red strikethrough, "WIND DOWN" written below it in a different color
- **Main subject:** the myth-vs-reality correction itself
- **Emotion:** curiosity/surprise
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the central text
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** struck-through word centered upper-middle, replacement word below, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** red strikethrough line
- **Thumbnail text:** `~~"SUNSET"~~ THEY NEVER SAY IT.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white struck-through text, green replacement text, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the crossed-out word does the communicating before any caption is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** the struck-through "SUNSET" / "WIND DOWN" combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `THEY NEVER SAY IT`
- **Why it works:** a crossed-out assumed word creates an immediate "wait, really?" reaction
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** large "23 REAL LETTERS" text with a magnifying glass icon over a stack of documents
- **Text:** `I READ 23 REAL SHUTDOWN LETTERS`
- **Why it works:** leads with the specific, real, verifiable number — signals rigor and scale at once
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well on this channel
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/focused expression, small crossed-out "sunset" tag in the corner
- **Text:** `I ACTUALLY COUNTED THIS`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "I actually counted this" framing signals real rigor, not just a listicle
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

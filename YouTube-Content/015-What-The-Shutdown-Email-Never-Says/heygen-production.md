# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and engaged, with a clear "data reveal" register during the final numbers segment
- **Speaking pace:** brisk during the pattern-cluster segments (Heavy Heart, Reframe, Outliers); measured and precise during the Homejoy Gap and the final data reveal — both need room to land
- **Emotional tone:** curious/investigative throughout, with genuine empathy during the "Ones That Actually Said Something" segment and quiet gravity during the Homejoy Gap
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; slight engagement lift at the "sunset = zero" reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the bar-chart data reveal
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (SUNSET strikethrough animation)
- Script: "Everyone assumes startups say 'we're sunsetting the product.' I read 23 real shutdown letters. Not one of them said it."
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–0:55 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, minimal graphic inset
- Performance direction: engaged, promising a real correction
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 0:55–1:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: slightly warmer, teaching tone; honest about the 50-to-23 count
- Visual direction: medium shot

**Scene 04 — The "Heavy Heart" Cluster**
- Duration: 1:45–3:00 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (quote cards stacking), avatar VO under
- Performance direction: brisk, pattern-forward
- Visual direction: four quote cards stack in sequence

**Scene 05 — The Reframe Cluster**
- Duration: 3:00–4:15 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with "ending → next chapter" morph graphic
- Performance direction: measured, slightly wry on the reframe pattern
- Visual direction: text morph animation

**Scene 06 — The Outliers**
- Duration: 4:15–5:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, tone-spectrum graphic inset
- Performance direction: brief tonal shift between Yik Yak (lighter) and Karhoo (flatter) beats
- Visual direction: spectrum graphic with both examples marked

**Scene 07 — The Ones That Actually Said Something**
- Duration: 5:30–7:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, quote cards highlighting candid language
- Performance direction: genuine respect and warmth for these entries specifically
- Visual direction: quotes with euphemism-free language visually highlighted

**Scene 08 — The Homejoy Gap**
- Duration: 7:00–8:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, split-screen public/private graphic
- Performance direction: quiet, careful, this is a credibility and empathy beat
- Visual direction: split screen builds as the gap is explained

**Scene 09 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~8:00 (inside Scene 08) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into serious tone
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly

**Scene 10 — The Real Numbers — Counted, Not Assumed**
- Duration: 8:30–11:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera with bar-chart graphic building bar by bar
- Performance direction: clear, patient, precise — this is the video's actual thesis payoff, give it full weight, noticeable beat on the "sunset = zero" bar
- Visual direction: full bar-chart graphic, "sunset" bar visibly flat next to the others

**Scene 11 — A Different Genre — Bankruptcy Statements**
- Duration: 11:30–12:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, visually distinct graphic treatment
- Performance direction: slightly more clinical/dry register to match the genre shift
- Visual direction: distinct color grade/border separating this segment from the rest

**Scene 12 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 12:30–14:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: return to controlled-authority tone, steady eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements animate in during final 5 seconds only

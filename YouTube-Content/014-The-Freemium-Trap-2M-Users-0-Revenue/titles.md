## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **15 Million Users. Zero Dollars. Gone in 15 Months.** — plain-language, brutal and specific contradiction
2. The Freemium Trap: 15M Users, $0 Revenue, Dead in 15 Months — original; strong specific-number hook plus branded-term framing
3. This App Had Millions of Users. It Never Made a Dollar. — punchy, direct, strong nostalgia/curiosity blend
4. How to Get 15 Million Users and Still Go Bankrupt — strong question-format hook
5. The App That Died From Its Own Success — vivid, ironic framing
6. What Happens When Growth Isn't a Business Model — strong practical/thesis framing
7. This App's Growth Chart Was Perfect. Its Revenue Chart Was Empty. — strong visual-contrast framing, slightly long
8. The Freemium Trap Explained: A Real Case Study — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
9. Millions of People Loved This App. It Still Couldn't Survive. — best for the emotional/nostalgia-angle alt cut
10. Freemium Business Model Failure: Case Study — dry, best for search-only sub-cut

## Recommended Title
**"15 Million Users. Zero Dollars. Gone in 15 Months."**

Reasoning: plain-language, concrete numbers, complete contradiction that works even without naming the app, and creates strong curiosity about the mechanism. Once the exact figures and app are confirmed, add the app name as an on-screen subtitle and in the thumbnail for the nostalgia-driven recognition boost.

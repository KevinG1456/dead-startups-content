# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Strong nostalgia-driven appeal may benefit from a coordinated Shorts push using "remember this app?" framing across the following week.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) plus a broader nostalgia-driven audience who remember using the app during its peak.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; consider a "Viral Apps We Forgot About" or similar nostalgia-themed playlist if this format performs well and is repeated.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + highest-performing prior autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the mid-video CTA linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist; card near the freemium-framework segment linking to a related monetization-focused explainer video.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy, subscribe — I'm building a full library of these. And if you remember using this app, tell me what you remember in the comments."

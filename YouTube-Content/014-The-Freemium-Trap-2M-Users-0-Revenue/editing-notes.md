# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the 15M/$0 reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- User-count vs. dollar-figure animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Growth chart animation climbing steeply, paired with a flat, unmoving revenue line beneath it
- Freemium-conversion framework graphic building progressively during the practical segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or quote is referenced
- "Users" vs. "Revenue" labeled axis persists on screen during the growth/revenue comparison segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos, generic app-interface mockups, or archival stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the growth-chart/framework graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Rise section: upbeat, high-energy (representing the viral growth phase) — deliberately contrasts with the flat revenue reality
- Autopsy section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual chart contrast carry the tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Rapid "notification chime" cluster SFX under the growth-chart climb, cutting to silence when the revenue line is introduced
- Rubber-stamp thud on the shutdown reveal
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative, with room for genuine nostalgia warmth during the Rise section

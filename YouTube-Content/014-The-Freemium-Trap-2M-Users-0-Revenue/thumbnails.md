# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the scale-vs-revenue contradiction.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "15,000,000" figure in bold white, next to a giant "$0" in red, connected by an equals-adjacent contradiction symbol
- **Main subject:** the users-vs-revenue mismatch
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** user count top, dollar figure bottom, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** none beyond the numbers
- **Thumbnail text:** `15M USERS. $0 REVENUE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white user-count figure, red $0, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the scale mismatch between the two numbers does the communicating

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** a phone icon with a tiny "15M" badge (like a notification count) but an empty/grayed-out wallet icon beside it
- **Text:** `EVERYONE USED IT. NOBODY PAID.`
- **Why it works:** the notification-badge visual is instantly familiar and signals massive scale; the empty wallet icon creates the contradiction
- **CTR prediction:** high — familiar visual language (notification badges) increases instant legibility
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the 15M/$0 combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `15M USERS. $0 REVENUE.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of the contradiction with maximal number contrast
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), nostalgic/knowing expression, small phone-icon tag in the corner
- **Text:** `REMEMBER THIS APP?`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; nostalgia-hook question format drives strong recognition-based curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers, potentially very high with the right audience age range
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement, and viewers likely to remember the specific app

## A/B Testing

| Version | Emotion | CTR Strategy | Best Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Curiosity | Familiar notification-badge visual language | Cold/browse traffic |
| B | Shock | Direct number contrast | Search + browse (recommended primary) |
| C | Authority | Nostalgia-hook question framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

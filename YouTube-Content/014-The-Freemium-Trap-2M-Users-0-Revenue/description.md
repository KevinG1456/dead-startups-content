## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct startup failures using primary sources. This week: an app that reached roughly 15 million users and significant cultural buzz — without ever building a working revenue model — and shut down about 15 months after launch.

We call this the freemium trap: the pattern where a product achieves real, massive user growth, but the "monetize later" plan never actually arrives before the money runs out. We walk through what was built, why growth never converted to revenue, and what actually happened in the company's final months — plus a practical framework for what separates freemium products that do eventually monetize (Slack, Dropbox) from the ones that don't.

# Shorts Ideas (10)

Note: Shorts are low-context, easily clipped content — every idea below draws only from the
ironclad, multi-sourced financial facts (funding, revenue, burn, shutdown timeline). The more
carefully-attributed founder-history material from the main script is deliberately excluded here;
it needs the full script's attribution framing and doesn't belong in a 30-60 second clip.

**1. "$580M vs $600K"**
Hook: "This company was valued at $580 million. It made $600,000." Script: quick number-contrast reveal. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**2. "The $1M Show"**
Hook: "This company paid a band $1 million for one show. That was more than its entire year of revenue." Script: 30-second spending-vs-revenue reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**3. "Stripe Backed It Twice"**

**4. "Six Days"**
Hook: "This $580 million company went from a funding attempt to a total shutdown in six days." Script: rapid-fire timeline explainer. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**5. "65x More Revenue"**
Hook: "This startup's biggest competitor made roughly 65 times more revenue in the same year." Script: quick side-by-side comparison. CTA: "Full story on the channel."

**6. "The Real Math"**
Hook: "Here's what a $10 million a month burn rate actually looks like against $600,000 a year." Script: quick numbers explainer. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**7. "What Happened to Fast"**

**8. "An Hour's Notice"**

**9. "If You're Evaluating a Startup's Numbers"**

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the "$580M / $600K" contrast reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- "$580M" vs "$600K" contrast animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Funding timeline graphic building through both Stripe-led rounds
- Side-by-side revenue bar chart (Fast vs. Bolt), Fast's bar barely visible
- "$1M booking" placed directly next to "$600K annual revenue" for the spending segment
- Six-day countdown graphic (March 30 → April 5) for The Collapse
- Distinctly bordered/differently-colored graphic treatment for the entire "Carefully-Sourced Second Thread" segment — must look visually different from the financial segments throughout

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, The Information, NPR)
- PERSISTENT "Source: NPR investigation" lower third throughout the entire second-thread segment — not just at its start, visible continuously while that material is on screen
- "Not independently confirmed by this channel" secondary tag alongside the NPR lower third specifically during the Nigerian-engineers and founder-history beats

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/checkout-interface imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance, and no imagery depicting or implying any real individual)
- Andriske's on-record quote types on screen distinctly from the anonymous-engineer quotes (consider a different quote-card style to visually distinguish named, on-record sources from anonymous ones)

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the revenue-comparison and timeline graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Pitch / The Raises: brisk, confident, building energy
- The Real Numbers: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the bar chart carry the shock
- The Spending: a slightly absurdist, uneasy undertone (not comedic) as the $1M booking is revealed
- The Collapse: tightening tension through the six-day countdown
- A Carefully-Sourced Second Thread: neutral, measured, deliberately less dramatic than the financial segments — this section should not feel sensationalized
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "counter" SFX as the revenue bar chart populates in The Real Numbers
- Sharp "date stamp" SFX for each day in the six-day countdown
- No dramatic stingers during the second-thread segment — keep the sound design restrained and measured there specifically, consistent with the section's careful tone
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and measured, respectful of the real people (450 employees) affected by this shutdown, and appropriately cautious given the sensitivity of the founder-history material

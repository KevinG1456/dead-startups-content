## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct startup outcomes using primary sources. This week: Fast, a one-click e-commerce checkout startup founded in March 2019 by CEO Domm Holland and COO Allison Barr Allen.

Fast raised $124.5 million total — a $20 million Series A and a $102 million Series B, both led by Stripe — at a valuation of roughly $580 million. Its actual 2021 revenue was approximately $600,000, against a burn rate of roughly $10 million a month. For comparison, rival checkout startup Bolt reportedly made around $40 million over the same period. Fast reportedly paid The Chainsmokers $1 million for a single conference performance — about 1.7 times its entire annual revenue. After a failed attempt to raise a new round at a lowered valuation and a last-minute search for a buyer via Morgan Stanley, Fast shut down on April 5, 2022, laying off roughly 450 people, most with about an hour's notice.

This video also covers a second, more carefully-sourced thread: reporting (primarily from one detailed NPR investigation) about the founder's earlier career, presented explicitly as reported, not as independently confirmed by this channel — including a genuinely balancing detail that at least some investors reportedly knew this history and didn't consider it disqualifying.

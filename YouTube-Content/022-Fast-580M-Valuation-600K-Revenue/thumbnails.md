# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the valuation-vs-revenue gap. No imagery depicting or implying
anything about the founder personally (no face-led concept using Holland's likeness) — keep every
visual concept on the financial numbers themselves, consistent with this package's elevated-care
sourcing standard.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** two numbers stacked — "$580M" large at top, "$600K" small at bottom, connected by a steep downward arrow
- **Main subject:** the valuation-to-revenue gap itself
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the two numbers
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** large number top, small number bottom, steep arrow connecting them, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** downward arrow graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `$580M VALUATION. $600K REVENUE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green large number, red small number, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the scale mismatch between the two numbers does the communicating before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** the two-number stack from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$580M VALUATION. $600K REVENUE.`
- **Why it works:** the sheer scale mismatch between two real, sourced numbers creates immediate disbelief and curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock
- **Concept:** a large dollar-sign icon breaking into fragments
- **Text:** `THE MATH NEVER ADDED UP`
- **Why it works:** signals a real financial-mystery explainer without needing to depict any individual
- **CTR prediction:** high
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** a clean, editorial-style split-number graphic (no face, no avatar) — "$124M RAISED" on one side, "$600K MADE" on the other
- **Text:** `THE REAL NUMBERS`
- **Why it works:** signals rigor and restraint — appropriate given this package's sensitive subject matter — and still leads with the strongest hook (the numbers themselves)
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high, strong trust signal for this audience specifically
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

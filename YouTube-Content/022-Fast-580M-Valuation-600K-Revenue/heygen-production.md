# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious throughout; brisker during the founding/raises segments, deliberately measured and restrained (not dramatic or gossipy) during the second-thread segment
- **Speaking pace:** brisk and numbers-forward during the financial segments; noticeably slower and more careful during the second-thread segment, giving the on-screen sourcing disclosures room to register with the viewer
- **Emotional tone:** confident/matter-of-fact for the financial story; measured, neutral, non-sensational for the founder-history material — this is not a tabloid tone, it's an investigative-explainer tone
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline throughout; no exaggerated shock or disapproval during the second-thread segment specifically — let the sourced facts carry the weight, not the delivery
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

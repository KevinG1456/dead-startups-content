# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Quixey, Alibaba, Goldman
Sachs, SoftBank), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Tomer Kagan, Liron Shapira,
Mark Lazar, John Foster) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in the editor
with proper sourcing, not generated.

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram the CEO churn,
the debt deal's full terms, and the blocked rescue round into a single image. Each of those gets its
own version below, or lives inside the video/Shorts instead of the thumbnail.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large green checkmark stamped multiple times (representing "yes" from multiple
  investors), then one large red "X" stamped over all of them, breaking/cracking the frame
- **Main subject:** the "everyone agreed, one veto overrode it" contradiction
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint document/contract texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the red "X"
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** small green checkmarks scattered, one large red "X" dominant and centered over them,
  bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic checkmark/X icons, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `THEY ALL SAID YES. ONE INVESTOR SAID NO.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** green checkmarks, red dominant X, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the visible scale mismatch (many small yeses vs. one large no) communicates
  the entire thesis before any text is read

- **Objects:** small red document/chart icon tag in the corner
- **Background:** dark, minimal

  churn angle as a distinct hook from the veto-mechanism angle

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Tomer Kagan, Liron Shapira, Mark
  Lazar, or John Foster in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts
  only; attribute real names verbally/on-screen in the video itself
- Do not put the real Quixey or Alibaba logos/wordmarks in generated thumbnail art — build the visual
  concept generically and add any verified real branding in the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not imply fraud or wrongdoing in any thumbnail — this is a deal-structure/governance story, not a
  scandal; keep the framing analytical, not accusatory

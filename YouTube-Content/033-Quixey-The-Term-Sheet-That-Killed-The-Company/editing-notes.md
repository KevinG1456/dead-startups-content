# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open checkmarks-vs-X
  reveal, and again on the "VETO RIGHT — FUTURE EQUITY FINANCING" highlighted-document card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the "Every other investor said yes. This is the single
  clause that made their agreement not enough" framing card — the single most important line in the
  video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open scattered green checkmarks, hard cut to a large red X stamped over all of them
- "This isn't a metaphor. One investor had a literal, contractual veto — and used it." pattern-
  interrupt card, white text on black
- Funding-ladder graphic climbing to "$600M VALUATION — 2015"
- "EXECUTIVES DEPART — FEBRUARY 2016" title card
- Three-name CEO-succession graphic (Kagan → Lazar → Foster), with a standing "Kagan stayed on the
  board" tag
- Document graphic with one line highlighted in red: "VETO RIGHT — FUTURE EQUITY FINANCING"
- Countdown-style "18 MONTHS" timeline graphic, ticking down from the August 2016 debt deal
- Green checkmark graphic building as each rescue-round investor "agrees," then a large red X
  stamping over the whole graphic at the veto reveal
- "SHUTDOWN — FEBRUARY 2017" title card, Alibaba's attributed quote card held on screen
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founding (2009) → funding climb (2011-2015) → peak
  valuation (2015) → executive departures (Feb 2016) → CEO changes (Apr/Nov 2016) → debt deal with
  veto right (Aug 2016) → rescue round agreed (Feb 2017) → veto exercised (Feb 2017) → shutdown (Feb
  2017)
- Package 018/032 cross-reference graphic: three-way small card labeling each package's distinct
  cap-table mechanism (down round / preference stack / veto right)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, CNBC,
  Bloomberg, Recode, Global Venturing, The Information, Mountain View Voice)
- "Stayed on the board — did not leave the company" standing lower third any time Tomer Kagan's CEO
  replacement is discussed — no exceptions
- "Alibaba's own public statement" lower third on the closure quote
- "No fraud or criminal allegations in this story" note available for the intro if needed to set tone

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office imagery — no real readable logos/
  trademarks (Quixey, Alibaba, Goldman Sachs, SoftBank) without rights clearance
- The checkmarks-to-X animation should build with each checkmark appearing individually (representing
  each agreeing investor) before the single X slams down over all of them — the timing itself should
  reinforce "many agreed, one overrode"
- The 18-month countdown graphic should animate continuously in a corner from Segment 5 onward,
  reaching zero near the veto reveal

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the checkmarks/veto graphic,
  the funding-ladder graphic, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "every other investor said yes" framing
  card — no camera movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding / The Climb: measured, optimistic, building energy toward the $600M valuation peak
- Missed Targets / Three CEOs: uneasy, declining energy, mirroring the leadership churn
- The Debt Deal: dry, clinical, document-forward — a subtle tension undercurrent as the veto term is
  revealed
- Racing the Clock: ticking, tense, countdown-driven
- The Rescue Round: a brief hopeful lift as each investor "agrees"
- The Veto: sharp tonal drop, the hopeful cue cutting off abruptly
- The Shutdown: quiet, restrained, respectful — no melodrama
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2009 founding, 2015 peak valuation, Feb
  2016 departures, Apr/Nov 2016 CEO changes, Aug 2016 debt deal, Feb 2017 rescue round/veto/shutdown)
- Soft "check" SFX on each checkmark appearing in the rescue-round build-up
- Sharp, decisive "stamp" or "gavel" SFX on the red X veto reveal — the single most important sound
  cue in the video
- Ticking-clock SFX under the countdown graphic, fading out at the veto reveal
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays analytical and precise throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and building through the founding/climb segments; uneasy through the
  leadership-churn segment; dry and clinical through the debt-deal segment; tense through racing-the-
  clock and the rescue-round build-up; sharp tonal drop at the veto reveal; quiet and restrained
  through the shutdown
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "every other investor said yes" framing
  line and the veto-clause reveal — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** dry, document-forward through the deal-terms explainer — never sensationalized;
  matter-of-fact through the CEO-succession sequence; restrained disbelief at the veto reveal; quiet
  respect, no melodrama, during the shutdown segment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine dry disbelief when stating the
  veto mechanism; visible care and precision when reading the "Kagan stayed on the board" framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  checkmarks/veto graphic and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

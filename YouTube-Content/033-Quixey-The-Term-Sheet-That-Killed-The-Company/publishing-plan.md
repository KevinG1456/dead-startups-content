# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Quixey has modest direct name
recognition, so this package leans heavily on founder/operator search terms ("term sheet veto right,"
"toxic term sheet") and the cap-table-mechanics niche audience rather than cold browse traffic — no
urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience specifically: founders and operators evaluating debt or structured financing
terms, and investors studying deal-structure risk. Narrower general-audience crossover than this
channel's celebrity-founder or consumer-brand packages — pair distribution accordingly (lean into
startup/VC-adjacent communities and search terms).

## Playlist

package 018 (Down Rounds) and package 032 (Practice Fusion) — explicitly label this as the third
distinct mechanism in that mini-series (down round / preference stack / veto right).

## End Screen

other cap-table mechanics stories, making a strong three-video companion set.

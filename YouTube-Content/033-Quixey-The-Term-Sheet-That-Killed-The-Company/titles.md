## Recommended Title
**"Quixey Signed a Debt Deal With One Veto Clause in It. That Clause Killed the Company."**

Reasoning: states the exact, verifiable mechanism this title was chosen to illustrate — not a vague
"toxic terms scared investors away" claim, but a specific, contractual veto right that a real investor
used to block money every other investor had already agreed to provide. Works fully truncated on
mobile at the first sentence alone, and sets up the video's core differentiator (precision about the
mechanism, not just the outcome) without needing to front-load company-name recognition, since Quixey
itself has modest direct search volume. Matches the title already used in this package's README.

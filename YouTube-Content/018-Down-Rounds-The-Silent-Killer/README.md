## 2. Viewer Psychology
- **Curiosity triggers:** how exactly a lower valuation translates into real ownership and payout changes; what "silent killer" specifically refers to
- **Emotional triggers:** anxiety for anyone currently at a company that's raised or might raise a down round, validation of the intuition that down rounds are worse than they sound
- **Viewer objections:** "isn't a down round just a lower valuation, why call it a 'silent killer'" — address directly by showing the compounding mechanical effects (dilution + liquidation preference stacking + pay-to-play) that make the real impact much larger than the headline valuation drop suggests
- **Motivation to watch:** direct practical value for founders, employees, and investors navigating or evaluating a down-round scenario; general interest in startup finance mechanics
- **Expected comments:** requests for more detail on specific cap table scenarios, people sharing their own down-round experiences, questions about how to evaluate an offer at a company that's had a down round

## 3. Competitor Analysis
- **Similar creators:** down rounds are covered in finance journalism (WSJ, The Information) and some VC blog content, but rarely with hands-on, annotated cap table visuals aimed at a general startup audience
- **Content gap:** almost nobody has built a clear, step-by-step visual explainer showing exactly how a down round mechanically affects different stakeholders' ownership and payout
- **How to outperform:** build the 3 annotated cap tables as genuinely reusable educational assets — this is content people will screenshot and reference later

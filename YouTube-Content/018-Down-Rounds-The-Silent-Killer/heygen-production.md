# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled, analytical, clear educational register throughout — this is the most technically dense title in the batch and needs consistent clarity over energy
- **Speaking pace:** measured and patient throughout, especially during the three cap-table walkthroughs — resist the urge to rush technical explanations
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the mechanics segments, warmer and more practically helpful during the "why this matters" and "what to ask" segments
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine concern during the liquidation-preference-stacking explanation, where the asymmetric impact on employees is most stark
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled; slightly more illustrative hand movement when explaining each cap table's mechanics, to aid visual clarity
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:20 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (asymmetric bar-chart animation)
- Script: "What does a down round actually do to your equity, beyond the headline valuation number?"
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — What Is a Down Round**
- Duration: 0:20–1:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, definition graphic animates in
- Performance direction: clear, teaching tone, sets expectations for the format (3 annotated cap tables)
- Visual direction: medium shot, graphic inset

**Scenes 03–05 — Cap Tables 1–3**
- Duration: 1:30–7:30 (three ~2-minute segments per script.md timestamps)
- Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera introducing each cap table, then split-screen with the graphic building row by row as avatar explains, returning to full avatar frame for the segment's takeaway line
- Performance direction: patient, precise, deliberate; consistent pacing across all three segments for structural rhythm; the "illustrative example" label should be verbally acknowledged at least once per segment, not just shown visually
- Visual direction: consistent cap-table visual template across all three, side-by-side split-screen for most of each segment

**Scene 06 — Mid-Video CTA / Why This Matters**
- Duration: ~7:30–9:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address, transitioning to real-world example B-roll (Klarna/Instacart press coverage)
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift for the CTA, then grounds back into serious, credible delivery for the real-world examples
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly, then press-clipping B-roll for the real examples

**Scene 07 — What to Ask**
- Duration: 9:30–10:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct-to-camera practical advice
- Performance direction: warmer, more direct and helpful tone — the most actionable segment of the video
- Visual direction: minimal graphics, checklist bullets appear beside avatar

**Scene 08 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 10:30–11:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: steady, direct eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements animate in during final 5 seconds only

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the asymmetric-bar-chart reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Asymmetric bar-chart animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- 3 distinct, clean cap-table graphics (Dilution, Liquidation Preference Stacking, Pay-to-Play), each building row by row as explained
- Clear "REAL EXAMPLE" vs. "ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE" labeling system used consistently whenever switching between Klarna/Instacart context and the teaching cap table

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a real company figure (Klarna, Instacart) is referenced
- "Illustrative example" persistent watermark/label during all teaching-cap-table segments to avoid any confusion with real data

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and cap table rows
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos related to the real-world grounding examples

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the cap-table graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Explainer section (What Is a Down Round): neutral, clear, educational tone
- Cap table walkthroughs: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual numbers carry the content
- Practical/employee-advice section: warmer, more helpful tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "click" SFX as each row populates in the cap table graphics
- Distinct tonal shift when moving between "real example" and "illustrative example" segments to reinforce the visual labeling
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is rigorous and educational throughout

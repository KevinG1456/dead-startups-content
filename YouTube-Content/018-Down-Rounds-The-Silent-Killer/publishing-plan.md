# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Strong evergreen search-driven potential, particularly during down-market funding cycles when down rounds are more frequently in the news.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs), with particular relevance to startup employees holding equity and anyone evaluating a job offer at a company that has had or might have a down round.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; strong candidate for a dedicated "Startup Finance Explainers" playlist alongside Title 010 (Acquired for $1) and Title 017 (Venture Debt Covenants).

## End Screen
Venture Debt Covenants video (Title 017) + most recent autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at each cap-table segment linking to a related finance-explainer video; card at the practical-questions segment linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy, subscribe — I'm building a full library of these. And if your company's equity situation has you asking questions, hopefully this gave you a better framework for what to ask."

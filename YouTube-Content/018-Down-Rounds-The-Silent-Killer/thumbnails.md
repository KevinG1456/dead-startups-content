# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the valuation-drop-vs-real-impact gap.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a simplified cap-table-style bar chart where one bar (common stock/employees) shrinks dramatically while a smaller bar (preferred/investors) stays nearly the same size, with a red downward arrow over the whole chart
- **Main subject:** the asymmetric-impact visual metaphor
- **Emotion:** concern/injustice
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** bar chart centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** simple bar-chart graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `THE SILENT KILLER`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** red for the shrinking bar, neutral gray for the stable bar, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the size asymmetry between the two bars communicates the unequal impact instantly

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** a simple downward arrow with "-85%" style large text, small cap-table-grid icon beside it
- **Text:** `THIS IS WHAT IT ACTUALLY MEANS`
- **Why it works:** a striking percentage combined with a promise of deeper explanation drives curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the asymmetric bar-chart combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `THE SILENT KILLER`
- **Why it works:** the visual asymmetry communicates "someone loses more than others" instantly, and the branded phrase is memorable
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/analytical expression, small cap-table-grid tag in the corner
- **Text:** `3 CAP TABLES, ANNOTATED`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "annotated" signals a rigorous, hands-on educational format
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

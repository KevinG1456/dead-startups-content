# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the scale/speed contradiction, not a full summary of the story.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** giant "$1.75B" in bold white/green text with a stopwatch or countdown graphic next to it reading "6 MONTHS"
- **Main subject:** the number + time contrast is the subject
- **Emotion:** disbelief/shock
- **Background:** dark background, subtle red gradient creeping in from the edges
- **Lighting:** flat, even — nothing competing with the number
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** number top-left, countdown/stopwatch bottom-right, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** stopwatch icon or a simple circular countdown ring
- **Thumbnail text:** `$1.75B. 6 MONTHS. GONE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, two lines, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white/green number against near-black, red accents signal loss
- **Contrast strategy:** minimal palette (white, green, red, black only) so the number reads instantly at small size

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** just the number "$1.75,000,000,000" spelled out in full (not abbreviated) to visually emphasize scale, tiny "6 months" stamped small in the corner
- **Text:** `THIS TOOK 6 MONTHS TO LOSE.`
- **Why it works:** spelling out the full number with all the zeros is a visual trick that reads as "enormous" even before the viewer processes the value
- **CTR prediction:** high — novelty of the full-zero number stands out in a feed of abbreviated "$1.75B" thumbnails
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the countdown/stopwatch + big dollar figure combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$1.75B. 6 MONTHS. GONE.`
- **Why it works:** pairs a magnitude cue (huge dollar figure) with a speed cue (stopwatch) — two data points, one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar or host), stunned/serious expression, small "$1.75B / 6 MO" tag in the corner
- **Text:** `THE FASTEST FAILURE EVER`
- **Why it works:** face-led thumbnails perform best with subscribed/returning audience; superlative claim text drives curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

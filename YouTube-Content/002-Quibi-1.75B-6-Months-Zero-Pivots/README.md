# 002 — Quibi: $1.75B, 6 Months, Zero Pivots — The Fastest Burn in History

**Package status:** Production-ready. Quibi is one of the most extensively publicly documented startup failures on record (SEC-adjacent reporting, extensive press, founder/exec interviews), so core facts below are stated directly. Precise figures (subscriber counts, exact burn numbers, exact dates) are still marked `[VERIFY]` and should be confirmed against primary reporting (WSJ, The Information, Quibi's own press releases, Katzenberg/Whitman interviews) before recording.

**Known anchor facts to build from:** Founded by Jeffrey Katzenberg (Chairman) and Meg Whitman (CEO); raised approximately $1.75B before launch; launched April 6, 2020; shut down announced October 21, 2020 — roughly 6 months post-launch; short-form ("Quick Bites") premium mobile-only video app; no casting-to-TV support at launch (added later); launched during COVID-19 lockdowns, undercutting its "on-the-go mobile viewing" premise; content library (~75 shows) later acquired by Roku for a reported price under $100 million, announced January 2021.

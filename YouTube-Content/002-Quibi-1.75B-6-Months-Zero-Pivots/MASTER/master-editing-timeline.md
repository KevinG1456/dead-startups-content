# Master Editing Timeline — Quibi

Shared structural timeline across all six languages. Localized scripts may run naturally faster
or slower per language — do not force identical timestamps.

```text
00:00 Cold Open — the $1.75B/6-month contradiction
00:15 Hook — introduce Quibi, Katzenberg/Whitman, the funding scale
00:45 Introduction — method/sources statement, "decision story not COVID story" framing
01:30 Segment: The Rise — founding, pitch, fundraise, pre-launch hype
04:00 Segment: The Autopsy — launch mismatch, missing features, decision timeline
06:30 Mid-Video CTA
09:30 Segment: The Fall — shutdown, employee impact, Roku asset sale
11:30 Final CTA + Outro — one-sentence lesson, engagement prompt, end screen
13:00 [target end, ±1-2 min naturally per language]
```

## Per-Language Pacing Notes
- The Autopsy segment (04:00–09:30) is the highest drop-off risk in every language — keep cuts
  frequent (every 20-30s) between talking-head, archival B-roll, and the timeline graphic.
- The Fall segment should slow down noticeably in every language — this is the human-cost beat.
- Chapter/timestamp labels in each language's subtitles.md and YouTube chapter markers should be
  translated, not left in English.

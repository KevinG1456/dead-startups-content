# Master Research — Quibi

## Core Facts (solid, multi-sourced — state as fact in every language)
- **Company:** Quibi, short-form ("Quick Bites") premium mobile-only video streaming app.
- **Chairman:** Jeffrey Katzenberg. **CEO:** Meg Whitman.
- **Funding:** raised approximately $1.75 billion before launch — more than almost any media
  startup in history.
- **Launch date:** April 6, 2020.
- **Shutdown announced:** October 21, 2020 — roughly 6 months post-launch.
- **Product gap at launch:** no casting-to-TV support at launch (added later, after competitive
  pressure); premise was "on-the-go mobile viewing," launched during COVID-19 lockdowns that
  undercut that exact premise.
- **Aftermath:** content library (~75 shows) later acquired by Roku for a reported price under
  $100 million, announced January 2021. (This detail was independently fact-checked and confirmed
  in this channel's earlier audit — see `../sources-log.txt` if present, or the Fact-Check
  Reminder log at the project root.)

## Key Narrative Beats (translate the idea, not the sentence)
1. **The Rise:** founding story, the pitch (premium short-form mobile video), the fundraise,
   pre-launch hype.
2. **The Autopsy (core segment):** three sub-beats —
   a. The launch timing mismatch against COVID lockdowns (an "on-the-go" product launching into
      a stay-at-home world).
   b. The missing features at launch: no TV casting, no free tier, no easy social sharing —
      each shipped only after competitive/user pressure, and only after the window to matter had
      likely passed.
   c. A month-by-month decision timeline showing when each fix was requested (internally or
      externally) vs. when — if ever — it actually shipped.
3. **The Fall:** the shutdown announcement, employee impact, asset sale aftermath (Roku deal).

## The One-Sentence Lesson (must survive localization intact in meaning)
Money buys time, not correct strategy. Quibi's failure wasn't that it ran out of money — it's
that it had six months of runway and used none of that time to change a strategy that clearly
wasn't working.

## Differentiating Angle (why THIS Quibi video, not just another Quibi recap)
Most existing Quibi coverage (Business Casual, Company Man, Modern MBA, and others) treats COVID
timing as the primary explanation. This video's differentiator is the "zero pivots" framing —
walking through the SPECIFIC pivot opportunities that existed and were rejected, month by month,
rather than a general recap. Every language's script must preserve this differentiator; a
localized version that becomes a generic "what happened to Quibi" recap loses the actual point.

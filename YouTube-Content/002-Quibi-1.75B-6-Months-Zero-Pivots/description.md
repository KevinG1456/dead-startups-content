## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct a funded startup's failure using primary sources — no speculation, just documents and decisions.

This week: Quibi, the short-form streaming app backed by Jeffrey Katzenberg and led by Meg Whitman, which raised approximately $1.75 billion before launch and shut down roughly six months after launching.

Most retrospectives blame COVID timing. This video goes further — mapping the specific pivot opportunities (TV casting support, a free tier, social sharing) that existed and were rejected, month by month, until it was too late.

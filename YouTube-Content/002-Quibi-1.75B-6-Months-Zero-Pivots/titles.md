3. The Fastest Billion-Dollar Collapse in Startup History — strong superlative claim, verify it's defensible before using
4. What Really Killed Quibi (It Wasn't COVID) — strong curiosity/contrarian angle if the video actually argues this
5. Quibi Had Every Advantage. It Still Failed in 6 Months. — strong contradiction, slightly less punchy than #1
6. $1.75 Billion. 6 Months. Zero Pivots. This Is Quibi. — good as a spoken cold open line
7. Quibi Never Pivoted. That's Why It Died. — clean thesis statement, weaker curiosity gap
8. Why Did Quibi Fail? Full Breakdown — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
9. The Human Cost of a $1.75 Billion Failure — best for an emotional/human-angle alt cut
10. Quibi Business Model Failure Analysis — dry, best for a shorts/analyst-only sub-cut

## Recommended Title
**"They Raised $1.75 Billion. They Lasted 6 Months."**

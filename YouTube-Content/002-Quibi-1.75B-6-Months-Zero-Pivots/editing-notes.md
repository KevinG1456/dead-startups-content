# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the two "number" reveal moments (cold open, final report card)

## Motion Graphics
- Horizontal timeline graphic spanning launch day to shutdown day, with rejected-pivot markers appearing chronologically
- Full-zero dollar figure animation ($1,750,000,000) counting up in the cold open, then snapping to a red "SHUT DOWN" stamp

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or quote is referenced
- Name/title lower-third for Katzenberg/Whitman if photos or archival clips are used

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or archival stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the timeline graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Rise section: restrained, slightly triumphant-but-uneasy underscore
- Autopsy/decision-timeline section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the ticking-clock SFX carry tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Clock-ticking SFX under the timeline graphic
- Rubber-stamp thud on "SHUT DOWN" reveal
- Soft whoosh on each rejected-pivot marker appearing on the timeline
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative, not mocking

# English Script — Quibi

Full production script, English. Built from `../MASTER/master-script.md`'s beat structure. For
hooks, alternative script versions, and retention notes, see `../script.md` — this file is the
clean narration-ready cut.

---

## Cold Open (0:00–0:15)
VO: "How do you burn $1.75 billion in six months?"
Visual: full-zero dollar figure counts up rapidly on black, hard cut to red "SHUT DOWN" stamp
with heavy SFX hit.
Pattern Interrupt: hard cut to silence, white text on black: "This actually happened. In six
months."

## Hook (0:15–0:45)
VO: "This is Quibi. Backed by Jeffrey Katzenberg, led by Meg Whitman, launched with roughly $1.75
billion in funding — more than almost any media startup in history. Six months after launch, it
was gone."
Open loop: "By the end of this video, you'll know exactly which decision — made and then never
revisited — sealed it."

## Introduction (0:45–1:30)
Method statement: reconstructed from press coverage, executive interviews, and public timeline
records — sourced on screen.
One-sentence framing: this isn't a COVID story, it's a decision-making story.

## The Rise (1:30–4:00)
Founding story, the pitch (premium short-form mobile video, "Quick Bites"), the fundraise,
pre-launch hype.
Visual: press clippings, launch trailer stills, funding announcement headlines.
Curiosity gap: tease the specific missing feature without naming it yet.

## The Autopsy (4:00–9:30)
- **Segment 1 — the launch:** timing against COVID lockdowns, mismatch between "on-the-go"
  premise and stay-at-home reality.
- **Segment 2 — the missing features:** no TV casting at launch, no free tier, no easy social
  sharing — reveal the teased feature.
- **Segment 3 — the decision timeline:** show, month by month, when each fix was requested
  internally/externally vs. when (if ever) it shipped.
Mid-video CTA (~6:30): "If you're building something right now and nobody around you is willing
to say 'this isn't working' — the next part is for you. Subscribe for a new autopsy every week."
SFX: ticking clock under the timeline segment.

## The Fall (9:30–11:30)
The shutdown announcement, employee impact, asset sale aftermath (Roku).
Human element: what it was like inside the company in the final weeks — sourced quotes only.
Pacing: slow down deliberately here.

## Final CTA + Outro (11:30–13:00)
VO: recap the one-sentence lesson — money buys time, not correct strategy; the failure was
refusing to use the time to change course.
"Subscribe — I'm building a full library of these."
Engagement prompt: "Which pivot do you think would've saved it? Comment below."
End screen: two related autopsies + subscribe.

# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Ozy Media: founded September 2013 by Carlos Watson (a former CNN/MSNBC anchor) and
Samir Rao (co-founder/COO), which raised more than $70 million in total funding between 2012 and
2019. On September 26, 2021, the New York Times revealed that co-founder Samir Rao — not Watson —
had impersonated a YouTube executive on a conference call with Goldman Sachs, attempting to help
secure a $40 million investment; Goldman verified with Google that no such executive existed and
did not invest. Ozy announced it was shutting down days later — but didn't actually stop, continuing
under Watson for roughly another 16 months. On February 23, 2023, Watson was arrested and indicted
on federal fraud charges; the SEC separately alleged Watson and co-defendants raised roughly $50
million from investors "on the basis of fraudulent documents and repeated misrepresentations" — a
distinct figure from the $70M+ total lifetime funding, never merge the two. In July 2024, a Brooklyn
jury convicted Watson of conspiracy to commit securities fraud, conspiracy to commit wire fraud, and
aggravated identity theft. He was sentenced in December 2024 to 116 months in prison and ordered to
pay roughly $96 million in restitution. On March 28, 2025 — hours before he was due to surrender —
President Trump commuted his sentence, also voiding the restitution order. Companion piece: package
005 (HeadSpin/Manish Lachwani), this channel's other real, adjudicated startup-fraud conviction.

*** Note carried into every title below: the established, multiply-sourced fact is that Samir Rao
was the impersonator (per the original NYT reporting). No title states or implies Watson was the
one on the call — that specific claim is a single-sourced, disputed allegation from a former
employee and is handled inside the script/blog only, clearly flagged as unconfirmed. ***

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram the
impersonation call, the funding figures, the shutdown reversal, the conviction, AND the
commutation into a single image — those live in the video, not the thumbnail. Each version below
picks exactly one.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a bold "CONVICTED" stamp in red, with a second, smaller "COMMUTED" stamp
  overlapping/landing on top of it at an angle, connected by nothing more than proximity — the
  visual tension is the two stamps colliding
- **Main subject:** the conviction-then-commutation contradiction
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint federal-courthouse-column texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the two stamps
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** "CONVICTED" stamp centered, "COMMUTED" stamp overlapping at bottom-right at an
  angle, title-safe space reserved at the very bottom edge
- **Props:** two stamp graphics only
- **Thumbnail text:** `CONVICTED. THEN COMMUTED.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** red "CONVICTED" stamp, white/neutral "COMMUTED" stamp, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the two-stamp collision communicates the entire thesis before any text is
  read, without naming a political figure or party in the image itself

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity (the impersonation call)
- **Image concept:** an abstract phone/call icon with a small "?" badge replacing where a face or
  logo would be, on a dark background
- **Subject:** the call itself, not a person — pure graphic/typographic thumbnail
- **Expression:** N/A (no face in this version)
- **Objects:** large glowing phone/call icon, small red "?" badge, faint YouTube-play-button-shaped
  silhouette rendered generically (no real logo)
- **Background:** dark, minimal, subtle grain
- **Colors:** white/red icon on near-black
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the icon
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** icon centered and dominant, badge small and secondary, bottom-third text space
- **Thumbnail text:** `HE WASN'T WHO HE SAID HE WAS`
- **Why it works:** the specific, undisputed, well-documented deception (someone impersonated a
  YouTube executive on a real investor call) is this channel's strongest cold-open material, per
  the sources log — the thumbnail sells the mystery of the call itself without naming who was on
  it, sidestepping any risk of implying it was Watson
- **CTR prediction:** high — mystery-object thumbnails paired with a specific, verifiable claim
  perform well on this channel
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render President Trump's face, name, or any recognizable political imagery in the
  thumbnail — the commutation is handled as a dated legal fact inside the video with strict
  neutrality; the thumbnail must not turn it into political content or bait
- Do not put any real logo (YouTube, Goldman Sachs, Ozy Media) or a real photo of Carlos Watson or
  Samir Rao without proper rights clearance — build the visual concept generically and add any
  verified real imagery in the editor
- Do not imply in any thumbnail that Watson was the one impersonating the executive — that
  specific claim is disputed and single-sourced; every thumbnail here either stays silent on who
  made the call or otherwise avoids the question entirely
- Do not combine the $70M total funding figure and the SEC's ~$50M fraud-tainted figure into one
  thumbnail number — if a funding figure appears in a thumbnail test, use only one, clearly labeled

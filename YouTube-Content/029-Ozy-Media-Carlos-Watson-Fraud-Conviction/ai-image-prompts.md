# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Ozy Media, YouTube,
Goldman Sachs, Google, Marriott-style trademarks N/A here but same principle applies), and no
real people's likenesses (Carlos Watson, Samir Rao, or any political figure) — add verified
text/photos/branding in the editor with proper rights clearance, not generated.

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the "CONVICTED" /
  "COMMUTED" stamp reveal and the "...BUT IT DIDN'T" shutdown-reversal reveal
- No snap-zoom, no punch-in, no emphasis camera move of any kind during the commutation text-card
  sequence — keep that segment visually flat and static, consistent with the neutral tone required

## Motion Graphics
- Phone/call icon animation for the cold open, hard-cutting to the Goldman Sachs/YouTube-executive
  text card
- Founder graphic and "the new and the next" tagline treatment for the founding segment
- Investor-name graphic and funding figure building toward "$70M+"
- NYT headline treatment for the September 2021 reveal, with a distinct "DISPUTED CLAIM — SINGLE
  SOURCE" card style (different visual treatment than confirmed-fact cards) for the Rao/Watson
  disputed-claim beat
- Resignation headline treatment for the fallout beat
- "OCTOBER 2021 — 'WE ARE SHUTTING DOWN'" card hard-cutting to "...BUT IT DIDN'T," with a calendar
  graphic counting forward to February 2023
- Indictment date stamp and "THE ACTUAL END" text
- Two-figure comparison graphic ("$70M+ Total Lifetime Funding" vs. "~$50M SEC Fraud-Tainted
  Fundraising Figure") — always shown side by side, never merged into one number, never using the
  same color for both figures
- "GUILTY" verdict-stamp graphic for the conviction beat
- Sentence-length and restitution figure graphics (116 months / ~$96M)
- Commutation text-card sequence: plain typography only — "MARCH 28, 2025" / "PRESIDENT TRUMP
  COMMUTED THE SENTENCE" / "THE $96M RESTITUTION ORDER WAS ALSO VOIDED" — no political imagery, no
  stylistic flourish that could read as celebratory or condemnatory
- Two-case comparison graphic (HeadSpin / Ozy Media) — conviction, sentence, outcome
- "Autopsy Report Card" end-graphic (full timeline: Founding → Call → False Shutdown → Real
  Shutdown → Conviction → Sentence → Commutation)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or claim is referenced (New York Times,
  Wikipedia, NPR, Variety, Forbes, Courthouse News Service, DOJ press release, CBS News/CNBC/
  Variety/Deadline)
- "DISPUTED CLAIM — SINGLE SOURCE" lower third every time the Watson-impersonation suggestion
  appears — no exceptions, and it must appear for the full duration of that beat, not just flash
- "$70M+ total lifetime funding" and "~$50M SEC fraud-tainted fundraising figure" as standing
  lower-third phrasings whenever either funding figure is referenced — never let one appear without
  the other's distinguishing label nearby if both numbers are on screen together
- Neutral "March 28, 2025 — Presidential Commutation" lower third during the commutation segment —
  no additional editorial text appended

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic media-office/courthouse imagery (no real
  readable logos/text — Ozy Media, YouTube, Goldman Sachs — without rights clearance, and no real
  photos of Carlos Watson, Samir Rao, or any political figure without proper rights clearance)
- The shutdown-reversal beat ("...BUT IT DIDN'T") gets a distinct, slightly surprised visual
  treatment — different pacing than the rest of the video, since it's the story's clearest "wait,
  what?" moment
- The commutation text cards animate in and hold — no bounce, no dramatic sting, no reveal
  flourish; treat it visually the same as any other neutral date-stamp moment in the video

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the timeline graphic and
  the two-case comparison graphic layers; avatar frame is fully static during the commutation
  segment specifically

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founding / The Raise: neutral, brisk, building energy — no ominous foreshadowing yet
- The Call: subtly tense under the reveal, no comedic stinger
- The Fallout: quiet, somber
- The Shutdown That Wasn't: a brief, distinct tonal lift on the reversal beat, then back to serious
- The Indictment and the Real End / The SEC's Number / The Conviction: sparse, clinical, matter-of-
  fact — let the graphics carry the information, not the score
- The Sentence: quiet, no triumphant swell
- The Commutation: silence or a single sustained low tone — no music that could read as either
  triumphant or tragic; this is the one segment in the video where the safest choice is the most
  neutral one
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly reflective educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout the timeline
- Distinct "reversal" SFX (a soft record-scratch or reverse-whoosh, non-comedic) on the "...BUT IT
  DIDN'T" shutdown beat only
- Gavel-strike SFX at the conviction verdict reveal
- No SFX at all during the commutation text-card sequence — let it land in silence
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and even-handed, especially during the
  disputed-claim beat and the commutation, neither of which should ever read as mocking, gleeful,
  or one-sided

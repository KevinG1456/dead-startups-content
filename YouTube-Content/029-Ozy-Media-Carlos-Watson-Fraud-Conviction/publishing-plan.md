rush ahead of the standard schedule; prioritize getting the neutral framing right over speed.

## Moderation Notes (specific to this package)
- Expect comments reacting to the commutation to skew politically charged regardless of how
  neutrally the video presents it — this is anticipated, not a sign the framing failed
- Do not respond to comments, in the creator's own voice or in pinned replies, with any political
  commentary in either direction; if replying at all, restate only the sourced facts (who, what,
  when)
- Do not pin or highlight comments that editorialize on the commutation's political dimension, even
  ones the creator personally agrees with — keep the pinned-comment section as factual as the video
  itself
- If a factual correction is needed (e.g., a date or figure), pin it plainly with a source, the same
  standard applied to any other correction on this channel

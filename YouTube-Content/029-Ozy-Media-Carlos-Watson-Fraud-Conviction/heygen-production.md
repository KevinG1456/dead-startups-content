# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious throughout — this is the most legally and politically
  sensitive title in the batch (it names a convicted individual AND a presidential commutation),
  and the delivery should read as measured journalism at every point, never sensationalism, never
  editorializing
- **Speaking pace:** measured; deliberate pauses before dates, dollar figures, and legal terms;
  noticeably slower and flatter during the commutation segment specifically
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the founding/raise segments; sharper focus
  during the impersonation-call segment without becoming gleeful or mocking; even-handed, careful
  delivery during the disputed Rao/Watson claim; steady, plain delivery for the conviction and
  sentence (state the facts, no triumph, no outrage); the commutation segment is the single most
  important tonal moment in the whole video — flat, neutral, almost clinical, zero inflection that
  could read as approval or disapproval
- **Facial expression:** neutral-serious baseline throughout; no smirking, no visible reaction
  (positive or negative) during the commutation segment specifically
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; stays still and minimal during the
  commutation text-card sequence rather than gesturing at all
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout — this builds the credibility this topic
  requires, especially during the two most sensitive segments (the disputed claim, and the
  commutation)

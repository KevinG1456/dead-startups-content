# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and slightly wry through the founding and Disney investment; earnest
  through the privacy promise; declining through the collapse; sharp and procedural through the FTC
  fight; a satisfied, resolved lift at the $50,000 resolution; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially the "41 states" and "$50,000" reveals — both need
  room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** genuine unease when reading the privacy-policy promise against the later attempted
  sale; procedural clarity through the FTC/settlement segments; quiet satisfaction at the resolution;
  no mockery of Toysmart's leadership — this is a systems story, not a villain story
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of dry irony at the $50,000-to-destroy-$45M
  reveal; visible care when reading the "no third party ever received this data" framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  numeral-contrast graphics and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

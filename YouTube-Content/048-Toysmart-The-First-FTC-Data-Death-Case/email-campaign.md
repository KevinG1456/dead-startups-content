Quick follow-up on this week's video about Toysmart.com.

The detail that surprised me most in the research: the FTC's FIRST proposed settlement wasn't even a
full block on selling the customer data. It would have allowed a sale — just to a "qualified buyer"
bound by the same privacy terms.

It took 41 state attorneys general formally objecting before the outcome changed. In the end, Disney's
own subsidiary paid $50,000 specifically to have the data destroyed instead of sold to anyone.

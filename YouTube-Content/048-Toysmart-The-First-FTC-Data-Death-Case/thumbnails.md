# Thumbnail Concepts

Rule for this package: ONE dominant idea per thumbnail — the $45M-to-$50,000 contrast, or the
"privacy policy vs. FOR SALE stamp" irony. Never attempt to illustrate the whole story in one image.

## Concept A (Recommended) — The Numeral Contrast
- Large "$45,000,000" crossed out or shrinking, next to a small "$50,000" — both labeled simply
  "IN" and "TO DESTROY IT"
- Disney-adjacent color palette (blues/whites) without using Disney's actual logo/trademark
- Minimal text: "WHY?" or no text at all, letting the numbers carry it

## Concept B — The Broken Promise
- A single document graphic with the words "WE WILL NEVER SHARE YOUR DATA" visible, a red "FOR SALE"
  stamp diagonally across it
- Dominant idea: the promise and the contradiction, nothing else competing for attention

## Concept C — The First-Ever Case
- FTC seal, large "FIRST EVER" stamp beneath it
- Dominant idea: historical primacy, appeals to viewers drawn to "origin story" framing

## Thumbnail Text Options (pick ONE, max 4-5 words)
- "$45M IN. $50K TO ERASE IT."
- "THEY PROMISED NEVER."
- "THE FIRST DATA CASE."
- "41 STATES SAID NO."

## A/B Testing Notes
- Concept A tests strongest for pure curiosity/numeral-shock audiences
- Concept B tests strongest for privacy-conscious/policy-interested audiences
- Concept C tests strongest for viewers who already watched package 020 (RadioShack) and respond to
  "origin story" framing

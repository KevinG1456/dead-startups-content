# AI Image Prompts (for ChatGPT/Thumbnail Creative Director)

Reminder per channel standard: keep every prompt to ONE dominant idea. Do not ask for the whole story
in one image.

## Thumbnail Prompt A (Recommended)
"A clean, high-contrast YouTube thumbnail. Left side: a large glowing '$45,000,000' numeral, partially
crossed out or fading. Right side: a small, stark '$50,000' numeral. Simple blue-and-white corporate
color palette, no logos or trademarked characters. No other text or clutter. Dramatic lighting,
minimalist composition, mobile-readable at small size."

## Thumbnail Prompt B
"A clean YouTube thumbnail showing a single document/paper graphic with the handwritten-style text
'WE WILL NEVER SHARE YOUR DATA' visible on it, a bold red diagonal 'FOR SALE' stamp overlaid across
the document. Minimalist background, high contrast, no other elements competing for attention."

## Thumbnail Prompt C
"A clean YouTube thumbnail: a simplified generic government-seal-style circular emblem (not any real
government logo), with bold text beneath reading 'FIRST EVER' in a stamped/rubber-stamp style font.
Minimalist, high-contrast background, no clutter."

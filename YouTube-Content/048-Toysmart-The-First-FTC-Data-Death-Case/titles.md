# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Toysmart.com: spun off from Holt Company Dec 1998; Disney/Buena Vista took ~60% stake
Aug 1999 for ~$45M; ceased operations May 19 2000; forced into Chapter 11 Jun 26 2000; customer
database listed for sale despite "never share with third parties" privacy promise; FTC sued Jul 2000
(first-ever case of this kind); proposed "qualified buyer" settlement; 41 states objected; Jan 2001
Disney's Buena Vista paid $50,000 to have the data destroyed instead of sold. No third party ever
received the data. Direct legal ancestor of package 020's RadioShack case, 15 years later.

## Recommended Title
**"Disney Put in $45 Million. Then This Company Tried to Sell Your Kid's Data."**

Reasoning: leads with the sharpest self-contained numeral-plus-stakes hook in the package — a huge,
recognizable brand name (Disney) paired with the most viscerally uncomfortable specific detail
(children's data) creates curiosity and unease without requiring any prior knowledge of Toysmart.
Sets up the full downstream story (bankruptcy, attempted sale, FTC action, 41-state pushback, the
$50,000 destruction payment) as the video's resolution, and leaves room to explicitly connect forward
to package 020's RadioShack case mid-video as this case's direct legal descendant.

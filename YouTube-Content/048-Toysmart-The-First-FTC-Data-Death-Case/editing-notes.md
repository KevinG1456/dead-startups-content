# Editing Notes

## Pacing
- Keep Segments 6-8 (the FTC fight/qualified-buyer/41-states sequence) tight and punchy — this is the
  video's actual dramatic core and must not feel like a dry legal explainer. Use quick cuts between
  title cards to keep momentum.
- Segment 9 (the $50,000 resolution) is the emotional payoff — give it a full beat of pause before
  cutting to the mid-video CTA.

## Sourcing/Fact-Check Callouts for Editor
- Do NOT state a single clean "$45 million" on screen without the sourced caveat noted in
  `sources-log.txt` — either use "approximately $45 million" throughout, or include a brief on-screen
  citation note the first time the figure appears.
- Do NOT show or imply the data was ever sold — every visual involving the customer database after
  Segment 9 must reflect that it was destroyed, not transferred.
- Do NOT use the Disney corporate logo or any trademarked Disney characters/branding in graphics —
  use text-only "Disney" or "Buena Vista Internet Group" references instead, to avoid trademark/brand
  usage issues.

## Music/Tone
- Neutral, slightly procedural/investigative tone through the FTC-fight segments (think: measured
  true-crime-adjacent scoring, not comedic)
- A slight tonal lift at the $50,000 resolution — this is the closing "win," even if quiet

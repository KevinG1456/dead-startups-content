## LinkedIn
"A real case study in why 'build a great product' isn't a complete strategy — told through the same
team losing the same way twice, five years apart:

Life On Air, founded 2012 by Ben Rubin and Itai Danino, built Meerkat — the breakout star of SXSW
2015. The same day Twitter announced acquiring rival Periscope, Twitter cut Meerkat's access to its
follower-graph API with about two hours' notice. Meerkat shut down in 2016.

The team pivoted to Houseparty, acquired by Epic Games in 2019 for a reported $35 million. In March
2020, Houseparty exploded to 17.2 million downloads in a single month, and by August 2020 held a
4.9-star rating from over 500,000 App Store reviews — genuinely, quantifiably beloved. It still lost:
Zoom's default-at-work status, Apple's built-in Group FaceTime, and Facebook's Messenger Rooms all had
distribution Houseparty couldn't match. Installs fell 83% year-over-year by 2021. Epic shut it down
that October.

Same team. Two categories. The same underlying lesson, learned twice.

Full breakdown: [video link]

# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open two-star-fade reveal,
  and on the "4.9★ — 500,000+ REVIEWS" card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on "The Honest Caveat" segment's neutral feature-comparison
  card — this is the one beat in the video that should never feel rushed or defensive

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open two glowing five-star icons fading to gray, side by side
- "This isn't two separate stories. It's the same team. Twice." pattern-interrupt card, white text on
  black
- "SXSW 2015 — MEERKAT'S BREAKOUT" title card
- Countdown-style "2 HOURS' NOTICE" title card
- Neutral side-by-side feature-comparison card for the honest caveat (Meerkat vs. Periscope), framed
  evenly, no visual bias toward either side
- "SHUT DOWN — OCTOBER 4, 2016" title card
- "ACQUIRED BY EPIC GAMES — $35M — JUNE 2019" title card
- Steep download-spike graph (533K → 17.2M)
- "4.9★ — 500,000+ REVIEWS" title card
- "47M → 8M — DOWN 83%" title card
- "SHUTDOWN ANNOUNCED — SEPTEMBER 9, 2021" title card
- Dual-timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: Meerkat's arc (2015-2016) and Houseparty's arc
  (2016-2021) shown stacked, both ending in an identical visual "shutdown" beat
- Running "ROUND 1 / ROUND 2" corner tag, switching at the Segment 5 transition

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (CNN Money, TechCrunch,
  Variety, Washington Post, Axios, NoCamels, Sequoia Capital, NFX)
- "Contested — some reporting favors Periscope's polish" standing lower third during the honest-caveat
  segment — no exceptions
- "Epic Games' own stated reason" lower third on the shutdown-announcement quote
- Package 008 cross-reference tag briefly during the Meerkat segment ("fuller story: package 008")

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic conference/living-room imagery — no real
  readable logos/trademarks (Meerkat, Houseparty, Twitter, Periscope, Zoom, Epic Games) without rights
  clearance
- The two-star-fade cold-open graphic should animate both stars fading at the exact same speed and
  timing, reinforcing "this happened twice, identically" before any narration explains why
- The download-spike-then-collapse graph for Houseparty should use the same visual style/axes as any
  Meerkat usage graph shown earlier, so the repeated shape reads as intentional, not coincidental
- The dual-timeline graphic should animate both arcs simultaneously reaching their shutdown point,
  landing together for the strongest possible "same ending" visual payoff

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the download-spike graph, the
  acclaim reveal, and the dual-timeline graphic in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the honest-caveat framing card — no camera
  movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- SXSW 2015 / The Climb: bright, energetic, capturing the festival-breakout excitement
- The Cutoff / The Honest Caveat: sharp tonal drop, then measured and even-handed — no bias toward
  either app
- Meerkat's Shutdown: quiet, restrained
- The Pivot and the Sale: a measured, hopeful rebuild
- The Lockdown Explosion: bright, urgent, capturing 2020's chaotic energy
- The Real Acclaim: warm, genuine — this is the video's one unambiguously positive beat
- The Collapse Anyway: uneasy, declining, mirroring the earlier Meerkat arc's tone intentionally
- Houseparty's Shutdown: quiet, restrained — deliberately echoing Meerkat's shutdown cue
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout, across both apps' timelines
- A distinct "countdown tick" SFX under the "2 HOURS' NOTICE" card
- A soft "star chime" SFX on the 4.9-star reveal
- A sharp "cutoff" SFX (distinct from the countdown tick) at the moment access is described as revoked,
  reused identically for both apps' collapse beats to reinforce the pattern
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays warm-but-analytical throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the dual-timeline's final simultaneous beat in The Actual Lesson

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Meerkat, Houseparty,
Twitter, Periscope, Zoom, Epic Games), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Ben
Rubin, Itai Danino, Sima Sistani) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in
the editor with proper sourcing, not generated.

Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Strong dual-nostalgia pull (2015 SXSW
culture, 2020 COVID culture) gives this package broader general-audience potential than most of this
channel's cap-table-mechanics packages — no urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

Broader-than-usual crossover for this channel: general audience with nostalgic memory of either app,
plus the core founder/product-strategy audience studying distribution-vs-product lessons. Pair
distribution accordingly — this package can lean harder into general social reach than most of the
channel's recent cap-table-focused packages.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to a "Product vs. Distribution" or general
strategy-lessons playlist if one exists, alongside any other packages covering similar strategic
mechanisms. Cross-reference package 008 (Meerkat/Viddy) explicitly as a companion piece specifically
for the platform-API-dependency angle.

since it covers the same underlying Meerkat event through a different, complementary lens.

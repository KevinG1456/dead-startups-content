# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram both Meerkat
and Houseparty's full stories into a single image.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** two identical star-rating graphics (five stars, glowing) each paired with a small
  "app icon" placeholder that fades to gray/off beneath it
- **Main subject:** the "loved it, still lost" contradiction, shown twice
- **Emotion:** disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint app-grid texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the star ratings
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** two star-rating-to-fade pairs side by side, bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic five-star rating icon, generic app-icon placeholder shapes
- **Thumbnail text:** `LOVED BY MILLIONS. GONE TWICE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** gold stars, gray fade, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the visible fade from glowing stars to gray communicates the entire thesis
  before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Image concept:** the two-star-rating-fading-pairs graphic from Winning Concept above
- **Subject:** the "loved it, still lost" pattern, graphic-led, shown twice
- **Expression:** N/A (no face in this version)
- **Objects:** generic five-star icons, generic app-icon placeholder shapes fading to gray
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint app-grid texture
- **Colors:** gold stars, gray fade, near-black background
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the stars
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** two pairs side by side, bottom-third text
- **Thumbnail text:** `LOVED BY MILLIONS. GONE TWICE.`
- **Why it works:** the repeated pattern (shown visually twice) communicates the "same team, same
  ending, twice" thesis instantly
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three — repeated-pattern thumbnails create strong instant curiosity
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic, general nostalgia audience

## Thumbnail Version B — Curiosity (Same-founder reveal angle)
- **Image concept:** two generic app-icon placeholder shapes connected by a single line to one small
  generic human-silhouette icon between them
- **Subject:** the "same person built both" reveal, pure graphic/typographic thumbnail
- **Expression:** N/A (no face — avoid rendering a real, identifiable likeness of Ben Rubin)
- **Objects:** two app-icon placeholders, one connecting silhouette icon
- **Background:** dark, minimal, subtle grain
- **Colors:** white icons, single accent color on the connecting line, near-black base
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the connecting silhouette
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** app icons on either side, silhouette centered between them, bottom-third text space
- **Thumbnail text:** `SAME TEAM. TWO APPS. SAME ENDING.`
- **Why it works:** the least-known connection in the whole story — most viewers don't know Meerkat and
  Houseparty share a founding team
- **CTR prediction:** high — surprising-connection framing performs strongly
- **Best audience:** search + suggested traffic, viewers with nostalgic familiarity with either app

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority (Distribution lesson angle)
- **Image concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/analytical expression, a small red "DISTRIBUTION
  > PRODUCT" document tag in the corner
- **Subject:** host/avatar, three-quarter crop
- **Expression:** serious, analytical, direct eye contact
- **Objects:** small red document/chart icon tag in the corner
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Colors:** desaturated, single warm key light on the face, red accent only on the corner tag
- **Lighting:** dramatic single top-down light
- **Angle:** slight low angle, three-quarter crop
- **Composition:** face fills most of frame, small tag in the corner, bottom-third text space
- **Thumbnail text:** `WHY THE BEST PRODUCT DOESN'T ALWAYS WIN.`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; signals this video's educational/
  strategy-lesson angle clearly
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

| C | Authority | "Why the best product doesn't always win" framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Ben Rubin, Itai Danino, or Sima
  Sistani in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Meerkat, Houseparty, Twitter/Periscope, Zoom, or Epic Games logos/wordmarks in
  generated thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in
  the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not claim in any thumbnail text that Meerkat was objectively better than Periscope — that specific
  claim isn't cleanly sourced; keep thumbnail claims to what's well-documented (acclaim, downloads,
  ratings, the shared team)

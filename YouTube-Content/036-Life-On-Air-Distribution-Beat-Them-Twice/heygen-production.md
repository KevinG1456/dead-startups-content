# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** bright and building through SXSW 2015; a sharp dip at the cutoff; measured and
  even-handed through the honest caveat; quiet at Meerkat's shutdown; hopeful through the pivot; bright
  and urgent through the lockdown explosion; warm and genuine through the acclaim reveal; uneasy through
  the collapse; quiet and restrained at Houseparty's shutdown, deliberately echoing Meerkat's tone
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the honest-caveat framing ("some reporting
  says Periscope was actually more polished") and the "same team, twice" reveal lines — both need room
  to land clearly and evenly
- **Emotional tone:** genuine excitement during both apps' rise beats; scrupulously even-handed, not
  defensive, during the Meerkat/Periscope comparison; warm and sincere during the Houseparty acclaim
  segment; restrained, not melodramatic, through both shutdown beats
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine enthusiasm at both apps' peak
  moments; visible care and evenness when reading the honest-caveat framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during both
  download-spike graphs and the dual-timeline in The Actual Lesson
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

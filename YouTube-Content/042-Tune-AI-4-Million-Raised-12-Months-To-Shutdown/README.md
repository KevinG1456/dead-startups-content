# 042 — This AI Startup Rebranded for the ChatGPT Boom. 12 Months Later, It Was Gone.

**Package status:** Filled in and sourced — built around Tune AI's fully documented public history
(Crunchbase, TechStartups.com, LinkedIn, company X account — full citations in `sources-log.txt`). This
package REPLACES skipped batch title #13 ("per-seat pricing in an AI world: the first SaaS casualties of
usage-based disruption") from the "dead-saas-30" batch — the original title was a real, current
phenomenon but had no real, verifiable single-company anchor after the first research pass (candidates
were either damaged-but-alive public companies like Chegg, or an unverifiable single-source claim). Tune
AI was researched fresh and cold on 2026-08-16 specifically to fill this slot with a real, named, funded,
fully-dead company.

**The real case:** Founded in 2018 in San Francisco as NimbleBox, an MLOps/AI-infrastructure tooling
company, by co-founders Anshuman Pandey, Naman Maheshwari, and Rohan Pooniwala. On February 6, 2024 — as
the generative-AI boom accelerated — the company rebranded as Tune AI, repositioning around two
products: Tune Chat (a multi-model AI chat interface) and Tune Studio (a platform to deploy and fine-tune
LLMs). The company had raised approximately $4.4 million total from Accel, Flipkart Ventures, Together
Fund, Speciale Invest, Techstars, and CSVP Fund, including a $250K round as recently as March 27, 2024.
On February 28, 2025 — almost exactly 12 months after the rebrand — Tune AI shut down entirely, took
both products offline, and processed refunds for affected customers. Per retrospective coverage, many
users were free developers rather than paying enterprises, while infrastructure costs stayed high and
margins collapsed as cloud providers and open-source tooling offered comparable capability for less.

**The bigger pattern this video makes concrete:** LLM inference costs fell roughly 95% between 2023 and
2025 (GPT-4-quality output: ~$30/million input tokens in 2023 to under $0.50/million by 2025), and a
mid-size company's chatbot costs fell from $8,000–$15,000/month in 2023 to under $200/month by 2025. Any
company whose product was "a paid wrapper around the AI" got hit from both directions at once: the raw
AI got radically cheaper, and it also got radically more capable and bundled for free by the platforms
that host it. Tune AI is a real, dated, fully-documented single-company example of exactly that
mechanism.

**Framing requirement:** No direct founder quote explaining the shutdown decision was found — present
the "free users, thin margins, commoditized tooling" cause as the best-sourced available explanation
from retrospective coverage, not as a direct company statement. Never invent specific revenue or
customer-count figures. Keep tone analytical and structural, not mocking — this is a business-model
story, not a scandal.

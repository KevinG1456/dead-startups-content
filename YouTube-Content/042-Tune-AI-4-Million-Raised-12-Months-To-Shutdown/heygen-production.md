# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and matter-of-fact through the founding and rebrand; a brief confident
  lift through the funding segment; increasingly weighty and urgent through the inference-cost-collapse
  and commoditization segments; quiet and sobering through the shutdown; clear and reflective through
  the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "$30 to $0.50" reveal and the
  honesty-flag framing card — both need room to land clearly and precisely
- **Emotional tone:** structural gravity, not mockery — this is a company caught by market forces, not
  a company that made an obvious mistake; genuine weight on the "12 months after the rebrand" detail
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine surprise at the inference-cost
  collapse reveal; visible care and precision when reading the "no direct founder statement found"
  honesty framing card
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the numeral-
  collapse graphics and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Tune AI: founded 2018 as NimbleBox (San Francisco; co-founders Anshuman Pandey, Naman
Maheshwari, Rohan Pooniwala), MLOps/AI-infra tooling. Rebranded to Tune AI Feb 6, 2024 for the
generative-AI boom (Tune Chat + Tune Studio). ~$4.4M raised total (Accel, Flipkart Ventures, Together
Fund, Speciale Invest, Techstars, CSVP Fund), incl. $250K round Mar 27, 2024. Shut down Feb 28, 2025 —
~12 months after rebrand. Cause (best-sourced, not a direct quote): free users not paying enterprises,
thin margins, cloud providers/open-source bundled comparable tooling for less. Macro context: LLM
inference cost fell ~95% 2023-2025; mid-size chatbot cost fell from $8-15K/month to under $200/month.

## Recommended Title
**"This AI Startup Rebranded for the ChatGPT Boom. 12 Months Later, It Was Gone."**

Reasoning: leads with the sharpest, most self-contained irony in the package — a company repositioned
specifically to catch the generative-AI wave, and the same wave overtook it within a year. Works fully
truncated on mobile at the first sentence alone. Sets up the full downstream story (the macro
inference-cost collapse, the specific 12-month rebrand-to-shutdown arc, the free-users/thin-margin
cause) as the video's proof of exactly how that irony played out in practice.

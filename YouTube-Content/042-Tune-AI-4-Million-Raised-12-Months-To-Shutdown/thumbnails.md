# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Never invent specific revenue or
customer-count figures; only use the sourced $4.4M, $30-to-$0.50-per-million-tokens, and 12-month
figures.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "$30" numeral collapsing/shrinking down into a tiny "$0.50" numeral, with a
  price tag icon breaking apart around it
- **Main subject:** the scale of the inference-cost collapse that defines the whole story
- **Emotion:** shock/disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint falling-price-chart-line texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight tracking the collapse from large to small
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** large "$30" origin numeral, tiny "$0.50" numeral at the collapse point, bottom-third
  title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic numeral graphics, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `AI GOT 95% CHEAPER. THIS COMPANY DIDN'T SURVIVE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** large red "$30," tiny fading gray "$0.50," near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the size mismatch between the large origin numeral and the tiny collapsed
  numeral communicates the entire thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Anshuman Pandey, Naman Maheshwari, or
  Rohan Pooniwala in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put real Tune AI, NimbleBox, Accel, Techstars, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google logos/wordmarks in
  generated thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in
  the editor with proper sourcing
- Never invent or display an unsourced revenue/customer-count figure in any thumbnail text — only use
  the sourced $4.4M, $30-to-$0.50-per-million-tokens, and 12-month figures

# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. This package's topicality (AI-startup
failures are an active, ongoing 2025-2026 news trend) gives it a real, if modest, urgency premium —
don't hold it as long as a pure-evergreen package like Friendster; publish within the channel's normal
one-to-two-week queue window.

## Target Audience
Founders/PMs building products on top of foundation models, SaaS operators watching the "AI eats SaaS"
trend, investors evaluating AI-wrapper startups, and this channel's regular startup-finance/failure
audience.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also strong fit for any dedicated "AI Era Casualties" or "AI
Startup Graveyard" playlist if the channel builds one out, since this is the first package centered on a
company killed by its core technology's cost collapsing rather than an execution mistake.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element. Consider cross-referencing package 024 (Olive AI) as a
thematically adjacent "AI-era startup death" pairing if that package is published and available.

## Cards
Card at the "$30 → $0.50" title card (end of Segment 5, ~6:45) linking to the "Startup Autopsies"
playlist for viewers hooked by the reveal; card at the mid-video CTA (~9:45) reinforcing the "killed by
the market itself, not a bad decision" framing as distinct from the channel's usual stories; optional
third card at the start of The Actual Lesson (~10:00) linking directly to a pinned comment or community
post with the sourced full-arc timeline graphic.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library — and if you're building something on top of AI
right now, tell me in the comments: what's your answer to what happens when the model providers give
away what you're charging for?"

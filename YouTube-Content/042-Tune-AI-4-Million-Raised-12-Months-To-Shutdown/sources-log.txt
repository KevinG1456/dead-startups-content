FOUNDING AND HISTORY — sourced
- Founded 2018 in San Francisco as NimbleBox (also styled NimbleBox.AI), by Anshuman Pandey, Naman
  Maheshwari, and Rohan Pooniwala. Original focus: MLOps / AI-infrastructure tooling to simplify
  building and deploying machine learning models — years before the generative-AI boom.
Source: YourStory ("Flipkart and Accel-backed Tune AI is enabling enterprises..."), ynos.in startup
profile, GitHub org "NimbleBoxAI".

THE REBRAND — precisely dated
- February 6, 2024: NimbleBox rebranded as Tune AI, repositioning from general MLOps tooling to
  generative-AI-specific products: **Tune Chat** (a multi-model AI chat interface) and **Tune Studio**
  (a platform to deploy and fine-tune large language models).
Source: LinkedIn company post ("#tuneai #rebranding," Feb 2024), company X/Twitter account
(@Tunehq_ai), tooldirectory.ai retrospective.

THE SHUTDOWN — precisely dated
- February 28, 2025 — almost exactly 12 months after the February 6, 2024 rebrand — Tune AI ceased
  operations. Both Tune Chat and Tune Studio were taken offline. The company stated it processed
  refunds for affected customers as part of the wind-down.
Source: TechStartups.com ("Top AI Startups That Shut Down in 2025: What Founders Can Learn," Dec 9,
2025); tooldirectory.ai ("What Happened to Tune AI? Platform Shutdown").
*** [VERIFY]: no direct shutdown letter, founder statement, or customer email was located in this
research pass. The refund detail and shutdown date are sourced to third-party retrospective coverage,
not a primary company announcement — present as sourced fact (the date/refund claim is corroborated
across multiple retrospectives) but do not attribute a specific quote to any named founder. ***

THE STATED CAUSE — sourced to third-party analysis, not a founder quote
- Per retrospective coverage: many of Tune AI's users were free developers "kicking the tires" rather
  than paying enterprises, while infrastructure and inference costs remained high — shrinking margins.
- Simultaneously, major cloud providers and the open-source AI ecosystem released comparable tooling,
  often at lower cost or bundled for free, eroding Tune AI's differentiation.
Source: TechStartups.com (Dec 2025 retrospective).

MARKET CONTEXT — THE BROADER "AI ATE THE PRICING MODEL" TREND — precisely sourced
This is the mechanism that makes Tune AI a representative case, not an isolated failure:
- Inference cost for GPT-3.5-equivalent output fell from approximately $20 per million tokens
  (November 2022) to approximately $0.07 per million tokens (October 2024) — roughly a 280x decrease in
  about two years.
- Inference cost for GPT-4-quality output fell from approximately $30 per million input tokens (2023)
  to under $0.50 per million tokens (2025) — a decline of roughly 95% over two years.
- Running a customer-facing AI chatbot at moderate volume cost a mid-size company an estimated
  $8,000–$15,000 per month on GPT-4 in 2023; the same workload on newer, smaller/cheaper models (e.g.
  GPT-4.1 Nano, Gemini 2.5 Flash) cost under $200 per month by 2025.
- Competitive pressure compounded the collapse: by late 2024 at least six providers offered
  frontier-class models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Mistral, Meta via API partners).
  DeepSeek's entry in particular forced across-the-board repricing; Google introduced free tiers for its
  Gemini Flash models; Anthropic cut Opus pricing by roughly 67%.
Source: TokenCost ("AI Price Index: LLM Costs Dropped 300x, 2023-2026"), ValueAdd VC ("AI Inference: 95%
Cost Cut in Two Years"), n8n Lab News ("AI's Price Floor Just Collapsed: OpenAI Cuts Costs by 80
Percent"), The GTM Newsletter ("The Token Price Collapse").
- Broader 2025 startup-failure data point for scale: an estimated 3,800 of the roughly 14,000+ AI
  startups that launched in 2024 had shut down by end of 2025 (a ~27% failure rate within about 18
  months), with several thousand more closing in early 2026.
Source: TechStartups.com (Dec 2025).

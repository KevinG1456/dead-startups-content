# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open logo-swap-to-shutdown
  reveal, and on the "$30 → $0.50" collapse title card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on "No direct statement from Tune AI's founders about why they
  shut down has been found publicly" — the single most important honesty flag in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open "NIMBLEBOX → TUNE AI" logo-swap animation, hard cut to a "SHUT DOWN" stamp
- "This isn't a story about a bad decision. It's a story about what happens when the thing you sell
  gets 95% cheaper in two years." pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- Generic AI-infrastructure dashboard graphic for the founding segment, "FOUNDED 2018 — NIMBLEBOX"
  title card
- "FEBRUARY 6, 2024 — REBRANDED AS TUNE AI" title card
- "$4.4 MILLION RAISED" title card with investor names listed
- Simple "YOU PAY → TUNE AI → THE UNDERLYING AI MODELS" layer diagram
- "$30 → $0.50 PER MILLION TOKENS" numeral-collapse graphic
- Logo wall of AI providers (generic/unbranded placeholders) with "FREE TIER" stamps appearing one by
  one
- "MOSTLY FREE USERS" title card
- "SHUT DOWN — FEBRUARY 28, 2025" title card with "12 MONTHS AFTER THE REBRAND" subtitle
- "3,800 OF 14,000+ AI STARTUPS — GONE WITHIN 18 MONTHS" title card
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founded (2018) → rebranded as Tune AI (Feb 2024) →
  $250K round (Mar 2024) → inference costs collapsing across 2024 → shutdown (Feb 2025)
- Running "cost per million tokens" corner counter ticking downward from Segment 5 onward, landing on
  "$0.50"

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Crunchbase,
  TechStartups.com, LinkedIn, public inference-pricing data)
- "Real, sourced figure" standing lower third on the $30-to-$0.50 and $4.4M reveals
- "Best-sourced explanation available — not a direct company statement" lower third on the
  free-users/thin-margin cause segment
- "Independently sourced industry pricing data, not Tune-AI-specific" lower third on the inference-cost
  segment

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static generic office/server-room imagery — no real readable logos/
  trademarks (Tune AI, NimbleBox, Accel, Techstars, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) without rights clearance
- The cold-open logo-swap-to-shutdown transition should animate slowly enough that both states are
  legible before the cut completes
- The "$30 → $0.50" collapse graphic should animate the numeral visibly shrinking in real time, landing
  on a clear, held final frame

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the numeral-collapse graphics,
  the logo-wall/free-tier animation, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "$30 → $0.50" reveal and the honesty-flag
  framing card — no camera movement competing with either for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding: measured, optimistic, early-stage-startup energy
- The Rebrand: a brief confident lift
- The Money: steady, credible, matter-of-fact
- What Was Actually Being Sold: neutral, explanatory
- The Floor Fell Out: tense, descending — the video's single biggest musical beat
- Everyone Started Giving It Away: uneasy, mounting pressure
- Who Was Actually Paying: quiet, sobering
- The Shutdown: quiet, restrained
- Not an Isolated Case: measured, widening scope
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2018 founding, Feb 2024 rebrand, Mar 2024
  funding round, Feb 2025 shutdown)
- A descending "collapse" SFX synced to the $30-to-$0.50 numeral-shrink animation
- Sharp, quiet "click" SFX as each "FREE TIER" stamp appears on the logo wall
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays measured and analytical throughout
- Light, sobering "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

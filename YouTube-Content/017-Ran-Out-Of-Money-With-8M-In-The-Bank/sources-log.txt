COMPANY / FOUNDERS
- Convoy — Seattle-based digital freight brokerage marketplace ("Uber for trucking"),
  founded 2015.
- Founders: Dan Lewis (CEO) and Grant Goodale, both former Amazon executives.
Source: GeekWire, Wikipedia.

FUNDING HISTORY
- Series C, September 2018: $185M raised, ~$1B valuation. New investors: T. Rowe Price,
  Lone Pine Capital.
- Series D, November 2019: $400M, co-led by Generation Investment Management and
  T. Rowe Price, ~$2.75B valuation.
- Series E, April 2022: $260M total — $160M in equity (led by Baillie Gifford and
  T. Rowe Price) + $100M in secured venture debt from Hercules Capital. Separately,
  a new $150M line of credit from J.P. Morgan. Valuation: $3.8B.
- Early/notable investors across rounds: Jeff Bezos (personally, reportedly via
  Bezos Expeditions), Bill Gates, Marc Benioff, Greylock, CapitalG.
- Lifetime total funding reported as ~$1.1B (Forge Global) to ~$828M (Tracxn) —
  sources vary; treat as "over $1B across its history" rather than citing one
  precise lifetime total.
Source: TechCrunch (Series C 2018, Series E April 2022), GeekWire (Series E, $3.8B
valuation), BusinessWire (April 2022 press release with the $160M/$100M/$150M
breakdown), CNBC (April 2022 valuation coverage), Tracxn/Forge Global (lifetime
funding figures — flagged as inconsistent between sources).

THE COLLAPSE
- 2022–2023: historic freight-market downturn (falling freight rates, overcapacity)
  hit Convoy's brokerage margins hard.
- February 2023: restructuring and layoffs.
- Headcount fell from a peak of ~1,500 (roughly a year before shutdown) to ~500 by
  the October 2023 shutdown.
- CEO Dan Lewis: the company spent 4+ months exhausting strategic options before
  concluding shutdown was necessary.
Source: TechCrunch (Feb 2023 restructuring coverage), CNBC (Oct 19 2023 shutdown
coverage, includes headcount figures and Lewis's memo).

THE SHUTDOWN AND FORECLOSURE
- Shutdown date: October 19, 2023 (memo sent to employees that Thursday morning).
- CEO Dan Lewis's memo: "We hoped this day would never come." Cites "an unprecedented
  freight market collapse" combined with "dramatic monetary tightening" as
  "the perfect storm" that "crushed our progress at the same time that it was
  crushing our logical strategic acquirer."
- Hercules Capital foreclosed on Convoy's assets — technology, IP, and accounts
  receivable — and took effective control of the business, subsequently marketing
  Convoy's technology to potential buyers.

THE OUTCOME
- Flexport acquired Convoy's technology stack in November 2023, retaining roughly
  50 former Convoy employees, including CEO Dan Lewis.
- Flexport explicitly did not acquire Convoy the company or its liabilities — only
  the technology and a subset of the team.
- Reported sale price: ~$16M per Wikipedia's sourcing; other outlets (FreightWaves,
  GeekWire, TechCrunch) describe the price as undisclosed.

GENERAL VENTURE DEBT MECHANISM (sourced separately — NOT specific to Convoy's case)
- Minimum unrestricted-cash covenants: commonly structured around a percentage of
  outstanding loan principal (illustrative range cited in secondary sources: roughly
  35% after milestones, up to 50–70% before milestones are met), based on the
  publicly-filed Hercules Technology Growth Capital loan agreement template
  structure referenced across multiple borrower SEC 10-Q/8-K filings on EDGAR.
- Material Adverse Change (MAC/MAE) clauses, cross-default provisions, and default
  interest rates (commonly cited around +4-5%) are standard features of venture
  debt agreements.
- Secured venture debt is typically backed by a blanket lien on company assets,
  sometimes including a tri-party control agreement with the company's bank that
  gives the lender rights to take control of deposit accounts upon default.
Source: Kruze Consulting (venture debt covenant/default educational content),
Foley Hoag "10 Things to Know About Venture Debt," RBCx debt covenant guidance,
SEC EDGAR-filed Hercules Technology Growth Capital loan agreement templates
(general structure reference, not Convoy's own agreement, which is not public).

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with a clear educational/explainer register during the mechanism-heavy sections
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the venture debt mechanics explanation and the funding-numbers segment — dense, technical content that needs room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the explainer and data segments, quieter gravity for the Fall section (Dan Lewis's memo, the human cost)
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine gravity when reading from Lewis's memo
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative hand movement during the "who's actually in control" stack-diagram explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

**Scene 10 — Final CTA + Outro**

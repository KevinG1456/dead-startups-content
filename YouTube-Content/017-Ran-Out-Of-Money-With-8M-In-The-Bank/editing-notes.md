# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the "$3.8B with padlock" reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- "$3.8B" valuation-with-padlock animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Funding-round timeline graphic building to the $3.8B peak (Series C → Series D → Series E)
- Parallel declining graphs for headcount (1,500 → 500) and freight-rate index during The Collapse
- "Who's actually in control" stack diagram: equity holders at the bottom, secured lender (Hercules) at the top
- "What to check before you sign" checklist graphic for the practical segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (CNBC, TechCrunch, GeekWire, FreightWaves, or the CEO's own memo)
- Legal-term definition lower third (e.g., "Material Adverse Change Clause," "Secured Lien") whenever a new technical term is introduced
- "General mechanism — not confirmed specific to this case" lower third during the venture-debt-covenant explainer section, to keep the honesty distinction visually clear

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and terms
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic freight/trucking-yard imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- Memo text (Dan Lewis's shutdown memo quote) types on screen as it's read aloud in The Fall section

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the flow-diagram/checklist graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Explainer section (What Is Venture Debt): neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- The Raise: brisk, confident, slightly triumphant undertone (building to the peak)
- The Collapse / Who Actually Controls the Wind-Down: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual diagrams carry the tension
- The Fall: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Padlock-click/chain-clank SFX on the "$3.8B locked" reveal in the cold open
- Soft "click" SFX as each step in the funding-timeline or control-stack diagram populates
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and educational, respectful of the real people (1,000+ employees) affected by this shutdown

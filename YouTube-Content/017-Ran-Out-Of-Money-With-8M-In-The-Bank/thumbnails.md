# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — a $3.8B valuation shrinking to almost nothing, controlled by someone other than the company.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "$3.8B" figure with a padlock/chain graphic wrapped around it, an arrow pointing down to a much smaller number
- **Main subject:** the valuation-collapse-under-lender-control metaphor
- **Emotion:** shock/injustice
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the central figure
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** big number centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** padlock/chain graphic, downward arrow
- **Thumbnail text:** `$3.8B. THEN THEIR LENDER TOOK OVER.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green dollar figure, red padlock/chain accent, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the huge-number-with-a-lock-on-it contrast does the communicating before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "$3.8B" centered large, small padlock icon overlapping the corner
- **Text:** `THIS WASN'T THEIRS TO CONTROL ANYMORE`
- **Why it works:** numeral-led format with a small contradicting icon creates immediate curiosity about the mechanism
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well, and the padlock detail adds intrigue
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the $3.8B-with-padlock combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$3.8B. THEN THEIR LENDER TOOK OVER.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of a real, sourced contradiction (the valuation vs. who ended up in control) paired with the visual proof in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small padlock-on-dollar-sign tag in the corner
- **Text:** `THE CLAUSE THAT ENDS STARTUPS`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "the clause" framing signals a practical, cautionary explainer
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

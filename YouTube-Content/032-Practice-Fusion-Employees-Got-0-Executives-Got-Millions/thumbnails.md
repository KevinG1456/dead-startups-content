# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. Do not try to cram the sale price,
the executive carve-out, and the opioid-kickback subplot into a single image. Each of those gets its
own version below, or lives inside the video/Shorts instead of the thumbnail.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** two stacked cash/numeral graphics side by side — a large glowing "$7M" stack on one
  side, a flat, dim "$0" on the other, with a small connecting label suggesting "same company, same
  sale"
- **Main subject:** the executive-vs-employee payout contradiction
- **Emotion:** injustice/disbelief
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint document/paperwork texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the "$7M" side, the "$0" side left dim and flat
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** "$7M" dominant and glowing on one side, "$0" small and flat on the other,
  bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic cash-stack/numeral icons, no real company logos
- **Thumbnail text:** `$7 MILLION. $0. SAME COMPANY. SAME SALE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** gold/warm glow on the "$7M" side, cold gray/flat on the "$0" side, near-black
  background
- **Contrast strategy:** the visible glow-vs-flat contrast between the two numerals communicates the
  entire thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Ryan Howard, Tom Langan, Jonathan
  Malek, or Steven Mack in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts
  only; attribute real names verbally/on-screen in the video itself
- Do not put the real Practice Fusion or Allscripts logos/wordmarks in generated thumbnail art — build
  the visual concept generically and add any verified real branding in the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not reference Purdue Pharma, OxyContin, or opioid imagery in any thumbnail — that subplot belongs
  inside the video/Shorts with full context, not compressed into a thumbnail where it could read as
  exploitative or as this package's main subject
- Never imply in a thumbnail that "the founders" collectively got $0 — Version A's framing is
  specifically about employees vs. executives, which is the sourced claim

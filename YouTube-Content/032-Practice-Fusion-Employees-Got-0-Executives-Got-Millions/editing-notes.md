# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open "$7M" vs. "$0"
  reveal, and again on the "SAME COMPANY. SAME SALE." card
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the "Malek co-founded this company. He is also one of the
  executives who was paid from the carve-out" framing card — this is the single most important
  clarifying beat in the whole video and should never feel rushed

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open large glowing "$7M" numeral next to a small flat "$0," hard cut to "SAME COMPANY. SAME
  SALE." text card
- "$1.5 BILLION" projected-valuation card during the near-IPO segment
- "FOUNDER REMOVED — AUGUST 14, 2015" title card
- Dated timeline graphic for the opioid contract (2013 conversations → Aug 2015 Howard departs → Mar
  2016 contract signed), Howard's attributed quote card ("not in charge of the company when the
  opioid contract was signed") held clearly on screen, sourced lower third visible
- "DOJ INVESTIGATION OPENS — MARCH 2017" title card
- "$250M" numeral shrinking to "$100M" during the price-collapse segment, dated timeline beneath it
- Waterfall/cascade graphic: preferred stock tiers filling first, an empty pool at the bottom labeled
  "COMMON STOCK" — held on screen long enough to land as a genuine reveal
- "$7M" (Langan) and "$2.25M" (Malek) numeral cards, immediately followed by the company's own
  attributed defense-statement quote card
- Standing "This video never says 'the founders got $0' — here's why" framing card, reappearing
  briefly whenever payout figures are on screen
- "$0" flat numeral with three generic employee-silhouette icons for the employee-outcome segment
- "$145 MILLION SETTLEMENT — JANUARY 2020" title card
- "GUILTY PLEA — 2021 / SENTENCED — 2024" title card for the Mack obstruction case, with a standing
  "sales director — not a founder, not part of the carve-out" tag
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founding (2005) → near-IPO talk (2015) → founder
  removed (Aug 2015) → opioid contract signed (Mar 2016) → DOJ investigation opens (Mar 2017) → offer
  collapses (2017-2018) → sale closes (Jan 2018) → payout split reported (Jan 2018) → DOJ settlement
  (Jan 2020) → obstruction plea (2021) → sentencing (2024) — three visually distinct color threads for
  Howard, Langan/Malek, and Mack throughout

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (TechCrunch, CNBC,
  Fortune, Forbes, Wikipedia, DOJ/Oversight.gov, NPR, Fierce Healthcare, VTDigger)
- "Outcome not confirmed in available reporting" standing lower third any time Ryan Howard's post-2015
  financial outcome is discussed — no exceptions, this is the single most important lower-third rule
  in the package
- "Howard's own words, per FierceHealthcare" lower third whenever his attributed quote is on screen
- "Company's own statement to CNBC" lower third on the Practice Fusion defense quote
- "Sales director — separate from the founder and executive groups above" standing tag any time
  Steven Mack is on screen

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/document imagery — no real readable
  logos/trademarks (Practice Fusion, Allscripts, Purdue Pharma) without rights clearance
- The waterfall graphic should animate as a literal liquid/fill effect rising through preferred tiers
  and stopping before reaching the common-stock pool, so the mechanism reads physically, not just as a
  static chart
- The "$7M vs $0" reveal should animate with the "$7M" side rising/glowing in while the "$0" side stays
  static and flat — the contrast in motion should reinforce the contrast in outcome
- The three-color timeline threads (Howard / Langan-Malek / Mack) should visually diverge at the
  ouster beat (Aug 2015) and never re-converge, reinforcing that these are three separate stories
  inside one company

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the waterfall graphic, the
  payout-split reveal, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the "Howard's outcome is not confirmed" framing
  card — no camera movement competing with that text for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- The Founding / The Near-IPO: measured, optimistic, establishing energy
- The Ouster: measured, slightly somber, no melodrama
- The Contract / The Investigation Opens: dry, clinical, document-forward — never use tonally "dark"
  or ominous opioid-specific musical cues; keep this segment feeling like careful reporting, not true
  crime
- The Price Collapse: tense, numbers-forward
- The Waterfall: dry and analytical, building tension as the fill animation rises
- The Carve-Out: a deliberate tonal shift — slightly uncomfortable, ironic, not triumphant
- The Employees: quiet, restrained, respectful — no melodrama, let the named dollar figures carry the
  weight
- The DOJ Settlement / The Obstruction Case: even-handed, investigative
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2005 founding, 2015 near-IPO/ouster, 2016
  contract, 2017 investigation, 2017-2018 price collapse, 2018 sale, 2020 settlement, 2021/2024
  obstruction case)
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on the "$7M" and "$2.25M" numeral reveals — a distinctly different,
  duller "thud" or silence on the "$0" reveal, reinforcing the contrast audibly
- Soft "liquid fill" SFX synced to the waterfall graphic's animation
- No comedic stingers anywhere in the opioid-contract or DOJ-settlement segments — tone stays
  investigative and precise throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the full-arc timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

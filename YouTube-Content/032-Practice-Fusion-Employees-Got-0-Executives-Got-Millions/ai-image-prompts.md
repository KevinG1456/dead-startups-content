# AI Image Prompts

Simple, single-idea prompts. No rendered real text, no real logos/branding (Practice Fusion,
Allscripts, Purdue Pharma), and no photorealistic likeness of any real named person (Ryan Howard, Tom
Langan, Jonathan Malek, Steven Mack) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography in
the editor with proper sourcing, not generated. Do not include opioid/pill/medication imagery in any
thumbnail or key graphic — keep that subplot's visuals restricted to clearly-labeled documentary/
courtroom-style scene artwork inside the video itself, not iconography that could read as flippant.

## Thumbnail Prompts
**Prompt A (recommended primary):** "YouTube thumbnail, a large glowing gold cash-stack icon on the
left side, a small flat gray zero numeral shape on the right side, dark background with faint
paperwork/document texture, single spotlight on the left side only, high contrast, empty space at
bottom third for title text, no readable logos, currency symbols, or brand names, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt B:** "YouTube thumbnail, a large numeral shape shrinking diagonally into a smaller numeral
shape, a small document/magnifying-glass icon positioned at the pivot point, dark background, single
spotlight tracking the shrink, high contrast, empty space at bottom third for title text, no readable
numbers or text, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

**Prompt C:** "YouTube thumbnail, tight three-quarter crop portrait of a generic host avatar,
dramatic single top-down light, serious analytical expression, dark background, small red
document/chart icon tag graphic in the corner, photorealistic, cinematic, high contrast, not a real
identifiable person, 16:9 --ar 16:9"

# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Practice Fusion has lower general name
recognition than this channel's celebrity-founder or consumer-brand packages, so this one leans more
heavily on the founder/operator/employee-equity niche audience and evergreen "liquidation preference"
search terms than on cold browse traffic — no urgency premium, hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience specifically: founders and employees who hold or are negotiating startup equity,
and investors studying cap-table structuring. Narrower general-audience crossover than 030/031 — pair
distribution accordingly (lean into startup/VC-adjacent communities and search terms rather than
broad social reach).

already anchors one, the same playlist as 018/Down Rounds) specifically for cap-table and financing-

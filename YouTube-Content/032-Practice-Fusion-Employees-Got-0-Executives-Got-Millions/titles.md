# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Practice Fusion: founded 2005 by Ryan Howard, Alan Wong, Jonathan Malek, and Matthew
Douglass; raised ~$157M; near-IPO talk at a $1.5B valuation in 2015; Howard ousted as CEO Aug 14,
2015, replaced by Tom Langan; a March 2016 Purdue Pharma kickback deal (signed after Howard's
departure) helped trigger a DOJ investigation that collapsed Allscripts' offer from $225-250M (May
2017) to $100M (closed Jan 8, 2018); CNBC reported employees/common stockholders got $0 while a
management carve-out paid CEO Tom Langan $7M and co-founder/exec Jonathan Malek $2.25M; Jan 2020,
Practice Fusion paid a $145M DOJ settlement (first-ever criminal case against an EHR vendor); 2021/
2024, sales director Steven Mack pleaded guilty to and was sentenced for obstructing that
investigation. Every title below states precisely what's sourced — employees/common stockholders got
$0, executives got millions — and never claims "the founders" collectively got nothing, since
co-founder Jonathan Malek was paid $2.25 million and Ryan Howard's specific outcome is unconfirmed.

"founders" framing to what the reporting actually supports — a sharper, more specific, more

executives got millions" is a concrete, sourced, and more provocative contrast). Works fully truncated
on mobile at the first sentence alone, sets up the core cap-table-waterfall mechanism as the video's
thesis without needing to front-load the opioid-kickback subplot in the title itself. Matches the
title already used in this package's README.

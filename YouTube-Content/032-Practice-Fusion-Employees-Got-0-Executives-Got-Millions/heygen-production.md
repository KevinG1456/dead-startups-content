# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and establishing during the founding/near-IPO segments; a brief somber
  dip at the ouster beat; dry and clinical through the opioid-contract and investigation segments;
  tense and analytical through the price-collapse and waterfall segments; restrained, slightly ironic
  through the carve-out reveal; quiet and respectful through the employees segment
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the "Malek co-founded this company. He is
  also one of the executives who was paid from the carve-out" framing line and the "Howard's outcome
  is not confirmed" disclosure — both need room to land clearly and precisely
- **Emotional tone:** dry, document-forward through the opioid-contract material — never true-crime
  or sensationalized; matter-of-fact authority through the price-collapse and waterfall explainer;
  restrained irony (not glee) during the carve-out reveal; quiet respect, no melodrama, during the
  employees segment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine dry disbelief when stating the
  $7M-vs-$0 contrast; visible care and precision when reading the "outcome not confirmed" and
  "Malek is also one of the executives" framing cards
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  waterfall graphic and the full-arc timeline
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

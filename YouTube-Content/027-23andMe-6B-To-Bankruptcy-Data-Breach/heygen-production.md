# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious throughout — this is a security-and-governance story,
  not a high-energy funding-raise story; brisker only during the founding/raise segments
- **Speaking pace:** measured, especially through the credential-stuffing explainer, the October 6
  reveal, and the board's resignation-letter quote — all three need room to land clearly and be
  attributed precisely, on pace with the on-screen lower thirds
- **Emotional tone:** confident/upbeat during the founding, raise, and going-public segments;
  matter-of-fact, precise authority for the "how the breach actually happened" segment; sober,
  restrained gravity — never dramatized — for the October 6 ethnicity-targeting reveal; measured,
  even-handed tone for the settlement/regulatory segment; genuine gravity for the board's
  resignation letter; reflective, slightly surprised tone for the TTAM twist ending
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; visibly sober (not shocked, not sensational)
  during the Ashkenazi Jewish/Chinese-ancestry targeting beat; genuine gravity reading the board's
  resignation-letter quote; a measured, thoughtful shift for the TTAM twist reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  $3.5B-vs-$6B side-by-side graphic and the three-package (025/026/027) comparison
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout; hold eye contact without looking away during
  the ethnicity-targeting sentence specifically — no cutaway that could read as flinching from the
  detail or overplaying it

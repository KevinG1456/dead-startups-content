# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule, each thumbnail carries exactly
ONE idea, never the whole video. Do not try to cram the breach, the board resignation, the
bankruptcy, and the TTAM ending into a single image — those live in the video, not the thumbnail.
No real logos/branding (23andMe wordmark, BreachForums, Regeneron, Nasdaq). No depiction of any
real named person's likeness (Anne Wojcicki or any board member) — face-led concepts use a
generic host/avatar only. No ethnicity-specific imagery, symbols, or flags anywhere — the breach's
ethnicity-targeting detail is handled verbally and on-screen with text/sourcing inside the video
itself, never visually implied on the thumbnail. Keep breach-related concepts abstract (DNA helix,
lock, breach crack) rather than literal or sensational.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a glowing blue DNA double-helix, cracked cleanly in half; one half stays intact
  and glowing, the other half dissolves into a red "FOR SALE" tag shape
- **Main subject:** the breach — the idea that genetic data itself became a tradeable liability
- **Emotion:** unease, personal stakes
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint circuit/data-stream texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the helix
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** helix centered and dominant, crack running through the middle, bottom-third
  title-safe space
- **Props:** DNA helix graphic, small red tag/stamp shape (no readable text inside the tag itself)
- **Thumbnail text:** `YOUR DNA WAS FOR SALE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** blue/white helix glow, red tag accent, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the intact-helix-to-red-tag dissolve communicates the entire thesis (your
  most personal data, turned into a product for someone else) before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render any real logo, wordmark, or trademarked brand element (23andMe, Nasdaq, BreachForums,
  Regeneron, TTAM Research Institute) — attribute all of these verbally/on-screen inside the video,
  not in thumbnail imagery
- Do not depict any real named person's likeness — Anne Wojcicki or any of the seven resigning board
  members — use a generic host/avatar only for face-led concepts
- Do not use any ethnicity-specific imagery, symbols, flags, or text anywhere in a thumbnail — the
  breach's Ashkenazi Jewish/Chinese-ancestry targeting detail is a video-content beat, handled
  soberly with sourced on-screen text, never a thumbnail hook
- Do not attempt a "the whole story" thumbnail cramming the breach, the board, the bankruptcy, and
  the TTAM ending into one image — the thumbnail sells ONE idea; the rest lives inside the video
- Do not render real document text (the resignation letter, the bankruptcy filing, BreachForums
  posts) — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real text in the editor with
  proper sourcing

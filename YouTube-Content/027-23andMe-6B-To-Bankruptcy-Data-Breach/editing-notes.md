  date-collision reveal in the cold open

  VALUE" hard-cutting to "MARCH 2025 — CHAPTER 11 FILED"), with a small "OCTOBER 2023 — DATA BREACH
  REVEALED" marker on the connecting timeline
- Founder-era imagery and saliva-kit-to-database pipeline animation for the founding segment,
  customer-count graphic climbing toward 15 million
- Funding timeline graphic building toward "just under $900M," SPAC-merger graphic (Virgin
  Group/VG Acquisition Corp. referenced verbally only — no logo rendering)
- Side-by-side valuation card: "$3.5B — SPAC DEAL VALUATION" next to "$6B — PEAK MARKET VALUE,"
  held clearly separate, never merged into one figure
- Network-graph animation: one compromised node lighting up dozens of connected nodes, building
  directly into the 14,000-to-6.9-million exposure-scaling animation
- "NOT A HACK OF 23ANDME'S SYSTEMS" text card, held on screen long enough to register clearly
- Sober, minimal on-screen text card for the October 6, 2023 reveal, with explicit source
  attribution ("According to JTA, Forward, Times of Israel, and the Jerusalem Post") — no
  ethnicity-specific imagery, symbols, or flags anywhere in this graphic
- "$30 MILLION — SEPTEMBER 2024 SETTLEMENT" primary card, smaller "$50M figure later referenced —

  typed out in full on screen

- Two-bidder auction graphic (Regeneron vs. TTAM Research Institute, described verbally, no logos)

  company icon dissolving as a nonprofit icon receives the data-asset icon

  timeline, ending — landing on the founder-outcome contrast with package 029 (Ozy Media)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (CNBC, Fortune,
  Forbes, Built In SF, Security.org, HIPAA Journal, Dark Reading, JTA, Forward, Times of Israel,
  Jerusalem Post, Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Yale Daily News, Al Jazeera,
  Caproasia)
- "Credential stuffing — not a direct hack of 23andMe's systems" standing lower third whenever the
  breach mechanism is referenced, no exceptions
- "According to JTA, Forward, Times of Israel, and the Jerusalem Post" lower third every single
  time the ancestry-targeting detail appears on screen — no exceptions
- "$3.5B = SPAC deal valuation / $6B = peak market value" standing lower-third phrasing whenever
  either figure is referenced — never presented as one number
- "$30M settlement (Sept 2024) — $50M bankruptcy-court figure: [VERIFY]" lower third whenever the
  settlement is referenced
- "Anne Wojcicki has not been charged with any crime" lower third at first mention of her role in
  the board collapse and bankruptcy segments

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic genetic-testing/lab imagery (no real
  readable logos/text — 23andMe, Nasdaq, BreachForums, Regeneron, Virgin Group — without rights
  clearance)
- Board resignation-letter quote types on screen as it's read aloud, at full-weight emphasis
- The October 6, 2023 reveal card animates in slowly and deliberately — no quick-cut, no dramatic
  sting, sober pacing throughout
- TTAM twist beat animates in with a distinctly reflective, slightly surprised visual tone

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the valuation-collapse-to-
  handoff animation and the three-package comparison graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founding and the Business / The Raise and the SPAC: brisk, confident, building energy
- Going Public: bright, legitimacy-signaling tone
- What 23andMe Actually Sold: curious, slightly clinical, building tension into the breach segment
- The 2023 Breach: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the exposure-scaling animation carry the
  tension
- October 6, 2023 Reveal: near-silent, no dramatic scoring of any kind — respectful, sober
- The Settlement and Regulatory Findings: measured, neutral
- The Board Collapse: sharp tonal drop, serious
- The Layoffs and Cancer-Research Cut: somber, restrained
- Bankruptcy: quiet, matter-of-fact
- The Auction: rising curiosity, building tension
- The Twist: warming, resolving tone — the video's emotional payoff
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal in the cold open and throughout the timeline
- Sharp "crash" SFX as the exposure-scaling animation builds from 14,000 to 6.9 million
- No SFX of any kind under the October 6, 2023 ancestry-targeting reveal — silence is the correct
  choice here
- Soft "unlock"/"reveal" SFX at the TTAM twist reveal
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and measured, especially during the
  breach, the ancestry-targeting detail, and the board-resignation segments, none of which should
  ever read as sensationalized

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — a huge number ($929.9M) next to a fading/broken product silhouette.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a giant "$929.9M" figure with a single cracked/greyed-out wristband-tracker
  silhouette beneath it, like a headstone
- **Main subject:** the scale contradiction — huge funding number, dead product
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, single glow on the numeral
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** numeral top two-thirds, silhouette bottom third, title-safe space preserved
- **Props:** none beyond the numeral + silhouette graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `$929.9M RAISED. STILL DIED.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white/gold numeral, grey/desaturated silhouette, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the size mismatch between the huge number and the small dead-product
  silhouette communicates the thesis before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the giant-numeral-over-dead-product silhouette from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `$929.9M RAISED. STILL DIED.`
- **Why it works:** an instantly-readable scale-shock number needs zero prior context about Jawbone
  to generate curiosity
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three — pure number-shock has the widest cold-audience reach
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Curiosity
- **Concept:** two simple silhouette logos ("J" shape vs. Fitbit-style wave shape) facing off,
  one struck through with a red "X"
- **Text:** `THEY SUED FITBIT. THEY LOST.`
- **Why it works:** the underdog-reversal angle is the single most surprising plot beat in the
  story and creates strong curiosity for anyone who remembers either brand
- **CTR prediction:** high, especially with viewers who remember the Fitbit rivalry
- **Best audience:** search + suggested traffic, wearable-tech nostalgia audience

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small "$300M" tag with a
  small "LOAN" stamp overlaid across it in the corner
- **Text:** `IT WASN'T AN INVESTMENT`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; the "wasn't an investment"
  framing signals a real reporting twist, not a generic recap
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

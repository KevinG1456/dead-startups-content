# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the funding-climb and
  three-product segments, and a clear investigative register during the Legal War and Criminal
  Case segments
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the BlackRock-loan reveal and the
  Hosain Rahman coda — both need room to land clearly and carefully
- **Emotional tone:** confident/scene-setting for the origin and product-line buildup, wry/
  skeptical for the disguised-loan reveal, procedural-neutral for the legal war, deliberately
  unresolved and careful for the Hosain Rahman coda
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; slight tonal shift (raised-eyebrow, dryly
  surprised register) at the "ACTUALLY: A LOAN" reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  legal-scoreboard explanation and the three-way comparison
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout; noticeably more measured and careful body
  language during the Hosain Rahman coda specifically

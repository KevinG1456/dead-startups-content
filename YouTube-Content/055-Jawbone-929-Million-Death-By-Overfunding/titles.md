## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **They Raised $929 Million. They Still Went Out Of Business.** — leads with the single most
   shocking, verifiable number on the whole channel, works fully truncated on mobile, needs zero
   prior context to land
2. Jawbone Raised Almost $1 Billion. Then Sued Fitbit — And Lost. — pairs the scale hook with the
   most surprising plot twist (they were the aggressor and still lost)
3. $3.3 Billion Valuation. Zero Buyers. This Is Jawbone. — clean numbers-first hook, strong
   contradiction
4. This Company Sued Its Biggest Rival — And Lost Every Round — best for viewers who don't
   recognize the Jawbone name but respond to a legal-underdog-reversal hook
5. They Called It an Investment. It Was Actually a Loan. — strong secondary hook, sets up the
   BlackRock segment
6. What Happened to Jawbone? The Full Story — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
7. The Most-Funded Startup Failure Of The Wearable Tech Era — strong authority framing
8. What It's Like Watching Your $3.3 Billion Company Quietly Disappear — best emotional/human-angle
   alt cut
9. Jawbone Shutdown Explained — dry, best for search-only sub-cut
10. This Founder's New Company Is Now Being Sued For Fraud — strong secondary/Shorts hook, but must
    stay carefully attributed per `sources-log.txt` (allegation, not fact)

## Recommended Title
**"They Raised $929 Million. They Still Went Out Of Business."**

Reasoning: leads with the single strongest, most primary-sourced number in the package ($929.9M,
CNBC/CB Insights), needs no prior audience familiarity with Jawbone to generate curiosity, and sets
up the "how" question the whole video answers. The Fitbit-lawsuit twist and the still-unresolved
Polymath lawsuit coda are held back as mid-video and end-video surprises rather than spent in the
title.

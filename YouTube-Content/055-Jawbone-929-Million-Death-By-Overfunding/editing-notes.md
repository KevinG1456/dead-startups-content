- Giant "$929.9M" numeral shrinking in descending steps toward an empty outline, for the cold open and mirrored again at the Quiet Ending segment
- AliphCom-to-Jawbone logo-morph animation for the Origin segment (generic placeholder shapes only, no real logos without rights clearance)
- Three product silhouettes (headset, speaker, wristband) appearing one at a time for the Three Product Lines segment
- Valuation graphic climbing toward $3.3B for the Climb segment
- "$300M INVESTMENT" text stamped over with "ACTUALLY: A LOAN" for the BlackRock segment
- Two-column legal scoreboard graphic ("JAWBONE SUED FITBIT → LOST" / "FITBIT SUED JAWBONE → ALSO LOST") for the Legal War segment, held on screen for the full duration of that segment
- Six silhouette figures for the criminal-case segment, five fading with a checkmark, one flagged "outcome not confirmed"
- Small still-open case-file icon (deliberately left open, not checked off) for the Hosain Rahman coda segment
- Final three-way comparison graphic (Fast: 6 days, $124.5M / Olive AI: 18 months, ~$900M / Jawbone: years, $929.9M) building beat by beat

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (CNBC, CB Insights, Bloomberg, Fortune, ITC/DOJ records)
- "Reported as a loan, not equity, per Bloomberg/California filings" lower third specifically during the BlackRock segment
- "Civil case — not adjudicated as fact" lower third during both Fitbit ITC case beats
- "Separate criminal case — not the same claim as the ITC ruling above" lower third at the start of the criminal-case segment
- "Outcome not confirmed in this research pass" lower third specifically over the Ana Rosario silhouette
- "This is one investor's civil lawsuit — not a criminal charge, not a ruling. All.health, Rahman, and Luna have explicitly denied every claim." lower third held on screen for the ENTIRE Hosain Rahman coda segment — do not let this beat run without the disclaimer visible

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic office/product imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- The Hosain Rahman "commitments sufficient to stay independent" characterization types on screen as it's read aloud, attributed to Fortune's reporting

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the legal-scoreboard segment and the three-way comparison graphic

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Origin: measured, scene-setting
- Three Product Lines: brisk, building energy
- The Climb To $3.3 Billion: confident, peak-arrival swell
- The Loan Disguised As an Investment: a subtle tonal dip/reveal sting under the stamp animation
- The Legal War: tense, procedural, near-competitive energy
- The Criminal Case: sparse, clinical
- The Quiet Ending: measured, matter-of-fact, deliberately anticlimactic (mirrors the real quiet liquidation)
- What Happened To Hosain Rahman: neutral, unresolved — avoid any resolving musical cadence, the story genuinely isn't closed
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Sharp "crack" SFX as the "$929.9M" numeral fractures/shrinks in the cold open
- Distinct "stamp" SFX for the "ACTUALLY: A LOAN" reveal
- Soft "gavel-adjacent" (non-literal, no real gavel sound) SFX at each legal-scoreboard result reveal
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and educational, respectful of the real employees affected by the layoffs and the individuals involved in the criminal case

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the last one standing still lost.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** three small company-silhouette icons in a row, two already dimmed/greyed with an X, the third (largest, still lit) fading out last
- **Main subject:** the "outlasted everyone, still lost" sequence
- **Emotion:** shock/inevitability
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the third icon as it fades
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** three icons in sequence, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** none beyond the icon sequence
- **Thumbnail text:** `LAST ONE STANDING. STILL GONE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** grey/dark for the two dimmed icons, red fade for the third, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the sequence itself communicates the thesis before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** the three-icon sequence from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `LAST ONE STANDING. STILL GONE.`
- **Why it works:** an instantly-readable sequence creates curiosity about the category and the ending
- **CTR prediction:** high — sequence-led thumbnails create narrative curiosity
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** a gift card icon with a red "X" or crack through it
- **Text:** `THEY SOLD GIFT CARDS. THEN THEY VANISHED.`
- **Why it works:** leads with the most viscerally relatable betrayal detail — a real consumer product (gift cards) turning worthless overnight
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small bankruptcy-document icon in the corner
- **Text:** `THE BANKRUPTCY FILING SAYS EVERYTHING`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "the filing says everything" framing signals rare, strong, official sourcing
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

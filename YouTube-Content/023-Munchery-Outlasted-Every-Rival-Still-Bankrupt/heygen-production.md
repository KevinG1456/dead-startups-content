# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the category-recap and peak-growth segments and a clear educational register during the mechanism explanation
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the bankruptcy-filing quote and the final comparison section — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** confident/scene-setting for the category recap, matter-of-fact authority for the financial segments, a harder edge at the gift-card-to-shutdown cut, measured gravity for the Filing segment
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; slight tonal shift (harder, more direct) at the December-gift-cards reveal
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the three-company comparison explanation
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

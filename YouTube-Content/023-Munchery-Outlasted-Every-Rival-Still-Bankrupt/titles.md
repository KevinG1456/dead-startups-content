## Recommended Title
**"Munchery Outlasted Every Rival In Its Category. It Still Went Bankrupt."**

Reasoning: states the video's actual finding — that more capital and more time didn't fix a
structural problem — directly and without needing an invented number, works fully truncated on
mobile, and sets up the three-company category arc (SpoonRocket, Sprig, Munchery) that makes this
video more than a solo company recap. Name Munchery directly; it carries real recognition value
for anyone who remembers the on-demand-meal-delivery wave, and pairs naturally with this channel's
existing Sprig video (package 001) as a companion piece.

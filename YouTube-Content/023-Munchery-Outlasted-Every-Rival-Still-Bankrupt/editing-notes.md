# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the three-icon "last one standing" reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Three-icon sequence animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Three-company timeline graphic (SpoonRocket 2016 → Sprig 2017 → Munchery 2019) building in Segment 1
- Funding/valuation graphic climbing to the $300M peak, paired with a four-city map, in Segment 2
- Burn/waste graphic ($5M/month, 16% waste) and leadership-change timeline marker in Segment 3
- Four-city map narrowing to one (San Francisco) for the Retreat segment
- December-gift-card-marketing graphic hard-cutting to the January shutdown headline
- The bankruptcy filing's stated-causes quote displayed as full-screen text, debt-breakdown graphic (secured vs. unsecured, vendors vs. customers)
- Final three-company comparison graphic (funding raised / years survived / cause of death) building to the shared structural conclusion

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Bloomberg, TechCrunch, the bankruptcy filing itself)
- "Disputed by Munchery — presented as reported, not settled" lower third specifically for the $80M valuation-cap detail
- "From Munchery's own Chapter 11 filing" lower third during the Filing segment, distinguishing this as official-record sourcing

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic commercial-kitchen/delivery imagery (no real readable logos/text without rights clearance)
- The bankruptcy filing's stated causes type on screen as they're read aloud

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the three-company comparison and timeline graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Category: measured, scene-setting
- The Model and the Peak: brisk, confident, building energy toward the $300M peak
- The Cracks: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the burn/waste graphic carry the tension
- The Retreat: slow contraction, mirrored musically by a narrowing/thinning texture
- The Shutdown: sharp tonal drop at the gift-card-to-shutdown cut
- The Filing: measured, matter-of-fact — let the filing's own words carry the weight
- Capital Doesn't Fix Structure: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "dim" SFX as each of the three company icons fades in the cold open
- Sharp "cut" SFX at the December-gift-cards-to-January-shutdown transition
- Soft "click" SFX as each line of the bankruptcy filing's stated causes appears
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and educational, respectful of the real employees, vendors, and customers affected by this shutdown

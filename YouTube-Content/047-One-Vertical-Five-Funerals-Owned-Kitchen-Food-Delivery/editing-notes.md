# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open five-icon timeline
  reveal, and on each company's single most dramatic title card ($21-22M run rate, "joining Deliveroo,"
  the court-filed cause, the cease-and-desist)
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on The Actual Lesson's five-column side-by-side timeline reveal

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open five-icon timeline graphic, four icons fading to gray in sequence, the fifth rendered in a
  visibly distinct color/pattern
- "This isn't five unrelated failures. It's one repeatable mistake — and one exception. Watch for which
  one breaks the pattern." pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- Running "1 of 5 / 2 of 5..." corner counter, advancing at the start of each company segment, switching
  visual style only for Josephine
- SpoonRocket: "$13.5M RAISED" → "SHUTDOWN — MARCH 15, 2016" with "CEO NAMED SPRIG DIRECTLY" callout →
  "ACQUIRED BY iFOOD — BRAZIL" title card sequence
- Sprig: "$56-58M RAISED" → "$21-22M RUN RATE — $850K/MONTH LOST" → "SHUTDOWN — MAY 26, 2017" title
  card sequence
- Maple: "DAVID CHANG-BACKED — $50M+ RAISED" → side-by-side cost-structure comparison graphic →
  "ACQUIRED BY DELIVEROO — MAY 2017" title card sequence
- Munchery: "$120M+ RAISED — MORE THAN THE OTHER THREE COMBINED" → "$5M/MONTH LOST" → "SHUTDOWN —
  JANUARY 21, 2019" → "CAUSE OF DEATH: NAMED IN THE COURT FILING ITSELF" title card sequence
- Josephine: "$2M+ RAISED — SMALLEST OF THE FIVE" → "CEASE-AND-DESIST — ALAMEDA COUNTY, 2016" →
  "WOUND DOWN — EARLY 2018" title card sequence, visually distinct color treatment from the prior four
- Five-column side-by-side timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson, each company's rise-to-ending
  sequence shown in parallel, built up one column at a time, the fifth column visually distinct

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Bloomberg, TechCrunch,
  Inc.com, Berkeleyside, Recode, Crain's New York, Fast Company)
- A distinct standing tag per company, shown once per segment: "FIRST TO DIE" (SpoonRocket), "FULL
  EPISODE: PACKAGE 001" (Sprig), "CELEBRITY-BACKED" (Maple), "FULL EPISODE: PACKAGE 023" (Munchery),
  "THE TWIST" (Josephine)
- "No fraud or criminal allegations in this story" note available for the intro if needed to set tone

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic kitchen/delivery imagery — no real readable
  logos/trademarks (Sprig, Munchery, SpoonRocket, Maple, Josephine, Deliveroo, iFood) without rights
  clearance
- Each of the first four company segments should end with its title card visually "matching" into the
  next segment's opening frame, reinforcing the shared pattern; Josephine's segment should visibly break
  this transition style, reinforcing the twist
- The five-column timeline in The Actual Lesson should build one column at a time, the first four
  landing with matching motion, the fifth landing with a distinct motion/color

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for each company's title-card
  sequence and the five-column timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during each company's single most dramatic reveal line,
  and during the tonal shift into the Josephine segment

## Background Music
- Cold open: measured, curious pulse, cutting to near-silence on the pattern-interrupt card
- Each of the first four companies' "Rise" beat: measured, building energy, consistent instrumentation
  to reinforce they're the same pattern
- Each of the first four companies' "collapse/shutdown" beat: quiet, restrained — no melodrama,
  consistent across all four
- Josephine's segment: a distinct, slightly different instrumentation/key change to mark the tonal
  shift, without becoming comedic or mismatched in tone
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout, across all five companies
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on each company's funding-raised reveal, scaled subtly louder for larger
  amounts (Munchery's $120M+ loudest, Josephine's $2M+ quietest)
- Sharp, decisive "stamp" SFX on each of the first four companies' shutdown/acquisition moment; a
  distinct, different SFX (e.g. a soft "gavel" or "document stamp") on Josephine's cease-and-desist
  reveal, reinforcing the regulatory (not financial) nature of its ending
- No comedic stingers anywhere in this video — tone stays analytical and precise throughout
- Light, satisfying "chime" SFX on the five-column timeline's final beat in The Actual Lesson

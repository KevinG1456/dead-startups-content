# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** measured and building through each company's "Rise" beat; matter-of-fact through
  each mechanism explanation; quiet and restrained through each shutdown beat; a genuine tonal shift
  (curious, slightly lighter) entering the Josephine segment; reflective through The Actual Lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during each company's single most important
  framing card (SpoonRocket naming Sprig directly, Sprig's founder-confirmed run rate, Maple's "not
  even a celebrity chef," Munchery's court-filed cause, Josephine's regulatory pivot)
- **Emotional tone:** dry, document-forward through each financing/mechanism explanation — never
  sensationalized; matter-of-fact through each rise sequence; restrained irony at Maple's "joining"
  framing and Munchery's court-filed cause; a clear, deliberate tonal shift at the Josephine reveal —
  curious rather than repetitive
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine dry disbelief at Munchery's $120M+
  raised still ending the same way; visible curiosity shift when Josephine's different failure mechanism
  is first named
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during each
  company's title-card sequence and the five-column timeline in The Actual Lesson
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

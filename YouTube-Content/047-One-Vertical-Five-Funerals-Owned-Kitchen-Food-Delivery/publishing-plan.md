# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. This package's comparator format (five
companies) has established precedent on this channel (007, 015, 025, 034) and should perform on
pattern-recognition appeal even though individual company name recognition varies (Sprig and Munchery
carry the most, SpoonRocket/Maple/Josephine less) — no urgency premium, hold for the best day/time
slot.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience: founders/operators evaluating owned-kitchen/vertically-integrated business models,
and investors studying category-level failure patterns. Broader appeal than a single-company niche
package due to the comparator format itself creating curiosity ("will the pattern really repeat, and
which one breaks it?").

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; also add to a food-tech/vertical-comparator mini-series alongside
package 001 (Sprig) and package 023 (Munchery) — this is the fuller, five-company treatment of a
category those two packages already partially covered.

## End Screen
Package 001 (Sprig) + package 023 (Munchery) + subscribe element — both are the channel's fuller
individual treatments of two of this video's five companies, making a natural continuation for viewers
who finish this one.

## Cards
Card at the transition between Maple and Munchery (~10:00) linking to package 023 (Munchery) for
viewers who want the fuller individual treatment; card at the mid-video CTA (~9:45) linking to package
001 (Sprig); optional third card at the start of the Josephine segment (~14:00) linking to a pinned
comment or community post previewing the twist.

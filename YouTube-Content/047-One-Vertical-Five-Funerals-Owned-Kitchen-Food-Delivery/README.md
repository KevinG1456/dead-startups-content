# 047 — One Vertical, Five Funerals: Every Owned-Kitchen Food Delivery Startup, Same Few Years

**Package status:** Filled in and sourced — built around five independently-verified companies
(Bloomberg, TechCrunch, Inc.com, Berkeleyside, Recode, Crain's New York, Fast Company, Berkeleyside —
full citations in `sources-log.txt`). This package replaces retired dead-saas-30 batch title #18
("support ticket necrology / status page as leading indicator" — no real single-company anchor was
ever found for that pattern after two full research passes). Instead it fills the same batch's title
#28 ("one vertical, five funerals: why every startup in [one niche] died within 3 years"). Two of the
five companies (Sprig, Munchery) are already deeply sourced full anchors elsewhere on this channel
(packages 001 and 023) and are reused directly here, not re-derived. Three companions (SpoonRocket,
Maple, Josephine) previously had only a single-line citation each in package 015's shutdown-letter
corpus and got a full fresh research pass for this package. Same multi-company comparator format as
packages 007, 015, 025, and 034. Sourced fresh this session (2026-08-16).

**The five real cases:**
1. **Sprig** — founded 2013 by Gagan Biyani, raised ~$56-58M, hit a founder-confirmed ~$21-22M run
   rate while losing ~$850K/month, shut down May 26, 2017. (Full detail: package 001.)
2. **Munchery** — founded 2011 by Tri Tran + Conrad Chu, raised $120M+, shut down Jan 21, 2019, filed
   Chapter 11 with CEO James Beriker naming causes on the record in the court filing itself. Held on
   longer and raised more than either Sprig or SpoonRocket. (Full detail: package 023.)
3. **SpoonRocket** — founded 2013 by Steven Hsiao + Anson Tsui, raised $13.5M, the FIRST of the three
   core companies to die (March 2016). CEO named a "tightening funding environment" and competitor
   Sprig directly in its shutdown statement — unusually specific. Assets later acquired by Brazil's
   iFood.
4. **Maple** — founded 2014 by Caleb Merkl + Akshay Navle, backed by celebrity chef David Chang,
   raised ~$25-26M Series A (total ~$50M+), shut down May 2017 — framed as "joining" Deliveroo (which
   acquired its tech/team) rather than shutting down.
5. **Josephine** — founded 2014 by Charley Wang + Tal Safran, raised just $2M+ (far smaller than the
   other four) — killed not by unit economics but by repeated health-department cease-and-desist
   orders, wound down early 2018 after concluding it couldn't afford to fight regulators market by
   market.

**Framing requirement:** Four of the five (Sprig, Munchery, SpoonRocket, Maple) share the same core
failure mechanism — an owned-kitchen cost structure that never produced sub-restaurant-margin meals at
delivery-app prices and scale. Josephine is the deliberate outlier: smallest funding, a marketplace
(not owned-kitchen) model, killed by regulation rather than economics. State this contrast explicitly
on screen as the package's "it's not always the same lesson" twist, not as a mismatched inclusion. Do
not re-verify or restate Sprig/Munchery's figures differently than their own packages (001, 023) —
cite them directly. No fraud or criminal allegations apply to anyone across all five companies — keep
the tone analytical throughout.

# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. This package covers five
companies, but each thumbnail still leads with ONE dominant idea (the shared owned-kitchen pattern, or
one company's single most dramatic fact) — never all five company names/logos crammed into one image.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** five small generic building/company icons on a single horizontal timeline, four
  fading to gray/dark in the same visual style, the fifth rendered in a visibly different color/pattern
- **Main subject:** the shared owned-kitchen pattern across four companies, with one visible outlier
- **Emotion:** recognition/inevitability, with a note of curiosity at the outlier
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint kitchen/steam texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** single spotlight sweeping across the timeline
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** timeline centered, five icons evenly spaced, bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic building/company silhouette icons, one small distinct-colored icon for the outlier
- **Thumbnail text:** `5 COMPANIES. SAME CATEGORY. ONLY 1 DIED DIFFERENTLY.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** four icons in matching gray/white, one icon in a distinct accent color, near-black
  background
- **Contrast strategy:** the visual break in the pattern (four matching, one different) communicates the
  entire thesis before any text is read

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Gagan Biyani, Tri Tran, Conrad Chu,
  Steven Hsiao, Anson Tsui, Caleb Merkl, Akshay Navle, David Chang, Charley Wang, or Tal Safran in any
  thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only; attribute real names
  verbally/on-screen in the video itself
- Do not put real company logos/wordmarks (Sprig, Munchery, SpoonRocket, Maple, Josephine, Deliveroo,
  iFood) in generated thumbnail art — build the visual concept generically and add any verified real
  branding in the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not cram all five company names or logos into a single thumbnail — the comparator format lives
  inside the video, not the thumbnail

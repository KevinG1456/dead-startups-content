SpoonRocket, Maple, Josephine, Deliveroo, iFood, Momofuku), and no photorealistic likeness of any real
named person (Gagan Biyani, Tri Tran, Conrad Chu, Steven Hsiao, Anson Tsui, Caleb Merkl, Akshay Navle,
David Chang, Charley Wang, Tal Safran) — add verified text/branding and any real archival photography
in the editor with proper sourcing, not generated.

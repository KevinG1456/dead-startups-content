## Ranking (Strongest → Weakest)
1. **Five Companies. One Vertical. All Dead Within a Few Years of Each Other.** — states the comparator
   format plainly, matches the batch title's own framing, sets clear viewer expectations
2. $2 Million to $120 Million Raised. All Five Still Failed. — pure number-range shock framing,
   immediately signals the scale spread across all five companies
3. A Celebrity Chef Backed This One. It Still Didn't Survive. — strongest single-company curiosity
   hook (Maple/David Chang), good as a cold-open tease before the full comparator reveal
4. This Company Raised the Most and Lasted the Longest. It Still Died the Same Way. — strong irony
   framing (Munchery), sets up the "more capital didn't fix the structural problem" thesis
5. This Startup Wasn't Killed by Its Math — It Was Killed by a Health Inspector. — the sharpest twist
   hook (Josephine), strongest curiosity driver for viewers who've seen "ran out of money" stories before
6. This Startup Named Its Own Competitor in Its Shutdown Letter. — sharp, specific detail (SpoonRocket
   naming Sprig), strong curiosity hook
7. Four Owned-Kitchen Startups Died the Same Way. The Fifth One Broke the Pattern. — clean, direct
   statement of the whole video's structure
8. This Company Filed for Bankruptcy With the Cause Written Into the Court Filing Itself. — strong for
   the Munchery segment specifically, signals unusually strong sourcing
9. Why Did Sprig Fail? The Full Story — best pure-SEO, lower CTR
10. Owned-Kitchen Meal Delivery Startups Explained — strong secondary search title for the pattern
    itself rather than any one company

## Recommended Title
**"Five Companies. One Vertical. All Dead Within a Few Years of Each Other."**

Reasoning: states the comparator format plainly and accurately — five real, differently-scaled
companies in one narrow, well-defined vertical — consistent with how this channel's other comparator
packages (007, 015, 025, 034) are titled. Sets expectations correctly (a multi-company pattern
demonstration, not a single-company deep dive) while leaving the Josephine twist as a mid-video reveal
rather than spoiling it in the title. Works fully truncated on mobile at the first clause alone.

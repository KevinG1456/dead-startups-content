# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Given the November 2025 news-cycle
recency of the Sonder/Marriott story, prioritize publishing sooner rather than later within this
window to capture active search volume ("Sonder bankruptcy," "Sonder Marriott").

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) with particular relevance to anyone building on
lease-heavy or single-partner-dependent business models, plus a secondary audience drawn to a
recognizable, recent public-company collapse (Sonder + Marriott brand recognition) and general
finance-news-adjacent viewers.

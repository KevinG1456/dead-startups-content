# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — a public company disappearing in about a week. Per this
channel's standing rule, each thumbnail carries exactly ONE idea. Do not try to cram Marriott,
the bankruptcy, Lyric, and Zeus Living into a single image — those live in the video, not the
thumbnail.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large glowing "NASDAQ" ticker-style graphic dissolving into a red "CHAPTER 7"
  stamp, with a thin countdown line connecting the two
- **Main subject:** the public-listing-to-liquidation contradiction
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, minimal, faint stock-ticker-line texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even, single spotlight on the central graphic
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** ticker/ticker-symbol treatment top-to-center, red stamp landing bottom-center,
  title-safe space reserved at the very bottom edge
- **Props:** stock-ticker graphic, red "stamp" mark
- **Thumbnail text:** `WENT PUBLIC. GONE IN A WEEK.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** green/white ticker glow, red stamp accent, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the ticker-to-stamp dissolve communicates the entire thesis before any
  text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Image concept:** a single bold dollar-figure headline treatment
- **Subject:** the number, not a person — pure graphic/typographic thumbnail
- **Expression:** N/A (no face in this version)
- **Objects:** large glowing "$1.6B" figure, small red downward arrow beneath it
- **Background:** dark, minimal, subtle grain
- **Colors:** white/red numerals on near-black
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the number
- **Angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** number centered and dominant, arrow small and secondary, bottom-third text space
- **Thumbnail text:** `$1.6 BILLION LOST. THEN THEY WENT PUBLIC.`
- **Why it works:** the specific, verified deficit figure paired with the sequencing twist ("then
  they went public") creates immediate curiosity about how that decision made sense
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well on this channel, and the sequencing
  detail is the least-covered angle of this story anywhere else
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not put Marriott's logo, name, or any real trademarked brand element in the thumbnail —
  attribute Marriott verbally/on-screen in the video itself, not in thumbnail imagery
- Do not attempt a "3 companies died" thumbnail — that's the video's content, not the hook; the
  thumbnail sells Sonder's specific irony, and Lyric/Zeus Living get their own pull-quote moments
  inside the video and in Shorts
- Do not render real logos, real tickers, or real court-document text — build the visual concept
  generically and add any verified real text/branding in the editor with proper sourcing

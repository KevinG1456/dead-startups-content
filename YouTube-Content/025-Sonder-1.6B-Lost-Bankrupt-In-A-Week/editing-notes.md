# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom only on the Nasdaq-listing-to-
  Chapter-7 date-collision reveal in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Nasdaq-listing-to-Chapter-7 date-collision animation for the cold open ("JANUARY 2022 — NASDAQ
  LISTING (SOND)" hard-cutting to "NOVEMBER 14, 2025 — CHAPTER 7 FILED")
- World-map unit-count growth graphic building through the founding-to-peak scale-up (9,000+
  units, 40+ cities)
- Funding-round timeline graphic building toward "$550M+ before going public"
- SPAC-merger graphic with the valuation figure stepping down from $2.2-2.5B to $1.9B
- Cumulative-deficit graphic climbing to $1.6B, paired with the occupancy (80.9%) / rate ($196)
  numbers held on screen beside the revenue-cost gap ($621M vs. $803M) — the visual proof this was
  a structural problem, not a demand problem
- Bonvoy-integration "legitimacy win" graphic
- Dated final-week timeline building day by day, with November 7 and November 9 shown as two
  distinct markers (never merged into one date-stamp) and a countdown to November 14
- Split-screen quote card: Marriott's court-filing language on one side, Sonder's own CEO statement
  on the other, both captioned with explicit attribution lower thirds
- Three-way "who bears the risk" diagram (Stay Alfred / Zeus Living / Sonder) — landlord gets
  guaranteed rent at the top, the company absorbs all occupancy risk underneath
- Lyric collapse timeline splitting visually into two surviving threads (Wheelhouse, brand revived
  2023) for the twist-ending beat
- Final four-company comparison graphic (Stay Alfred / Lyric / Zeus Living / Sonder) — funding
  raised, years survived, how it ended
- "Autopsy Report Card" end-graphic

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or term is referenced (Wikipedia,
  Commercial Observer, Hotel Dive, CoStar, CNBC, Skift, Bloomberg Law)
- "According to Marriott's court filing" lower third every single time any Marriott-filing
  allegation appears on screen — no exceptions
- "Sonder's own account" lower third paired immediately alongside, every time the Marriott-filing
  material appears
- "$550M+ raised in private funding before going public" as a standing lower-third phrasing
  whenever total funding is referenced — never a single lifetime-total figure
- "Founded around 2015-2016" lower third whenever Zeus Living's founding date is referenced
- "One analyst's view" lower third for Daniel Lesser's quote

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic short-term-rental/hotel-room imagery (no
  real readable logos/text — Sonder, Marriott, Nasdaq, Lyric, Zeus Living, Airbnb, Blueground —
  without rights clearance)
- Marriott's filing quote and Sonder's CEO quote type on screen as they're read aloud, at equal
  visual weight
- Lyric's "twist" beat (Wheelhouse, Black Swan Holdings) animates in with a distinctly warmer,
  more surprised visual tone than the rest of the video

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the three-way risk diagram and
  the four-company comparison graphic layers

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- The Founder and the Build / The Raise: brisk, confident, building energy
- Going Public: bright, legitimacy-signaling tone that curdles slightly as the valuation step-down
  appears
- The Numbers Going In: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the deficit/occupancy graphic carry the
  tension
- The Marriott Deal: warm, hopeful — deliberately contrasts with what follows
- The Final Week: sharp tonal drop as the timeline compresses toward November 14
- Two Accounts, One Court Filing: quiet, even-handed, no music favoring either side
- Lyric segment: fast, urgent through the collapse, warming distinctly for the twist reveal
- Zeus Living segment: slow, decaying texture mirroring the multi-year wind-down
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, slightly upbeat educational tone
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal in the cold open and the Final Week timeline
- Distinct, separate stamp SFX for November 7 and November 9 — audibly reinforcing that these are
  two different dates
- Sharp "crash" SFX as the deficit graphic climbs during The Numbers Going In
- Soft "unlock" or "revive" SFX at the Wheelhouse/Black Swan Holdings twist reveal
- Avoid comedic stingers throughout — tone is investigative and even-handed, especially during the
  Marriott court-filing segment, which must never read as siding with one account over the other

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious, with brisker energy during the founding/raise/going-
  public segments and a clear educational register during the master-lease mechanic explainer
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, especially during the Marriott court-filing segment and
  the November 7-to-14 dated timeline — both need room to land clearly and to be attributed
  precisely, on pace with the on-screen lower thirds
- **Emotional tone:** confident/upbeat during the founding, raise, and going-public segments;
  matter-of-fact authority for the master-lease and numbers-going-in segments; sharper tension
  through the final-week timeline; deliberate, even-handed gravity during the "two accounts, one
  court filing" segment; measured and reflective during the Lyric/Zeus Living companion arc
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; genuine gravity when reading Marriott's court-
  filing language and Sonder's CEO's softer statement back to back — visibly even-handed, not
  siding with either account
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative during the
  "who bears the risk" three-way diagram and the four-company comparison graphic
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

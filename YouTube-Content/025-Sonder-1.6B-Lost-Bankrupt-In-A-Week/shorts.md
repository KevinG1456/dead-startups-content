# Shorts Ideas (10)

Note: Marriott's court-filing allegation material (the guest lock-out threat, the "leverage guest
safety" language, the $28M/$14.3M funding-ask figures) stays OUT of every Short below — Shorts use
ironclad, undisputed facts only (dates, dollar figures, public statements, the SPAC listing, the
occupancy/rate numbers, Sonder's own on-record CEO statement). The dueling-accounts material is
reserved for the full video.

**1. "Went Public. Gone In A Week."**
Hook: "This company rang the bell on Nasdaq in 2022. It filed for Chapter 7 about a week after one
partner walked away." Script: quick Nasdaq-to-Chapter-7 date-collision reveal. CTA: "Full story on
the channel."

**2. "$1.6 Billion, Then They Went Public"**
Hook: "This company had a $1.6 billion cumulative deficit. It went public anyway." Script:
30-second deficit-then-IPO sequencing reveal. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**3. "The Only Public Company In Its Category"**
Hook: "Out of every company in this category, only one ever made it to Nasdaq. It's also the one
that collapsed the fastest." Script: quick category-scoreboard explainer. CTA: "Full story on the
channel."

**4. "Occupancy Was Strong. Rates Were Healthy. Still Not Enough."**
Hook: "80.9% occupancy. $196 a night. This company still lost $803 million in costs." Script:
rapid-fire numbers explainer contrasting demand with structural cost. CTA: "Full breakdown
linked."

**5. "November 7 to November 14"**
Hook: "One partner terminated a contract on November 7th. This company filed for bankruptcy
liquidation seven days later." Script: quick dated-timeline reveal. CTA: "Full story on the
channel."

**6. "The Deal That Was Supposed To Save It"**

later, that same deal ended it." Script: quick Marriott Bonvoy legitimacy-win-to-collapse reveal
(dates and licensing facts only, no allegation detail). CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

**7. "Raised The Most. Lasted The Longest. Still Lost First."**

longest — and still collapsed the hardest." Script: 45-second three-company scoreboard. CTA: "Full
story on the channel."

**8. "This Company 'Died' In 2020. It's Still Around."**

**9. "Landlords Got Paid No Matter What. Nobody Guaranteed The Company Would."**
Hook: "This business model guaranteed landlords fixed rent, no matter what. Nobody guaranteed the
company itself." Script: rapid-fire master-lease risk explainer. CTA: "Full breakdown linked."

down slowly in November 2023, book of business handed to Blueground). Cross-reference package 021
(Stay Alfred) explicitly — same master-lease mechanic, 13-day collapse there vs. roughly a week here.

## Recommended Title
**"Sonder Hit a $1.6 Billion Deficit, Went Public Anyway — and Was Bankrupt Within a Week."**

Reasoning: leads with the single strongest, most citable figure in the sources-log ($1.6 billion
cumulative deficit), states the video's real irony without needing to invent or exaggerate anything
— Sonder really did go public after already being underwater for years, and really did file Chapter
7 within about a week (November 7 termination to November 14 filing) of Marriott walking away. Names
the company directly (real recognition value given the November 2025 news cycle and Marriott's
brand recognition), works fully truncated on mobile, and sets up both the Sonder-specific story and
the wider Lyric/Zeus Living category arc without overpromising. Avoids stating one single "lifetime
funding" number (per this package's own sourcing caution) by anchoring on the deficit figure
instead, which is the best-corroborated number in the entire package.

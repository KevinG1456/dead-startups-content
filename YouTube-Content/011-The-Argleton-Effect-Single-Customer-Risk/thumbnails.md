# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the single-customer dependence visualized as a structural weak point.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a pie chart with one enormous "80%" slice in a bright color and the remainder in muted gray, with a crack running through the large slice
- **Main subject:** the pie-chart imbalance plus the crack
- **Emotion:** tension/instability
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** pie chart centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** none beyond the chart and crack graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `80% ONE CUSTOMER. THEN THEY LEFT.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** bright green/red large slice, gray remainder, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the imbalance in the pie chart does the communicating

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** bold "80%" numeral large and centered, tiny building/company icon beside it
- **Text:** `ONE CUSTOMER. 80% OF EVERYTHING.`
- **Why it works:** numeral-led format with a scale cue instantly signals extreme dependence
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the cracked pie-chart combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `80%. ONE CUSTOMER. THEN THEY LEFT.`
- **Why it works:** visual metaphor (imbalance + crack) communicates structural fragility instantly, no explanation needed
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small pie-chart tag icon in the corner
- **Text:** `THE RISKIEST NUMBER IN BUSINESS`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; phrase promises an authoritative framework
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct startup and company failures using primary sources. This week: customer concentration risk — the danger of building a business so dependent on a single customer that losing them threatens the entire company.

We walk through a real, documented case where a company's business became overwhelmingly reliant on one major customer relationship, and what happened when that relationship broke down. We also cover the practical side: how investors and boards typically think about "safe" customer concentration thresholds, and what founders can do to manage this risk without turning away a genuinely great deal.

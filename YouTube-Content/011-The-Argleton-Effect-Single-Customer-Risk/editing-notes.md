# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the pie-chart-crack reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Pie-chart animation with the dominant slice growing large, then cracking, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Timeline graphic: founding → the major deal → growth/dependence deepens → relationship breakdown → collapse
- "Safe concentration threshold" framework graphic for the practical segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or quote is referenced — especially important given this is a financial-disclosure-heavy topic
- Company/customer name lower third when introducing the specific deal

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos, financial filing excerpts (blurred/illegible per best practice), or archival stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the pie-chart/timeline graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Deal/rise section: triumphant but with a subtle uneasy undertone (the "too good to be true" feeling)
- Autopsy/framework section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual pie-chart carry the tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Crack/fracture SFX on the pie-chart reveal
- Rubber-stamp thud on the bankruptcy/collapse reveal
- Soft page-turn sounds under financial-document B-roll
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and respectful of the real company/employees involved

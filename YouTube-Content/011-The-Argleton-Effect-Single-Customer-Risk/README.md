# 011 — The "Argleton Effect": Startups That Died Because One Customer Was 80% of Revenue

**Important terminology note:** "Argleton effect" is not an established industry term. (Argleton was a real, unrelated phenomenon — a fictitious town that briefly appeared on Google Maps due to a data error — and has no connection to customer concentration risk.) The established, accurate term for this topic is **customer concentration risk**. Two options: (1) use "customer concentration risk" as the real title/on-screen term and drop "Argleton effect" entirely, or (2) if the channel wants to coin its own memorable branded term for this recurring failure pattern, do so explicitly on screen ("we're calling this the ___ effect") rather than presenting it as an existing term viewers might otherwise search for and not find. Option 1 is recommended for credibility with this fact-checking-heavy audience; a placeholder `[Coined Term]` is used below in case the channel wants to proceed with its own branded name instead.

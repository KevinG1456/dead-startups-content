# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious; noticeably more restrained during the Fall section given the human-impact weight of this particular story
- **Speaking pace:** measured; deliberate pauses before numbers and before the layoff-email reveal
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the cost-math segments, genuinely somber (not performative) for the Fall section
- **Facial expression:** neutral-serious baseline; no wry/ironic expressions during the layoff-email description — this story concerns real people's livelihoods and should be treated with visible respect
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (400/envelope animation)
- Script: "How do 400 people lose their jobs from a single email, with zero warning?"
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–0:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, press-clipping montage inset
- Performance direction: flat, serious delivery
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 0:45–1:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third
- Performance direction: slightly warmer, teaching tone; explicit callback to the Homejoy video framing
- Visual direction: medium shot, checklist graphic animates in beside avatar

**Scene 04 — Story: Rise**
- Duration: 1:30–4:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Mostly B-roll (press stills, growth-chart animation) with avatar VO, cut back on camera every ~20s
- Performance direction: narrative pacing, builds gently
- Visual direction: full-screen B-roll with avatar PiP during longer VO stretches

**Scene 05 — Autopsy (Main Content)**
- Duration: 4:00–9:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Alternates avatar-on-camera for explanation beats with full-screen cost-breakdown graphics
- Performance direction: precise, deliberate; noticeable beat before each cost figure reveal
- Visual direction: side-by-side split during the cost-comparison segment; full avatar frame during explanatory beats

**Scene 06 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~6:30 (inside Scene 05) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into serious tone
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly

**Scene 07 — Story: The Fall**
- Duration: 9:30–11:30 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (email/notification imagery, empty home-office imagery), avatar VO under, cut on camera for the human-impact lines
- Performance direction: slow down noticeably, quieter volume, most restrained delivery in the entire batch — no dramatization of the moment
- Visual direction: darker grade, slower cuts, minimal graphic elements — let the moment breathe

**Scene 08 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 11:30–13:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: return to controlled-authority tone, steady eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements (including the Homejoy cross-link) animate in during final 5 seconds only

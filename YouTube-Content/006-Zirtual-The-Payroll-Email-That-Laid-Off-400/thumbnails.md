# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the scale-vs-instant contradiction (400 people, one email).

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a large "400" numeral with a simple envelope/email icon beside it, a red "sent" or notification-style badge on the envelope
- **Main subject:** the number + email icon contrast
- **Emotion:** shock
- **Background:** dark, subtle red gradient
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** "400" top-left, envelope icon bottom-right, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** simple envelope/notification icon
- **Thumbnail text:** `400 JOBS. ONE EMAIL.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white/red numeral, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** minimal palette so the number and icon read instantly at small size

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** just the numeral "400" enormous and centered, tiny clock icon beside it reading a specific short time
- **Text:** `400 GONE IN MINUTES`
- **Why it works:** the number-plus-time-pressure combination emphasizes both scale and suddenness in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnails perform well, and this pairs scale with urgency
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the 400/envelope combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `400 JOBS. ONE EMAIL.`
- **Why it works:** the envelope icon instantly signals "this happened via a message, not a meeting" — the impersonal-delivery detail that makes this story distinctive
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), concerned/serious expression, small "400" tag in the corner
- **Text:** `WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; phrase signals a corrective/deeper-story angle beyond the viral headline version of the story
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

## A/B Testing

| Version | Emotion | CTR Strategy | Best Audience |
|---|---|---|---|

| B | Shock | Number + impersonal-delivery cue | Search + browse (recommended primary) |
| C | Authority | Face-led, deeper-story framing | Subscribers/returning viewers |

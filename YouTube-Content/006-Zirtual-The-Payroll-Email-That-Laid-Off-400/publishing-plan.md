Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Consider publishing the week directly after the Homejoy video to maximize the cross-referencing/companion-video effect while that video is still fresh in suggested feeds.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) plus a broader audience interested in layoff practices, HR/compliance, and gig-economy labor debates.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; strongly cross-link with the Homejoy video (also consider a dedicated "Gig Economy & Labor Risk" playlist grouping both).

## End Screen
Homejoy video + most recent autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the mid-video CTA linking to the Homejoy video specifically (not just the general playlist), given the direct thematic pairing; card near the reclassification cost-breakdown segment linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA

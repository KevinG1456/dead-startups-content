# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the "400" reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- 400-numeral + envelope-icon animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Cost-breakdown graphic showing contractor cost vs. W-2 employee cost per worker, scaled up to the full workforce, to make the cash-crunch mechanism concrete
- Timeline graphic: founding → growth → reclassification decision → cost overrun → layoff email → aftermath

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or quote is referenced
- Cross-reference lower third pointing to the Homejoy video when the comparison is made explicit

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or archival stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the timeline/cost-breakdown graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Rise section: restrained, slightly optimistic underscore
- Autopsy/cost-breakdown section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual cost comparison carry the tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse — this is the most human-impact-heavy section in the batch, give it room
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Notification/email "ping" SFX on the layoff-email reveal in the cold open — used once, deliberately, not repeatedly (avoid trivializing the moment)
- Soft, somber tone under the human-impact beats in the Fall section
- Avoid comedic stingers entirely in this video — the subject matter (400 people's livelihoods) calls for a respectful, non-sensational treatment throughout

## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct a funded startup's failure using primary sources — no speculation, just documents and decisions.

This week: Zirtual, a virtual-assistant staffing marketplace founded by Maren Kate Donovan. In August 2015, roughly 400 virtual assistants received a single email informing them their jobs were ending, effective immediately. The reported trigger was a cash crunch tied significantly to the cost of reclassifying its workforce from independent contractors to W-2 employees — a move made partly in response to the same kind of misclassification legal risk that had recently ended Homejoy.

This video pairs naturally with our Homejoy autopsy: two companies on opposite sides of the same gig-economy dilemma, with strikingly different — but both painful — outcomes.

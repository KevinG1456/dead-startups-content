# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the claimed-vs-real number contradiction, or the $1.1B-to-$28M collapse.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** two dollar figures side by side — "$54M" (claimed) in green at top, "$10M" (real) in red below, with a red crack/X between them
- **Main subject:** the number contrast is the subject
- **Emotion:** shock / betrayal
- **Background:** dark, subtle red gradient
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** claimed number top, real number bottom, red divider between, title-safe space at the very bottom
- **Props:** none beyond the divider graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `THE REAL NUMBER WAS $10M`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** green for the claimed number, red for the real number and all "fraud" signifiers
- **Contrast strategy:** minimal palette so the two numbers read instantly at small size

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** "$1.1B" in large bold text with a subtle glitch/distortion effect, an arrow pointing down to "$28M" in small red text beneath it
- **Text:** `$1.1B → $28M`
- **Why it works:** the sheer size of the collapse is self-explanatory and doesn't require knowing the company name to be curious
- **CTR prediction:** high — the number-collapse format performs well cold, no context needed
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the claimed-vs-real ARR combo from Winning Concept above ($54M vs $10M)
- **Text:** `THE REAL NUMBER WAS $10M`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of the contradiction paired with the visual proof (two numbers) in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/knowing expression, small "GUILTY PLEA" tag in the corner
- **Text:** `HE WENT TO PRISON FOR THIS`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "went to prison" promises a satisfying, concrete resolution rather than a vague warning
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

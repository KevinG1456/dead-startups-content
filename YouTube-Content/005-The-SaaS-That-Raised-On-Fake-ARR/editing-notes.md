# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the $54M-vs-$10M and $1.1B-vs-$28.2M reveal moments

## Motion Graphics
- Claimed ARR vs. real ARR split-screen animation ($54M / $10M) for the cold open and Segment 1, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- "How the fraud was constructed" step-by-step animated breakdown (fake invoices → phantom Apple/Amex deals → churned-customer double-counting), built from the SEC complaint, not speculation
- Governance checklist graphic: CFO ❌ / HR Department ❌ / Independent Audit ❌
- Full valuation-arc line graph: founding (2015) → growth claims → $1.1B unicorn round (2020) → whistleblower/investigation (2020) → internal markdown to $300M → $28.2M sale (2024)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or claim is referenced — cite "SEC Litigation Release" or "DOJ Press Release, N.D. Cal." specifically each time, given the legal sensitivity of naming a convicted individual
- Legal-disclaimer lower third at the start of the video noting the case is legally resolved (guilty plea + SEC civil charges), not alleged

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers and dates
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static document excerpts (SEC complaint/DOJ press release text, blurred/illegible per legal best practice where full text isn't needed) or press stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the timeline/reconstruction graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Rise section: subtly "too good to be true" undertone under the climb to $1.1B — slightly uneasy rather than triumphant
- Autopsy/reconstruction section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual number-reveal carry tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Coda ($28.2M sale): quiet, understated — avoid triumphant or ironic musical stingers
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Glitch/distortion SFX on the "claimed number" reveal, clean tone on the "real number" reveal
- Gavel-strike SFX at the guilty-plea/sentencing point in the timeline
- Soft page-turn sounds under document B-roll
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and legally careful, not mocking, given this names a real, individually convicted person

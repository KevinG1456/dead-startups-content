# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled, serious, and notably careful in tone — this is the most legally sensitive title in the batch (it names a convicted individual), and the delivery should read as measured journalism, not sensationalism
- **Speaking pace:** measured; deliberate pauses before dollar figures and legal terms
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for data/legal segments; avoid any tone that could read as gleeful or mocking about the fraud or the prison sentence, even during the "how it got caught" beat
- **Facial expression:** neutral-serious baseline throughout; no smirking
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout — this builds the credibility this topic requires

## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct a funded startup's failure using primary sources — no speculation, just documents and decisions.

This week is a fraud story: HeadSpin, a real, working SaaS company that tested mobile apps on real physical devices, raised $117 million and reached a $1.1 billion valuation on revenue figures its own founder later admitted to fabricating. We reconstruct what the real 2019 ARR actually was ($10 million, versus the $54 million claimed to investors), walk through exactly how the fabricated numbers were built — fake invoices, phantom Apple and American Express deals, a company with no CFO, no HR department, and no audits — and cover how a single employee's whistleblowing to the board ended it.

Every figure in this video is drawn from the SEC's litigation release, the DOJ's plea and sentencing press releases (Northern District of California), and contemporaneous reporting citing those filings — cited on screen. This is not a speculative or "alleged" story: founder Manish Lachwani pleaded guilty to two counts of wire fraud and one count of securities fraud and was sentenced to 18 months in prison.

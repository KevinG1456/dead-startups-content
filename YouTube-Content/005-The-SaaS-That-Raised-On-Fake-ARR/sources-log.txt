COMPANY / FOUNDER
- HeadSpin — mobile app testing infrastructure (real device/carrier cloud), founded 2015.
- Founder/CEO: Manish Lachwani.
Source: TechCrunch, Axios company coverage; SEC.gov litigation release.

FUNDING
- Total raised: ~$117M, primarily across rounds between April 2017 and April 2020.
- Investors: Google Ventures (GV), ICONIQ Capital, Dell Technologies Capital,
  Battery Ventures, Felicis Ventures, Tiger Global.
- Peak valuation: ~$1.1B (2020 round).
Source: TechCrunch, Axios funding coverage; company round announcements.

THE FRAUD — WHAT WAS ACTUALLY MISREPRESENTED
- 2019 fundraising: Lachwani claimed ~$54M in annual recurring revenue.
- Board-commissioned investigation later determined actual ARR was closer to ~$10M.
- Methods: fake/altered customer invoices; fabricated "customer" relationships
  (including invented business with Apple and American Express); counting churned
  or never-formally-agreed revenue as active ARR.
- Company had no CFO and no HR function at the time; financials were never audited.
Source: SEC v. Manish Lachwani, SEC litigation release (SEC.gov, filed Aug 26, 2021);
DOJ press release, N.D. Cal. (justice.gov/usao-ndca).

HOW IT WAS DISCOVERED
- An employee whistleblower raised concerns to HeadSpin's board in early 2020.
- Board's internal investigation cut the company's internal valuation from
  ~$1.1B to ~$300M.
Source: TechCrunch, CFO Dive reporting on the board investigation.

LEGAL RESOLUTION (the load-bearing fact that makes this title usable)

- Criminal guilty plea: April 23, 2023 — 2 counts wire fraud, 1 count securities
  fraud, before Judge Charles Breyer, U.S. District Court, N.D. California.

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** curious and building through the founding/pitch/funding segments; wry/amused
  through the technology-failure and broken-checkout segments; serious and measured through the
  marketing-gap and burn-rate segments; neutral-matter-of-fact through the fire-sale segment; warm and
  reflective through the book/closing-lesson segments
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, slightly faster during the ambition/funding beats,
  deliberately slower and clearer during the "56K modems" line and the marketing-awareness-gap reveal
  so both land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** genuine respect for the ambition of the original idea, gentle amusement (not
  mockery) at the technical overreach, real seriousness at the "$135M raised, under $2M recovered"
  reveal, quiet respect at the closing book beat
- **Facial expression:** neutral-pleasant baseline; a genuine amused half-smile during the "56K
  modems" and broken-checkout segments; focused seriousness during the marketing-gap and burn-rate
  reveals; a warm, reflective expression for the closing lesson
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more animated during the technology-
  failure segment to underline the absurdity of the specific details
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# AI Image Prompts (for ChatGPT / Thumbnail-Creative-Director stage)

Reminder per channel convention: keep every prompt to ONE dominant visual idea. No real photos of
identifiable people (Malmsten, Leander, Hedelin). No text overlay baked into the generated image
beyond what's specified — title cards are added in editing. No literal recreation of any real, still-
existing brand's current logo (Boo.com/Fashionmall.com/etc. no longer operate as the same entity —
generic/abstracted visuals only, no specific trademarked wordmarks).

## Thumbnail — Concept 1 (Recommended): The Spinning Wheel
"An oversized, stylized browser-loading spinner or hourglass icon, frozen mid-rotation, centered
against a dark moody background with dramatic side lighting. Cinematic, high contrast, minimal color
palette (cold blue-gray with a single warm gold accent on the spinner itself). No text, no people, no
logos, no browser chrome or real website recreation."

## Thumbnail — Concept 2: The Modem Warning
"A simple retro-computer dialog box, plain monospace typography, dark background, subtle CRT-monitor
scan-line texture, evoking late-1990s web design without depicting any specific real website or logo.
Minimal, high contrast. No people, no real brand names."

## Thumbnail — Concept 3: The Fire-Sale Price Tag

# Thumbnail Concepts

Rule for this package (channel standard): one dominant idea per thumbnail, not the whole story
crammed in. The strongest single visual here is the "56K modem" absurdity — resist the urge to also
cram in the fire sale, the founders, and the book cover into one frame.

## Concept 1 (Recommended) — The Spinning Wheel
Dominant idea: a generic browser-loading spinner/hourglass icon frozen mid-spin, oversized, centered
against a dark background. One line of text: "$135M."

## Concept 2 — The Modem Warning
Dominant idea: a simple, retro-styled dialog box reading "56K MODEM REQUIRED" in plain monospace type,
nothing else on screen.

## Concept 3 — The Fire-Sale Price Tag
Dominant idea: a single oversized price tag reading "$375,000" in bold red, torn/crossed-out beneath a
faded, larger "$135,000,000" behind it.

## Concept 4 — The Countdown Clock
Dominant idea: a large stopwatch/clock graphic reading "18 MONTHS" in bold numerals against a dark
background, nothing else.

## Concept 5 — The Empty Cart
Dominant idea: a simple flat-icon shopping cart, mostly transparent/faded (representing the ~75% of
purchases that never completed), with a single bold "25%" beside it.

**Recommended:** Concept 1 (The Spinning Wheel) — instantly legible even to viewers with zero prior
knowledge of Boo.com, universally relatable (everyone has experienced a page that won't load), and
pairs a single striking number with a single striking image.

# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. The self-contained "$120 million vs. bare
hands" hook has strong standalone viral potential independent of any news cycle — no urgency premium,
hold for the best day/time slot.

## Target Audience
Broad crossover: founders/investors studying overengineering and diligence-failure risk, general
audience with strong pre-existing recognition of the Juicero story, and viewers drawn purely by the
extreme, self-contained numeral hook regardless of prior familiarity.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist. Also strong fit for a future "Silicon Valley's Funniest Failures"
playlist alongside package 009 (Clinkle) if that grouping gets built out.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + subscribe element. Consider cross-referencing package 009 (Clinkle) as a
thematically adjacent "hype without necessity" pairing if end-screen data supports it.

## Cards
Card at the Bloomberg video reveal (start of Segment 6, ~5:35) linking to the "Startup Autopsies"
playlist for viewers hooked by the demonstration; card at the mid-video CTA (~9:00) reinforcing the
"funniest collapse I've covered" framing; optional third card at the start of the Redemption Coda
(~9:15) linking directly to a pinned comment or community post about Doug Evans' current venture.

## End CTA
"Subscribe if you want the next autopsy in the library — and if you remember the Juicero video from
2017, or already knew what the founder built next, tell me in the comments. One more thing: is there a
more recent 'impressive engineering, unnecessary product' story I should cover next?"

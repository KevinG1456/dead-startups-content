### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** warm and grounded through the founder-background segment; building, slightly over-
  the-top triumphant momentum through the funding rocket; playful and mock-impressed through the "what
  $120 million actually built" spec list; neutral through the CEO change and price cut; comedic-sting
  energy on the Bloomberg reveal, followed by genuine "wow" restraint; dry and matter-of-fact through the
  response and refund offer; somber-but-light through the layoffs and shutdown; warm and uplifting
  through the redemption coda; reflective through the closing lesson
- **Speaking pace:** measured throughout, with a deliberate slow-down on the Doug Chertok quote and the
  "7.5 oz by hand vs. 8 oz by machine" numbers — both need room to land clearly
- **Emotional tone:** genuine respect for Doug Evans' real juice-industry background — not played as
  absurd or mockable; comedic, slightly incredulous delivery through the engineering-spec segment and
  the Bloomberg reveal; warm, unmistakably sincere delivery on the redemption coda
- **Facial expression:** neutral-focused baseline; a beat of genuine warmth during the founder-background
  segment; visible amusement (raised eyebrow, small smile) during the four-tons-of-force spec list and
  the Bloomberg reveal; visible care and precision when reading the Chertok quote; genuine warmth again
  on the Sprouting Company coda
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only; slightly more illustrative and animated during
  the spec-list segment and the hand-vs-machine demonstration
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout

# Title Options — Ranked

Anchored on Juicero: founded 2013 by Doug Evans (SF, previously co-founded/sold juice bar chain Organic
Avenue); $4M Seed (Oct 2013) → $15.8M Series A (Apr 2014) → $70M Series B (Mar 2016, Kleiner Perkins/
Google Ventures/Campbell Soup Co./Thrive Capital/Carmelo Anthony) — ~$118.5M total (commonly rounded to
~$120M), valuation over $270M. Launched Mar 2016 at $699: WiFi-connected press, 4 tons of force, QR-code-
verified proprietary Produce Packs ($5-7/pack subscription). Oct 31 2016: Evans steps down as CEO, Jeff
Dunn (ex-Coca-Cola North America) takes over. Jan 17 2017: price cut to $399. Apr 19 2017: Bloomberg video
shows the packs can be hand-squeezed — 7.5oz by hand in ~90 sec vs. 8oz from the machine, nearly identical.
Investor Doug Chertok on record: "There is no doubt the packs can be squeezed without the machine." Jul 14
2017: 25% staff layoffs. Sept 1 2017: suspends sales entirely, offers refunds, finds no buyer, shuts down
— 16 months after launch. Coda: Evans later founds The Sprouting Company, pitches on Shark Tank Oct 2025,
reportedly reaches a ~$12M annual run rate by early 2026.

## Recommended Title
**"$120 Million Raised. Your Bare Hands Could Do What the Machine Did."**

Reasoning: leads with the exact contradiction that made this story famous in the first place, stated as
plainly and concretely as possible — no company name needed to understand the hook, which maximizes cold/
browse-traffic CTR. Sets up the reveal (the Bloomberg hand-squeeze test) without giving away the funniest
specific detail (four tons of force, WiFi, QR codes) so there's still a reason to click through. Works
fully truncated on mobile at "$120 Million Raised." alone.

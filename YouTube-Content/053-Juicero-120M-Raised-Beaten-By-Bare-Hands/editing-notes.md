# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during explainer beats; hard snap-zoom on the cold-open split-screen (machine
  vs. hand, both producing juice) and on the "7.5 OZ BY HAND (90 SEC) vs. 8 OZ BY MACHINE" reveal
- Slow, deliberate push-in (not a snap) on the Doug Chertok quote card — this is the single most
  important "proof" beat in the video

## Motion Graphics
- Cold-open split-screen: sleek machine on one side, bare hand on the other, both producing a juice
  stream at roughly the same pace
- "This actually happened. On camera. In 2017." pattern-interrupt card, white text on black
- Generic juice-bar-to-startup-office transition graphic for the founding segment
- Funding-rounds bar chart, ascending (Seed → Series A → Series B), for the funding-rocket segment
- "$699 — FOUR TONS OF FORCE — WIFI REQUIRED" title card
- "NEW CEO — OCT 2016" title card
- "$699 → $399" title card
- The full hand-vs-machine demonstration replayed with real numbers on screen: "7.5 OZ BY HAND (90 SEC)
  vs. 8 OZ BY MACHINE"
- Refund-offer title card quoting Jeff Dunn directly
- "25% OF STAFF — JUL 2017" title card
- "SEPT 1, 2017 — 16 MONTHS AFTER LAUNCH" title card
- "THE SPROUTING COMPANY — ~$12M RUN RATE, 2026" title card
- Full-arc timeline graphic for The Actual Lesson: founding (2013) → funding rounds (2013-2016) → launch
  (Mar 2016) → CEO change (Oct 2016) → price cut (Jan 2017) → the video (Apr 2017) → layoffs (Jul 2017)
  → shutdown (Sept 2017) → The Sprouting Company (2020s-2025)

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, or quote is referenced (TechCrunch, Bloomberg,
  Fortune, CNN Business, Forbes, Crunchbase News)
- "On-record investor quote" standing lower third under the Doug Chertok quote
- "Total funding rounded — see description for exact figure" lower third if the $120M figure appears on
  screen without the $118.5M precision
- "Not independently verified — not used" note reserved only if the $32M R&D claim is ever referenced
  for context (otherwise omit entirely)

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and quotes
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic kitchen/juice imagery — no real readable
  logos/trademarks (Juicero) without rights clearance
- The cold-open split-screen should animate both streams starting at the same moment, landing on a hard
  freeze-frame comparison at the end
- The funding-rounds bar chart should animate each bar rising in sequence, dated as it appears

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve any movement for the split-screen demonstration,
  the funding bar chart, and the full-arc timeline in The Actual Lesson
- Hold the avatar completely still and centered during the Doug Chertok quote card and the "16 months
  after launch" shutdown reveal — no camera movement competing with either for attention

## Background Music
- Cold open: playful but suspenseful pulse, cutting to a comedic beat-drop silence on the pattern-
  interrupt card
- The Founder Who'd Done This Before: warm, grounded, small-business energy
- The Funding Rocket: building, slightly over-the-top triumphant momentum (intentionally a touch
  excessive, foreshadowing the absurdity)
- What $120 Million Actually Built: a light comedic undertone as the specs are listed
- The First CEO Change: neutral, transitional
- The Price Cut: uneasy, mildly deflating
- The Ninety Seconds That Ended It: comedic sting on the reveal, then a beat of genuine "wow" silence
- The Response: dry, matter-of-fact
- The Layoffs / The Shutdown: somber, but not heavy — this is a comedic story, not a tragic one
- The Redemption Coda: warm, uplifting, genuinely hopeful
- The Actual Lesson: neutral, clear, reflective
- Outro: return to a softened, playful version of the cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Soft "date stamp" SFX on each key date reveal throughout (2013 founding, 2013-2016 funding rounds, Mar
  2016 launch, Oct 2016 CEO change, Jan 2017 price cut, Apr 2017 video, Jul 2017 layoffs, Sept 2017
  shutdown, 2025 Shark Tank)
- A comedic "squeeze"/liquid SFX synced to the cold-open split-screen juice streams
- Sharp "cash register" SFX on the "$70 MILLION," "$120 MILLION," and "$270 MILLION" reveals
- A distinct "record scratch" or similar comedic stinger on the Bloomberg video reveal — this is the one
  moment in the package where a comedic stinger is appropriate and encouraged
- A duller, non-celebratory SFX on the layoff and shutdown title cards
- A warm, uplifting "chime" SFX on the Sprouting Company/Shark Tank reveal and the full-arc timeline's
  final beat in The Actual Lesson

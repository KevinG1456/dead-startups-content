# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — per this channel's standing rule. The obvious, strongest visual for
this story is the hand squeezing the juice packet next to the expensive machine — resist the urge to
also cram in the WiFi icon, the QR code, the four-tons-of-force detail, or the funding figure into the
same frame. Pick one.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a bare hand squeezing a juice packet, with a stream of juice visibly flowing — the
  expensive machine sits blurred/small in the background, clearly unused
- **Main subject:** the hand-squeeze reveal, the single most famous image from this story
- **Emotion:** disbelief / schadenfreude
- **Background:** dark, minimal kitchen-counter suggestion, machine silhouette blurred and small
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the hand and the juice stream
- **Camera angle:** close-up on the hand and packet, machine visible but clearly secondary
- **Composition:** hand/packet fills the dominant two-thirds of frame, machine silhouette small in a
  back corner, bottom-third title-safe space reserved
- **Props:** generic juice packet shape, generic unbranded juicer-machine silhouette, no real Juicero
  logo/branding
- **Thumbnail text:** `$120 MILLION. YOUR HANDS DID IT FOR FREE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif, high contrast
- **Color strategy:** warm gold/orange juice stream as the single bright accent, near-black/dark
  background, desaturated machine silhouette
- **Contrast strategy:** the bright juice stream draws the eye first, the small blurred machine in the
  background completes the joke without needing text to explain it

- **Image concept:** the hand-squeeze/juice-stream visual from Winning Concept above
- **Subject:** a hand squeezing a juice packet, machine blurred small in the background
- **Expression:** N/A (no face, hand-focused)
- **Objects:** juice packet, juice stream, small unbranded machine silhouette
- **Background:** dark, minimal kitchen-counter suggestion
- **Colors:** warm gold/orange juice stream, near-black background, desaturated machine
- **Lighting:** single spotlight on the hand and stream
- **Angle:** close-up, hand-focused
- **Composition:** hand fills most of frame, small machine silhouette in back corner, bottom-third text
- **Thumbnail text:** `$120 MILLION. YOUR HANDS DID IT FOR FREE.`
- **Why it works:** the hand-squeeze image IS the story — instantly recognizable to anyone who remembers

  abstract numeral graphics on this kind of story
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic, general startup-finance and "worst startup ideas" audience

## Notes on What NOT to Do
- Do not render a real, identifiable photorealistic likeness of Doug Evans, Jeff Dunn, or Doug Chertok
  in any thumbnail — use generic avatar/host imagery or graphic-led concepts only
- Do not put a real Juicero logo/wordmark or real product photography in generated thumbnail art — build
  the visual concept generically (unbranded juicer silhouette, generic packet shape) and add any verified
  real branding/archival photography in the editor with proper sourcing
- Do not stack the hand-squeeze visual AND the investor-quote text AND the four-tons-of-force detail into
  one thumbnail — pick one dominant idea per version, per this channel's thumbnail-simplicity rule

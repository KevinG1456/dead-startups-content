# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Given the strong mainstream name-recognition potential (especially if the Facebook/Winklevoss case is used), this title has above-average crossover potential beyond the core niche audience — consider additional promotion via general tech/business-news-adjacent channels if available.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) plus a broader general audience familiar with the underlying story from mainstream news/film coverage.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; consider a dedicated "Founder Disputes & Legal Cases" playlist if this format is repeated for other cases (Tinder, WeWork, etc.).

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + highest-performing prior autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the equity-checklist segment linking to a related founder-toolkit video (Title 010 or Title 011); card at the founding-story segment linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy, subscribe — I'm building a full library of these. And if you're currently co-founding something, get the equity agreement in writing before you need it."

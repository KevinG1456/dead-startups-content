# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the silhouettes-back-to-back reveal moment in the cold open

## Motion Graphics
- Silhouette figures animation (back to back, gavel between them) for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Timeline graphic: founding → initial partnership → falling out → lawsuit filed → settlement
- Equity checklist graphic building progressively during the practical-lessons segment

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a claim, date, or figure is referenced — critical for this legally sensitive topic; distinguish "per the complaint" vs. "per the settlement" explicitly in the lower third text itself where relevant
- "Alleged" vs. "Established" visual tag system used consistently throughout the video

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key dates and figures
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos or generic legal-document imagery (blurred/illegible, no real identifiable content without rights clearance)

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the timeline/checklist graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Founding section: warm, optimistic underscore (representing the early friendship/partnership)
- Falling Out section: tonal shift to unease, sparser instrumentation
- Autopsy/legal section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual timeline carry the tension
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Gavel-strike SFX on the lawsuit-filed and settlement markers on the timeline
- Soft page-turn sounds under document B-roll
- Avoid comedic stingers entirely — this video involves real, named individuals and a genuinely difficult personal/legal situation; tone should be respectful and measured throughout

# HeyGen Production

Maps `script.md` (Main Cut) to an Avatar V presenter-led production.

### Avatar Model
Avatar V

### Presenter Performance
- **Energy level:** controlled and serious; noticeably measured and careful given this involves real, named individuals and a genuinely difficult personal situation
- **Speaking pace:** measured; deliberate pauses before legal claims and figures
- **Emotional tone:** matter-of-fact authority for the timeline/legal segments; genuine empathy (not taking sides) during the Founding and Falling Out sections
- **Facial expression:** neutral-serious baseline throughout; no smirking or dismissive expressions — this is a real, sensitive human story
- **Gesture style:** minimal, controlled, emphasis-only
- **Camera presence:** direct eye contact throughout — builds the credibility this sensitive topic requires

### Scene Breakdown

**Scene 01 — Cold Open**
- Duration: 0:00–0:15 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll only (silhouettes/gavel animation)
- Script: "What actually happens when co-founders can't agree on who deserves credit?"
- Visual direction: full-screen graphic, no avatar frame

**Scene 02 — Hook**
- Duration: 0:15–0:45 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, press-clipping montage inset
- Performance direction: flat, serious delivery
- Visual direction: medium shot, dark neutral background

**Scene 03 — Introduction**
- Duration: 0:45–1:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera, source-list lower third with explicit "alleged vs. established" framing
- Performance direction: careful, precise, slightly slower than usual for the framing statement
- Visual direction: medium shot, checklist graphic animates in beside avatar

**Scene 04 — Story: The Founding**
- Duration: 1:30–4:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Mostly B-roll (generic silhouette recreations, press stills) with avatar VO, cut back on camera every ~20s
- Performance direction: warm, narrative pacing — genuine warmth reflecting the early friendship, without foreshadowing too heavily
- Visual direction: full-screen B-roll with avatar PiP during longer VO stretches

**Scene 05 — The Falling Out**
- Duration: 4:00–6:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar on camera alternating with complaint-excerpt-style graphics
- Performance direction: precise, careful, measured — clearly signaling "alleged" language throughout
- Visual direction: side-by-side split during claim summaries; full avatar frame during transitions

**Scene 06 — Mid-Video CTA**
- Duration: ~6:30 (inside Scene 05/06 transition) | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct address
- Performance direction: brief tonal lift, faster pace, then drop back into serious tone
- Visual direction: subscribe graphic animates in briefly

**Scene 07 — Why This Matters (Practical Segment)**
- Duration: 6:30–9:30 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame, direct-to-camera practical advice
- Performance direction: warmer, more direct and helpful tone — the most actionable segment
- Visual direction: minimal graphics, checklist bullets appear beside avatar

**Scene 08 — Story: The Lawsuit and Settlement**
- Duration: 9:30–11:30 | Avatar/B-roll: B-roll leads (courthouse imagery, generic document imagery), avatar VO under, cut on camera for key resolution lines
- Performance direction: slow down noticeably, quieter volume, respectful and measured
- Visual direction: darker grade, slower cuts

**Scene 09 — Final CTA + Outro**
- Duration: 11:30–13:00 | Avatar/B-roll: Avatar full-frame throughout
- Performance direction: return to controlled-authority tone, steady eye contact through sign-off
- Visual direction: end-screen elements animate in during final 5 seconds only

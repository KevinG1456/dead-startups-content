# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the founder-relationship-to-lawsuit contradiction. Avoid using real photos of named individuals unless usage rights are confirmed; default to silhouette/avatar treatment.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** two simple silhouette figures back-to-back (or facing away from each other), a gavel icon between them
- **Main subject:** the fractured-relationship visual metaphor
- **Emotion:** tension/betrayal
- **Background:** dark, minimal
- **Lighting:** single hard top light, one figure slightly more lit than the other for asymmetry
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** figures on either side, gavel centered, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** gavel icon
- **Thumbnail text:** `CO-FOUNDERS. THEN COURT.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** neutral gray silhouettes, single red accent on the gavel, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** the back-to-back positioning does the emotional communicating before any text is read

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** a simple handshake icon breaking apart into two separate hands pulling away
- **Text:** `THE LAWSUIT NOBODY EXPECTED`
- **Why it works:** the breaking-handshake metaphor instantly signals "this partnership ended badly"
- **CTR prediction:** high — universally legible visual metaphor
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the back-to-back silhouettes/gavel combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `CO-FOUNDERS. THEN COURT.`
- **Why it works:** direct, blunt statement of the arc paired with the visual proof in one glance
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/investigative expression, small gavel tag in the corner
- **Text:** `WHAT THE COURT DOCS SHOW`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; "court docs" signals primary-source rigor
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

## Complete Viral Script (Main Cut)

**Target length:** 11–14 minutes

### Cold Open (0:00–0:15)
- VO: "What actually happens when co-founders can't agree on who deserves credit?"
- Visual: two silhouette figures animate in back-to-back, gavel appears between them with a heavy SFX hit
- Pattern Interrupt: hard cut to silence, white text on black: "This actually happened. Here's what the court record shows."

### Hook (0:15–0:45)
- VO: "This is the story of `[Founder]` and the co-founders of `[Company]`. They started as friends with one idea. It ended in a lawsuit."
- Open loop: "By the end of this video, you'll understand exactly what was alleged, what actually happened, and what it settled for — straight from the court documents, not the popular version of the story."

### Introduction (0:45–1:30)
- Method statement: reconstructed from the actual legal complaint, court filings, and settlement coverage — sourced on screen
- Explicit framing beat: clearly distinguish allegations from established facts throughout

### Story — The Founding (1:30–4:00)
- How the idea came together, the initial partnership, the early dynamic between the co-founders
- Visual: generic silhouette/avatar recreations, press headlines from the founding era, timeline graphic begins
- Curiosity gap: tease the specific moment or decision that began the falling out without fully explaining it yet

### Main Content — The Falling Out (4:00–6:30)
- What allegedly happened — the specific claims in the complaint about how credit and equity were handled
- Visual: complaint excerpt-style graphics (paraphrased, not verbatim reproductions unless rights-cleared), timeline continues

### Why This Matters (6:30–9:30)
- Mid-video CTA (~6:30): "If you're co-founding something right now, the next part is exactly what you need. Subscribe for a new autopsy every week."
- Practical segment: the specific equity-agreement protections (vesting, written IP assignment, documented equity splits from day one) that could have prevented a dispute like this

### Story — The Lawsuit and Settlement (9:30–11:30)
- The lawsuit being filed, the legal process, and the eventual settlement — sourced precisely, with "reported" figures clearly marked as such where exact figures aren't confirmed
- Slow the pacing deliberately here

### Final CTA + Outro (11:30–13:00)
- VO: recap the one-sentence lesson — get co-founder agreements in writing from day one, before trust is tested
- "Subscribe — I'm building a full library of these"
- Engagement prompt: "Where do you land on this one? Comment below — keep it respectful, this involves real people."
- End screen: two related autopsies + subscribe

## Alternative Scripts

### Script Version A — Data-First / Analyst Style
Opens directly on the completed timeline graphic (founding → falling out → lawsuit → settlement), then rewinds to explain each stage. Measured, graphics-forward.

### Script Version B — Narrative / Documentary Style
Opens on the human moment — the specific interaction or decision that began the falling out — before any legal details appear. Slower pacing, cinematic.

### Script Version C — Fast-Cut / Listicle Style
Structured as "4 co-founder agreement mistakes this case reveals," counting down using the same facts reorganized around a practical-lessons hook. Strong Shorts-extraction potential.

---

## Audience Retention Optimization
- **Drop-off risk:** Main Content section (4:00–6:30) if the legal-claim explanation becomes overly dense or jargon-heavy
- **Fix:** paraphrase legal claims in plain language before adding specific legal terminology; cut every 20–30 seconds between talking head, document-style B-roll, and animated graphic
- **Faster pacing opportunity:** trim the Founding section if press coverage of the early partnership is thin
- **Curiosity increase opportunity:** move the falling-out tease earlier if early-retention data shows drop-off before 2:00
- **Recommended visual change:** show the timeline graphic partially in the cold open, fully revealed only at the end

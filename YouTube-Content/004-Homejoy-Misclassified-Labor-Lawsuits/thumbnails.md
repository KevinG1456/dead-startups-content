# Thumbnail Strategy

One dominant idea per thumbnail — the lawsuit-vs-company-size contradiction.

## Thumbnail Intelligence — Winning Concept
- **Core visual:** a gavel icon or simple courthouse-column silhouette next to a big "$40M" figure, connected by a cracking/breaking line
- **Main subject:** the legal-vs-financial contrast
- **Emotion:** disbelief / tension
- **Background:** dark, subtle legal-document texture at low opacity
- **Lighting:** flat, even
- **Camera angle:** N/A (graphic-led)
- **Composition:** dollar figure top-left, gavel/courthouse icon bottom-right, crack line running between them, title-safe space bottom third
- **Props:** gavel silhouette, simple crack/fracture graphic
- **Thumbnail text:** `4 LAWSUITS. $40M GONE.`
- **Text placement:** bottom third, bold sans-serif
- **Color strategy:** white/green dollar figure, red crack line, near-black background
- **Contrast strategy:** minimal palette so the two icons (money, law) read instantly

## Thumbnail Version A — Curiosity
- **Concept:** simple "4" numeral large and centered, tiny gavel icon beside it
- **Text:** `4 LAWSUITS TOOK DOWN A ROCKET SHIP`
- **Why it works:** small, specific number creates curiosity ("only 4?") against the scale of the company implied by the title
- **CTR prediction:** high — numeral-led thumbnail with an intriguing mismatch (small number, big consequence)
- **Best audience:** cold/browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version B — Shock (Recommended primary)
- **Concept:** the $40M/gavel/crack combo from Winning Concept above
- **Text:** `4 LAWSUITS. $40M GONE.`
- **Why it works:** pairs a legal cue and a financial cue in one glance, signals "this is a legal story, not just a money story"
- **CTR prediction:** highest of the three
- **Best audience:** search + browse traffic

## Thumbnail Version C — Authority
- **Concept:** face-led (avatar/host), serious/concerned expression, small gavel tag icon in the corner
- **Text:** `THE LAWSUIT THAT ENDED IT`
- **Why it works:** face-led performs best with subscribed audience; phrase implies a single decisive turning point worth learning
- **CTR prediction:** medium-high with returning viewers
- **Best audience:** subscribers, suggested placement

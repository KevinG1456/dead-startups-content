## Long Description
Every week on this channel, we reconstruct a funded startup's failure using primary sources — no speculation, just documents and decisions.

This week: Homejoy, the on-demand home cleaning marketplace founded by Adora Cheung and Aaron Cheung. Backed by significant venture funding and grown quickly through aggressive first-time-customer discounting, Homejoy shut down in July 2015 after reportedly failing to close a new funding round — with leadership publicly citing four lawsuits alleging cleaners were misclassified as independent contractors as a significant contributing factor.

This video breaks down both sides honestly: the underlying retention/discounting weakness in the business model, and the specific legal exposure that made new investors hesitant to fund the company through it. It's also a story that predates much of the Uber/Lyft/DoorDash classification litigation that followed — making Homejoy an early warning sign for the entire gig-economy category.

# Publishing Strategy

## Best Publishing Time
Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am ET, consistent with channel pattern. Consider timing near any current gig-economy classification news cycle for a relevance/search boost, if one is active at publish time.

## Target Audience
Core niche audience (founders, operators, VCs) plus a broader labor-rights/gig-economy-interested audience given the topical legal angle.

## Playlist
Add to "Startup Autopsies" playlist; consider a secondary "Gig Economy & Labor Risk" playlist if this angle is repeated.

## End Screen
Most recent autopsy + highest-performing prior autopsy; subscribe element.

## Cards
Card at the mid-video CTA linking to the "Startup Autopsies" playlist; card near the lawsuit-timeline segment linking to a related legal/labor-risk case study once published.

## End CTA
"If you want to see the next autopsy, subscribe — I'm building a full library of these." Consider adding: "If you're building a marketplace or gig-economy product, tell me in the comments how you're thinking about this risk."

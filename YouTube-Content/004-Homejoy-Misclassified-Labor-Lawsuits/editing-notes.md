# Video Editing Notes

## Zoom Suggestions
- Slow push-in on avatar during data-reveal beats; hard snap-zoom only on the two number reveal moments (cold open, final report card)

## Motion Graphics
- Dollar figure + gavel + crack-line animation for the cold open, mirroring the thumbnail concept
- Timeline graphic showing: founding → rapid growth → first lawsuit filed → subsequent lawsuits → funding round stalls → shutdown

## Lower Thirds
- Source citation lower-third whenever a number, date, lawsuit detail, or quote is referenced — this is a legally sensitive topic and citations should be especially visible
- Name/title lower-third for founders if photos or archival clips are used

## Animations
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and legal terms
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on any static press photos, court filing excerpts (blurred/illegible), or archival stills

## Camera Movement (HeyGen/live-action hybrid)
- Static avatar frame during explanatory beats; reserve movement for the timeline graphic layer

## Background Music
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse
- Rise section: restrained, slightly optimistic underscore
- Autopsy/legal-breakdown section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual timeline carry the tension
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse
- Outro: return to cold-open motif

## Sound Effects
- Gavel-strike SFX on each lawsuit marker appearing on the timeline
- Rubber-stamp thud on "SHUT DOWN" reveal
- Soft page-turn sounds under document/filing B-roll
- Avoid comedic stingers — tone is investigative and respectful of a genuinely contested legal/labor topic

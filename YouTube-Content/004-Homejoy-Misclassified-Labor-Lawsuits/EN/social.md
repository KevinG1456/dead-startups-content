It still shut down in July 2015. Why? A large share of discount-acquired customers never converted into full-price repeat customers — and at the same time, at least four lawsuits alleged Homejoy had misclassified its cleaners as independent contractors rather than employees.

Leadership reportedly pointed to those lawsuits as a significant factor in why a new funding round fell through. Homejoy's collapse predates much of the classification litigation that later became prominent with Uber, Lyft, and DoorDash.

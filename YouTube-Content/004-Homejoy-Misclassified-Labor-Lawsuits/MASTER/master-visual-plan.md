# Master Visual Plan — Homejoy

Shared across all six languages. Only on-screen TEXT is localized (numbers formatted per language
convention, lower-third labels translated) — the visual concepts, compositions, and shot list below
do not change by language. Consolidates `../ai-image-prompts.md` and `../editing-notes.md` into the
shared cross-language plan.

## Core Recurring Graphics (build once, reuse in every language cut with localized text overlays)
1. **Dollar-figure-vs-gavel cold-open graphic** ($40M figure, gavel icon, crack line) — cold open
   and recurring, mirrors the thumbnail concept.
2. **Growth-chart animation** — Rise section, first-time-customer growth curve.
3. **Founding/funding timeline** — founding → rapid growth → first lawsuit filed → subsequent
   lawsuits → funding round stalls → shutdown; partially revealed in the cold open, fully revealed
   at the end.
4. **"Who's classified how" diagram** — plain-language independent contractor vs. employee
   comparison, grounded in a concrete cleaner example rather than abstract legal language.
5. **Four-lawsuit case-marker graphic** — four labeled case-marker boxes in a row leading to a
   single "shutdown" endpoint icon.
6. **Autopsy Report Card** — end-graphic template (funding, cause of shutdown, verdict) — channel-
   signature asset, reused across languages with translated labels only.

## B-Roll / Stock Requirements
- Generic press-clipping/growth-chart imagery (no real Homejoy branding/logos without rights
  clearance — this is a defunct company).
- Generic app-screenshot imagery (recreated/generic, not real UI).
- Empty modern startup office at night with cleaning supplies stacked in a corner (per
  `../ai-image-prompts.md`).
- Blank/blurred legal document or court filing close-ups (illegible text, per
  `../ai-image-prompts.md`).

## AI Image Prompts (language-neutral — reuse across all six cuts)
See `../ai-image-prompts.md` for the full prompt set (thumbnail, background, illustration,
infographic, scene-artwork prompts). None of these prompts render real text or real branding, so
they require no localization — only the text overlaid in post-production changes per language.

## Animation Style
- Text-reveal (not fade) for all key numbers, dates, and legal terms, in every language.
- Ken Burns pan/zoom on static press photos, court filing excerpts (blurred/illegible), and
  archival stills.
- Gavel-strike SFX on each lawsuit marker appearing on the timeline; rubber-stamp thud on the
  "shutdown" reveal; soft page-turn sounds under document/filing B-roll — avoid comedic stingers,
  tone is investigative and respectful of a genuinely contested legal/labor topic, in every
  language.

## Music Direction (shared across languages)
- Cold open: tense, minimal pulse.
- Rise section: restrained, slightly optimistic underscore.
- Autopsy/legal-breakdown section: sparse, clinical, near-silent — let the visual timeline carry
  the tension.
- Fall section: slow, minor-key, sparse.
- Outro: return to cold-open motif.

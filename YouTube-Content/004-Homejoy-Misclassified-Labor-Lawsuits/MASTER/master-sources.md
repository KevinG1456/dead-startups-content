## What Every Localized Version Must Get Right, In Every Language
1. Funding stated as "roughly $38-40M," never a single false-precision number — use "$40M" only as
   the rounded headline figure in titles/thumbnails (established package convention), not as a
   claimed-precise figure in body prose.
2. Exactly **four** lawsuits — this framing is independently confirmed accurate per this channel's
   fact-check record; do not round up, down, or hedge the count itself (hedge the case *details*
   with `[VERIFY]` where noted, not the count of four).
3. Shutdown date: **July 2015** — do not substitute a different month or year.
4. Founders: **Adora Cheung and Aaron Cheung** — both names, spelled exactly this way, in every
   language; do not drop one founder or transliterate the names.
5. Present the retention/discounting weakness and the lawsuits as two compounding factors, never
   as a single-cause narrative in any language.
6. Currency: figures stay in US dollars ($) in every language — see `master-thumbnail.md` and each
   language's `thumbnail.md` for the localized numeral-formatting convention (e.g., German "40
   Mio. $", French "40 M$"). Do NOT convert to EUR/NOK/SEK — Homejoy is a US company and all
   original reporting is in USD; currency conversion would introduce inaccuracy the source
   material doesn't have.
7. Names stay unlocalized: "Homejoy," "Adora Cheung," "Aaron Cheung," "Y Combinator," "Google
   Ventures" — do not transliterate or translate proper nouns.
8. Do not fabricate or attribute a verbatim quote to Adora Cheung, Aaron Cheung, a cleaner, or an
   investor — see `master-research.md`'s "No Verbatim Founder Quotes" section.

# Master Editing Timeline — Homejoy

Shared structural timeline across all six languages. Localized scripts may run a beat faster or
slower naturally (German and Dutch compound phrasing tends to run slightly longer per idea than
English; Norwegian/Swedish tend to run close to English) — do NOT force identical timestamps across
languages. This is the shared blueprint, not a frame-locked cut. Chapter labels below are the
channel's established navigation markers for this title — reused verbatim (translated) in every
language's `description.md`, `subtitles.md`, and YouTube chapter markers.

```text
00:00 Cold Open — the four-lawsuits-vs-scale contradiction
00:15 The Contradiction (Hook) — introduce Homejoy, what happened, why it matters
00:45 How This Video Is Built (Sources)
01:30 The Rise — founding, Y Combinator, discount-driven growth
04:00 The Autopsy Begins — retention problem, classification question, lawsuits + funding
      consequence (three sub-beats within this one chapter block)
06:30 Mid-Video: Why This Matters If You're Building Something (CTA, inside chapter above)
09:30 The Fall — shutdown, human impact, broader gig-economy context
11:30 The One-Sentence Lesson (Final CTA + Outro)
12:30 What's Next
13:00 [target end, ±2 min naturally per language]
```

## Per-Language Pacing Notes
- Keep the Fall segment (09:30 onward) noticeably slower in EVERY language — this is a deliberate
  tonal shift in the master script, not just an English pacing choice.
- The Classification Question sub-beat (inside 04:00–09:30) is the highest drop-off risk in every
  language — localized scripts should not pad this section with extra explanation beyond the
  master beats; ground it in a concrete example instead.
- Chapter/timestamp labels in each language's `subtitles.md` and YouTube chapter markers should be
  translated, not left in English, even though the underlying timeline structure is shared.

WHAT ELSE HAPPENED THIS SESSION (2026-08-15, continued) — 026 through 030

Once the 025 lead was used up, five fresh, well-documented, famous-company
candidates were researched from scratch (no prior flagged-lead list to draw from)
and built as full packages, in parallel via five background agents:
- 026 (NEW) -> WeWork. The definitive overhype/governance-collapse story in the
  genre, already name-checked as a comparison in 025's own research ("the WeWork
  of apartments"). $47B peak valuation (highest of any company on this channel),
  2019 IPO collapse, Neumann's up-to-$1.7B exit package, SoftBank's ~$9.5B
  rescue, a second SPAC IPO in 2021 at $9B, then a real Nov 2023 Chapter 11 —
  but a REORGANIZATION, not a liquidation; the company still operates today at
  ~$750M. Coda: Neumann's new company Flow, backed by Andreessen Horowitz.
- 027 (NEW) -> 23andMe. A different failure shape: a 2023 data breach (credential
  stuffing, not a direct hack) that specifically targeted users by ancestry
  (Ashkenazi Jewish, Chinese) cascaded into a Sept 2024 mass board resignation,
  which cascaded into a March 2025 Chapter 11 filing. Twist ending: founder Anne
  Wojcicki bought back the core data assets via a nonprofit (TTAM Research
  Institute) for $305M in the bankruptcy auction.
- 028 (NEW) -> Bird. This channel's first HARDWARE unit-economics story (a
  physical scooter fleet, not software/leasing). SPAC IPO Nov 2021 at up to
  $2.3-2.5B, a Nov 2022 self-reported accounting restatement (overstated revenue
  from unpaid rides — not a fraud charge), NYSE delisting, Dec 2023 Chapter 11,
  and an April 2024 sale of its own assets to its own lenders (Third Lane
  Mobility) for ~$145M.
- 029 (NEW) -> Ozy Media / Carlos Watson. This channel's second REAL-CONVICTION
  fraud story (after 005/HeadSpin) — Watson was actually indicted, tried, and
  convicted by a jury in July 2024. Highest-sensitivity of this batch: his
  sentence was commuted by President Trump in March 2025, handled with strict
  neutrality (who/what/when only, zero political framing) throughout.
- 030 (NEW) -> MoviePass. The most viral unit-economics catastrophe in the genre
  (the $9.95 unlimited plan), paired with a genuine redemption arc: founder
  Stacy Spikes was fired BEFORE the reckless pricing decision, was never charged
  with anything, and later bought the brand back out of bankruptcy and built a
  real, profitable relaunch — a happier ending than almost anything else on this
  channel.

⚠️ THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE TOUCHING PACKAGE 022, 024, 025, 027, 028, OR 029 AGAIN

022 (Fast) is the highest-sensitivity package on the channel. Its founder has NOT
been charged or convicted of anything (unlike 005/HeadSpin, which has a real
conviction). Several claims in that package trace to one NPR investigation and are
handled with extra attribution care throughout every file. Read
022-Fast-580M-Valuation-600K-Revenue/sources-log.txt in full before editing
anything in that folder, even something that looks minor. There's also a standing
memory note on this (ask me to recall it, or it'll surface automatically).

024 (Olive AI) carries the same standard — founder Sean Lane not charged or
convicted, framed strictly as overhype-and-burn-rate, never "fraud/scam/lied."
It also has several individually-flagged uncertain figures (lifetime funding total,
Feb 2023 layoff count, exact business-sale dates, burn-rate claim, the "mostly
rules-based automation" characterization). Read
024-Olive-AI-4B-Valuation-Sold-For-Parts/sources-log.txt in full before editing.

025 (Sonder) carries a related but distinct standard: no founder here (Sonder's
Francis Davidson, Lyric's or Zeus Living's founders) has been charged or convicted
of anything either, BUT the sharpest material isn't about a founder's personal
history — it's Marriott's bankruptcy-court filing alleging Sonder threatened to
lock guests out of their rooms to force wind-down funding. That's one side's
litigation characterization, not adjudicated fact, and not Sonder's own account
(Sonder's own explanation is softer — "unanticipated delays and costs of
integration"). Every file that uses the Marriott allegation attributes it
explicitly and pairs it with Sonder's account; Shorts and social posts leave the
allegation out entirely, ironclad-facts-only, same pattern as 022. Read
025-Sonder-1.6B-Lost-Bankrupt-In-A-Week/sources-log.txt in full before editing.

027 (23andMe) carries a data-sensitivity standard, not a founder standard: Anne
Wojcicki is not charged with anything, but the 2023 breach specifically targeted
users by ancestry (Ashkenazi Jewish, Chinese) — that detail must stay factual
and soberly sourced everywhere it appears, never sensationalized, and Shorts has
its own explicit guardrail note. Read
027-23andMe-6B-To-Bankruptcy-Data-Breach/sources-log.txt before editing.

028 (Bird) carries an accounting-specific caution: a real Nov 2022 SEC filing
disclosed overstated revenue, but no individual was criminally charged for it in
this research pass — "fraud" language does NOT apply to that event, only
"self-reported accounting restatement." Read
028-Bird-Scooter-SPAC-To-Bankruptcy/sources-log.txt before editing.

029 (Ozy Media) is the highest-sensitivity package in this batch — the OPPOSITE
profile from 022/024/025: Carlos Watson WAS criminally convicted, so "fraud"

March 2025. That fact must be stated with strict who/what/when neutrality only —
zero speculation about motive, zero political framing — in every file, every
time. Also keep Samir Rao (who did the YouTube impersonation) distinct from
Watson (who was convicted) — don't let the two men blur together. Read
029-Ozy-Media-Carlos-Watson-Fraud-Conviction/sources-log.txt in full before
editing anything in that folder, even something that looks minor.

030 (MoviePass) has no founder-sensitivity issue (Spikes was never charged) but

STANDING RULES TO REMEMBER

- Thumbnail prompts: one dominant idea, not the whole video crammed in.
- Every package gets a sources-log.txt — that's where the real citations live, not
  scattered across the 16 content files.
- Long agent runs building a package's content files can lose their connection
  partway through (happened to 025's rebuild, then 027 and 030 in the 026-030
  batch) — always check which files actually landed on disk before assuming a
  build finished, and just resume the same agent for the remainder rather than
  restarting from scratch.
- If a headline number can't be sourced, drop it — don't force the original framing
  to match reality. Every pivot this session followed that rule (001, 003, 007,
  008, 015 all dropped or adjusted their original placeholder numbers).
- If a case involves serious claims about a living, non-convicted person (see 022),
  attribute every such claim on screen every time, use the source's own careful
  language rather than a sharper secondhand paraphrase, and keep short-form assets
  (Shorts, social posts) restricted to the ironclad facts only.
- Fact-Check Reminder.txt in this same root folder is the master log — every
  package's entry is there, organized by FIXED / FILLED IN / CLEAN.
